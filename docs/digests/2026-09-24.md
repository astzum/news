# Thu 24 Sep 2026

**The three that matter:** Anthropic ran ~950 Claude agents across DNA sequence databases and
came out with a phage enzyme system that then survived wet-lab work. Radicle disclosed that
every released version sends repository traffic in cleartext and can be tricked into serving
private repos to an impersonated peer, with no patch available. And Colibrì, a pure-C engine,
runs a 744B MoE in about 10 GB of RAM by streaming its experts off NVMe.

## AI research + releases

### Claude agents found a CRISPR-adjacent enzyme system and the lab work held up
By Anthropic's own account, its life sciences group ran ~950 agents for 21 hours and 210 million
tokens: 200,000 reverse transcriptases narrowed to 3,500 candidate systems, then 20. Lab work
confirmed one — array-associated RTs, a phage RT plus repeat array — expresses distinct short
RNAs. Function unknown, and the RT itself was already described; the system's structure is what's
new.
[announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)

### GPT-6 Astra broke a 1941 Enigma message that had resisted since 2005
The MVUEH intercept — German Army, 10 July 1941, 82 letters. Carter Leffer pointed the model at
Crypto Cellar's unbroken list; it picked the target itself, wrote its own Enigma simulator and
Bombe, cribbed on a repeated ROSENOW, and recovered wheel order 253 rather than the daily key's
512. Validated 15 September. The plaintext turns out to near-duplicate a neighbouring message.
[write-up](https://www.cryptocellar.org/bgac/the-mvueh-break.html)

### Google's new TTS models take direction line by line
Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS shipped 23 September in the Gemini API and AI Studio.
Describe a voice in prose or clone one from 30 seconds of audio, with per-line emotion direction;
100+ languages, SynthID watermarking, top of Hume's Voice Design benchmark at 71.4. Replication is geoblocked in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, India, Illinois and Texas. No
pricing published.
[announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-text-to-speech/)

## Dev + infra

### Radicle's private repos have been readable on the wire since day one
Two critical protocol flaws, reported in June and August, disclosed 23 September. Traffic goes
out in cleartext, and peer authentication can be bypassed by impersonating an allow-listed Node
ID — together, read in transit, then fetch whole private repos on demand. Every released version
is affected and there is no patch; the fix swaps the custom Noise protocol for iroh. Stop using
private repos.
[disclosure](https://radicle.dev/2026/09/23/disclosure-of-vulnerability-in-network-protocol)

### Claude Code's AGENTS.md support was off whenever telemetry was off
Added in 2.1.277 as a built-in plugin gated on a remote feature flag: with telemetry disabled the
flag never arrives, so the file silently doesn't load — which is exactly how CI runners are
configured. Fixed in 2.1.281, and the docs now list the remaining cases, including the first
session after an upgrade. On older versions, put `@AGENTS.md` in a CLAUDE.md.
[report](https://blog.szypowi.cz/p/claude-code-reads-agents.md-only-when-telemetry-is-on/) ·
[docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)

### Parquet got a float encoding that decodes about 10x faster
ALP landed in parquet-format 2.14.0 this month. It encodes decimal-ish doubles — money,
coordinates, measurements — in batches sharing an exponent and factor, with exceptions for values
that don't round-trip. Against PLAIN or BYTE_STREAM_SPLIT plus ZSTD it decodes roughly 10x faster
and does single-value reads thousands of times faster, at a slightly worse ratio. Only the
Rust `parquet` 60.0.0 crate has it yet.
[post](https://parquet.apache.org/blog/2026/09/22/alp-adaptive-lossless-floating-point-encoding-in-apache-parquet/)

## Homelab

### One C file runs a 744B model in 10 GB of RAM by streaming experts off NVMe
Colibrì keeps GLM-5.2's dense part — ~17B params at int4, 9.9 GB — resident, and leaves the
19,456 routed experts (~19 MB each, ~370 GB) on disk, staged by a per-layer LRU with
one-layer-ahead prefetch. Apache-2.0, no BLAS, no Python, no GPU required. The honest number from
its own profiling page: 2.9 tok/s on a CPU box.
[repo](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

### Seattle voted to ban personalised grocery pricing
The Fair Pricing and Transparency Act (CB 121267) passed 22 September and goes to Mayor Wilson.
It bars grocery retailers from setting prices off personal data — browsing history, real-time
location, inferred income, family size, health conditions. Note the direction: it constrains
sellers, not the shopping agents shown yesterday to quote wealthier-seeming users higher.
[press release](https://advocacy.consumerreports.org/press_release/seattle-city-council-votes-to-ban-surveillance-pricing-in-sale-of-groceries/)
