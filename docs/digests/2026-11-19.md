# Thu 19 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** A memory-corruption bug in OpenJDK's hand-written
Base64 assembly gives unauthenticated code execution on Jenkins, Jira,
Confluence and Keycloak in their default configs. nginx shipped ten security
advisories in one release, two of them remote code execution over HTTP/2.
Codeberg's domain vanished from its registrar account with no warning and took
the whole forge offline.

## Dev + infra

### Sixty-five bytes of Base64 get you RCE on most Java servers
CVE-2026-114938 is an out-of-bounds write in HotSpot's x86-64 Base64 intrinsic:
64 `A`s and an `=` make it compute a negative length and write 62 bytes past
the array. Calif demonstrated pre-auth RCE in Keycloak, Jira, Confluence,
Artifactory and Jenkins. Only OpenJDK 17.0.6+ on AVX-512 VBMI CPUs. Mitigate
with `-XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics`.
[source](https://calif.io/java4shell/)

### nginx ships ten advisories at once, two of them unauthenticated RCE
Wiz, Rapid7, Xint, Ikotas Labs and Metnir independently found CVE-2026-123633
and CVE-2026-123007. Both need HTTP/2; Wiz rates both 9.2 and got code
execution against nginx serving plain static files. The flow-control bug dates
to 1.9.5 in 2015. Fixed in 1.31.9 and 1.30.7 — where nginx's own page rates the
use-after-free merely "medium".
[source](https://www.wiz.io/blog/nginx-http2-rce-vulnerabilities)

### Codeberg's domain disappeared out of its registrar account
codeberg.org went to `clientHold` and the forge dropped off the internet for
roughly three hours on 18 November. NameSilo later confirmed a suspension and
forwarded the case to its abuse team; Codeberg says it got no email, saw no
suspicious logins and had not missed a renewal. Standing up codeberg.eu as a
fallback was judged a multi-month job.
[source](https://social.anoxinon.de/@codebergstatus/117636437840688845)

### Quad9 leaves France on 24 November, with Belgium queued behind it
A French court fined the Swiss foundation daily for not blocking copyright
domains, so it is withdrawing its resolver from the country entirely —
including from the hospitals, schools and government bodies it cannot notify.
Cisco already pulled OpenDNS from France, Portugal and Belgium. A Belgian court
has ordered Cloudflare, Google and Cisco to start blocking by early January.
[source](https://blog.cloudflare.com/quad9-withdrawal-from-france/)

## AI research + releases

### A 1.8M-parameter model clears Sudoku-Extreme with 345x less compute
Energy-Based Reasoners learn an energy function over latent reasoning states
and treat looped updates as gradient descent on it, so the model scores its own
candidates and knows when to stop — no external verifier. 99.9% on
Sudoku-Extreme, best-in-class solution coverage on N-Queens and graph
colouring. The headline doing the rounds says 385x; the paper says 345x.
[source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08977)

### Cerebras moves prefill onto AMD GPUs for 5x the tokens per megawatt
Prompt processing runs on Instinct GPUs, decode stays on wafer-scale CS-3s, and
the KV cache moves between them over RDMA. The 5x is measured against a
CS-3-only baseline on a GPT-OSS-120B endpoint, not against GPUs. AWS, Fireworks
and two others have early access; broad rollout is promised "by the end of
2027".
[source](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/cerebras-launches-disaggregated-inference)

## Homelab

### Redict is deprecated; move your containers to Valkey
Drew DeVault has ended the copyleft Redis fork at 7.3.6, saying Valkey won the
diaspora. He still recommends against going back to Redis despite its return to
a copyleft licence, because the contributor licence agreement makes a second
rug-pull easy. Migration is `mv /etc/redict.conf /etc/valkey.conf` and a
different image.
[source](https://redict.io/posts/2026-11-17-redict-deprecated/)

## Science + space

### Webb watched a star eat its companion 47 days after the blast
AT 2025abao in Andromeda: a 0.7-solar-mass star spiralled into a supergiant
seven times the Sun's mass, 350 times its radius and 18,000 times brighter, and
was dragged into its core. Webb's mid-infrared data 47 days after the eruption
peak — the earliest follow-up yet for such an event — gave the companion its
first mass estimate.
[source](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasa-data-clinches-first-of-its-kind-stellar-merger/)
