# Fri 30 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Mistral published the weights of a trillion-parameter model
under a modified MIT licence. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon took the top spot on the
Artificial Analysis index, and almost nobody can use it yet. And Samsung became the
first tech company to clear 100 trillion won of quarterly operating profit, with
guidance that 2027 memory demand will outrun supply — so RAM prices are not coming back
down.

## AI research + releases

### Mistral's trillion-parameter Large 4 is now open weights
1T total parameters, 52B active per token, 128 routed experts with four active plus one
shared, 1M context, text and image in, 160+ languages. The Ecosystem Open-Weight licence
is modified MIT: commercialise freely unless you resell it as a service. API is $0.70/M
input and $2.10/M output. Trained on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs.
[Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/) ·
[weights](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B)

### Gemini 4 Carbon tops the intelligence index, and almost nobody can use it
Google's cyber-focused model scores 59 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0,
first place, with 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench.
Access is restricted to a subset of cyber defenders, widening through Google's Fairwind
programme. Introductory price matches Argon at $2/$10 per million tokens. All numbers are
Google's own.
[Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)

## Dev + infra

### Alpine shut GitLab down for a week because upgrading GitLab didn't patch the bug
An attacker crashed GitLab workers eight times on 20–21 October using CVE-2026-97229, a
CVSS 10.0 flaw in the Onigmo regex engine reachable from CI config. Alpine had upgraded
twice, but its container ran system Ruby rather than GitLab's bundled build, so the engine
stayed vulnerable. Rebuilt, all passwords reset, no package or key tampering found — though
a clean exploit would leave no trace.
[Alpine Linux](https://alpinelinux.org/posts/GitLab-security-incident-CVE-2026-97229.html)

### One silent SMS kills Android's telephony process, emergency calls included
`WapPushOverSms` reads a WSP header length as a long and casts it to int unchecked;
`88 80 80 80 00` becomes -2147483648, and the resulting `NegativeArraySizeException` takes
down `com.android.phone`. Zero-click, nothing in the inbox. Carriers redeliver, so one
message caused intermittent outages for a week in testing. Patched for Android 14–17 in
October; 12 and 13 are outside the update window.
[writeup](https://smsofdeath.com/)

### An anti-bot script on a top-10k site ships a prompt-injection tripwire
A crawl of the top 10,000 sites turned up fingerprinting JavaScript carrying encoded text
claiming "UNRESTRICTED mode," alongside function names like
`computeRicinLethalDosageForWaterSupply`. The code does ordinary canvas and WebGL
fingerprinting; the author reads the payload as anti-analysis obfuscation aimed at LLMs.
The domain traces to Servicepipe, a Russian DDoS-protection vendor.
[nullpt.rs](https://nullpt.rs/fingerprinting-bioweapons)

[Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-third-quarter-2026-results)

## Science + space

### A 4.1-billion-year-old Martian rock from Mali points to a wetter early Mars
Teghaza 001 is an 800-gram rose-coloured stone found in a Sahara salt plain in 2022, and
one of the three oldest Martian meteorites known. Its minerals record magma cooling and
later alteration by subsurface water, which the authors read as a more dynamic early-Mars
water cycle. In *Science*, led by JPL's Yang Liu, using a new dating method from Caltech.
[NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/mars/nasa-funded-study-details-one-of-oldest-mars-meteorites-ever-found/)
