# Wed 25 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** OpenAI and Anthropic each published an AI-written,
Lean-checked proof that VP ≠ VNP, hours apart, and neither has been read by a
human mathematician. A measurement paper finds PyTorch's KL divergence
significantly wrong across most of its useful range. Stalwart's mail server now
speaks Exchange's protocols in the AGPL build.

## AI research + releases

### Two labs posted AI-written proofs of VP ≠ VNP, hours apart
Anthropic's Lean 4 formalisation landed 23 Nov 20:04 UTC: Hamiltonian cycle
circuits need more than m^(c(log m)^(1/3)) gates over every field. OpenAI's
manuscript, dated the 23rd, was committed at 01:20 on the 24th with a
border-circuit bound of exp(Ω((log n)^(5/4))). Both say no human has checked the
proof. Neither settles P vs NP.
[Anthropic](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/main/vp-vnp) · [OpenAI](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/VP-and-VNP-are-different-November-23-2026)

### PyTorch's KL divergence is wrong where it matters most
The negative values people have been clamping to zero were the visible part.
Across 8.5M certified inputs, 18 closed-form families and `F.kl_div`, over 85%
of FP32 values carry ≥1% error near KL 1e-7, and ~30% go negative below that.
One extra precision level does not fix it. Affected: distillation (up to 97%
top-1 disagreement), quantised layer rankings, GPT-2 circuit scores.
[source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30835)

## Dev + infra

### OpenAI and Cisco open-sourced eBPF stopgaps for unpatched boxes
Shirudo lets an operator write a "shield" — an eBPF program that blocks the
behaviour an exploit needs while legitimate requests still pass — test it, then
deploy it to a running service with no restart. Their demo blocked a
Java4Shell attempt against live Elasticsearch. The hard part is distinguishing
exploit from request when both use the same syscall.
[source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo)

### Kubernetes v1.38 removes the kubectl WebSocket escape hatches
`KUBECTL_REMOTE_COMMAND_WEBSOCKETS` and `KUBECTL_PORT_FORWARD_WEBSOCKETS` go
away; strip them from CI now, since behaviour is unchanged anyway. `kuberc`
graduates to stable and its v1 API drops `name` as an alias for `command` in
credential-plugin allowlists. cgroup v1 still boots behind `failCgroupV1:
false`, but treat that as a countdown.
[source](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/11/23/kubernetes-v1-38-sneak-peek/)

## Homelab

### Stalwart 1.2.0 answers Outlook as if it were Exchange
MAPI over HTTP, EWS, ActiveSync and Autodiscover are all in 1.2.0 — users type
an address and password, and get mail, calendars, contacts, tasks and notes in
one account from clients that never learned IMAP properly. Crucially this is in
the AGPL Community edition. Exchange 2016 and 2019 lost support in October 2025
and Subscription Edition is the last major version.
[source](https://stalw.art/blog/microsoft-exchange-replacement/)

## Hardware

### A sub-$2,000 32GB Radeon loses 3:1 to a 5090, which is the point
ServeTheHome ran Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on ASUS's Turbo Radeon AI PRO
R9700. The RTX 5090 leads by about 3:1 at high concurrency and 2:1 in code
generation at low user counts — so two 300W R9700s are a defensible answer at
current prices. Note the flagship ships 32GB, less than the 48GB W7900 it
succeeds.
[source](https://www.servethehome.com/asus-turbo-radeon-pro-ai-r9700-review/)

## Science + space

### Homo erectus was in Chinese rainforest 184,000 years ago
Nine teeth from two Guangxi caves, dated 324–184 ka off 43 radiometric ages,
put H. erectus in mainland Asia long after the previous ~400 ka limit — and in
closed rainforest, an environment assumed to be ours alone. Palaeoproteomics
assigns four teeth to a previously unsampled population; a fifth matches
nothing known, which the authors read as possible Denisovans alongside them.
[source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11155-6)
