# Wed 28 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** An unreleased OpenAI model has produced a paper claiming the
Exponential Time Hypothesis is false, and three complexity theorists are checking it. An
agent swarm formalised the classification of finite simple groups in Lean in 24 days,
finding an error in the published proof on the way. And Project Zero's six-month
head-to-head has an LLM bug-finder out-scoring Fuzzilli on V8, 45 actionable bugs to 37.

## AI research + releases

### OpenAI's internal model has written a refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis
The paper gives a deterministic algorithm that exactly counts the satisfying assignments of
any k-CNF in polynomial space and 2^(n/(log n)^(1/2+o(1))) time; the 3-SAT case is what
refutes ETH. Dated 17 October, revised 25. A working group led by Russell Impagliazzo,
Ramamohan Paturi and Ryan Williams is examining it, and the materials went public early at
their request.
[preprint](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf)

### An agent swarm formalised the classification of finite simple groups in 24 days
43.1 million lines of Lean, 2.05M declarations, 100 milestones, drawn from 715 papers and
books — the first-generation proof, kernel-checked end to end. Axiom Math opened the Slack
channel on 1 October and called it done on the 26th. The agents also found a false equality
in an Aschbacher–Smith lemma, with SL₂(5) as the counterexample.
[Axiom Math](https://axiommath.ai/research/the-enormous-theorem-formalized/) ·
[proof map](https://cfsg.axiommath.ai/)

### Six months on V8: the LLM found more bugs than the fuzzer, and mostly different ones
Fuzzilli logged 37 actionable vulnerabilities, BigSleep 45, with about 20 in common. Asked to
rediscover historic Fuzzilli bugs, BigSleep got roughly 62%, up to 70% with different
prompting. Samuel Groß's read: fuzzers still win on non-local and optimiser-induced bugs,
LLMs on multi-step logic and newly added language features. Neither was tuned for cost, so
no cost-per-bug number.
[Project Zero](https://projectzero.google/2026/10/fuzzing-v-AI.html)

## Dev + infra

### Ghostty compresses idle scrollback and parses ASCII 5.7× faster
Seven months, 172 contributors, 2,889 commits. Hidden scrollback is compressed while the
terminal is idle for 70–90% less resident memory, so the default limit went from 10MB to
50MB. Parser throughput 128 → 725 MB/s; `cat` of a 150MB file on macOS 1.5s → 566ms. Ten
security advisories fixed, including a paste-protection bypass on carriage returns.
[Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/docs/install/release-notes/1-4-0)

### A Pi Zero with the Pi 4's SoC is coming, and so is a Pi 4 Model A

No prices, no dates, no official word.

## Science + space

### A 237-million-year-old amphibian lived its whole life in open sea
Two near-complete skeletons from Guizhou, 74cm long and almost entirely armoured, named
*Gerrothorax maritimus*. Strontium isotopes in the bones match contemporaneous seawater, and
the fossils lie articulated far from any land mass of the time. No living amphibian is
exclusively marine; the authors argue the body armour pushed salt handling onto gill and gut.
[Nature Ecology & Evolution](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-026-03209-9)
