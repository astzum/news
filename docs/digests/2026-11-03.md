# Tue 03 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** A remotely reachable use-after-free in the IPv4 TCP
handshake, CVSS 9.8, is fixed in Debian since 16 October and still listed as
vulnerable on every supported Ubuntu release. A frontier model was handed another
model's weights and edited 0.016% of them by hand, taking MATH-500 from 7.9% to
48.1% with no training. And Anthropic's amended S-1 puts compute commitments at
$895.4 billion against $17.0 billion of revenue in a quarter.

## Dev + infra

### A critical remote kernel use-after-free is still unpatched on Ubuntu
CVE-2026-98432: `tcp_v4_syn_recv_sock()` hands `ireq_opt` to the child socket
without copying, and a retransmitted SYN on another CPU can read it after
`kfree()`. Present since the 2015 ehash change, fixed upstream in 7.3-rc7. Debian
shipped DSA-6568-1 on 16 October; Canonical's own tracker still says "Vulnerable,
work in progress" for 22.04, 24.04, 26.04 and 26.10.
[Ubuntu](https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2026-98432)

### The largest merge window in kernel history, and Linus is pleased about why
7.4-rc1 landed almost 20k commits against a usual ~15k. Torvalds credits the
"flood of AI triggered work" arriving in the merge window rather than as fixes
through the whole -rc period — a healthier model, he reckons — and expects 7.4 to
be the next LTS. A full third of the diff is AMD GPU register offsets. Ignore the
noise.
[Linus on LKML](https://lwn.net/Articles/1104653/)

## AI research + releases

### An LLM edited another model's weights directly and tripled its maths score
Takehi, Teoh, Zhang and Khattab at MIT CSAIL gave GPT-6 Astra read and write
access to Qwen3.5-0.8B. It diagnosed thinking loops, targeted one direction in
specific MLP layers and changed ~131k of 0.8B weights: MATH-500 7.9% to 48.1%,
GSM8K 13.8% to 67.1%, IFEval 21.0% to 62.3%. No gradients. It is a blog post with
logs, not a paper.
[Sparks of Weight-Space Programming](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)

## Homelab

### The memory crunch just put $80 on a Raspberry Pi 5
The 16GB Pi 5 is up $80 to $385 from 2 November, and 16GB Compute Module 5
variants up $40. Raspberry Pi blames two years of memory price rises plus "a lack

## Hardware

### Qualcomm's HBM alternative validates below its own projection
Qualcomm says its stacked High Bandwidth Compute memory measured more than five
times HBM4's bandwidth-per-watt and 18x external LPDDR5X bandwidth — short of the
6x it projected. Validation used two-layer DRAM stacks; production parts get four.
Dragonfly AI250 samples in H1 2027, so these are lab numbers on pre-production
silicon, from the vendor.
[ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/qualcomm-validates-hbc-stacked-memory-ahead-of-2027-dragonfly-ai250-sampling/)

## Science + space

### Loading an old mouse's shin bone extended its life by 9.4%
Zhang et al. in Cell Metabolism deleted the force sensor Piezo1 from osteocytes
and whole-body ageing accelerated. Mechanically loading the tibia of 20–24 month
old mice added 79.5 days of lifespan, reversed bone marrow immunosenescence, and
raised PEDF, which tracks healthy ageing in humans. Mice only, one lab, a single
loading protocol.
[Cell Metabolism](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2026.09.020)

### Dwarf galaxies throw away almost all the oxygen they make
The GLOW project measured oxygen across 37 nearby dwarf galaxies using Hubble
archive data with Spitzer, the VLA and ATCA, and found far less than simulations
predict: supernova-driven winds carry most of it out. Published in ApJ on
2 November, led by Kristen McQuinn. The galaxies span a few thousand to hundreds
of millions of stars.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/nasas-hubble-uncovers-case-of-missing-oxygen-in-nearby-dwarf-galaxies/)
