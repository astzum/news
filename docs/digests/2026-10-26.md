# Mon 26 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** C++26 failed its ISO ballot over contract assertions, which
has never happened before. German retail DDR4 rose 19% in one month while DDR5 finally
flattened. And Cerebras is serving a three-trillion-parameter model entirely out of
on-chip SRAM.

## Dev + infra

### C++26 failed its DIS ballot
Robert Seacord posted the tally: 12 of 20 voting P-members in favour — 60% against a
required 66.7% — and 9 negative votes from 22 member bodies, where 41% is far past the
25% ceiling. The objection is contract assertions, which went into the draft earlier
this year over sustained opposition. A first for C++.
[Robert Seacord](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/robertseacord_balloting-has-completed-for-c26-unfortunately-activity-7519795140887035918-5PWL)

### A memory-safe systems language with no heap, no GC and no lifetime annotations
Wouter van Oortmerssen designed Goose years ago and never had time to build it; the
README credits Claude Fable with the compiler implementation, benchmarking and docs —
about thirty thousand lines of C++. Every dynamic value lives inline on
compiler-managed data stacks, so references need no annotations. Prebuilt binaries for
Linux, macOS and Windows.
[Goose](https://aardappel.github.io/goose/)

## Hardware

### DDR5 has stopped climbing; DDR4 and graphics cards have not
3DCenter's monthly index of German retail best prices has DDR5 up only 2.7% in a month,
but still 5.6× its July 2025 level. The spread is the news: DDR4 jumped 19.0% in that
single month, SODIMM 6.8%, internal SSDs 9.6%, and graphics cards 15.6% — now 1.65×
pre-crisis. Hard drives barely moved.
[3DCenter](https://www.3dcenter.org/artikel/speicherkrise-preisindex-oktober-2026)

### Three trillion parameters across 59 wafers, with no HBM in the path
Cerebras splits Kimi K3 one or two layers to a wafer — 35 for the linear-attention
layers, 24 for the dense ones — so every weight sits in on-chip SRAM and only
activations cross between chips, about two microseconds per RDMA hop. 4-bit weights cut
storage from 12TB to 1.5TB. Claimed: 1,000–2,000+ tokens/sec.
[Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/how-we-deploy-trillion-parameter-frontier-models-on-cerebras)

## Science + space

### Swift stopped observing after 22 years and reenters within weeks
Launched November 2004 for a two-year mission, it made its final observation on 20
October. Below 171 miles the drag is now too strong to hold a target steady. The tally:
over 2,000 gamma-ray bursts, more than 1,400 supernovae, roughly 7,000 papers, 1.5
million UV/optical images, and first-light-on-target in about a minute.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/swift/nasas-swift-mission-ends/)

### The first individualised mRNA cancer therapy to pass a Phase 3 has its numbers out
Presented at ESMO on Saturday: at 24.6 months median follow-up, intismeran autogene plus
pembrolizumab cut recurrence or death by 42% against pembrolizumab alone (HR 0.58,
n=1,137) — 82.2% versus 70.0% recurrence-free at two years. Distant metastasis or death
fell 38%. Overall survival is immature and missed significance (HR 0.77, p=.33).
[OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/adjuvant-intismeran-autogene-plus-pembrolizumab-improves-rfs-and-dmfs-in-melanoma)

## AI research + releases

### A fine-tuned Inception v3 beat two frontier models at classifying skin lesions
Magnus Lynch, a dermatologist at Guy's, ran GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 against the
CNN on 200 biopsy-confirmed lesions from ISIC's MILK10k, six diagnostic categories. CNN
62%, both LLMs 42%; on the 30 melanomas, 13 correct versus 8 and 11. It is a blog post
with data and code attached, not a paper.
[Lynch Lab](https://lynchlab.net/llm-skin-cancer)
