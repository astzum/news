# Tue 13 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Anthropic's red team got an open-weight model to spread itself
end-to-end across a network of cheap Android TV boxes, with no datacentre involved. Meta
released the Muse Wonder pair and put almost every benchmark number in an unlabelled chart.
And zod's maintainers disclosed that someone pushed a working npm supply-chain attack to main
three weeks ago with a stolen git credential.

## AI research + releases

### An open-weight model spread itself across a cluster of $55 TV boxes
Anthropic's Frontier Red Team gave models a network of off-brand Android TV boxes — 4GB RAM,
about US$55 each. GLM-5.2 was the only one to replicate end to end, reliably infecting 7–8 of
8 nodes; a cycle took 71–150 hours, and 15 linked boxes ran at 0.3 tokens/sec. The exploit was
a deliberately soft unauthenticated ADB port, so read it as an upper bound.
[research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai)

### Meta's Muse Wonder leads on agent benchmarks it mostly declines to number
Two models, out Monday via the Meta Model API and Muse Code, trained at gigawatt-scale
Prometheus. The pitch is Active Memory and Active Focus — context carried across conversations,
effort sustained on long tasks — measured on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark, FocusBench and
SWE-Marathon. The scores live in charts with no printed values, and the full safety report is
"available soon."
[announcement](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-wonder)

## Dev + infra

### A working npm attack sat in zod's main branch for a day in September
Disclosed in the 4.6.6 notes on 11 October: six commits pushed to main on 21 September with a
compromised git credential, reverted the same day, never published. The payload resolved its
C2 host from an Ethereum contract call over public RPCs, and `release.yml` was rewired to
publish on any `package.json` bump. Releases now stage and wait for approval.
[release notes](https://github.com/colinhacks/zod/releases/tag/v4.6.6)

### Cryptographic Right Answers gets its post-quantum rewrite
Valsorda's update to the canonical pick-this-not-that list, out Monday. Key exchange becomes
X25519+ML-KEM-768; signatures ML-DSA-44, with classical algorithms dropped outright;
asymmetric encryption HPKE with MLKEM768-X25519, or age with post-quantum keys. The symmetric
half barely moves — 256-bit keys, HMAC-SHA-256, SHA-2, Argon2id or scrypt, passkeys over
passwords.
[post](https://words.filippo.io/right-answers/)

### The kernel's one policy meeting of the year went to LLMs
About 30 maintainers meet annually, by invitation, to settle what email can't. The 2026
session, held 8 October in Prague, was dominated by large language models and their effect on
the development community; security bugs and long-term succession planning took what was left.
Corbet's writeup is subscriber-only until 22 October, so the substance is still behind glass.
[LWN](https://lwn.net/Articles/1099594/)

## Hardware

### Intel's Crescent Island is now the GPU D270, and it is an LPDDR5X bet
Announced Monday at the OCP Global Summit: 1.3 PFLOPS FP8, 1.5TB/s, 350W, air-cooled
double-wide PCIe Gen5. LPDDR5X rather than HBM — 160GB on Intel's own card, up to 480GB on
partner builds, which Intel rates at 300B parameters per GPU. Sampling now, no launch date,
which makes 2027 the realistic read. Intel's first datacentre AI GPU since Ponte Vecchio.
[Intel](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-10-12-intel-accelerates-open-infrastructure-for-the-agentic-ai-era) · [ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/intels-crescent-island-ai-server-gpu-gets-a-name-and-specs-meet-the-intel-gpu-d-series/)

## Homelab

### A Wi-Fi 7 router makes a 320Msps software-defined receiver
Replacement firmware for the Gemtek W1700K turns its MediaTek MT7996 radio into a wideband
receiver: raw 14-bit IQ at 40–320Msps, 640Msps on 2.4GHz, tunable across 2.4/5/6GHz. Samples
land in a 1GiB ring on the AN7581 and stream over 10GbE to a Linux host with a spectrum and
waterfall web UI. Needs a 3.3V serial adapter. Transmit is disabled.
[repo](https://github.com/h0m3us3r/w1700k-sdr)

## Science + space

### Antarctic sea ice hit its third-lowest maximum on record
NSIDC and NASA put the 2026 peak at 17.59 million km², reached on 14 September — third lowest
in a satellite record that starts in 1978, and well short of the 1981–2010 median. It is
measured by passive microwave sensors, which read through cloud, so a bad imaging season
doesn't explain the gap.
[NASA Earth Observatory](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/antarctic-sea-ice-grows-to-its-2026-maximum/)
