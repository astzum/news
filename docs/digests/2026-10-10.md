# Sat 10 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Cloudflare has acquired the Deno team, and Deno the runtime
gets one more year of releases before development stops. Python 3.15 shipped with
explicit lazy imports and UTF-8 as the default encoding. And two groups independently
demonstrated a working thorium-229 nuclear clock, published the same day.

## Dev + infra

### Cloudflare bought Deno and will wind the runtime down
Ryan Dahl's post commits to a year of monthly bug-fix and security releases, then ends
development of the runtime. Deno Deploy runs six more months, with migration help for
paying customers moving to Workers. JSR survives on Cloudflare infrastructure, and
rusty_v8 continues so it can land in workerd. Plan accordingly if Deno is in production.
[announcement](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare)

### Python 3.15 lands explicit lazy imports and makes UTF-8 the default
Out 9 October. PEP 810 lets you mark an import lazy, which is the realistic fix for slow
CLI startup; PEP 686 flips the default encoding so `open()` stops guessing from locale.
Also `frozendict` and `sentinel` builtins, unpacking in comprehensions, and a JIT now
worth 7–8% on x86-64 Linux and 11–12% on AArch64 macOS.
[release](https://www.python.org/downloads/release/python-3150/)

### Let's Encrypt certificates drop to 64 days in February
Staging switches on 14 October, production on 10 February 2027, and the last 90-day
certificate expires 11 May 2027. Authorisation reuse falls from 30 days to 10. If renewal
runs on ARI you need do nothing; if a cron job hard-codes 60, 80 or 83 days, that is the
thing to go find now. 45 days follows in 2028.
[post](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/10/07/64-day-certs.html)

## AI research + releases

### Microsoft shipped a decision model, which makes four in a fortnight
Microsoft-Decision-1 returns calibrated probabilities over a fixed answer set rather than
text — routing, classification, grading, verification. It claims best accuracy across 36
benchmarks and ~150,000 held-out questions, roughly 35x faster than GPT-6 Sol, at $0.042
per million input tokens with output free. In Foundry now. After Clef, OpenAI's decisions
API and Strands Decider, this is a category.
[announcement](https://commandline.microsoft.com/microsoft-decision-1-model-foundry/)

### A Lean proof does not mean the English proof was right
Bastounis, Circelli and Hansen place the task of resolving ambiguity in natural-language
mathematics at SCI = ∞ in the Solvability Complexity Index — strictly harder than the
halting problem at SCI = 1. They then point at OpenAI's Navier-Stokes announcement, where
the Lean formalisation proves something other than the prose argument. Preprint, 6 October.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08144)

## Science + space

### Two groups built a thorium-229 nuclear clock, published the same day
Both stabilise a continuous-wave laser to the 148nm nuclear transition in a
room-temperature thorium-doped calcium fluoride crystal. One reaches 3x10⁻¹² fractional
instability per root-second and uses it to bound ultralight dark matter coupling to the
strong force; the other reaches 5x10⁻¹³, with two independently grown crystals agreeing at
the 10⁻¹³ level.
[Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11084-4) · [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11122-1)

## Homelab

### Raspberry Pi OS now installs on an old laptop again
The desktop image for PC and Mac is back after five years, rebuilt on Debian Trixie,
64-bit only, Wayland by default, with the Raspberry Pi Connect client included for remote
access. Live USB with persistence, or Calamares to disk. Intel Macs yes, Apple Silicon no.
Raspberry Pi were explicit that the gap since Bullseye was resourcing and may recur.
[announcement](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/raspberry-pi-desktop-now-available-for-pc-and-mac/)

## Hardware

### Oxide raised $445M and says it is already profitable
Eclipse led, with AMD joining as a strategic investor; the money goes to inventory and
manufacturing capacity against a backlog the company says exceeds supply. The detail worth
noting is the claim to have paid income tax on ordinary operations — a rack-scale server
company with its own firmware stack, selling enough computers to turn a profit.
[post](https://oxide.computer/blog/our-445m-series-d)
