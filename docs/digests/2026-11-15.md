# Sun 15 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Project Zero scrapped its 30-day post-patch grace
period and will be down to 30-day disclosure by 2028, because LLMs have made
patch-to-exploit time near zero. A forensic read of released model weights says
DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group have all been training on
export-controlled Blackwell GPUs. PC DRAM contract prices are set to rise another
13–18% this quarter.

## Dev + infra

### Project Zero drops patch soak time and starts a countdown to 30 days
Since 2021 the policy was 90 days to fix plus 30 days of silence after a patch
shipped. Natalie Silvanovich's argument: attackers now reverse a patch almost
instantly, so soak time just manufactures false secrecy. Disclosure is now 90 days
from report, 60 from February 2027, 30 from January 2028. Grace periods and variant
policy are gone.
[source](https://projectzero.google/2026/11/disclosure-policy.html)

### Cloudflare open-sourced Kitesurf, its browser engine for agents
It runs on Workers with no Chromium container anywhere: Blitz for HTML and layout,
Stylo for CSS, Boa for JavaScript, all Rust compiled to WebAssembly. Apache 2.0,
and it speaks the Chrome DevTools Protocol, so Puppeteer clients attach. Worth a
look if you want agent browsing without running a headless-Chrome fleet.
[source](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf-open-source/)

## AI industry

### Chinese frontier models carry the fingerprint of Blackwell GPUs
Will DePue's method recovers traces of a model's initial values from its published
weights, then searches for the hardware configuration that reproduces them. Six
models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group released since April 2026
match Blackwell; earlier ones from the same labs match Hopper. It shows which chips
were used, not where they sat or how they were obtained.
[source](https://unemploymentlabs.com/checkpoint-forensics)

## Homelab

### ggml finally cut a 1.0.0
The library under llama.cpp and whisper.cpp pruned its deprecated public API and
the legacy DIAG_MASK ops, and bumped the RPC protocol to v9 so incompatible peers
are refused rather than misbehaving. Also FP8, few-row quantised matmuls and MoE
fusion on CUDA and Metal, a Vulkan FlashAttention prefill path, new WebGPU kernels.
Rebuild anything you link directly.
[source](https://github.com/ggml-org/ggml/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

### Zyphra's ZONOS3-Mini does voice cloning at 100M active parameters
Apache 2.0, 43 languages, small enough to run in real time on a laptop or phone —
or in a browser tab on WebGPU with nothing installed. Clone from a sample, then
steer emotion and pace down to individual words. The plausible self-hosted
replacement for a paid TTS API, if it holds up on your own voices.
[source](https://huggingface.co/Zyphra/ZONOS3-Mini)

## Hardware

### PC DRAM contract prices climb another 13–18% this quarter
TrendForce's read: notebook shipments fell 15.8% quarter-on-quarter in 3Q26 as
retail prices bit, finished-goods inventories are swelling, and OEMs are trimming
4Q26 and 1Q27 memory orders. Client SSD contracts rise under 5%. The bit that
matters for next year's builds — OEMs plan lower-spec memory and fewer-core CPUs,
and suppliers are tentatively cutting PC DRAM supply for 2027.
[source](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261111-13303.html)

## Science + space

### JWST found interstellar-grade ice in the β Pictoris disk
NIRSpec spectra of the debris disk show amorphous water ice — under 7% crystalline
— mixed with CO₂ at roughly 1:10, detected in both ¹²CO₂ and ¹³CO₂. That mixing
ratio implies the ices formed in cold conditions, likely in the interstellar medium
rather than in the disk itself, and the even distribution of icy grains requires
frequent collisions to sustain.
[source](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adw7770)
