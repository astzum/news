# Mon 23 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Two million canary prompts caught three compromised
inference providers and a pile of ignored zero-retention flags. A use-after-free
in Ghostscript's font loader turns any image upload into command execution.
BepiColombo is in orbit at Mercury, the first spacecraft to get there since
MESSENGER.

## AI industry

### Two million canary prompts found three breached inference providers
Dragos's HD Moore seeded prompts with unique URLs and credentials from July to
November, then watched who used them. Marketplace resellers read customer
prompts through ordinary logging; some providers ignored zero-retention flags;
requests to US providers surfaced at Chinese organisations. OpenRouter has
off-boarded three providers and pulled 125 endpoints from ZDR eligibility.
[research](https://www.dragos.com/blog/ai-supply-chain-security-canary-tokens) ·
[response](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/)

### Anthropic's chip suppliers are now its shareholders
The IPO closed at $83 a share with the underwriters' full option exercised:
1.127bn Class A shares, ~$93.5bn gross, trading on Nasdaq as ANT since 19
November. Separately, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC
took 313m Class N shares for ~$26bn. Total raise ~$119.5bn.
[source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close)

## Dev + infra

### Microsoft's 365 outage was a July bug that only bit the newest servers
The final post-incident report names lock contention in a Windows component
enabled on M365 gateways in July. It fired only on newer high-core SKUs; lab
load tests used older hardware with fewer cores. A Teams failover, 14% of
capacity out for a deployment and US peak traffic did the rest — over 80% of
North American gateways went unresponsive, and patching each needed two
restarts.
[source](https://tianyin.github.io/misc/pir_microsoft_365_11-12-2026.pdf)

### A crafted image upload reaches Ghostscript and runs commands
CVE-2026-125396 is a use-after-free in Ghostscript's Type 1 font loader that
yields a write-what-where primitive, used to flip the state enforcing `-dSAFER`.
Paulos Yibelo's path is mundane: a `.pdf` containing PostScript, handed to
Ghostscript by a thumbnailer via ImageMagick. Fixed upstream in 10.08.0 on 8
September; Debian 12 is still listed vulnerable.
[source](https://pwn.ai/upload2shell/research.html)

## Science + space

### BepiColombo is in orbit at Mercury, eight years after launch
ESA confirmed capture on 21 November into a 590 x 11,640 km polar ellipse. It is
only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury, after MESSENGER ended in 2015.
The ESA and JAXA orbiters separate on 9–10 December; MPO then spirals down to
400–1,500 km by March 2027, with science operations starting in April.
[source](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03803-8)

## AI research + releases

### OpenAI wants perplexity to be the industry's shared speed limit
Jakub Pachocki published OpenAI's internal held-out log-perplexity: ~0.99 at
GPT-3.5, ~0.91 at GPT-4, now below 0.8, with unsolved-maths results appearing at
~0.76. He expects the next capability jump in the 0.68–0.70 window and proposes
labs commit to a shared ceiling until monitoring improves. He concedes the
datasets aren't comparable across labs yet.
[source](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)

## Homelab

### A $575 switch does 24x10G and 2x100G, with one measured caveat
ServeTheHome put RFC2544 through a Centec CTC7132-based ZX530S-24S2CQ bought on
AliExpress: full 440Gb/s aggregate line rate with zero loss from 128-byte frames
up, 837ns max latency, 24.6W idle. At 64 bytes it plateaus at 541.8 Mpps and
drops everything past ~82% of line rate. First real test of that chip anywhere.
[source](https://www.servethehome.com/centec-zx530s-24s2cq-review/)

## Hardware

### Skylake-X's next-page prefetcher only works forwards
Intel's manuals describe the next-page prefetcher as triggering on sequential
access toward either page boundary, for both Ivy Bridge and Redwood Cove. Walking
1,000 pages backwards, devsh0 measured 1,000 STLB misses — nothing prefetched.
Forwards it fires on crossing cache line 52 into 53 and fills exactly one page
ahead, into the dTLB. No practical use, as the author says.
[source](https://devsh0.github.io/microarchitecture,/intel,/computer-architecture/2026/11/17/reversing-the-next-page-prefetcher-in-skylake-x.html)
