# Wed 11 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Google DeepMind's agents produced a machine-checked
proof that Signal's actual implementation — not just its protocol design — meets
its security properties. AMD launched EPYC "Venice" on a new socket at 256 cores
per chip, and independent testing says the dense Zen 6c core now beats the
high-frequency one per clock. Fossil CO2 emissions are projected to fall this
year, partly because of an oil shock rather than clean energy.

## AI research + releases

### Agents proved Signal's real code secure, not just its design
Humans wrote the specifications, Gemini Carbon agents wrote the proofs — over six
million lines of Lean — covering the compiled implementation, where real
vulnerabilities actually hide. Four weeks for work that historically takes expert
teams years, with Lean checking everything and only 1% of the codebase flagged for
human review. Two previously fixed bugs, reintroduced, were proved insecure.
[source](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)

## AI industry

### Amazon swapped Sonnet 4.6 for a small decision model and cut cost 98%
Labelling tickets into 20 fixed categories is a decision, not generation, so two
engineers replaced the generative call with Strands Decider — a Gemma 4 E4B build
that returns a probability per option instead of JSON. It beat production Sonnet on
a held-out ticket set, needs about 19GiB and 640ms on a single A10G, and saves
millions a year.
[source](https://strandsagents.com/blog/amazon-saves-millions-replacing-sonnet-with-strands-decider/)

## Hardware

### AMD's Venice arrives at 256 cores and a new socket
EPYC 9006 ships on TSMC N2 and the new SP7 socket. The flagship 9996 is 256C/512T
with 1GB of L3 at 600W, 16 channels of DDR5-8000 and PCIe 6.0 — AMD claims 33% more
cores and 20% higher IPC than Turin. The high-frequency 9686F quietly gained 300MHz
over its summer spec and ships at 5.3GHz.
[source](https://www.servethehome.com/amd-epyc-9006-results-are-here-and-amd-delivers-a-knockout-part-deux/)

### Zen 6c is a genuinely different core now, and wins per clock
Chips and Cheese measured both: 6c carries a bigger reorder buffer (640 against
576), integer register file and load queue, and at a matched 4.1GHz leads the
high-frequency part by 6.7% in SPEC CPU 2026 integer. Its 128MB L3 absorbs memory
stalls the classic core's 48MB does not. VNNI throughput roughly doubles on both.
[source](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/amds-zen-6-family-feeding-the-beast)

## Dev + infra

### .NET 11 brings union types to C#
C# 15 adds union types and closed hierarchies, so application state can be
expressed and handled with compiler-checked patterns. Also: a Native AOT CLI host
and warm MSBuild server to cut everyday command overhead, built-in Zstandard
compression, OpenAPI 3.2 and async validation in ASP.NET Core, MAUI moving to
CoreCLR. This is a two-year STS release; .NET 10 remains the LTS option.
[source](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/announcing-dotnet-11/)

### Synaptics shut down a Linux DisplayLink driver with patents, not copyright
Mike Lothian wrote a Rust DRM/KMS driver with LLM help after the vendor binary kept
crashing, got three generations of dock lighting panels, and received a
cease-and-desist listing 125 patents, then a demand for $25,000 rising by that much
weekly. OIN could not help — codecs are excluded from its Linux System Definition.
Repo down, driver stays local.
[source](https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1102549/acbc92e00cee3a3f/)

## Homelab

### KubeVirt graduated, which weakens the case for a side hypervisor
CNCF graduated the project that runs VMs as Kubernetes custom resources — same
declarative APIs, same Argo, Prometheus and Velero tooling you already point at
containers. Ten years old, 2,000-plus contributors, with ByteDance, Cloudflare and
NVIDIA running it in production. If a separate hypervisor exists beside your
cluster only for the one appliance that will not containerise, reconsider.
[source](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2026/11/10/cloud-native-computing-foundation-announces-kubevirt-graduation/)

## Science + space

### Fossil CO2 is projected to fall this year, partly for the wrong reason
The Global Carbon Budget puts 2026 fossil CO2 at 38.2 billion tonnes, down 0.4%,
with oil down 1.9% — read as a short-term response to the energy shock from the Iran
conflict. Solar, wind and EVs covered two thirds of demand growth. Including land
use, total emissions still rose to 43.3Gt, and the remaining 1.5°C budget is 150Gt.
[source](https://news.exeter.ac.uk/faculty-of-environment-science-and-economy/fossil-co2-emissions-decrease-in-2026/)
