# Fri 09 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** OpenAI withdrew three of the maths manuscripts its internal
model wrote, after a sign error in one took two dependent papers down with it. ext4 has
marked `data=journal` deprecated, with removal set for January 2028. And Webb placed the
most distant fast radio burst yet in a dwarf galaxy too young for a neutron-star merger.

## AI research + releases

### OpenAI withdrew three of the maths papers its model wrote
A sign error in "Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds" invalidated the
construction two dependent papers relied on, so all three are gone. Fourteen more were
revised and thirteen re-cited. The catalogue came from ~4,000 posed problems at about three
hours of Pro thinking compute per result; 300 of 719 top-line results are formalised in Lean.
[history](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/history.md)

## Dev + infra

### ext4's data=journal mode is deprecated and goes in January 2028
Ts'o's last-minute 7.3-rc7 pull marks the mount option deprecated in `super.c` and the admin
guide — three files, six lines. The same pull names the ext4 dev branch in MAINTAINERS so
Sashiko and other review bots can find the primary development tree, which is a quiet sign of
kernel workflow adapting to its new readers.
[pull request](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-ext4/ase1cbY1IuELJ7jQ@mit.edu/)

## Homelab

### llama.cpp can keep MoE experts in host RAM and still run them on the GPU
Merged 7 October: `--moe-cache-mib N` holds an LRU cache of expert tensors in VRAM and only
uploads misses. 1.62x on a 4090 at a 77% hit rate, 2.20x on a 5090 at 89%, 1.3–2.5x on an AMD
R9700. Single GPU, batches of 32 tokens or fewer; multi-GPU regressed 25–34% until a
follow-up PR.
[pull request](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/29887)

### Batching zswap stores is worth 51% throughput on a 16GB machine
Large folios get carved into eight-page batches, so allocation, compression and zsmalloc
stores happen per batch and take the acomp mutex once rather than per page. On usemem at 60%
over-commit with ZSTD and 64kB mTHP: 11,794 → 17,810 KB/s, time to free memory down 40%,
large-folio swapout fallbacks halved. Posted 8 October, not merged.
[patch series](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-mm/20261008182814.27450-1-kanchanapsridhar2026@gmail.com/)

### Whistle does speech-to-text from a 16.9MB file on CPU
Eight-layer encoder with simple-attention blocks, eight-layer decoder with gated
cross-attention, five-beam search, no dependencies, seventeen targets down to
microcontrollers. On an M4 Pro CPU: 11.1ms to first token against Whisper Base's 73.2ms, and
1,319 tokens/s against 266. Beats Whisper Base on LibriSpeech and FLEURS, loses on TED-LIUM
and AMI. Seven languages.
[post](https://cactuscompute.com/blog/whistle)

### NVIDIA's Olympus core out-IPCs Zen 5 while clocking at 3.3GHz
Chips and Cheese measured Vera, the 88-core server part replacing Grace: a 10-wide
out-of-order core with unusually large reorder structures, 2–3 cycle L1D, 10-cycle L2 from

## Science + space

### Webb put the most distant fast radio burst in a tiny, very young galaxy
FRB 20240304B, found by MeerKAT in March 2024, sits at redshift 2.148 — roughly three billion
years after the Big Bang. Its host is a dwarf galaxy about a thousand times less massive than
expected, forming stars fast enough that most are under 30 million years old. That favours
magnetars over mergers. In Science, and it is one object.
[release](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/webb-measures-distance-to-farthest-fast-radio-burst-suggesting-origin/)

### Solar switchbacks start at the surface and get reshaped on the way out
Solar Orbiter's Solar Wind Analyser found a distinctive charged oxygen and carbon mix inside a
switchback at its March 2022 perihelion, tying the S-shaped kink to interchange reconnection
where open and closed field regions meet. Waves and turbulence then rework it in transit — both
processes, not one or the other.
[release](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Solar_Orbiter/Solar_Orbiter_tracks_origin_of_mysterious_magnetic_switchbacks)
