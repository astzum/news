# Wed 18 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** A single writable field in Optimizely's platform
served crypto-stealing malware from a CDN that 178 big-name sites already
trusted. Windows 11 records which coding agent you ran and queues it for upload
as optional diagnostics. Voyager 1 is now one light-day from Earth.

## Dev + infra

### Optimizely's own CDN served malware to 178 big-name brands
Report URI's sweep on 16 November found injected code in 186 of 1,611
Optimizely Web snippets — BBC, Zoom, GitLab, Salesforce, HSBC, Ledger among
them. The payload swapped crypto addresses in page text and clipboard, skimmed
Luhn-valid cards, exfiltrated non-HttpOnly cookies and html2canvas screenshots,
and chained iOS exploit kits. Rebuilt 26 times in one day, and the loader came
from `cdn.optimizely.com` with Optimizely's own certificate.
[source](https://blog.report-uri.com/worlds-largest-brands-infected-with-malware-via-optimizely/)

### Windows 11 logs which coding agent you use and ships it to Microsoft
Xusheng reverse-engineered `MissionControlAggregator.dll` and found matchers for
four CLI agents (`copilot`, `claude`, `codex`, `agy`), five desktop ones
(plus `cursor`, `antigravity`) and twelve tool categories, flattened into a
`Segments.SubCohorts` bitmap. Renaming `cmd.exe` to `codex.exe` set the Codex
bit. It rides DiagTrack as *optional* diagnostic data — enabled by default in
his US install.
[source](https://xusheng.dev/posts/windows-coding-agent-telemetry/)

### Solid 2.0 kills its own metaframework and ships diagnostics written for agents
Five years after 1.0, promises and async iterators are first-class in the
reactive graph, so loading state and cancellation drop out of app code.
`createResource`, `batch`, `startTransition`, `produce` and `createMutable` are
gone. SolidStart is retired — Vite plugins cover it. Dev builds emit coded
reactivity errors pointing at a `SKILL.md` repair guide. 23 RCs, 971 PRs.
[source](https://www.solidjs.com/blog/solid-2-0-the-long-hold/)

## AI research + releases

### Thinking Machines' "Small" model wants 500GB of VRAM
Inkling 2 Small is Apache-2.0 open weights: 594B total parameters, 17B active,
8 of 384 experts per token, 1M context, trained natively in NVFP4. The card's
own hardware requirement is two B300s or four H200s. It leads its comparison
table on BrowseComp at 90.2%. Small for a frontier MoE; not small for anything
you own.
[source](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Small)

### OpenAI's new flagship is 20% cheaper than the one it replaces
`gpt-6.2-astra` lists at $8/$40 per million tokens against `gpt-6-astra`'s
$10/$50; `gpt-6.2-sol` holds GPT-6.1 Sol's $2/$10. Both get 1.05M context, 128K
max output, cached input at $0.40 and $0.10. Knowledge cutoffs are 18 May and 1
June 2026. The launch post 403s for us, so these come from OpenAI's own docs.
[source](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models)

## AI industry

### Microsoft's cheap coding model is cheap at a promotional price
MAI Code 2 averages 63.2% pass across DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal-Bench 2.1,
SWE-Atlas and FrontierCode v1.1, against GPT-6.1 Sol's 64.1 and Opus 5.5's

scores 43.1 to Opus's 57.5. Cost per task is $0.309 — and the footnote says
that's a 50% promotional price.
[source](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/)

## Science + space

### Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth as of today
Launched in 1977, the probe passes 16 billion miles (25.9 billion km) out —
far enough that light from Earth now takes a full day to reach it, making a
round trip two. NASA marked it on 17 November by asking people to flash "VGR"
in morse from their phones after sunset, and hosts a JPL panel on both probes
on 19 November.
[source](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-invites-media-public-to-join-voyager-1-one-light-day-activities/)
