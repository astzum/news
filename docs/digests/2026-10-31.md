# Sat 31 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** For about eleven hours on 29 October, Cursor's extension
marketplace served a backdoored build of the official Claude Code extension that
planted an attacker's SSH key, and uninstalling it is not enough. Anthropic's red
team used an unreleased internal model to write working exploit chains against
Chrome, Safari, Android, Windows and the Linux kernel — 62 Chrome CVEs are already
patched. And PostgreSQL 19 is slipping to November because LLM-found bugs in a
forty-year-old codebase have buried the committers.

## Dev + infra

### Cursor's marketplace served a backdoored Claude Code extension for eleven hours
Versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 of `Anthropic.claude-code` were live between 2:27am and
1:35pm PT on 29 October. The payload added an SSH key labelled `svc-telemetry`,
installed a LaunchAgent or SYSTEM scheduled task, and retried a reverse tunnel every
60 seconds. Enabling sshd needs admin rights and mostly failed; the key and
persistence landed anyway. Cursor rebuilt the marketplace and rotated keys. No root
cause published.
[Cursor](https://cursor.com/blog/claude-code-extension-security-notice)

### PostgreSQL 19 slips to November because LLMs found forty years of bugs
Back-patched commits to the stable branch between feature freeze and 25 September
went from 724 to 1,169 year on year; those reaching an older branch too went 248 to
433. Three large features — `FOR PORTION OF`, SQL/PGQ, MERGE/SPLIT PARTITION — were
reverted between 27 August and 15 September. Robert Haas's point: finding bugs got
much easier, fixing them didn't.
[Robert Haas](https://rhaas.blogspot.com/2026/10/postgres-19-is-late-and-its-ais-fault.html)

## AI research + releases

### Claude wrote working exploit chains for Chrome, Safari, Android and the Linux kernel
Anthropic's Frontier Red Team used an unreleased internal model. 62 Chrome CVEs are
patched as of Chrome 156, all critical or high, 13 of them sandbox escapes; Safari got
a data-only same-origin bypass; CVE-2026-98432 is a zero-click root RCE on any Linux
host with a multipath TCP listener, 48 minutes to leak a kernel pointer. Go uses MPTCP
by default, so patch.
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-vulnerabilities-with-claude) ·
[LWN](https://lwn.net/Articles/1104192/)

### Someone compiled a RISC-V CPU into Qwen 3.5's weights
Gate and write strength at 1 with one-hot keys turn three in four of Qwen 3.5 4B's
Gated DeltaNet layers into exact addressable RAM. Divyansh Agrawal compiled a RISC-V
core into eleven of them. It passes riscv-tests, ran 18 million instructions clean,
and lifts NUPA arithmetic from 48.6% to 100%. Costs: 18.8 GB of weights, seconds per
sum. Posted 22 October.
[divagr.com](https://divagr.com/blog/qwenputer)

## Hardware

### Nvidia's 88-core Vera holds its own against x86 in independent testing
Tom's Hardware took remote access to a dual-socket Vera reference platform and ran
SPEC CPU 2026 itself: 1.5% ahead of dual EPYC 9755 on intrate, from 352 threads
against 512, and well clear per core. Python and GCC compile are the strong suits;
SEAL crypto and Zstd decompression are not. 88 cores, up to 1.2 TB/s LPDDR5X,
250–450W configurable TDP.
[Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/benchmarking-nvidias-vera-cpu-for-the-data-center-standalone-vera-tested-against-granite-rapids-ap-and-epyc-turin)

### Intel's Fab 28 shutdown hits the one process it can't route around
Intel's 10-Q confirms the mid-October accident at Kiryat Gat caused employee
casualties and injuries and halted the fab for about two weeks, restart due by
month's end. Q4 takes scrap, cleanup and idle-capacity charges; lower volumes drag
gross profit into the first half of 2027. Intel still can't estimate the cost. Fab 28
runs Intel 7 — Xeon 6 I/O dies, Emerald Rapids.
[Intel 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000050863/000005086326000191/intc-20260926.htm)

## Homelab

### An internet-facing Synology NAS got ransomwared through a two-year-old Pwn2Own bug
A DS918+ with the DSM web interface exposed and 18 months of missed patches had its
files encrypted; the logs point at CVE-2024-10441, the CVSS 9.8 plugin-daemon RCE
Synology fixed in November 2024 after Pwn2Own. The owner's 3-2-1 backups held, so
nothing was lost. The lesson keeps recurring: don't expose a NAS web UI, and don't
assume DSM updates itself.
[Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/tech-whizzs-internet-accessible-synology-nas-gets-bit-by-ransomware-attack-from-18-month-old-remote-code-execution-flaw-security-updates-neglected-in-a-series-of-catastrophic-errors) ·
[advisory](https://www.synology.com/en-global/security/advisory/Synology_SA_24_20)

## Science + space

### Voyager 1 bought itself more time, then went quiet for 48 hours
In early September engineers swapped three heater devices for lower-power alternatives
— the "Big Bang" — so the remaining instruments run past summer 2027, when one would
otherwise have been switched off. The probes lose about 4 watts a year. Then the Deep
Space Network missed two passes on 18–19 October; the signal returned on the 20th
after a thruster group failed over to backups.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/voyager/2026/10/29/nasas-voyager-1-conserves-power-resolves-brief-communications-pause/)
