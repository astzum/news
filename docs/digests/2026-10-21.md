# Wed 21 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Eight eMudhra intermediate CA private keys were compromised
and Mozilla has pulled the root from its trust store. A Linux Foundation survey puts
GenAI's coding-time saving at about half and the share of incoming pull requests that
are AI slop at a quarter. And DHS has ordered DeepSeek — plus anything built on it —
off every federal civilian agency's IT.

## Dev + infra

### Eight eMudhra intermediate CA keys were compromised and the root is now untrusted
Reported to Mozilla on 17 October; the keys would let an attacker issue working HTTPS
certificates for domains they do not control. OneCRL blocks went out within three hours
and reached standard Firefox configurations by 22:59 UTC. Firefox 158.0.1, ESR 115.43.1
and 153.5.1 followed for users whose settings block OneCRL, and NSS 3.131.1 removes the
root outright.
[Mozilla](https://blog.mozilla.org/security/2026/10/20/protecting-firefox-users-following-the-compromise-of-emudhra-intermediate-certificates/)

### A quarter of incoming open-source pull requests are judged AI slop
Linux Foundation Research surveyed project communities and found code-authoring time
down 52% and proposal discussion down 19% — against 25% of incoming PRs rated
low-quality AI output, rising to 31% on large projects. 59% of maintainers see more
contributions than before, 71% say the barrier to entry dropped, and 53% disagree that
any of it improved community trust.
[Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/new-lf-research-survey-shows-generative-ai-cuts-open-source-coding-time-in-half-but-25-of-incoming-pull-requests-are-considered-ai-slop)

### OpenBSD 8.0 gives amd64 a 128x larger kernel address space
Out 20 October, the project's 61st release. Kernel virtual address space on amd64 goes
from 4G to 512G; arm64 gains hibernate and bounce buffers; riscv64 gains the vector
extension and the Advanced Interrupt Architecture. vmm now runs 64 vCPUs per guest with
x2apic. LibreSSL 4.4.0, OpenSSH 10.6, Intel Arc A-series in inteldrm(4), 13,000+
prebuilt amd64 packages.
[OpenBSD](https://www.openbsd.org/80.html)

## AI research + releases

### DeepSeek open-sourced the inference stack behind its own production service
DeepInfer is Apache 2.0 and is what DeepSeek says powers its large-scale serving:
attention DP/CP and MoE expert parallelism, draft-token acceleration, asynchronous
scheduling, batch-invariant inference, deterministic sampling. It targets NVIDIA SM100
and Huawei Ascend 950, and so far supports only V4.1-Flash. The published throughput
curves are measured on 4 GPUs for prefill, 64 for decode.
[GitHub](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer)

### Mistral's Voxtral 3 is a voice stack rather than a model drop
Four models — Small and Mini transcribe, a realtime variant, a TTS — plus an agent SDK
that streams listening, tool calls and speech in one pipeline. Claimed 4.1% WER on
FLEURS-45 against 16.0% for Whisper large-v3, and 8.7% realtime at 240ms.
$0.0035–0.008/min to transcribe, $13 per million characters to speak. API and private
deployment only; no weights.
[Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/)

## AI industry

### DHS has ordered DeepSeek out of every federal civilian agency
The Federal Register notice published 20 October invokes the Federal Acquisition Supply
Chain Security Act to bar Hangzhou DeepSeek and affiliates from civilian procurement and
to strip existing installs. The broad part: it also covers "third-party products and
services developed using DeepSeek implementations", which sweeps in a lot of fine-tunes
and wrappers. Docket DHS-2026-0628.
[Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/20/2026-21447/notice-of-order-regarding-exclusion-and-removal-of-deepseek-products-from-federal-civilian-executive)

## Hardware

### AMD is folding ZenDNN into Intel's oneDNN
Both vendors now agree oneDNN is the default open-source library for x86 acceleration in
PyTorch, and AMD joins the UXL Foundation as a steering member — one tuning path instead
of two. No timeline given, and AMD's next-gen AI Compute Extensions instructions arrive
"through future oneDNN updates", so this is a roadmap, not a shipped change.
[AMD](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-intel-collaborate-simplify-ai-development-on-x86-cpus-pytorch/)

## Science + space

### Aurora ran 13.4 trillion particles twice to hunt one wrinkle in inflation
Argonne's HACC team modelled 13.4 trillion dark-matter particles across 28.6 billion
light-years, then did it again with the initial phases flipped so statistical noise
cancels when the pair is analysed together. Each run took roughly 8,100 nodes and 50,000
GPUs; both plus most of the analysis finished in about four days, writing 85PB through
DAOS and keeping 8PB.
[Argonne via Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-aurora-supercomputer-powers-largest-cosmological.html)
