# Fri 16 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Citizen Lab found mercenary spyware being delivered through
ordinary programmatic ad inventory on 75,000 apps, no click required. Meta released the
weights for an 816B-parameter model and says two Mac Studios can run it. And Ubuntu 26.10
is out with coreutils fully migrated to Rust.

## Dev + infra

### Targeted ads are a working delivery channel for zero-click spyware
Citizen Lab analysed about 4 billion ad bidding records from March to June 2026; 19% of
bids carried a script tag pointing at servers run by Discover-Tech, an Israeli firm linked
to Candiru. The script fingerprints the device and returns an exploit only to selected
targets. 75,373 apps and 120,768 sites served it, including BBC and NYT inventory.
[Citizen Lab](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/)

### Ubuntu 26.10 ships coreutils entirely in Rust
Stonking Stingray, out 15 October with Linux 7.3 and GNOME 51. cp, mv and rm were the last
GNU holdouts; uutils now covers all of it. dbus-broker replaces dbus-daemon on both buses,
and OpenSSL 4 arrives with Encrypted Client Hello. Nine months of support, so this is where
the breakage surfaces before 28.04 LTS inherits it.
[release notes](https://documentation.ubuntu.com/release-notes/26.10/)

## AI research + releases

### Meta put an 816B open-weight model on two Mac Studios
Muse Spark Open is 816B parameters with 42B active and a 1M-token context, weights on
Hugging Face under a licence with "limited commercial terms". Meta's pitch: two M5 Ultra
Mac Studios, 512GB of unified memory each at 1.2TB/s, hold the whole model. llama.cpp, MLX
and vLLM from day one. "Most capable US open-weight model" is their claim, not a number.
[Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open)

Six METR staff were given employee-like access — company hardware, office space, internal
systems — to audit risk assessments and incidents, and can publish findings without
Anthropic's veto, subject to narrow redactions. SecureBio is reviewing the bio and chem

[announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators)

## Homelab

### A 24B reasoning model with 2B active weights, shipped in GGUF
Liquid AI's LFM2.5-24B-A2B: 64 experts with top-4 routing, 131k context, 2.45B parameters
active per token, so it runs where a dense 24B would not. GGUF, MLX and ONNX checkpoints at
release and llama.cpp support; they measure 184 tok/s on an M5 Max. The licence is Liquid's
own lfm1.0, not Apache — read it before building on it.
[Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-24b-a2b)

### Memtest86+ 9.00 runs on ARM64, including the Raspberry Pi
Version 9.00 adds UEFI and ACPI support for ARM64, tested on Snapdragon X laptops and on
Pi 4 and Pi 5 with community UEFI firmware. Also new: a bus stress test aimed at DDR5
signalling faults, AVX2 and NEON SIMD tests, SPD CRC checking, reports written to USB with
F6, and serial logging you can wire into CI.
[memtest.org](https://www.memtest.org/)

## Science + space

### NOAA puts an 83% chance on the strongest El Niño since 1950
NASA's satellites tracked the buildup: a Kelvin wave near Micronesia in January, sea level
30–40cm above normal in the eastern Pacific by April, surface temperatures about 1.7°C above
average. PACE also sees the equatorial Pacific turning bluer — less phytoplankton, because
the nutrient upwelling that feeds it has stalled.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/nasa-satellites-observe-historic-2026-el-nino/)
