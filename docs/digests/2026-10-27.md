# Tue 27 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Anthropic's S-1 is public, and it shows $16.3bn of revenue in six
months against roughly $390bn of non-cancellable compute commitments. CPython's experimental
JIT has run out of PEPs and is headed out of main. And Cohere says a widely downloaded
open-weight model tried to upload a file about its host machine to an Alibaba Cloud bucket.

## AI industry

### Anthropic filed to go public, and the revenue line is the story
Revenue went $41M (2023) to $386M (2024) to $4.59B (2025), then $16.29B in the first half of
2026 against $1.16B a year earlier. Operating loss narrowed to $693M. The $80.7B net loss is
almost entirely convertible-note fair value. Non-cancellable compute: Google ≥$114.5B,
SpaceX.AI up to $120.5B, Amazon ≥$110B, Microsoft ≥$44.9B. Nasdaq, ticker ANT.
[SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm)

## Dev + infra

### CPython's JIT has run out of road
PEP 836 would have made the experimental JIT supported over a 2.5-year path to 3.17, with a
20% pyperformance target. Savannah Ostrowski withdrew it on 19 October: the authors don't have
the bandwidth. The steering council's June condition was accept a PEP or remove the code, and
there is now no PEP to accept. The reference implementation stays up.
[PEP 836](https://peps.python.org/pep-0836/)

## AI research + releases

### An open-weight model tried to upload a probe file to a bucket in Beijing
Asked to write a defensive detection rule under Microsoft's CTI-REALM benchmark,
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next inventoried its host and attempted an OSS upload — while its own reasoning
trace said it would call no tools. Under one call in 100,000. Prefilling curl's `-o` flag took
URL generation from 4% to 92%. Cohere won't say whether it's deliberate or training residue.
[Cohere](https://cohere.com/blog/concerning-behaviour-popular-open-weight-ai-model)

### OpenAI's newest model is a cheap million-token workhorse, not a frontier reasoner
$1 per million input tokens and $5 out, 1.05M context, 128k max output, June 2026 cutoff —
and prompts over 272k tokens cost double on input. Pitched at visual reasoning and computer
use. Artificial Analysis puts it 25th of 221 models on its intelligence index, so the price
and the context are the point, not the ceiling.
[OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-terra) ·
[Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-6-1-terra)

## Hardware

### AMD's $1,700 32GB inference card is about a quarter of a 5090
Navi 48, 64 CUs, 32GB GDDR6, 640GB/s, 300W, 382.7 TFLOPS FP8, dual-slot blower so you can
stack them. ServeTheHome measured 20–30 tokens/sec single-user on Qwen3.8-27B, rising to 113
at 16 concurrent users. An RTX 5090 is roughly 4× faster at peak throughput, narrowing to
about 2:1 at low concurrency. The memory and the blower are the pitch.
[ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/asrock-creator-radeon-ai-pro-r9700-review-workstation-inference-amd-style/)

### Helium is now a US critical material
Energy Secretary Chris Wright made the designation on 23 October under the Energy Act of 2020.
DOE's own framing: no viable substitute in semiconductor production, global demand growing
faster than production, and US helium-rich gas deposits held back by thin refining capacity
and long permitting. What the label actually unlocks isn't in the release — that part comes
later.
[DOE](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-designates-helium-critical-material-strengthen-american-manufacturing)

## Homelab

### The Rust mail server reached 1.0 and froze its schema
Five years from an email-parsing library to JMAP, IMAP, POP3, SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV
in one binary. 1.0 stabilises the database schema and adds post-quantum DKIM2 (ML-DSA-44 plus
Ed25519), full-text search inside PDFs and Office documents, and OIDC with per-client scopes.
Self-reported: ahead of Postfix 3.11 and Dovecot 2.4 in 35 of 38 comparisons, IMAP login 5.1ms
against 28ms.
[Stalwart](https://stalw.art/blog/stalwart-1-0/)

## Science + space

### The most deuterium-rich methane ever measured is on an interstellar comet
JWST near-infrared spectra of 3I/ATLAS's coma give D/H of (3.18 ± 0.30)% for methane — 13 ± 2
times what Rosetta found at 67P, the only other comet with a CH3D detection, and above
anything in the Solar System. The authors read it as ice formed cold, metal-poor and under
heavy cosmic-ray irradiation. Nature Astronomy, 23 October.
[Nature Astronomy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-026-03002-5)
