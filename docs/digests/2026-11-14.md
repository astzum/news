# Sat 14 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Claude read 1,009 pages of Gödel's shorthand workbooks
and surfaced 663 theorems nobody knew he had proved. Edge0 open-sourced a
145-billion-parameter model that runs in 29GB on a laptop. Christoph Hellwig
released declustered parity RAID built into XFS, with writes he measures at up to
20x mdraid.

## AI research + releases

### Gödel proved far more than anyone knew, and it took two days to find out
Jan von Plato's Helsinki team had been reading Gödel's 1,009 pages of 1940s
workbooks at about a page a week, much of it in obsolete Gabelsberger shorthand.
Given the transcriptions, Claude returned a 650-page digest in a couple of days and
flagged 663 theorems Gödel had proved, sketched or had an idea for — plus 14
abandoned themes in logic.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths)

### A 145B open-weights model that fits in 29GB
Edge0-145B is a sparse MoE with roughly 10B parameters active per step, sharing two
large matrices across each group of 64 experts rather than giving every expert its
own. Edge0 reports 29GB peak memory, 1,100+ tokens/s prefill and 80+ tokens/s decode
on a MacBook Pro at about 120W. Weights, a 120B sibling and the inference stack are
all out.
[source](https://edge0.ai/blog/introducing-edge0-145b-frontier-ai-capabilities-on-your-own-computer)

## Dev + infra

### Parity RAID now lives inside XFS, and claims 20x mdraid on writes
XRAID sits under zoned XFS and aggregates physical zones into logical ones. Parity
covers file data only; metadata redundancy is delegated to MD mirroring, which suits
small in-place updates better. Rebuilds spread across every disk with spare capacity
and reorder dynamically. Eighteen months of work, zero to two parities, and
explicitly nowhere near an upstream submission.
[source](https://marc.info/?l=linux-xfs&m=179457402052262&w=2)

## AI industry

### Seven months of Project Glasswing, and the arithmetic still doesn't close
VulnCheck counts 442 CVEs attributable to Anthropic — 0.5% of this year's 93,450 —
with two exploited in the wild. The ledger lists 37,966 findings against 217 fixes,
1,654 duplicates and 419 withdrawals, yet the claimed true-positive rate has risen
from 90.8% to 94.2%. curl rejected 19 of 34 reports as not vulnerabilities.
[source](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/anthropic-glasswing-november-update)

### Brave's search API will take payment per request instead of an API key
Endpoints behind x402 answer an unpaid call with HTTP 402 and a price; the agent
signs a USDC payment on Base and retries. No account, no key management, $0.005 per
request for LLM Context or for web, news, image, video and local search. Brave runs
the facilitator itself and is covering Base gas fees for now.
[source](https://brave.com/blog/search-api-agentic-payments/)

## Homelab

### An air purifier with no root certificates is an air purifier you own
The Coway Airmega IconS ships with no trust store at all — during setup it fetches
Amazon's root CA over plain HTTP from an S3 bucket, then validates its cloud
connections against that. Serve it your own CA instead and every connection is
yours. The rig is a Raspberry Pi in AP mode, nftables and mitmproxy, with an LLM
writing the firewall rules.
[source](https://scottjg.com/posts/2026-11-11-reverse-engineering-my-coway-air-purifier-to-ditch-the-cloud/)

## Hardware

### Japan's money broke ground on a New York fab, and no node was named
The first of two 300mm fabs at GlobalFoundries' Malta site: roughly $10B initially
plus $570M in New York State incentives, under a US-Japan programme worth up to
$21B. About 900,000 wafers a year when fully operational, online 2029. Japan
provides the capital, GF the technology and operations. The release says
"foundational semiconductors" and never names a process.
[source](https://gf.com/news-and-events/news/globalfoundries-breaks-ground-on-first-new-semiconductor-fab-under-landmark-us-japan-initiative/)

## Science + space

### Five flat years of climate policy, priced out to 2100
Current policies now point to 2.7°C by 2100, up from 2.6°C a year ago; even full
implementation of every pledge and net-zero target lands at 1.9°C. Positive and
negative policy moves have cancelled out for five consecutive years. Returning below
1.5°C by 2100 would need emissions falling 4% a year to 2030, then 7%, then 9%
through 2050.
[source](https://climateactiontracker.org/publications/warming-projections-global-update-2026/)
