# Wed 14 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Anthropic replaced git in its monorepo with a server-first
version control system of its own and published the design. Someone finally tripped over a
soundness hole that has been sitting in Rust's borrow checker for about a decade. And a
NeurIPS paper shows a capability can be locked inside model weights so that evaluations
provably cannot elicit it.

## Dev + infra

### Anthropic replaced git in its monorepo with a server-first VCS
Scion, built in five months and fully live since September, drops the "every clone is a
repository" model: clients keep bounded caches, servers do the heavy work, every query is an
API. Merges went from 2,500/day in May to over 30,000 by September; checkouts fell from
minutes to seconds at 200x less CPU and memory than their git mirroring. Not open source.
[engineering post](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control)

### A soundness hole has been open in Rust's borrow checker for eleven years
Filed 13 October by jsnell: a struct with a `T: 'static` bound will accept non-static
references through covariant field assignment, and its `Drop` impl then reads them as
static — use-after-free from safe code. The types team called it eleven years old; the
reproducer compiles back to 1.18. Downgraded to P-high because it needs a hostile `Drop`.
[issue](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/164250)

### curl 8.23.0 is measurably smaller and faster, from tests rather than a rewrite
Out today. The gains came out of the performance test rig Stenberg set up in August: base64
encoding over 2x faster, URL encoding ~4x, date parsing 2x, printf 5–15%. The easy handle
shrank from 4,256 to 3,064 bytes and the multi handle from 900 to 656. A 512MB download uses
15% less memory excluding the download buffer.
[post](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/10/13/a-slightly-faster-slimmer-curl/)

## AI research + releases

### A capability can be hidden in weights so evaluations provably cannot elicit it
Draguns et al. embed an encrypted network inside model weights, gated on a secret trigger by
a cryptographic digital locker, and argue it stays hard to elicit or interpret even with full
weight access. It survived fine-tuning, weight noising, gradient trigger search and latent
adversarial training, using chemical-reaction prediction as the stand-in hazard. NeurIPS 2026.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928)

## Homelab

### EmbeddingGemma 2's audio encoder makes a usable local transcriber
The encoder is left untouched; only a reader that maps audio slices into text space was
trained, then the nearest of ~229,000 English words wins. About 5% word error rate at 288x
real time — an hour of audio in 12.6 seconds on a base M3 MacBook Air with 8GB. Reader
training took under two hours on 8xH100. 715MB, Mac only.
[writeup](https://eg2.haku.sh/)

## Hardware

### Marvell's second-gen CXL expanders double host bandwidth and move to Gen6
Structera X 3406 and 3506, briefed 12 October: CXL 3.2, 256GB/s to the host against 128
before, six memory channels, up to four hosts sharing one pool. The DDR4 part takes 18 DIMMs
for 9TB per controller, which is a use for retired DDR4; the DDR5 part takes 12 and does
250GB/s. 5nm, LZ4 compression, no availability date.
[ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/marvell-structera-x-3406-and-3506-cxl-3-2-memory-expanders/)
