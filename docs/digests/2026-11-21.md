# Sat 21 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Three-dimensional Navier–Stokes solutions can go
singular in finite time, which settles the Millennium Prize problem against
global regularity. Two separate proofs of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis
went public within hours of each other, one written with Claude and one
entirely by hand. The OpenSSL bug circulating as a 9.8 is rated Low by OpenSSL.

## Science + space

### Navier–Stokes blows up, and the Millennium problem falls the other way
Alpöge and Buckmaster construct smooth, compactly supported, divergence-free
data on R³ whose solution goes singular at rate (T−t)^−1/4 in Ḣ¹, disproving
statements (A) and (B) as Fefferman formulated them. A companion paper with
Coiculescu does the same for Euler. Both are Lean-formalised, both heavily
AI-assisted, and the certificate the Navier–Stokes proof rests on is so far
only a hash.
[source](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/)

### Two claimed proofs of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, hours apart
Levent Alpöge says he and Claude found one and formalised it in Lean; Terence
Tao points to the Zulip thread where verification is only starting. Ravi Vakil
then released Alessandro Masullo's 940-page *The Music of the Primes* —
no AI, with Brian Conrad having checked it for months — from the middle of an
AMS meeting. Neither is confirmed.
[source](https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/)

## Dev + infra

### The OpenSSL base64 overflow being passed around as a 9.8 is rated Low
`EVP_EncodeBlock()` overflows its output-length arithmetic near INT_MAX and
writes past a correctly sized buffer. OpenSSL's own CVE record calls it Low:
TLS, QUIC and CMS never encode two-gigabyte buffers, and the calling
application has to supply the length itself. The fix is still an open pull
request with no advisory behind it.
[source](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-121481)

### Vim Classic is a supported Vim fork with a no-generative-AI rule
Drew DeVault's long-term-support fork reaches 8.3.1 with applicable Vim patches
up to 8.2.3237 and Python 2 support removed. It is the second release, Gentoo
and Alpine package it already, and it stays charityware like Vim. The stated
reason for existing is maintenance without generative AI tools.
[source](https://vim-classic.org/news/vim-8.3.1-released.html)

### The European Commission got Matrix onto 40,000 laptops in nine months
Proof of concept in December 2025, every Commission laptop by September 2026,
on Element Server Suite Pro that the Commission hosts and operates itself
through sovereign cloud providers and EU Login. Federation with outside servers
is still unresolved, and for governance reasons rather than technical ones.
Element's own write-up of its own customer, so discount accordingly.
[source](https://element.io/blog/how-the-european-commission-rolled-out-matrix-to-more-than-40-000-staff/)

## Hardware

### An AI-designed CPU claims 4.59x perf/watt against a soft baseline
Architect Labs' Frostbite compiles programs to interaction nets, where
dependencies are explicit, so it drops out-of-order machinery and cache
coherence entirely. Agent swarms took it from ISA to GDS on 7nm, 500MHz final.
Read the number carefully: the comparison core is dual-issue in-order, not a
modern out-of-order design, and nothing has been taped out.
[source](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)

## AI research + releases

### A 27B model rewrote its own weights and gained 6.6 points
Handed its own weights, its graded answers and the ability to run itself, the
model diagnoses a failure and edits the weights behind it — forward passes
only, no gradients. Gains hold on unseen problems across eight sessions, and a
deliberately damaged copy recovers to over 80% of original accuracy. Preprint,
one model family, very large framing.
[source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885)
