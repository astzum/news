# Sun 18 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** PostgreSQL lost eight to ten features from the 19 branch because
LLM-assisted review found more defects than the beta had time to fix, and GA slipped to 19
November. DeepSeek's Flash refresh lands with MIT weights and a Terminal-Bench score 11 points
up on its predecessor. And Home Assistant's Supervisor has quietly been checking your secrets
against Have I Been Pwned for years.

## Dev + infra

### LLM-assisted review raised the bar on code written years ago
Eisentraut, on the receiving end of several reverts: most of the defects were edge cases that
in past cycles would have surfaced over five years and been fixed piecemeal. Review now hands
you "a list of like forty defect reports" per feature — three-line fixes with fixed overhead
each — and beta runs out. Not vibe coding; the code predates it.
[Peter Eisentraut](https://peter.eisentraut.org/blog/2026/10/09/the-reverts-will-continue-until-morale-improves) · [postgresql.org](https://www.postgresql.org/message-id/f161fbb7-e193-42eb-bec7-4cfc1af74a50%40postgresql.org)

### RPKI now validates 70% of routed IPv4 prefixes, and China did most of the moving
On 28 September, 70.3% of routed IPv4 prefixes were valid, against 54.0% a year earlier; China
went from 3.7% to 83.0% of address space. The enforcement figure is the better one: invalid
announcements reach about two vantage points, versus 596 for valid ones. Sixty per cent of
unprotected v4 space is American, roughly a quarter of that DoD.
[IPinfo](https://ipinfo.io/blog/rpki-protects-70-internet-routes)

## AI research + releases

### DeepSeek's Flash refresh ships MIT weights and an 11-point Terminal-Bench jump
The 1017 update claims 42.3 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 31.2 for the previous Flash, and 77.0
on DeepSWE v1.1 — above Opus 5's 74.0, while HLE at 42.6 against 56.3 is not close. 763B
weights under MIT, 1M context, shorter outputs. No Jinja chat template: prompt encoding is a
reference Python file or DeepSeek's Rust library.
[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017)

### Benchmarks miss the work 90% of American working hours go into
Mercor mapped 29 agentic benchmarks onto O*NET's 923 occupations and 18,797 tasks. Only 924
tasks have any coverage, and 208 occupations have a task with five or more benchmark prompts;
six of 22 job families have none at all. Coverage tracks wage bill and AI-exposure tags, so
lawyers get measured and court clerks don't.
[Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/research/labor-index/benchmark-map/)

## Homelab

### Home Assistant has been checking your secrets against Have I Been Pwned
Supervisor hashes options marked as passwords and sends the first five characters of the SHA-1
to HIBP, matching locally — k-anonymity, the same trick a password manager uses. It has done
this for years, undocumented, which is the actual complaint. Disable with `ha security options
--pwned=false`. Frenck apologised for a dismissive first reply and promised docs.
[GitHub](https://github.com/home-assistant/home-assistant.io/issues/49314)

## Hardware

### Someone finally measured N1X: prefill is strong, decode loses to the M5 Max
ServeTheHome's $5,899 Surface Laptop Ultra is 10 Cortex-X925 plus 10 A725 cores, a 48-SM
Blackwell iGPU with roughly RTX 5070 shader counts, and 128GB of LPDDR5X at up to 300GB/s.
Bandwidth is the ceiling, so local-model decode favours Apple's M5 Max 128GB. Gaming is
hit-or-miss: anti-cheat largely doesn't run on Arm Windows.
[ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/microsoft-surface-laptop-ultra-review-rtx-spark-n1x/)

## Science + space

### FAST found a hydrogen cloud with no stars in it
J1351+0039 holds 4.8×10⁵ solar masses of neutral hydrogen and a dynamical mass of 1.3×10⁸,
with nothing optical down to 28.1 mag/arcsec² — a dark-to-baryonic ratio near 200, and gas held
up by pressure rather than rotation. That makes it an isolated candidate for a cloud
reionisation left starless. Preprint, 24 September, unrefereed.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29852)
