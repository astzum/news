# Mon 02 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Linus merged a pile of overlayfs fixes on Friday whose
origin is an LLM code review — info leak, use-after-free and NULL deref, all
reachable by unprivileged users, in the filesystem every container image sits on.
Nvidia's published Rubin specs are now lower than the ones it announced, with no
explanation, as the first production parts land. And the mysterious 60-second ripple
in European grid frequency turns out to be grid batteries all checking their charge
on the minute.

## Dev + infra

### An AI code review found unprivileged kernel bugs in a mature filesystem
Amir Goldstein's `ovl-fixes-7.3` pull, merged 31 October, credits Chris Mason's AI
review for an info leak, use-after-free and NULL deref bugs reachable by
unprivileged users, plus an RCU walk stall with remote layers and data loss with
`nfs_export`. Twenty patches in the tag, one of them a new VFS helper. Overlayfs is
the container storage default.
[commit ca62fc8](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/ca62fc8c6b1841039aee6de1ef03c9863751ec04)

### Bunny revoked every platform TLS certificate after a CDN node's host was breached
An infrastructure provider reported unauthorised access via third-party server
management software; the node was in Armenia. Bunny stopped routing through it
within 20 minutes, wiped it and revoked its access. Neither investigation found
evidence the node itself was touched, but cached content, Pull Zone configs and TLS
certs sat on it. Managed certs were reissued by 31 October; self-managed ones are
your job.
[bunny.net](https://status.bunny.net/incidents/p6gf1by9kr7m)

### KDE is building a terminal on Ghostty's VT engine
Eike Hein's Kwi wires `libghostty-vt` into Qt/QML with Kirigami and a
GPU-accelerated Qt Scene Graph, claiming 3x–35x over Konsole depending on the
benchmark — 870fps against Konsole's 23 on DOOM-fire-zig. There is no tagged v0.1.0
yet, only nightly Flatpaks, and Hein says to temper expectations. The reuse of
Ghostty's parser is the interesting part.
[KDE](https://blogs.kde.org/2026/11/01/introducing-kwi/)

## Hardware

### Nvidia quietly cut Rubin's published bandwidth as the first hardware shipped
ServeTheHome lined up Nvidia's current spec page against its earlier disclosures:
per-GPU HBM4 bandwidth falls from 22TB/s to 19.2TB/s and NVLink from 3.6TB/s to
3.0TB/s, while scale-out networking goes the other way, 0.4 to 0.45TB/s. No
explanation was offered. These are the numbers shipping Rubin parts carry, so size
capacity off them.
[ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/nvidia-updates-rubin-gpu-specs-as-first-production-hardware-arrives/)

### A white LED with no rare earths at all, in production this quarter
Seoul Viosys says WICOP 3 makes white light from gallium nitride alone — no YAG
phosphor, no silicone, no lead frame — in a chip 25µm by 10µm putting out 0.015
lumens. Efficacy is near 100lm/W now, with 200 claimed within two years, at about
half the build cost of a conventional white LED. Q4 mass production.
[Seoul Semiconductor](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72453208/seoul-semiconductor-ends-need-for-rare-earths-in-white-leds?publisherId=58763726)

## AI industry

### Opening a shared ChatGPT link could hand over the whole account, for $5,000
Mukul Goyal chained a MathLive message listener that never checked origin, an HTML
Preview that fed it markup, a `srcdoc` iframe inheriting chatgpt.com, and a YouTube
oEmbed endpoint to get past CSP. That gave arbitrary JavaScript in an authenticated
session and access to conversations never shared with the attacker. Reported 18
October, fixed 26 October, bounty $5,000.
[Parameter](https://www.parameter.ai/labs/we-found-a-gap-at-openai)

## Science + space

### Grid batteries all check their charge on the minute, and the grid wobbles
Dirk Witthaut's group at Jülich, with Cologne and KIT, traced the regular 60-second
frequency ripple to large battery systems running automatic state-of-charge checks
synchronised to the top of each minute. In Ireland the one-minute signal quadrupled
between 2016 and 2021. The spikes wear turbines in hydro plants. The fix is to
randomise the check times.
[Forschungszentrum Jülich](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/news/archive/press-release/2026/mystery-of-60-second-fluctuations-in-power-grids-solved)
