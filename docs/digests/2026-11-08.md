# Sun 08 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Libghostty's C ABI is frozen at 1.0, which unblocks
Neovim's switch off libvterm and a ten-year-old scrollback bug. An Android app
containing no code at all can put a phone in a crash loop that only a factory reset
clears, and Google has declined to treat it as a security bug. Microsoft Research's
AI Frontiers group has been absorbed into Microsoft AI and every founding leader has
walked.

## Dev + infra

### Libghostty 1.0 freezes a C ABI for terminal emulation
Hashimoto's extraction of Ghostty's core now carries an enforced stability policy.
JetBrains IDEs already ship it — over 12.5 million active users by JetBrains' own
count — and Neovim's approved PR replacing libvterm was waiting on exactly this
tagged release so distributions can package it. That switch closes a ten-year-old
scrollback-reflow issue. Turborepo, devenv and OrbStack embed it too.
[source](https://mitchellh.com/writing/libghostty-1-0)

### A malformed ZIP entry bricks Android phones, and Google won't patch it
Bitdefender found Android mishandles a ZIP entry's compression method and declared
uncompressed length, so reading the file raises SIGBUS in whichever process touched
it. Install a crafted APK — no executable code, no permissions, never opened — and a
core system process crash-loops through reboots and safe mode. Reproduced on a Pixel
10 running Android 17; Android 10 and up affected.
[source](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/labs/ghostmap-android)

### Asahi Linux puts its two largest drivers on the mailing list
The 7.3 report submits macaudio (~3,100 lines, after core ALSA changes) and the AVD
video decoder (~13,000 lines, three months downstream) upstream, plus initial
Thunderbolt support. M3 loses the installer's EXPERT flag and gains sleep and DCP.
The M3 GPU — a real break from M1/M2 — has reached first-triangle in m1n1. M4 and M5
devicetrees boot to a TTY.
[source](https://asahilinux.org/2026/11/progress-report-7-3/)

## AI research + releases

Thinking Machines' second generation runs 17B active parameters, takes image input

ships closed-weight through Tinker first, with Apache 2.0 weights due once external

## AI industry

### Microsoft Research's AI Frontiers group is gone, and its founders with it
John Langford says the ~50-person group behind Autogen, the Fara computer-use agent,
Dion3 and the Full Bandwidth Transformer was reorged into Microsoft AI in early
September, where he found the ex-Inflection leadership's scaling-first,
"aggressively not research" philosophy incompatible. He left; so, he writes, did
every other founding AIF leader. He also declined to go back to MSR.
[source](https://hunch.net/?p=13764006)

## Homelab

### llama.cpp will now run cache-missed MoE experts on the CPU
`--moe-cache-cpu-share` computes experts absent from the VRAM cache on the CPU
instead of uploading them, and a new `scripts/moe-cache-tune.py` sweeps it against
`--moe-cache-mib` to find a config for your machine. On the author's desktop a 6,464
MiB cache went 24.2 to 47.6 tok/s at share 0.95 — the gain is biggest when VRAM is
the binding constraint.
[source](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/30966)

## Hardware

### Bluetooth gets a 7.5 Mbps physical layer
The SIG has adopted High Data Throughput, a new LE PHY topping out at 7.5 Mbps,
nearly four times the 2M PHY. The pitch is as much spectral efficiency and keeping
the radio off longer as raw speed. The payoff is standardised lossless and
high-resolution audio, which the SIG puts in 2027 — not a firmware update to
anything you own.
[source](https://www.bluetooth.com/blog/bluetooth-high-data-throughput-delivers-a-higher-level-of-wireless-performance/)

## Science + space

### A condensate on the Chinese space station reached 9.7 picokelvin
An optical dipole trap held 2.0 × 10⁴ atoms through 7.0 seconds of free expansion at
an equivalent 9.7 pK (+3.2/−2.7), against a prior record of 38 pK over 2 seconds in
atom-chip magnetic traps. Optical traps give better optical access and work on
non-magnetic species, so the real result is a class of microgravity experiments that
was previously unavailable.
[source](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aea5324)
