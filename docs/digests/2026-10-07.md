# Wed 07 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** The DNS root changes its key-signing key on 11 October, and a
validating resolver that doesn't already trust KSK-2024 will fail every TLD at once.
OpenSSH 10.6 disables the LZ77 dictionary coder because the shared compression context
leaks plaintext across multiplexed channels. And Mistral Large 4 is a 1T-parameter
open-weight model pitched hardest at the cyber work closed models refuse.

## Dev + infra

### OpenSSH 10.6 turns off most of what Compression did
Released today. The headline fix disables the LZ77 dictionary coder after "Crossing the
Streams" showed a chosen-plaintext attack recovering secrets from one channel by poking the
session's shared compression dictionary through another. Compression gets much weaker — use
application-level compression instead. Hybrid post-quantum `ssh-mldsa44-ed25519` is now
enabled, and `ssh` refuses `$` and `\` in command-line usernames.
[release notes](https://www.openssh.org/releasenotes.html#10.6)

### The DNS root switches signing keys on 11 October
KSK-2017 (key tag 20326) stops signing the root DNSKEY set and KSK-2024 (38696) takes over
— only the second time this has happened. KSK-2024 has been published in the root since
January 2025, so current software already ships it, but a validating resolver that lacks it
breaks *every* top-level domain, not some. Check unbound, BIND and Pi-hole trust anchors.
[post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/root-ksk-2024-rollover/)

### Polars 2.0 makes the streaming engine the default, and row order the caller's problem
That default is what forced the major bump: `join`, `group_by` and `unpivot` no longer
guarantee row order unless you pass `maintain_order=True`. Spill-to-disk is now on by
default, kicking in at ~80% of RAM with a 64GB disk budget, though only sort, window
functions and many expressions support it — joins and group-bys don't spill yet.
[release](https://pola.rs/posts/release-polars-2/)

## AI research + releases

### Mistral Large 4 is aimed squarely at the cyber work closed models refuse
1T parameters, 49B active, natively multimodal, trained on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs in
Mistral's own European datacentres. Preview API today; weights at the end of the month. The
sharp claim is security: 82% on the Artificial Analysis reproduce-and-patch test, 93% on
Cybench, where Mistral points out Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra score near zero because
they decline the task.
[announcement](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)

### Models will name a problem's fatal flaw and still report it as solved
Thirty mechanics problems, each paired with a version made physically impossible by changing
one given. Across three recent models, 12 of 90 replies failed to reject the flawed version
— and in 11 of those, the model stated the flaw, answered a corrected problem, and still
returned status "solved". Accuracy scoring hides this completely.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06668)

## AI industry

### erdosproblems.com has stopped accepting proofs
Thomas Bloom froze new comments and proof claims, removed the open/solved labels, and
dropped credit attribution in favour of "it is known that…". The site holds 1,221 problems
and over 9,000 comments, and sees 10–25k visitors a day. His reason: unexplained
AI-generated proofs filed to claim priority were displacing the people who wanted to
understand the mathematics.
[post](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/blog:9)

## Homelab

### EmbeddingGemma 2 puts multimodal retrieval in 191MB of RAM
740M parameters, Apache 2.0, text/code/images/video/audio in one embedding space. Quantised
it needs ~191MB active RAM for text-only weights, ~567MB for full multimodal, and drops to
270M params if you skip the vision and audio encoders. 8K context, Matryoshka truncation
down to 128 dimensions. MTEB Code went 68.76 → 78.68.
[post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/)

### Tapo plugs can now be driven with Third-Party Compatibility switched off
The toggle arrived in firmware 1.4.0 and has been quietly 403-ing scripts ever since.
`tapo` v0.11.1 implements TPAP, TP-Link's SPAKE2+ handshake, so the switch can stay off.
Also lands H200/H500 camera hub support and plug schedules, and drops the legacy AES
protocol. Third security-driven lockout of third-party clients in three years.
[post](https://mihai.dinculescu.dev/posts/tapo-speaks-tpap/)
