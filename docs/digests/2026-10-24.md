# Sat 24 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Every GitLab release since 7.0 has carried an
unauthenticated remote code execution bug in its regular expression compiler, and the
patch is out. Anthropic and Theorem had Claude write 180 million lines of machine-checked
proof that 123 open-source libraries cannot crash. And eMudhra is leaving the public
certificate authority business altogether rather than try to rebuild trust.

## Dev + infra

### Every GitLab since 7.0 shipped an unauthenticated RCE in its regex compiler
CVE-2026-110264 scores a flat CVSS 10.0: an integer overflow when compiling a crafted
regular expression, reachable without logging in. Patched 21 October in 19.5.1 and
backported as far as 18.11.13. GitLab.com and Dedicated are already covered; self-managed
is not. Two detections ship with it — watch for Onigmo worker crashes and Org-mode render
error bursts.
[GitLab](https://docs.gitlab.com/releases/patches/patch-release-gitlab-19-5-1-released/)

### eMudhra is quitting the public CA business rather than rebuilding trust
After its intermediate keys were cracked, the board voted to withdraw from global SSL/TLS
entirely. The company says the line was about 1% of revenue and blames free competitors
plus "disproportionate social media scrutiny". Existing certificates get free replacements
through partner CAs, with revocation kept running until all are replaced or expire. Its
enterprise PKI and eSign products continue.
[eMudhra](https://emudhra.com/en/press-release/emudhra-to-exit-global-ssl-tls-certificate-business)

### OpenSSL 4.1.0 ships DTLS 1.3 and four known DTLS 1.3 bugs with it
Out 23 October. RFC 9147 lands alongside GREASE, an IKEv2 KDF, DTLS in the listener API,
and AVX-512 work on AES-CBC decryption and ML-DSA. The release notes name four open DTLS
1.3 issues, including retransmissions silently dropped after a KeyUpdate. Windows-on-Itanium
and Windows CE targets are gone, as are the `no-ecdsa` and `no-ecdh` build options.
[OpenSSL](https://github.com/openssl/openssl/releases/tag/openssl-4.1.0)

## AI research + releases

### Claude wrote 180 million lines of Rocq to prove 123 libraries cannot crash
Anthropic and Theorem proved no-crash properties for 239 fuzz entry points across 123
OSS-Fuzz libraries at the RISC-V assembly level — roughly 600,000 proof lines per binary.
The caveats are large: single-threaded static binaries, inputs under 64MB, modelled Linux
only. Writing the proofs turned up real bugs, including a rediscovery of CVE-2026-56132 in
libexpat. A subset is published.
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)

### Kimi K3.1 Pro lands within a point of Opus 5.5 on Terminal-Bench
The new 3T-class open-weight model scores 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 66.4% for
Claude Opus 5.5 and 58.2% for GPT-6 Astra, with a one-million-token context. A Highspeed
variant runs 2–3× faster. It is live on the Kimi API now; weights are promised by
4 November, which is when the comparison starts to mean something.
[Kimi](https://www.kimi.ai/blog/kimi-k3-1-pro)

ServeTheHome's MSI XpertStation WS300 is NVIDIA's DGX Station GB300 design: 72-core Grace,

## Homelab

### A Roborock vacuum can keep its maps on your LAN without being rooted
`local_roborock_server` runs the vendor cloud backend yourself — Docker Compose or a Home
Assistant add-on, live maps and controls intact, no internet. No soldering or bootloader
unlock. The cost is a domain you control, local DNS rewriting and a real TLS certificate.
Most v2-protocol models work; entry-level Q series such as the Q7 does not yet.
[local_roborock_server](https://github.com/Python-roborock/local_roborock_server)

## Science + space

### Spinning black holes might be able to split in two, but only just
A Physical Review D paper works out when a horizon can take "pants" topology — one black
hole becoming two. The area law rules it out for Schwarzschild, and Kerr kinematics block
it except possibly near-extremal. Gregory-Laflamme in higher dimensions, superradiance and
modified entropy-mass relations each open a window that mostly closes again. No probability
attached.
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24642)
