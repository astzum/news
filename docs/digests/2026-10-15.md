# Thu 15 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Gemini 4 Argon is out of its cyber-defender sandbox and on the
paid API, at introductory pricing that doubles on 1 December. Torvalds posted his first
LLM-assisted patch series and review found a race condition in it. And Trail of Bits
tested 25 package managers: two verify the integrity of everything they download.

## Dev + infra

### Torvalds' first LLM-assisted patch series already has a race condition in it
Ten patches that notice when a folio is hot and defer its release until after an RCU
period, so lookups can skip reference counting — an order-of-magnitude speedup on a
read()-heavy benchmark. LWN reported it on 14 October, along with the review that found
the race. Worth watching for how the kernel handles provenance, not for the patches.
[LWN](https://lwn.net/Articles/1100449/)

### An AI scanner found a libcurl use-after-free a hostile server can turn into RCE
CVE-2026-92392, rated HIGH: after an HTTP/2 stream reset a lingering pointer to a freed
easy handle lets the server write into reclaimed memory. Reported by Luna Tong at
Anthropic via their OSS Scanner, fixed in 8.23.0. The real fix is structural — curl is
replacing internal handle pointers with transfer IDs looked up on demand.
[writeup](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/10/14/a-uaf-turned-rce/)

### Only two of 25 package managers verify everything they download
Trail of Bits tested 25. Fewer than half use tamper-checking lockfiles by default; only
Go and vcpkg verify integrity on every fetch. Nine have no lockfile protection at all,
nine ship no signatures at all, and cpanminus still defaults to plain HTTP, so an on-path
attacker can inject code. Where signatures exist they are mostly opt-in.
[research](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/10/14/package-managers-have-an-integrity-problem/)

### Go's net/http stopped throwing away connections on unread response bodies
Transport now drains an unread HTTP/1 body in the background — up to 50ms or 256KB —
instead of abandoning the connection, so the pool gets reused without the usual
io.Copy-to-Discard dance. Their example: 100 requests to index.golang.org, 710ms on Go
1.26 against 150ms on 1.27. The change shipped in August; the writeup is from yesterday.
[post](https://go.dev/blog/http-connection-reuse)

## Science + space

### Roman's main camera is beating its image-quality requirement by a third
NASA activated the Wide Field Instrument's 18 detectors in September and has now finished
alignment: 63 nanometres RMS against a 90nm requirement, after nudging the secondary
mirror about a sixteenth of a hair's width. The coronagraph came up slightly out of focus
and has been corrected. Roman launched 30 August.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/roman/2026/10/14/nasa-roman-team-checks-out-main-instrument-sharpens-telescopes-focus/)

### JAXA's Phobos sample-return mission launches next week
MMX goes up on an H3 from Tanegashima on 20 October Japan time, carrying the 25kg
French-German rover Idefix. Mars orbit August 2027, Phobos orbit a month later, rover
deployment early 2029, then the spacecraft itself lands in 2030 to collect at least ten
grams — returning to Earth, via a landing in Australia, in 2031.
[ESA](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Mars_moon_lander_set_to_launch_on_19_October)
