# Wed 04 Nov 2026

**The three that matter:** Opening Postman writes a plugin and a startup hook into
Codex and Claude Code, and marks its own hook trusted so you never see a prompt.
Yesterday's "an LLM edited another model's weights" result turns out to be ten
different experiments, and the headline one writes Python and runs the model.
And Marvell is paying $115 billion of implied enterprise value for Lumentum.

## Dev + infra

### Postman installed itself into your coding agent and approved its own hook
Opening Postman 12.33.4 on macOS wrote a plugin, a marketplace entry and a
SessionStart hook into `~/.codex/config.toml`, instructing the agent to load
`postman:api-engineer` before any non-trivial API work — plus a Claude Code plugin
and a remote MCP connection. The installer then writes its own `trusted_hash`, so
the hook needs no approval. Open since 25 October, no response from Postman.
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/postmanlabs/postman-app-support/issues/14370)

### RADV's maintainers now say they may close your LLM-written merge request
Samuel Pitoiset added nine lines to `docs/drivers/radv.rst` on 2 November:
LLM-assisted MRs need a real understanding of the codebase, reviewing them has a
cost, and reviewers may close them at their discretion. LLM-written issue reports
are discouraged outright; generated reproducers are fine if you have checked them.
Written before the flood, not after.
[Mesa MR 45668](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/merge_requests/45668)

## AI research + releases

### Yesterday's weight-editing result does not do what its abstract says
Matthew Honnibal tabulated all ten experiments. "Just by looking at the raw weights"
holds for three, on 17K–172K-parameter networks. The headline Qwen result writes
Python and runs the model on 50 batches of practice problems — an ML engineer with a
REPL. Simon Willison reproduced that on a laptop: Qwen 3.8 27B named three of four
game networks, ~50 minutes each.
[Honnibal](https://honnibal.dev/blog/astra-read-write-misleading) ·
[Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/qwen-can-interpret-model-weights/)

## Hardware

### Marvell is paying $115 billion for the optics in front of the switch
An all-stock deal announced 2 November: 4.500 Marvell shares per Lumentum share,
about $1,132 a share, a 23.9% premium to the 28 October close, and $115 billion of
implied enterprise value. Lumentum holders end up with roughly a third of the
combined company. Marvell claims a $550 billion addressable market and $1bn of
run-rate synergies. Closing is not expected until H1 2028.
[SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001633978/000119312526442758/d163169dex991.htm)

### A data-centre TPU ran a model in orbit and wrote a haiku about it
Google's Project Suncatcher prototype launched 1 October; on 30 October it reported
the first inference from a data-centre-class TPU in low orbit, running Gemma 4 and
downlinked over Svalbard. The point is not the poem — it is that the TPU trays
survived commissioning. Thermal and radiation testing comes next, then twin
interconnected satellites in 2027.
[Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response/)

## Science + space

### Swift comes down this morning, 22 years after launch
NASA puts re-entry at about 3:30am EST on 4 November, give or take 11 hours, for the
3,200-pound Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory; science operations ended 23 October. Most
of it burns, some components are expected to survive, confined between 20.6°S and
20.6°N by the orbit. Stated risk of harm to anyone: 1 in 2,100. A commercial boost
spacecraft failed to capture it in August.
[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/swift/2026/11/02/nasas-swift-observatory-to-re-enter-atmosphere/)

### A phrase read from a Herculaneum scroll nobody had ever read
PHerc. 306B was excavated in the 1750s and micro-CT scanned at the ESRF last year.
Liam Warner and Nicola Bodill's ink detection made one diagonal stroke visible,
which settled αι against mu or nu and gave τὸ δι᾽ αὑτὸ αἱρετόν — "that which is in
itself desirable", an Epicurean phrase. $50K First Letters Prize, awarded 2 November
out of 25 submissions.
[Vesuvius Challenge](https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/writing-seen-in-pherc-306b-for-the)
