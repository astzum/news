# Sun 11 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** Typesafe AI raised $870M at a $7.5B valuation for models that
make decisions inside software instead of producing text. A new probe reads deception off
a model's activations more reliably than a frontier model reads its transcript. And
Bitwarden's app-store builds move to a commercial licence, with self-hosting untouched.

## AI industry

### Typesafe AI raised $870M at $7.5B for "machine-native" models
a16z led, with Sequoia and existing investor DCVC; Martin Casado joins the board. The
product, Jev, is in early access and described only as a "System One model" that decides
within applications. The post claims a third of the Fortune 500 and millions saved, and
offers no benchmark or technical detail. Fourth entrant in this category in two weeks.
[announcement](https://typesafe.ai/blog/series-ai)

### Anthropic's usage policy rewrite takes effect 12 November
Weapons prohibitions now cover software and components that make weapons work, including
arming autonomous vehicles. Surveillance language tightens: no tracking without consent,
and Claude may not decide who to investigate, arrest or charge. Hardware-connected
deployments need an operator who can observe and stop the equipment. The blanket ban on
personalised campaign targeting is gone.
[policy update](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update)

## AI research + releases

### Probes read deception off activations better than a frontier model reads transcripts
Hollinsworth and co-authors train a probe that aggregates across many layers and tokens,
reaching 98.8% AUC on SHADE-Arena — past a Claude Opus 3.5 text-monitoring baseline — and
up to 99.7% separating a model's real hidden goal from decoys. Detection gets better as
the monitored model scales. Training data released as FIBS. Preprint, 8 October.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12445)

## Dev + infra

### SQLite 3.54 drops Windows XP and folds sqlite3_analyzer into the shell
Out 9 October: about 4% fewer CPU cycles than the last release and 7.5% fewer than a year
ago. Vista or later now required. The standalone analyzer is deprecated in favour of a
`diskused()` extension in the CLI. Terminating SQL with a bare `go` or `/` line is gone
unless you build with the legacy flag.
[release notes](https://sqlite.org/releaselog/3_54_0.html)

### Unison Cloud is now MIT-licensed, including the parts that weren't
Nimbus the worker node, the Haskell API server and the web UI all opened on 8 October; the
client already was. That makes the whole distributed runtime self-hostable, with the
company selling support instead of hosting. Worth a look if Unison's content-addressed
code model appealed but the hosted dependency didn't.
[post](https://www.unison-lang.org/blog/unison-cloud-open-source/)

## Homelab

### Bitwarden's app-store builds move to a commercial licence
From the next release, the desktop, mobile and browser builds distributed through official
stores are commercially licensed. The GPLv3 source keeps being published to GitHub, and
self-hosting is explicitly unchanged — the stated target is people repackaging and
reselling. Future features get licensed case by case, which is the part worth watching.
[announcement](https://community.bitwarden.com/t/published-version-update-in-app-stores/102750)

## Hardware

### A 128GB Ryzen AI Max+ 395 mini-PC manages 24.7 tok/s on Phi-4 14B
ServeTheHome measured that on the GMKtec EVO-X3 in MLPerf Client 1.6.1, with 1.46s to
first token, against 128GB of soldered LPDDR5X-8000 and a 40-CU Radeon 8060S. Selectable
power modes are 54W, 85W and 140W. It placed last among the 395 boxes tested on both CPU
and GPU, so memory capacity is the reason to buy one, not speed.
[review](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x3-mini-pc-review/)

## Science + space

### A 100nm glass bead stayed entangled with light at room temperature
Deplano, Pontin, Marino and Marin held the sphere in an optical tweezer inside a cavity and
measured a separability parameter of 0.918 ± 0.029, below the classical bound of 1, holding
across more than 40kHz of bandwidth with no cryogenics. The correlations persist in the
light leaving the cavity, which is the property that would make this a quantum memory.
[paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aeh1375) · [CNR-INO](https://www.ino.cnr.it/?p=27288)
