# Fri 02 Oct 2026

**The three that matter:** arXiv now allows two submissions per author per month, after
volume grew fourfold in a decade. Reddit will stop serving RSS on 13 November and move
every bot onto its Developer Platform. And Cloudflare has published two Apache-2.0
decision models that beat the incumbent on latency by more than tenfold.

## AI industry

### arXiv now caps you at two submissions a month
In force from 1 October: two submissions per calendar month, three active at any time,
across all categories. Rejections count against the limit; papers deleted before
announcement don't. The stated cause is volume — 9,869 submissions in September 2016
against 40,363 last month — overwhelming volunteer moderators. Plan conference pushes
around it.
[policy](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/)

### Reddit retires RSS on 13 November and herds bots onto Devvit
RSS goes because it has become "a common surface for large-scale scraping and automated
abuse." Mods get the Discord Relay Devvit app; for feeds outside a community you moderate
there is no replacement. Apps and bots must register on the Developer Platform before 12
January 2027 — over 14,000 have. Old Reddit narrows to recently active accounts.
[announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/modnews/comments/1wubgvt/continuing_our_infrastructure_updates_whats/)

## AI research + releases

### Cloudflare's Clef undercuts Jev's latency by more than 10x
Clef and Clef-flash are Apache-2.0 on Hugging Face and hosted on Workers AI: rank-256
adapters over frozen Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.5-9B backbones. Clef-flash medians 38.8ms
against Jev's 524.1ms and scores 98.76% case-exact on BFCL. 64k context against Jev's
32k, and Clef carries a vision encoder. The RL fine-tuning platform is partner-only for
now.
[announcement](https://blog.cloudflare.com/clef-decision-models/)

### Letting a model edit its own context beats growing the window
Context Language Models hand the model its context as an editable file it maintains
itself. Reported: 11.4% better accuracy for 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus, 5%
better for 59% fewer on 12-hour EdgeBench, and RL lifting Qwen3.5-9B 47.6% on
BrowseComp-Plus while using 12% fewer FLOPs. Author list includes Lewis, Zettlemoyer and
Koh.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37725)

## Dev + infra

### rustc got 4.57% faster in two months, almost everywhere
Nethercote's September roundup: 555 of 629 benchmarks improved, 74 regressed. The LLVM 23
upgrade alone was 1.2% mean wall-time, PGO on Clippy was worth up to 18%, and a dataflow
CFG traversal change cut cranelift-codegen wall-time by about 30%. Polonius alpha and the
new trait solver are still being tuned.
[post](https://nnethercote.github.io/2026/09/30/how-to-speed-up-the-rust-compiler-in-september-2026.html)

### Scott Chacon argues the SHA-256 default will split Git in two
His case: SHA-1 and SHA-256 repositories cannot mix, submodules must match, conversion
invalidates every existing signature, and most non-core Git libraries still lack SHA-256
support — so maintainers end up shipping two of everything. He would rather see
independent tree-hash headers in signed objects than a global migration.
[essay](https://blog.gitbutler.com/git-3-sha-256)

## Homelab

### Debian fixed 33 rsync CVEs by shipping a whole new rsync
DSA-6527-1, 28 September: trixie moves to 3.5.0+ds1-0+deb13u1, covering CVE-2026-53783
through 53803 and CVE-2026-70452 through 70464. Impact spans local privilege escalation,
access-restriction bypass, information disclosure, DoS and arbitrary code execution. A
minor-version jump inside stable security is unusual — if anything on your network serves
rsyncd or rrsync, patch and re-read the module config.
[advisory](https://lists.debian.org/debian-security-announce/2026/msg00440.html)

### Every ESP32 has been a software-defined radio the whole time
ESPARGOS found an undocumented debug path — with LLM help — that dumps raw baseband I/Q
past the fixed-function Wi-Fi modem, on parts from the original ESP32 up to the C61.
esp-sdr is GPL-3.0 and reaches 80 MS/s with 12–69 MHz of analog bandwidth depending on
chip; the C5 covers 5 GHz. Captures have gaps and gain is uncalibrated.
[repository](https://github.com/ESPARGOS/esp-sdr)
