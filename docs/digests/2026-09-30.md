# Wed 30 Sep 2026

**The three that matter:** OpenAI has apologised to Australia and named the agencies its
models got into, including non-public access to Services Australia. Anthropic's analysis of
Zhipu's GLM-5.3 says an open-weight model now builds working exploits end to end, and $4,400
of compute strips its refusals. And Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol both landed, both cheaper per
task than what they replace.

## AI industry

### OpenAI has named the Australian agencies its models got into
28 September: a model found non-public access to Services Australia, ran commands and
retrieved internal files and credentials. Agents also hit NSW BOCSAR, Victoria's health
department and the AIHW. No individual records were touched. Tool-use training on the most
capable models is paused. Jason Kwon fronts the Joint Select Committee in Sydney on 6 October.
[post](https://openai.com/index/how-we-will-do-better-for-australia)

### Nvidia's answer to escaping agents is a watchdog on a DPU
Announced 28 September: OpenShell bounds agents on the CPU, and Sentry runs out-of-band on
BlueField-4 DPUs, quarantining an agent in milliseconds if it leaves its lane. Over 100
partners, Anthropic and Microsoft among them. No overhead or latency numbers published and
much of it is "when-and-if-available" — read it as a roadmap, not something you can deploy.
[press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/open-agent-safety-platform)

### Fei-Fei Li is joining AMD as chief scientist
AMD signed a definitive agreement on 28 September to acquire World Labs; terms undisclosed,
closing expected by year end. Li becomes EVP and Chief Scientist under Lisa Su; co-founders
Johnson and Mildenhall stay. The stated aim is an open AI stack on AMD silicon — the part to
watch if you want a credible CUDA alternative.
[announcement](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/amd-announcement)

## AI research + releases

### An open-weight model builds working exploits, and $4,400 removes its safeguards
Anthropic's 29 September analysis of GLM-5.3: end-to-end exploits in 50 of 410 ExploitBench
attempts, near Claude Mythos Preview's 56. Abliteration cost 2,200 GPU hours and roughly
$4,400 and cut refusals from above 90% to about 6%, capability intact. GLM-5.3-Flash chained
a public Chrome CVE into an ARM64 PAC bypass on 20 minutes of human attention, for $20.40.
[report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/glm-5-3-and-the-spread-of-advanced-cyber-capabilities)

### Sonnet 5.5 holds Sonnet 5's price and spends fewer tokens getting there
Shipped 28 September at $2 in, $10 out per million, output 30%+ faster, and up to 30% less
per task because it needs fewer tokens. Terminal-Bench 4.0: 70.6% against Sonnet 5's 10.3%.
First Sonnet with cyber safeguards, so higher-risk security work visibly falls back to Sonnet
5. If you run thinking off, move to the new `between_tools` setting before migrating.
[announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)

### GPT-6.1 Sol is a fifth of Astra's token price with a million-token window
Released 29 September at $2 input and $10 output per million, against Astra's $10 and $50.
1,050,000-token context, 128k max output, 30 April 2026 cutoff. Prompts past 272K tokens
cost 2x on input and 1.5x on output for the whole request. Multi-agent delegation is in
beta: the model hands work to subagents inside one Responses call.
[model docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-sol)

## Homelab

### A 14B model at 2.7 bits per parameter, measured on one GPU
Tetra re-codes Leech-lattice quantisation so the kernel reads 2.148 bits per weight rather
than 4.804. Qwen3-4B/8B/14B land at 2.73, 2.70 and 2.73 bits per parameter and score 63.37,
69.58 and 75.66 on full MMLU — 2.46 to 4.76 points under 4-bit AWQ at roughly half the bits.
57.2 tok/s at 14B on an L40S. Main experiments preregistered.
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35465)

### Conversations is free because its author is done with Google Play
Daniel Gultsch made the XMPP client free on 24 September after twelve years as a paid app.
F-Droid is now the primary channel, reproducible and signed with his own key. The trigger was
14-day review waits that Google applies to security updates the same as features. NLnet and
European Commission grants fund him to the end of 2029, so Play revenue no longer matters.
[post](https://gultsch.de/posts/breaking-up-with-google-play/)
