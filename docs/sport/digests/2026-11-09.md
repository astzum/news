# Mon 09 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea go into the international break fifth after a flat 1–0 loss at Sunderland, decided by a penalty given at one end and one waved away at the other. Brighton are now second, five behind Arsenal, and Manchester United shipped four at home and somehow still drew 4–4 with Aston Villa. At Interlagos, Lando Norris won in the wet to hand Mercedes the constructors' title, with Verstappen second from 21st on the grid.

## Chelsea

### One penalty given, one refused, and Chelsea's attack produced almost nothing
Brian Brobbey spun Levi Colwill on 36 minutes, both men with a fistful of shirt, and Enzo Le Fée scored the only goal. Minutes earlier Luke O'Nien had chopped Cole Palmer down, but VAR ruled the contact started outside the box. Colwill was hooked at half-time; Danny Welbeck headed the best chance over.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-sunderland-1-0-chelsea) · [BBC analysis](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c5m2ddz437nzo)

### Seven first-teamers out, and Alonso expects most of them back for Leeds
Josh Acheampong felt something on Saturday and was left out entirely — Alonso says he should return after the break. Emi Martínez is training in a protective mask after jaw surgery and needs "one more week, ten days". Jorrel Hato, Jamie Gittens, Reece James, Maxence Lacroix and João Pedro remain out.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/alonso-we-didnt-produce-enough-to-deserve-to-win)

## Premier League

### Manchester United concede four and still take a point off Aston Villa
Senne Lammens let Alysson's shot through his legs after 48 seconds and teed up Nicolas Jackson's on four minutes; Leny Yoro later headed John McGinn's free-kick towards his own goal for 4–2. Joshua Zirkzee on 90+2 and Patrick Dorgu's 96th-minute header saved Michael Carrick, for now. United are 18th.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/08/manchester-united-aston-villa-premier-league-match-report)

### Brighton are second and the only side keeping Arsenal honest
Diego Gómez arrowed a left-footer in from 25 yards on 15 minutes, Igor Thiago was sent off on review for catching Chema Andrés in the face on 23, and Pascal Gross converted a penalty before the break. Brentford never had a shot on target. Sixth win in seven for Brighton.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cqkgkwj1rpl8t)

## Formula 1

### Norris wins a wet-dry-wet Interlagos and Mercedes seal the constructors'
Pole to flag, 14.6 seconds clear, his third win of the year. Kimi Antonelli spun on his reconnaissance lap, spun the lead away on lap 20, spun again, and still finished fifth — with Russell fourth, enough to put Mercedes out of reach on 652 points. Hamilton ninth on his 400th start.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-beats-verstappen-in-wet-sao-paulo-gp-thriller-as-mercedes-win-constructors-championship.7JN7MXLZf5lk5l0C3gfKGI)

### Verstappen came from 21st on the grid to finish second
Thirteenth by the end of lap one, inside the top eight by lap five, and past team-mate Isack Hadjar for second when the Frenchman ran wide at Turn 1. Hadjar held third for a Red Bull double podium, and the fans voted Verstappen driver of the day. He is second in the championship, 111 back.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/driver-of-the-day-verstappen-gets-fan-vote-for-p21-to-podium-performance-in-sao-paulo.6lAa8RxSlJMqHxsNM0rAKF)
