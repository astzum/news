# Wed 11 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's women had 32 shots at Häcken and nothing to show for it — 0–0, two goals ruled out, two off the bar — while the men sit idle until Leeds on the 22nd. The morning's real story is Old Trafford: Michael Carrick's future gets decided at a meeting in Monaco today, and the recommendation on the table is to sack him. Also: F1 is going ahead with Qatar and Abu Dhabi, Victor Wembanyama scored 40 in 25 minutes, and Kirk Cousins is a Viking again.

## Chelsea

### Chelsea rack up 32 shots in Gothenburg and still can't beat Häcken
Two goals disallowed — handball at a corner, then offside — plus two off the crossbar and Ellie Carpenter crossing all night. Livia Peng's saves held the other end. Four Women's Champions League games, no goals conceded, second in the league phase, but that is two draws running.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-hacken-0-0-chelsea)

## Premier League

### Carrick's United future gets settled 800 miles away, in Monaco
Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox fly out today for a long-scheduled strategy meeting with Sir Jim Ratcliffe, and BBC Sport says their recommendation is that the head coach goes. The Glazers get consulted too, so an announcement may not land until Thursday. United are 18th, one point above Palace.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cmy0r041ed0po)

## Formula 1

### F1 stands Imola down and will finish the season in the Middle East
Imola had been told to prepare a race on the Abu Dhabi dates in case the Iran conflict forced a move; on Tuesday it was stood down. Qatar runs 27–29 November, Abu Dhabi 4–6 December. BBC Sport says senior paddock figures are uneasy about missile strikes near the Strait of Hormuz.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/formula1/articles/c53989e97lxwo)

## NBA

### Wembanyama's 40 points in 25 minutes is the fastest since 1951
He added 11 rebounds and nine blocks against Phoenix, had 22 by half-time, and sat out the fourth. Only Sleepy Floyd in 1991 has reached 40 in under 26 minutes since minutes were tracked. San Antonio lost their first two games and have now won nine straight; Devin Booker's 32 was irrelevant.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50440246/victor-wembanyama-scores-40-points-25-minutes-spurs-win-9th-straight)

### Flagg puts 43 on Indiana at 19 and Dallas hang on by three
43 points, 10 rebounds, six assists and three steals, finished off with two pull-up 18-footers in the final minute. Kyrie Irving added 35 in his first back-to-back since the ACL tear that cost him last season. It was Flagg's fifth 40-point game; every other teenager in league history has ten between them.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50440099/flagg-finishes-43-points-mavericks-outlast-pacers)

## NFL

### Minnesota give up a third-round pick to get Kirk Cousins back
Cousins, 38, rejoins the club he left in 2023 with Minnesota's offence 31st in both yards and passing, and 16 points or fewer in five of eight games. He had 20 touchdowns and eight picks for a 4-5 Raiders side that now hands over to rookie Mendoza. San Francisco took Keenan Allen off Indianapolis.
[source](https://www.nfl.com/news/kirk-cousins-vikings-raiders-trade)
