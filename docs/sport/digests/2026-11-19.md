# Thu 19 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's men had nothing on — Leeds at the Bridge on Sunday morning — and the women kicked off against Paris Saint-Germain at Plough Lane as this landed. Emiliano Martínez captained Argentina on his return from jaw surgery. Erling Haaland's penalty six minutes into added time knocked Denmark out of the Nations League, and Quentin Grimes scored 47 to drag the Lakers to a ninth straight win.

## Chelsea

### Martínez returns as Argentina captain a month after jaw surgery
Lionel Scaloni gave him the armband for the post-Messi era, and his first game since the fracture he took in Chelsea's 2–0 win at Everton ended 7–0 against Costa Rica. His 47th international clean sheet on a 71st cap equals Sergio Romero's Argentina record. Aaron Anselmino debuted off the bench.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/internationals-martinez-returns-and-anselmino-debuts-in-huge-argentina-win)

## Internationals

### Haaland's 96th-minute penalty knocks Denmark out of the Nations League
Denmark led 2–0 inside 14 minutes through Rasmus Højlund and Pierre-Emile Højbjerg and needed only to avoid defeat. Haaland pulled one back before half-time, Martin Ødegaard levelled on 83, then a VAR handball call against Joachim Andersen gave Haaland the winner. Level on nine points, Norway go to the quarter-finals on head-to-head.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/17/nations-league-northern-ireland-hungary-norway-denmark)

## Premier League

### The refereeing panel logged six errors last weekend, a joint record
The Key Match Incidents Panel found six on-field mistakes in one round, matching early 2023-24 for the most it has recorded. Only two were judged missed VAR interventions: James Tarkowski's hold on Caleb Yirenkyi's hair, and Kai Havertz being pulled down by Nobel Mendy. Both were unanimous 5–0 votes.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c69q8jy924y4o)

### Juan Mata retires at 38, twice Chelsea's player of the year
Two and a half years at the Bridge brought the Champions League, the Europa League and an FA Cup, with 18 goals in 82 league games, then eight years at Manchester United. He finished at Galatasaray, Vissel Kobe, Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory. "I will never stop being a footballer."
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c8p3vwe1rrzqo)

## Formula 1

### Piastri takes a 10-place grid penalty in Las Vegas
McLaren have fitted a fifth internal combustion engine after a reliability problem, one past his allowance for the year, so the Australian drops ten from wherever he qualifies. The team think the straights give him a real chance to recover; they are 17 points behind Red Bull for third in the constructors'.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/piastri-set-for-10-place-grid-penalty-at-las-vegas-grand-prix.5eT1wWFQzCfOS6W2tvTtLk)

## NBA

### Grimes' career-high 47 rescues the Lakers from 18 down
He made ten threes — only Kobe Bryant had done that for the Lakers — the last with 1.7 seconds left to force overtime, and finished it with two free throws. Luka Dončić added 36 and seven assists. A ninth straight win, 13–2, with Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler out injured.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401910005)
