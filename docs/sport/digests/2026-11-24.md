# Tue 24 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea: nothing from Cobham, no game until Nottingham Forest away on Saturday morning. Fifa confirmed England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland will host the 2035 Women's World Cup — the first World Cup of any kind in Britain since 1966. In the NBA, Josh Giddey racked up 22 assists and still lost, and Boston went down by a point to a Warriors side missing Stephen Curry.

## Football

### Fifa hands the 2035 Women's World Cup to the four home nations
The associations were the only bidders and were ratified at a specially arranged virtual Congress: 48 teams, 104 matches, 39 days, 19 proposed venues with 14 of them in England. The same meeting approved Mexico, the United States, Costa Rica and Jamaica as hosts for 2031.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cx30g83716lzo)

## Premier League

### Raya misses Dortmund, but Arsenal dodge the operation they feared
Arteta said the shoulder Raya hurt claiming a cross at Newcastle on Saturday needs more tests but "doesn't look too serious", and needs no surgery — which "would rule him out for months". He sits out Wednesday morning's Champions League game. Arsenal are still six clear.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cjx2rqpr7l9lo)

## Formula 1

### Verstappen wins the race of the year, then compares it to Mario Kart
352 overtakes, 35 lead changes, six-tenths at the flag — and the winner isn't having it. "For me personally this isn't Formula One, the way that we are racing. It looks great on TV but this isn't how it should be." He blames the energy-deployment rules he's criticised all season.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/nov/23/max-verstappen-red-bull-f1-formula-one-las-vegas-grand-prix)

## NBA

### Giddey's 22 assists are the most by a Bull in 41 years, and Chicago still lose
The Australian had 23 points, 14 rebounds and a career-high 22 assists, and his layup with seven seconds left wiped out a seven-point deficit to force overtime. Then Jamal Murray scored eight of his season-high 45 in the extra period and Denver won 151–145. Jokic: 37-18-11, his 202nd triple-double.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401910032)

### Boston lose a fourth straight, by a point, to a Warriors team without Curry
Golden State hadn't won in five, had just gone 0-5 on the road and used a seventh different starting lineup in seven games — and still held on 88–87. Brandin Podziemski's 18 led them. Jayson Tatum (27) missed a free throw to tie with 56 seconds left, then a jumper at the buzzer.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401910035)
