# Fri 23 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea: no game and no news, Tottenham at the Bridge 03:30 Sunday. ## Champions League

### Rice rescues Arsenal in Munich, but Saka's quad 'does not look good at all'
Eberechi Eze, on for Saka after ten minutes, put Arsenal ahead on 25. Alphonso Davies levelled on 35 and Harry Kane's penalty on 61 turned it, before Rice struck on 82. Arteta told TNT Sports the injury "does not look good at all". Seven points from three games.

### Carrick keeps the United job for Sunday while the club shops around
BBC Sport reports Carrick has been told of no imminent change after the 4–1 at Como, but that United have begun identifying replacements — Eddie Howe, Antonio Conte and Julian Nagelsmann are all interested. Bruno Fernandes keeps the captaincy despite publicly questioning the tactics. Seven games without a win.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cm1wvl5524rno)

## Cycling

### The 2027 Tour starts in Edinburgh and is decided against the clock
ASO's Paris presentation confirmed 3,479km over 21 stages from 2 July: Edinburgh–Carlisle, Keswick–Liverpool, then Welshpool–Cardiff before the race crosses to Cherbourg. Six mountain stages with summit finishes at the Grand Colombier, Les Saisies and the Col du Portet, plus 71km of individual time trial — the most since 2012.
[source](https://www.letour.fr/en/overall-route)

## Formula 1

### Next year's shorter grands prix are a one-season stopgap
The FIA's newly published 2027 rules cut the standard race distance from 305km to 290km, sparing teams a bigger fuel tank as the engine split moves from 53/47 to 58/42. Article B2.5.2 puts 305km back in 2028, when the ratio hits 60/40. Monaco stays at 260km throughout.
[source](https://racingnews365.com/f1-to-reverse-contentious-rule-change-after-only-one-season)
