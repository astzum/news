# Thu 05 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea had no game, but the Premier League's incident panel ruled Cole Palmer should have been sent off for violent conduct against Tottenham — a ban that would have covered the Manchester United win and Sunday's trip to Sunderland. Liverpool threw away two leads and lost 3–2 at Dirk Kuyt's Fenerbahce. Manchester United came from 2–0 down to beat Roma, and Haas have dropped Esteban Ocon for the rest of the season. Arsenal and Manchester City are kicking off in Europe as you read this.

## Chelsea

### The incident panel says Palmer should have been red-carded against Spurs
The Premier League's Key Match Incidents panel voted 3:2 that Chris Kavanagh should have shown a straight red for Palmer's 70th-minute barge into Mateus Fernandes, calling it dangerous contact to the head. That is a three-match ban — he served one. No retrospective action follows, so he plays at Sunderland.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cmdx35len5ggo)

### Chelsea's under-19s are out of the Youth League to Flora Tallinn
Chelsea led twice at Cobham through Mathis Eboue after 94 seconds and Ibrahim Rabbaj, and lost 3–2: Sander Alamaa equalised, Marten Kukkonen levelled again, then Alamaa converted a 78th-minute penalty. Two-time winners of the competition, knocked out by an Estonian side, with Xabi Alonso watching.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cm0ldr0d460do)

## Champions League

### Liverpool lead twice in Istanbul and still lose to Kuyt's Fenerbahce
Trey Nyoni's 25th-minute volley and Alexander Isak's header on 69 both went straight back: Mason Greenwood's free kick wrongfooted Alisson, Archie Brown finished a corner, and Vedat Muriqi headed the winner on 79. Nyoni, 19, is Liverpool's second-youngest Champions League scorer. Their perfect league-phase record went on Kuyt's first night in interim charge.
[source](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/liverpool-fenerbahce-champions-league-match-report)

### Matheus Cunha scores twice in three minutes, then gets sent off
United were 2–0 down inside 17 minutes to Matías Soulé and a Donyell Malen penalty. Bruno Fernandes had a spot kick saved, scored the retake given for encroachment, and Cunha struck on 83 and 86 before a stoppage-time second yellow. A first win in 10 for Michael Carrick, whose job looked gone at half-time.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/03/manchester-united-roma-champions-league-match-report)

### Aston Villa never got near Barcelona
João Gomes gifted Lamine Yamal the opener on seven minutes and Raphinha added two inside 12 second-half minutes. It ends Villa's perfect league-phase record after wins over Club Brugge, Fenerbahce and Viking; Unai Emery has now lost 15 of 16 at the Nou Camp.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/03/barcelona-aston-villa-champions-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Haas drop Ocon for the rest of the season
The team statement says Ocon "will no longer compete on its behalf" in 2026, confirming the mid-weekend removal in Mexico is permanent. Rafael Câmara, the Brazilian rookie already signed for 2027, partners Ollie Bearman at Interlagos. Ocon has 198 starts and one win, and no 2027 seat looks available.
[source](https://www.haasf1team.com/news/team-statement-driver-update-0)
