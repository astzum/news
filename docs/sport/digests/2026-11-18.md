# Wed 18 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's men are still on the break — Leeds at the Bridge on Sunday, nothing from Cobham worth the name — but Estêvão scored and made two as Brazil put seven past Singapore. Jürgen Klopp's Germany hammered the Netherlands 5–0 in Berlin to win the group, and Andy Robertson's record 103rd Scotland cap was ruined by a 4–0 in St Gallen. Orlando ended Boston's perfect home record.

## Chelsea

### Estêvão scores and sets up two as Brazil batter Singapore 7–0
He teed up Vinícius Júnior on 21 so the Madrid forward could finish an early hat-trick, then put Endrick in, then had one ruled out for a foul. His own came early in the second half, deflected off a half-cleared free-kick. Off on 70 to an ovation; seven goals in 16 caps.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/internationals-estevao-nets-and-assists-in-big-brazil-win)

### Chelsea face PSG tomorrow without a centre-forward
Sonia Bompastor confirmed the hamstring Melvine Malard felt against Manchester City rules her out of both games in this block, with ten days before a clearer picture. Kadeisha Buchanan is fit and Giulia Dragoni back in the squad; Lauren James and Naomi Girma are not back until after the break.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/bompastor-updates-on-buchanan-dragoni-malard-and-matsukubo-ahead-of-psg)

## Internationals

### Klopp's Germany put five past the Netherlands to take the group
Derry Scherhant curled one in on 41, Florian Wirtz lifted a shot in off the post on 60 and hammered a second on 71, with Nico Schlotterbeck's header and Brajan Gruda's tap-in either side. Germany's biggest win over the Dutch since 1959 seeds them for Friday's quarter-final draw, away from England and France.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/16/nations-league-roundup-germany-netherlands-greece-serbia)

### Robertson's record cap turns into a 4–0 hiding in St Gallen
Lewis Ferguson poked Zachary Athekame's hopeful cross past his own keeper on 22, then Winsley Boteli, Alvyn Sanches and Zeki Amdouni picked off a second-string Switzerland side. Robertson became Scotland's most-capped player and finished a distant third in a kind group; Sébastien Pocognoli is three months into the job and already in trouble.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/16/switzerland-scotland-nations-league-match-report)

## NBA

### Orlando end Boston's perfect home record and a six-game run
Desmond Bane's 24 points and eight rebounds drove a fourth-quarter rally for 115–105, Orlando's first win in Boston since December 2022 and their fourth in a row. Jayson Tatum had 24 and 12 but five of the Celtics' 11 turnovers. Boston were on the second night of a back-to-back and are 11–3.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401909994)
