# Sat 31 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Nothing finished while you were asleep — no Premier League, no NBA, no NFL, just an hour of practice in Mexico City. Chelsea host Manchester United at 23:30 tonight without Maxence Lacroix, and Kimi Antonelli can be world champion by Monday breakfast.

## Chelsea

### Lacroix is gone until after the international break
Alonso confirmed the hamstring Lacroix felt at Anfield will keep him out "a few weeks" and not back before the November break — he had started every game since joining from Crystal Palace. Jorrel Hato is also out. Caicedo is fine ("no issue"), and Pep Chavarria is in contention after three matches.

## Formula 1

### A top-nine finish in Mexico makes Antonelli champion
Tenth or worse still does it unless Russell or Hamilton win the race. He leads 365 to Russell's 242 and Hamilton's 241 with five rounds left, which already puts Verstappen and Leclerc out of it. Antonelli skipped Thursday's media with a viral illness, then went second in FP1, 0.659s off Leclerc.

## NBA

### Portland lose Ja Morant for at least a month
He felt a twinge and walked off in Thursday's 124–103 home defeat to Memphis; the Blazers said Friday it is a Grade 2 left hamstring strain and he will be reassessed after four weeks, roughly fifteen games. Portland had won their first three before Memphis turned up.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50329588/ja-morant-least-four-weeks-injury-vs-grizzlies)

### Edwards has knee surgery and could miss two months
An MRI on Tuesday found damage in his right knee that scans before camp and during the playoffs had missed, so Minnesota sent him for an arthroscopic cleanup. "We're just concerned about his long-term health," said Tim Connelly. The same knee limited him to 61 games last season.
[source](https://www.nba.com/news/reports-anthony-edwards-to-undergo-arthroscopic-procedure-on-right-knee-miss-up-to-two-months)

## NFL

### Chicago pay two first-rounders for Jeffery Simmons
Tennessee get 2027 and 2028 first-round picks plus a 2027 fourth, and send back a 2028 fifth. Two firsts for an interior defensive lineman is close to unheard of. Simmons was the Titans' 2019 first-rounder, a four-time Pro Bowler and five-time captain; the Bears are buying for January.
[source](https://www.tennesseetitans.com/news/titans-trade-dt-jeffery-simmons-to-the-chicago-bears-in-exchange-for-two-first-round-picks)
