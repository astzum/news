# Wed 21 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea had no game and no news — Emiliano Martínez is still out with the jaw he broke at Everton, and Tottenham come to Stamford Bridge on Sunday. ## Chelsea

### Martínez's broken jaw leaves Penders in goal for the next three
The clash of heads with Jarrad Branthwaite in Saturday's win at Everton fractured his jaw; Chelsea called Sunday's surgery successful and Martínez, 34, says he'll be back "in a few weeks". Penders, 21, kept a clean sheet on debut and starts against Tottenham, Liverpool in the Carabao Cup and Manchester United.

## Premier League

### Webb defends the VAR that let Botman stay on
Howard Webb called Sven Botman's 18th-minute challenge on Nicolas Jackson in Newcastle's 1–0 defeat at Villa a "high-end reckless challenge", but backed Stuart Attwell for leaving the yellow card alone: no stud contact, no scissor action. VAR has now intervened 21 times in seven rounds, against three at the same stage last season.

## NFL

### Terry McLaurin's season is over
Washington's leading receiver tore the patellar tendon in his left knee cutting on a route late in the first quarter at San Francisco on Monday; Dan Quinn confirmed it on Tuesday. The Commanders are 1–4–1, down to Stefon Diggs and Treylon Burks, and McLaurin carries a $34m cap hit next year.
