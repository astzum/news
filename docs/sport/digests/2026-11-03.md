# Tue 03 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Kimi Antonelli is the Formula 1 world champion, at 20 the youngest in the sport's history and Italy's first since 1953 — he only needed ninth in Mexico City and finished third. Max Verstappen won it from fifth while the two Ferraris drove into each other at Turn 4 on lap one. Chelsea: nothing new out of Cobham, Sunderland away Sunday. Newcastle v Everton kicks off as you read this.

## Formula 1

### Antonelli wraps up the title with four races still to run
He went into Sunday needing ninth, started fourth, kept out of a weekend full of carnage and passed Leclerc at Turn 1 after the red-flag restart to finish third. At 20 he takes Sebastian Vettel's youngest-champion record off him by three years, and he is the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/mexico-city-lowdown-all-the-key-moments-as-antonelli-secures-maiden-title-and-verstappen-denies-ferrari-victory.3mPqECWFPlJsdg4jUOZUXz)

### Ferrari hand Verstappen the win by racing each other off the track
Hamilton led from pole until Leclerc came down the outside. Both ran off at Turn 4, Leclerc cut the grass to take the lead and got five seconds for it, and Verstappen slipped through, holding Hamilton off by 0.799s. Fred Vasseur: "Wasting a victory is hard. It's stuck in my throat."
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1298/mexico/race-result)

## NBA

### A Warriors rookie puts up 37 on a night Curry and Green were rested
Yaxel Lendeborg shot 13 of 19 with six threes in the 124–112 win over the Clippers — the most by a Golden State rookie since Curry's 42 in 2010, and a fourth straight win. Two 30-point games in his first seven puts him alongside Wilt Chamberlain and Rick Barry in club history.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909905)

### Doncic has now scored 37 or more four games in a row
45 points, a season-high 15 rebounds, six assists, five steals and eight threes in the Lakers' 131–125 win over Toronto. He had all 45 inside three quarters, which was just as well: a 26-point third-quarter lead was down to single figures before he and Austin Reaves shut it down.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909906)
