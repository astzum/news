# Mon 23 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Liverpool took Manchester United apart 4–0 on Mauricio Pochettino's first day in charge — Leny Yoro off after eight minutes, 2–0 by the 13th — and United end the weekend 19th. Brighton lost 2–0 at Hull to two stoppage-time goals, so Arsenal are still six clear. Max Verstappen won a Las Vegas Grand Prix with 35 changes of lead and Oscar Piastri came from 17th to third. Chelsea: no men's game until Nottingham Forest away on Saturday morning.

## Chelsea

### Chelsea's women stay top despite another afternoon of wasted chances
Hannah Hampton's first-half saves kept the 0–0 at Liverpool level, then Chelsea dominated after the break without scoring, Khiara Keating denying Sjoeke Nusken late. Manchester City lost 1–3 at home to Tottenham on Sunday, so the two-point lead holds at 27 to 25. Lucy Bronze: "that's been the story of our season".

## Premier League

### Pochettino's United lose a man in eight minutes and the game by the 13th
Yoro was sent off for hauling down Alexander Isak, Dominik Szoboszlai curled in the free kick on 10 and Florian Wirtz made it two on 13. Rio Ngumoha (66) and Víctor Muñoz (82) finished it. Eight points from 11 games is United's worst return since 1972-73.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/22/liverpool-manchester-united-premier-league-match-report)

### Hull sucker-punch Brighton twice in stoppage time
Konstantinos Tzolakis kept Brighton out all afternoon. Once João Costinha went off for a second yellow on 89, Hull broke twice and Oli McBurnie scored both, on 90+2 and 90+5. A Brighton win would have cut Arsenal's lead to three; instead it stays six, and Hull have their first win since August.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/22/hull-brighton-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Verstappen holds on through 35 lead changes in the best race of the year
George Russell led repeatedly before locking up into the barriers and falling to ninth, and a last-lap problem for Kimi Antonelli let both McLarens through. Piastri's drive from 17th to third — his first podium since Miami in May — pulls McLaren to within nine points of Red Bull in the constructors'.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/las-vegas-lowdown-all-the-key-moments-as-verstappen-wins-remarkable-race-and-ferraris-drivers-collide-again.62D9SeuQXSboAspMm7N0pN) · [source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/piastri-believes-las-vegas-podium-was-exactly-the-kind-of-result-we-needed.6Vjx9TJwtUhgP05nqxbVkL)

### Ferrari's drivers crash into each other again and Vasseur has had enough
Leclerc tipped Hamilton into a spin with 22 laps left, took a ten-second penalty and finished tenth, three places behind him. Second race-wrecking clash in three grands prix. Vasseur: "It's a lack of respect for the team, for the sponsor, the same speech as after Mexico."
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/formula1/articles/c6j9mepj0j1go)
