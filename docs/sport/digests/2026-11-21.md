# Sat 21 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Not much happened, but plenty is about to. Xabi Alonso ruled Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix and Jorrel Hato out of Chelsea's game with Leeds tonight, with Moises Caicedo fit to start; the club was also fined £150,000 by the FA over discriminatory chanting at Charlton in January. The FIA agreed to put V8s back in Formula 1 from 2030, and the Bucks lost a 12th straight to go 1–15.

## Chelsea

### Alonso loses three more for Leeds but gets Caicedo back
Joao Pedro (thigh) and Lacroix (hamstring) still are not training with the group, and Hato's back problem will "take a while". Better news elsewhere: Caicedo is "ready to start", Josh Acheampong is available and Emiliano Martinez is "selectable". Reece James, out since September, "still needs some weeks".
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/xabi-alonso-confirms-chelsea-team-news-for-leeds)

### Tom Brady is in talks over buying a small Chelsea stake
Nothing is signed, and this is the Guardian's reporting, not the club's: Brady sold his Birmingham City share this month, which sources say was to clear the way for Stamford Bridge. He is close to Behdad Eghbali, whose Clearlake Capital holds 86.5% since Boehly and Walter sold up in September.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/20/tom-brady-chelsea-talks-stake-birmingham-city)

## Premier League

### Saka may not be back before Christmas, Arteta admits
He has not resumed even light training since limping off at Bayern Munich last month. Tests during the break ruled out surgery and pointed to another four weeks, though Arteta would not commit to the 20 December United game: "Maybe it is three and then it is five." Zubimendi also misses Newcastle.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/20/bukayo-saka-injury-may-not-play-arsenal-before-christmas-mikel-arteta)

## Formula 1

### The FIA has signed off on V8s for 2030
Unanimous agreement between the FIA, F1, the teams and the engine makers on a 2.1-litre turbocharged V8 on sustainable fuels with limited hybridisation, in a lighter car — the first V8s since 2013. It still has to pass the World Motor Sport Council in Shanghai next month, so it is a framework, not a rulebook.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-confirms-unanimous-agreement-on-framework-for-2030-power-units.AZ7EFvWPyGPH0xG1JS8Ud)

### Five drivers carry grid penalties into Las Vegas
Antonelli drops five places for a sixth engine, Stroll ten, and Bearman starts from the back having blown four allocations at once. Piastri and Sainz already had ten-place drops. Both titles are gone, so Sunday is about second — Verstappen 279, Russell 262, Hamilton 261 — and Red Bull's 17-point hold on third.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-bearman-and-stroll-set-for-grid-penalties-at-las-vegas-grand-prix.37Vixk6Mt1gvZlVXDsO8S)
