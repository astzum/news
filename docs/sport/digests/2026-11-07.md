# Sat 07 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea had no game but plenty of news — Emi Martínez is close to a return in goal at Sunderland on Sunday night, and Cole Palmer has been left out of another England squad with Thomas Tuchel barely bothering to soften it. Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football without telling his national coach first. Sunderland lost at home to Dinamo Zagreb four days before Chelsea arrive, Brighton won in Spain, and Golden State have turned 0–3 into 6–3.

## Chelsea

### Martínez is training in protective headgear and could start at Sunderland
Xabi Alonso says the call on the goalkeeper, out since jaw surgery after the Everton win, comes after Saturday's session at Cobham — he is training well and ahead of schedule. Moisés Caicedo is a doubt with calf discomfort. Jorrel Hato, Maxence Lacroix and João Pedro are all out until after the international break.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/xabi-alonso-confirms-chelsea-team-news-for-sunderland)

### Tuchel on dropping Palmer: 'he misses too many opportunities to impress'
That was the entire explanation, given after England were assured Palmer was fit for the Nations League games against Croatia and Spain. He has featured in three of Tuchel's 26 matches and last played in March. Morgan Rogers goes instead, on the back of three goals for Chelsea.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c9e8ll62rp68o) · [squad](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/chelsea-attacker-called-up-by-england)

## Europa League

### Sunderland's European unbeaten run ends four days before Chelsea visit
Luka Stojković lashed in the opener and then set up Dion Drena Beljo before the break as Dinamo Zagreb won 2–0 at the Stadium of Light. Wilson Isidor had a header ruled offside, Kevin Danso hit the bar. Sunderland are bottom of the Premier League with one win in nine.

## Internationals

### Ronaldo quit Portugal without telling the manager who was picking him
Jorge Jesus found out from the Instagram video, a day before he was due to name his Nations League squad: "Until then, I knew nothing about it. He was one of the players on my squad list." Ronaldo finishes on 146 goals in 234 caps, both men's world records.
