# Wed 28 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Harry Kane won the Ballon d'Or at the Palladium, the first English winner since Michael Owen in 2001 and the first ceremony ever held outside France. Chelsea did not play: the news is that Joao Pedro is out for weeks with a thigh injury, with Liverpool away in the cup at 07:00 Thursday. Manchester United have decided to keep Michael Carrick, so he takes the worst United side in years to Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

## Chelsea

### Joao Pedro is out for weeks and Emegha is finally in a squad
Tests confirmed the thigh injury the Brazilian picked up before Saturday's Tottenham defeat. Alonso would not put a number on it: out "for a few weeks". Chelsea lose five goals and three assists in seven games. Emmanuel Emegha, signed from Strasbourg in the summer and still without a debut, travels to Anfield.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/xabi-alonso-confirms-chelsea-team-news-for-liverpool)

## Football

### Kane ends England's 25-year wait for a Ballon d'Or
Two hours into a ceremony in his home city, his name came out of the last envelope ahead of co-favourites Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappe. The case: the European Golden Boot, a central role in Bayern's Bundesliga title, England captained to a World Cup semi-final. Alexia Putellas took a third women's award.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/26/harry-kane-ends-englands-25-year-wait-for-a-mens-ballon-dor)

### Carrick keeps his job and gets Stamford Bridge as the audition
Jason Wilcox and Omar Berrada kept him on despite two wins in twelve, a Carabao Cup exit to Brighton and 16th place, a point above the drop. Eddie Howe is Wilcox's preferred replacement and wants it; United would owe Newcastle about £3m. The reprieve looks a lot like time to line Howe up.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/27/manchester-united-eddie-howe-newcastle-compensation-michael-carrick)

## NBA

### Jalen Williams has a broken fibula and a dislocated ankle, and that is the better half of the news
Surgery, and Sam Presti would not set a timetable until after it, which for this injury usually means the season. Oklahoma City also lose Cason Wallace to surgery on a torn labrum. Both were All-Defensive picks; the champions are 4–0 and have just been gutted anyway.
[source](https://www.nba.com/news/thunders-jalen-williams-has-fractured-leg-dislocated-ankle-and-will-need-surgery)

### A 24-year-old rookie drags Golden State to their first win
Yaxel Lendeborg put up 31 points, 12 rebounds and six assists in a 134–114 rout at Denver, drawing 13 free throws; Curry went 8-of-8, seven of them in the third. The Warriors were 0–3 and seven-point underdogs. Lendeborg's 87 points in four games is a Warriors rookie record since Wilt Chamberlain.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50294832/yaxel-lendeborg-31-points-12-rebounds-warriors-beat-nuggets-first-win)

## NFL

### Seattle buy a Pro Bowl guard for a third and a fifth
Indianapolis, 2–5 and done pretending, sent Quenton Nelson to the Seahawks for a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth. He has made the Pro Bowl in all eight of his seasons, was first-team All-Pro three times and has started 136 games. Seattle are 4–3 and fixing the one broken thing.
[source](https://www.colts.com/news/colts-trade-g-quenton-nelson-to-seattle-seahawks-for-2-future-nfl-draft-picks)
