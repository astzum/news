# Sun 22 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Leeds tore Chelsea apart 4–1 at Stamford Bridge, and Chelsea lost Moises Caicedo to a back problem inside 22 minutes on the way to eighth. Arsenal needed a late Noni Madueke goal to draw at Newcastle and stay six clear, and lost David Raya to a shoulder injury. Kimi Antonelli was quickest in Las Vegas qualifying but drops five places, so Max Verstappen has pole for this afternoon's race.

## Chelsea

### Leeds take Chelsea apart at Stamford Bridge
Emmanuel Emegha scored on his full debut (25'), and it unravelled from there: Noah Okafor levelled three minutes later and got a second on 58, Dominic Calvert-Lewin converted a 53rd-minute penalty after a shot struck Morgan Rogers' arm, and Brenden Aaronson finished a counter in stoppage time. Chelsea are eighth.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-chelsea-1-4-leeds)

### Alonso puts the collapse down to losing Caicedo at 22 minutes
The captain took a knock in an aerial collision, needed lengthy treatment and went off for Jordan Henderson. Alonso would only call it "a knock" and said they would know more today: "After the change with Moi, we lost completely the momentum. They started believing, we started not believing."
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/alonso-updates-on-caicedos-game-changing-injury)

## Premier League

### Madueke salvages Arsenal's lead at the top, and Raya goes off hurt
Eberechi Eze put them ahead on 33; Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw turned it around after the break before Madueke equalised on 78. Raya was withdrawn on 70 with what the Guardian describes as a potentially significant shoulder injury. Arsenal remain top, six clear of Brighton and Manchester City.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/21/newcastle-arsenal-premier-league-match-report)

### Bournemouth make it nine wins in a row and go fourth
Evanilson's 19th-minute finish — a sixth straight Premier League game with a goal — and Justin Kluivert's effort three minutes into first-half stoppage time saw off Nottingham Forest 2–0. It is a club-record run in all competitions under Marco Rose, and Forest are out of the bottom three only on goal difference.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/21/bournemouth-nottingham-forest-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Antonelli is fastest in Las Vegas and hands Verstappen pole anyway
The new champion's 1:33.893 was 0.258s clear of everyone, then a five-place grid penalty for a power unit change dropped him to sixth. Verstappen starts first with George Russell alongside him. Piastri qualified seventh and starts 17th after his own ten-place drop; both Ferraris missed Q3.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-quickest-in-las-vegas-gp-qualifying-as-verstappen-inherits-pole-position.6R7udh8iYMHunSOGI6zStK)

## NBA

### Harden's 51 moves him past Kobe Bryant for 50-point games
Fifteen of 26 from the field, seven of 11 from three and all 14 free throws in a season-high 40 minutes, as Cleveland beat Indiana 128–119 in the NBA Cup. It was his 26th 50-point game — only Wilt Chamberlain (118) and Michael Jordan (31) have more — and his first since November 2025.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50546191/james-harden-explodes-51-points-propels-cavs-pacers)
