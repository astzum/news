# Sun 25 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea lost a derby they had the better of for an hour, undone by their stand-in goalkeeper punching a corner into his own net and then by Cole Palmer getting himself sent off. Manchester City's perfect start is gone too — Nicolas Jackson scored twice for Aston Villa — so Arsenal are level at the top. Ferrari swept every practice session in Austin and qualifying is at 8am.

## Chelsea

### Penders punches a corner into his own net and Spurs win at the Bridge
Tottenham had won at Stamford Bridge twice in 42 visits. Mike Penders, starting for the injured Emi Martínez, punched a 77th-minute corner into his own net under pressure from van Hecke. Palmer went three minutes later — second yellow, his first senior red — and Mathys Tel curled in from outside the box on 87.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-chelsea-0-2-tottenham-hotspur)

### João Pedro is the next one out, and nobody knows for how long
Alonso said the forward "felt something" in Friday's training and has fluid around the injury, with tests to follow. So Chelsea played a derby without their first-choice keeper and their centre-forward, and Geovany Quenda made his first league start instead. They stay fourth on 13.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/alonso-explains-joao-pedro-absence-and-penders-and-quenda-selections)

## Premier League

### Jackson scores twice and Manchester City's seven-win start is over
A thumping finish on six minutes, then a low shot that squirmed under Gianluigi Donnarumma on 60. Erling Haaland had headed City level in between. One clean sheet in six games is the real problem: City keep top spot on goal difference alone, and Enzo Maresca called both goals avoidable.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c6kg7z700k7jo)

### Arsenal draw level at the top without ever getting out of second gear
Ezri Konsa rammed in from a third successive corner on seven — Arsenal's first set-piece goal of the season — and Gabriel headed the second on 68 after a VAR check. Arteta made five changes off the back of Munich and still got a second straight clean sheet. Five conceded in eight.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/24/arsenal-everton-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Ferrari sweep all three practices in Austin, Hamilton fastest of the lot
His 1:34.893 was 0.168s clear of Verstappen, with Leclerc third after losing a quicker lap to track limits — a theme all weekend, 128 laps deleted in FP2 alone. Antonelli and Russell were fourth and fifth. Qualifying is at 08:00 Sydney; the title can be settled in Monday's race.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fp3-hamilton-goes-fastest-in-final-practice-session-for-the-united-states-gp-ahead-of-verstappen-and-leclerc.1KQ5x8wUAtBETnHxKDNldC)

## NBA

### Golden State give up a 17-0 run in four minutes and lose to a rookie's floater
It is the second-longest fourth-quarter run against a Steve Kerr team, behind Houston's 21-0 in 2018. Cameron Boozer, the No. 3 pick, hit a nine-foot floater with seconds left to win it in his second NBA game, finishing with 25 points and 13 rebounds.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50263923/warriors-implode-grizzlies-17-0-run-caps-stunning-comeback)
