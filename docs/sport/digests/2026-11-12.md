# Thu 12 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Nothing in football that counts and no F1 until Qatar, so the morning belongs to Old Trafford: Mauricio Pochettino is ready to replace Michael Carrick, who is expected to go inside 48 hours. Chelsea are idle until Leeds on the 22nd, with a dozen players scattered across international duty. The NBA gave us Paul George putting 36 on his old club and Utah losing Lauri Markkanen for a month.

## Chelsea

### Emiliano Martínez is in line to play again on Argentina duty
The club says the keeper — on the bench against Sunderland after surgery on a fractured jaw — could get back on the pitch during this window. Aaron Anselmino, 21, has a first senior Argentina call-up, and Geovany Quenda is in line for a Portugal debut in their first post-Ronaldo squad.

## Premier League

### Pochettino will take the United job the moment it is offered
BBC Sport's sources say the USA manager is ready, with Carrick expected to be relieved of his duties inside 48 hours and $10m (£7.5m) owed to release Pochettino from the contract he signed in August. US Soccer already has a succession plan. United are 18th, one win in ten.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cqvg25pj36mvo)

## NBA

### Paul George turns the Tatum–Brown reunion into his own homecoming
The man Philadelphia traded for Jaylen Brown went 14-of-22 with six threes for 36 points on his return there, and Boston won the meeting of the East's top two by 22. Jayson Tatum had 21 and a poster dunk on LeBron James; Brown, in early foul trouble, managed 13.
[source](https://www.nba.com/news/starting-5-november-11-2026)

### Markkanen's fractured hand leaves Utah with one fit starter
ESPN's Tim MacMahon reports a non-displaced fracture in the right hand, suffered against Dallas on Friday and missed by the first X-ray — at least a month out. Jusuf Nurkić hasn't played all season and Jaren Jackson Jr is six to eight weeks away with a torn MCL. The Jazz are 1-10.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50454130/sources-jazz-star-lauri-markkanen-least-1-month)

## NFL

### Cincinnati walked away from an agreed trade over a compensatory pick
Zac Taylor confirmed the Bengals pulled out of sending a 2027 sixth-rounder to Philadelphia for edge rusher Arnold Ebiketie once they realised completing it would forfeit a 2027 third-round compensatory pick. "It was on our end," he said. Cincinnati are 6-2, his best start in eight years, and did nothing else at the deadline.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50454351/bengals-zac-taylor-apologizes-failed-trade-eagles)
