**Overnight:** Chelsea: no news; Nottingham Forest away, 07:00 Saturday. ## Premier League

### Tarkowski's first red in 351 games ruins Moyes' record night
Seventy-four yellows and never a sending off, until the Everton captain followed through on Yehor Yarmoliuk on 40 minutes and Craig Pawson upgraded it on VAR's advice. Keane Lewis-Potter had already scored twice; Vitaly Janelt (67) and Fábio Carvalho (90) finished the 4–0. Tarkowski misses the Merseyside derby.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cmqx5eynvpqno)

### The FA rescinds Yoro's red card, and the head of referees says it was right
Leny Yoro went after eight minutes at Anfield for bringing down Alexander Isak. Mauricio Pochettino said it was "never a foul", a three-man FA commission agreed, and Yoro can face Brentford on Saturday. Howard Webb, on Sky, called the sending-off correct and "certainly not clearly wrong".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/ck868g93djl5o)
