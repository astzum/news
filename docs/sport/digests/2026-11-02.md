# Mon 02 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's men were idle — Sunderland away next Sunday, no news worth the name — but the women won 6–0 at Charlton to go back top of the WSL. Arsenal won at Anfield for the first time in fourteen years and lead by three. And Lewis Hamilton has his first Ferrari pole; the Mexico City Grand Prix is starting as you read this, with Kimi Antonelli needing ninth to be champion.

## Chelsea

### Chelsea Women retake the WSL lead with a 19-minute burst at Charlton
Nathalie Björn headed in after three minutes on her first league start in a year, Melvine Malard scored either side of Ellie Carpenter, and it was 4–0 inside 19 minutes. Aggie Beever-Jones and Giulia Dragoni finished it. Lauren James pulled up with a hamstring problem before half-time and will be assessed.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-charlton-athletic-0-6-chelsea-women)

## Premier League

### Arsenal's first league win at Anfield since 2012 puts them three clear
Alisson passed short to Jérémy Jacquet on 16, Ødegaard intercepted, and Declan Rice's low cross left Kai Havertz a tap-in — his first goal in ten appearances. Liverpool had 17 shots and not one on target, and a five-match winning run gone. Van Dijk was booked for sarcastically applauding the referee.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/01/liverpool-arsenal-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Hamilton takes his first Ferrari pole, three years after his last one
A 1:16.514 at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, nine thousandths clear of George Russell, with Leclerc third. It is Hamilton's 105th pole and his first since the 2023 Hungarian Grand Prix. Antonelli starts fourth and needs ninth or better to take the title with four races to spare. Piastri went out in Q2, 12th.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/hamilton-clinches-first-pole-with-ferrari-in-qualifying-at-the-mexico-city-gp.4GffzFhlnOxOjbHHMc0AJI)

### Lawson loses eighth on the grid to an ERS rule he broke after the line
The FIA found his MGU-K power was cut faster than permitted just after he crossed the line on his quickest Q2 lap. Racing Bulls reported it themselves; the stewards said a breach stands whether or not it gained anything. New engine parts on top of that send him off from the pit lane.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/lawson-disqualified-from-mexico-city-gp-qualifying-for-technical-breach.1LIHCl2P75cYj0cGJgyZhx)

## NBA

### Oklahoma City's thin frontcourt gets taken apart in Houston
Jabari Smith Jr scored 30 and Marcus Smart 24; an 18–4 run in the second quarter made it 55–34 and the Rockets never led by fewer than 12 after half-time. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's 26 was nowhere near enough. The champions, missing Chet Holmgren and Jalen Williams, have lost two straight after starting 4–0.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909902)

### Memphis are the last unbeaten team in the league at 6–0
Cameron Boozer had 27 points and nine rebounds and GG Jackson hit all five of his threes in a 21-point first half as Sacramento were put away 136–105. That equals the best start in Grizzlies history, 2014-15 — and they are doing it with Ja Morant out for at least four weeks.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909899)
