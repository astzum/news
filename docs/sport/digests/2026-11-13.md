# Fri 13 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Two Premier League managers went in a day — Michael Carrick at Manchester United, with Mauricio Pochettino all but signed, and Pierre Sage at bottom club Crystal Palace, with Thomas Frank in advanced talks. Chelsea have no game until Leeds on the 22nd and no fresh news beyond Jorrel Hato's withdrawal from the Netherlands squad. Harry Kane started at Wembley on a record 126th England cap; that game and Italy v France were still going at 7am.

## Chelsea

### Hato skips the Netherlands squad and Alonso wants him back for Leeds
The defender has now missed Chelsea's last three games and is sitting this window out entirely, though the club says Xabi Alonso expects him back by the end of the break. Romeo Lavia was suspended for Belgium's win in Turkey and returns for Italy on Sunday; Malo Gusto's France lead that group.

## Premier League

### United sack Carrick and Pochettino starts work next Thursday
One win in eleven and a place in the relegation zone finished him fifteen league games into a two-year deal. Pochettino takes the USA through two Concacaf quarter-final legs against Haiti first, so his first day at Carrington is expected to be Thursday the 19th, three days before Liverpool away.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/12/michael-carrick-sacked-manchester-united-mauricio-pochettino)

### Palace bin Sage after five months with the worst defence in the league
Two wins from ten, four straight league defeats, 24 conceded and bottom of the table; three of his coaching staff went with him. Thomas Frank is in advanced talks. Sage inherited a side that had won the FA Cup and a European trophy under Oliver Glasner and took it backwards fast.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cv5y18lkk87do)

## Formula 1

### Racing Bulls keep Lawson and Lindblad for 2027
No change at the junior team, confirmed by the team itself: Lawson has 89 points to the rookie Lindblad's 38, and four straight double-points finishes have them fifth on 114, 45 clear of Alpine and chasing the best constructors' finish in the outfit's history. Las Vegas is next, on the 22nd.
[source](https://www.visacashapprb.com/int-en/2027-driver-line-up-announcement)

## NBA

### Curry tears an oblique and the Warriors' eight-game run dies with it
He grabbed his right side early in the first quarter at Houston, nailed one 28-footer through it, then went for an MRI. Out at least two weeks and seven games. Kerr: "Concern is high." With Butler, Moody and Porzingis already gone, 8-4 looks very fragile.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50462899/warriors-stephen-curry-least-2-weeks-obli)

### Lakers make it seven straight but limp out of Milwaukee
Luka Dončić's 32 and an 11-2 record — best in the league — came at a cost. Austin Reaves landed badly chasing a fast-break dunk and did not return; X-rays on the heel were negative, Achilles ruled out, imaging Thursday. Dončić turned an ankle late and says he is fine.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50459249/reaves-exits-heel-injury-banged-lakers-win)
