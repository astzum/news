# Fri 30 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea are out of the Carabao Cup, beaten 6–5 on penalties at Anfield after Wesley Fofana blazed the last one into the Kop — a fifth shootout defeat to Liverpool. Arsenal went out too, and far more embarrassingly: League Two Fleetwood Town beat them 5–3 on spot-kicks. Manchester United come to Stamford Bridge at 23:30 Saturday.

## Chelsea

### Fifth shootout against Liverpool, fifth loss
Morgan Rogers and Cody Gakpo traded penalties inside four minutes, Mike Penders saved from Gakpo and Barcola, and Marco Palestra was denied the winner. Then sudden death: Barco hit the post, Mamardashvili saved from Emegha, and Joe Gomez — who has never scored for Liverpool — buried his before Fofana ballooned the last.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-liverpool-1-1-chelsea-6-5-on-pens)

### Lacroix's hamstring goes and the defence thins again
He felt it chasing Gakpo just after Liverpool's equaliser and gave way to Levi Colwill at half-time. Alonso wouldn't guess: "we need a couple of days to check what the injury's going to be." Caicedo and Gusto also came off. Joao Pedro is still out for weeks.
[source](https://www.dazn.com/en-GB/news/football/liverpool-chelsea-carabao-cup-reaction-xabi-alonso-maxence-lacroix-injury/1anqw9i4ydyux10hbr7esg5d9t)

## Carabao Cup

### Fourth-tier Fleetwood knock Arsenal out at Highbury
Aaron Loupalo-Bi, on loan from Fulham, spun 17-year-old Marli Salmon and scored on 21; Gyokeres equalised on 50. Max Dowman went off on 85 for reacting to Harrison Neal. Fleetwood scored all five penalties, Jay Lynch saved from Bruno Guimaraes, Conor Haughey finished it. They host Everton next.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/27/fleetwood-town-arsenal-carabao-cup-fourth-round-match-report)

### Everton drop Grealish and win anyway
He is understood to have missed Monday's training without permission; Moyes and chief executive Angus Kinnear left him out. James Garner, Hayden Hackney and Thierno Barry all scored off Newcastle errors, and Pickford was superb. Newcastle had 28 shots and Matthias Jaissle called the defeat "shocking".
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/29/everton-newcastle-carabao-cup-fourth-round-match-report)

## NBA

### Dallas hand the champions their first loss
Cooper Flagg put up 26 points and eight assists and P.J. Washington added 18 in a 120–96 win. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander shot 7-of-19 for 21. Oklahoma City, gutted by injuries since Jalen Williams' broken leg, drop to 4–1; the Mavericks are 3–2.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909891)

### Boozer's first triple-double keeps Memphis unbeaten
Eighteen points, 12 rebounds and 12 assists from the rookie in a 124–103 win at Portland, Cedric Coward adding 17. The Grizzlies are 5–0, as are Houston, who beat Milwaukee by 32. Deni Avdija had 24 for a Portland side that arrived 3–0.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909895)
