# Thu 22 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea: no game, no news, Tottenham at the Bridge on Sunday morning. The Champions League did the heavy lifting — Como beat Manchester United 4–1 and Michael Carrick is now winless in seven, Liverpool came from two down to win 4–3 at Anfield, and Phil Foden helped himself to a hat-trick. Arsenal were level at Bayern as this went out, but Bukayo Saka limped off after ten minutes. LeBron James's 76ers ruined the Knicks' banner night.

## Champions League

### Como's greatest night leaves Carrick winless in seven
Nico Paz scored inside two minutes and again on 57, teed up Anastasios Douvikas for the second and watched Jacobo Ramón head the fourth. Joshua Zirkzee's 90+1 consolation was all United managed. Three points from three league-phase games, and their worst run in all competitions since 2015.
[source](https://www.skysports.com/football/como-vs-manchester-united/5463087564982780232)

### Liverpool needed seven goals to stay perfect
Two down inside half an hour at Anfield — Georges Mikautadze punishing a loose Virgil van Dijk header, then Sergi Cardona — before Alexis Mac Allister's header and Ronald Araújo's at 45+2 squared it. Mac Allister's second and Florian Wirtz made it four; Tani Oluwaseyi's 87th-minute volley came too late. Nine points from nine.
[source](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/liverpool-villarreal-champions-league-match-report)

### Foden's hat-trick covers for Haaland's sore foot
Elliot Anderson turned in a rebounded free-kick on 12, Foden made it two on 34, and after Stavros Pilios replied on 52, Rayan Cherki and Foden twice more saw AEK Athens off 5–1. Haaland sat out with the foot he hurt against Ipswich. City's second league-phase win, three games in.
[source](https://gulfnews.com/sport/football/foden-hat-trick-fuels-man-city-rout-of-aek-athens-1.500717714)

### Saka limps off in Munich, Arsenal level at the restart
Bukayo Saka went off after ten minutes holding his left thigh; Eberechi Eze replaced him and scored on 25. Alphonso Davies levelled ten minutes later and it was still 1–1 just after half-time at 07:00 Sydney. Arsenal haven't put a figure on Saka's injury. Aston Villa led Viking 2–0.
[source](https://www.skysports.com/football/bayern-munich-vs-arsenal/live/577643)

## NBA

New York raised their first title banner in 53 years, then lost 106–100 to a Philadelphia side rebuilt around James and Jaylen Brown. Tyrese Maxey's 28 points led it; James had 14 points, nine rebounds and seven assists in his record 24th season. Jalen Brunson shot 6-of-19.
