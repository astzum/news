# Sun 01 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea are back on track — 2–0 at home to ten-man Manchester United, Morgan Rogers with the pick of the goals and Cole Palmer the penalty. Manchester City lost at home to Brighton and are only top on goal difference until Arsenal play Liverpool. Qualifying in Mexico City starts in an hour.

## Chelsea

### Rogers and Palmer end a bad week at United's expense
Rogers picked the ball up in midfield on 50, breezed past Harry Maguire and Lisandro Martínez and clipped it over Senne Lammens. Thirteen minutes later Martínez elbowed Wesley Fofana at a corner: VAR review, second yellow, penalty, Palmer. Three goals were ruled out for offside. Chelsea go fourth on 16.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/match-report-chelsea-2-0-manchester-united)

## Premier League

### Brighton win at Manchester City at the eighteenth attempt
Yasin Ayari tapped into an empty net on 11 after Matheus Nunes missed his clearance and Maxim De Cuyper returned it. City had the rest of the game and no finish — their first blank at home in the league in 23 matches, and a second straight defeat. Brighton are third on 19.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/31/manchester-city-brighton-premier-league-match-report)

### Mateta's 13th-minute red leaves Pierre Sage hanging on
His studs caught Micky van de Ven's heel; Michael Oliver gave yellow, then upgraded it at the monitor. Archie Gray headed in Kudus's corner on 25, Jan Paul van Hecke a second on 73: Spurs' first three-win run in 616 days. Palace sit in the bottom three and Sage may not survive.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/31/tottenham-crystal-palace-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Verstappen and Lawson each lose a grid place for a practice scrap
They tangled at Turn 12 in FP2 — Verstappen on a prep lap, Lawson cooling down — wrecking a Red Bull floor and a Racing Bulls front wing. The stewards called it unacceptable given a warning issued that morning; both admitted it was "clumsy". One-place drops apply to tonight's grid.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-and-lawson-get-grid-penalties-after-clumsy-mexico-city-practice-clash.2m2ey8Pl7g5IfQfGeepilD)

## NBA

### Oklahoma City lose Chet Holmgren for eight to ten weeks
He had biological treatment on Friday for a small patellar tendon tear. That is a third frontline injury in a week for the champions — Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace both needed surgery after Sunday's win over the Clippers — and they have pulled out of signing Ochai Agbaji to rethink the roster spot.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50336717/sources-thunder-no-longer-signing-ochai-agbaji)

### Irving and Flagg hand Houston their first loss
Kyrie Irving scored 26 of his 35 after half-time, including the jumper that put Dallas ahead for good at 102–100, and Cooper Flagg added 33 off 13-of-19 shooting. Kevin Durant and Amen Thompson both missed late free throws. Memphis, idle at 5–0, are now the only unbeaten team left.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909283)
