# Sat 14 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's Morgan Rogers scored England's opener at Wembley, Harry Kane's penalty won it 2–1 against Croatia on his record 126th cap, and Rogers then called the idea that Cole Palmer doesn't want to play for England "a lie"; Chelsea's women host WSL leaders Manchester City tonight without Lauren James or Naomi Girma. Manchester United's Pochettino hire is official — he cried through his USA farewell, and it cost United about $10m — while Bruno Fernandes has rejected a second contract offer from them. Italy beat France 3–1 in Milan to hand Zinedine Zidane his first defeat as France coach, and Cade Cunningham scored a career-high 52 in a double-overtime win at Washington.

## Chelsea

### Rogers scores England's opener, then goes in to bat for Palmer
Josko Gvardiol tangled his feet under Jude Bellingham's volleyed cross and Rogers slashed it in from close range on 24. Afterwards, asked about the idea that his childhood friend Cole Palmer doesn't want to play for England, Rogers called it "a lie" and "nonsense" — Palmer, dropped again, has played three of Tuchel's 27 games.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c6e9kjne5y8vo)

### Bompastor rules James and Girma out of the Manchester City decider
Lauren James and Naomi Girma are both "doing good" but won't play in this block, so Chelsea host the WSL leaders without either. City are two points clear after Chelsea's draw with Everton; both sides are unbeaten, and a home win at the Bridge retakes top spot.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/bompastor-updates-on-james-and-girma-availability-ahead-of-man-city)

## Premier League

### Pochettino's move to United is official and cost about $10m
US Soccer confirmed it on Friday; Pochettino cried through the press conference, having signed a deal in August to stay through 2030. He says United's first contact came only last Sunday. He takes the USA through two Concacaf quarter-finals against Haiti first, then starts at Anfield on 22 November.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/13/mauricio-pochettino-usmnt-manchester-united)

### Fernandes has turned down a second United contract offer
BBC Sport's Simon Stone reports the captain, 32 and out of contract at the end of the season, has rejected United's second proposal. The club can trigger a 12-month extension to 2028 and almost certainly will before 1 January, because after that he could sign a pre-contract abroad.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cm782d4pz9jyo)

## Internationals

### Italy come from behind to hand Zidane his first defeat
Gianluigi Donnarumma passed to Michael Olise, who scored on 10. Pio Esposito levelled on 26, Davide Frattesi drilled in on 63 and Esposito added a penalty on 75 — brother Sebastiano having blazed one wide on 60. Italy's first home win over France since 1962 puts them top of Group A1 on head-to-head.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/12/nations-league-italy-france-spain-czechia)

## NBA

### Cunningham's 52 sees off a Washington side that refused to lose
Trae Young's buzzer three forced overtime, rookie AJ Dybantsa's midrange jumper forced a second, and the Wizards led 128–121 early in the first. Cunningham — 16 of 25, 17 of 18 from the line, 11 assists, no turnovers — dragged Detroit through 144–140. Ausar Thompson got a first triple-double.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401909977)
