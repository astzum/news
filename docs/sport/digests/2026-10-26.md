# Mon 26 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Michael Carrick is probably finished at Manchester United — Bournemouth won 3–0 at Old Trafford with Bruno Fernandes dropped to the bench, and United sit 16th with one win in eight. Liverpool beat Brighton to go fourth, which nudges Chelsea down to fifth; they have no game until Anfield on Thursday morning. Kimi Antonelli starts the United States Grand Prix from pole at 7am, with the title within reach.

## Chelsea

### Alonso keeps Penders in goal at Anfield after the own goal that beat Chelsea
The 21-year-old Belgian keeps the gloves for Wednesday's League Cup fourth round. "It's part of the business; you need to deal with mistakes and we keep confidence in Mike," Alonso said. Penders had denied Marmoush and Tel in the first half before punching into his own net.

## Premier League

### Carrick's job is hanging by a thread after Bournemouth stroll Old Trafford
Rayan on 38, Marcus Tavernier on 43, Evanilson from a daft angle on 71, boos at the whistle. United have won one of eight league games, have not won in the top flight since 30 August and are 14 points off the top. Carrick dropped Fernandes after the captain publicly questioned his tactics.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/crgj9wnpd1lvo)

### Isak scores in a third straight league game and Liverpool finally win at home
Florian Wirtz's deflected shot on 17 went down as a Pascal Gross own goal, then Isak side-footed in Bradley Barcola's cross on 21 — his sixth in six league appearances. First home league win of Liverpool's season, and the end of Brighton's four-match winning run. Up to fourth on 15.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/25/liverpool-brighton-premier-league-match-report)

## Formula 1

### Antonelli takes his seventh pole of the year and Ferrari's practice form evaporates
A 1:34.035 on his first Q3 run, 0.179s clear of Verstappen, and nobody got close after — both Red Bulls had their final laps deleted for track limits. Ferrari had swept all three practices and qualified fourth and fifth. Russell sixth, Piastri seventh, Norris a dismal eighth.

## NBA

### Jokic erases a 23-point hole and tells booing Denver fans not to come back
The Nuggets trailed 53–30 in the second quarter of their home opener and won 141–135 in overtime, Jokic with 35 points, 15 rebounds and 10 assists — his 199th triple-double, ten behind Westbrook. Jamal Murray scored 42. "Those fans who booed us, they don't need to come anymore," Jokic said.
