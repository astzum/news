# Mon 16 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** No Premier League until Saturday, and Chelsea's stake in the night was Pedro Neto on the pitch as Portugal were taken apart 4–1 by Denmark in Braga. ## Chelsea

### Neto was on for the worst 45 minutes of Portugal's post-Ronaldo era
Neto started and came off with just under half an hour left as Portugal were beaten 4–1 at home by Denmark, their first defeat of the campaign. Geovany Quenda was an unused substitute; the loanee Filip Jørgensen sat on Denmark's bench. Portugal still top League A Group 4 by three points.

## Internationals

## NFL

### Maye's ankle goes on the first drive as Detroit win 35–10 in Munich
Drake Maye hurt his ankle on New England's opening drive and didn't return; Mike Vrabel said the severity would wait for tests back in the States. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 153 and two scores and caught seven for 99, and Amon-Ra St Brown got two more. Detroit, 7–2, also lost Aidan Hutchinson.

## NBA

### Giannis' 71 points in two nights have Miami above .500
Antetokounmpo had 34 points, 14 rebounds and eight assists against Detroit two nights after 37 against Cleveland. Miami blocked 14, one off the franchise record, with nine players getting at least one. They started 0–4 and are now 7–6, winners of seven of nine; Cade Cunningham's 33 counted for nothing.
