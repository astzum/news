# Fri 20 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's women beat Paris Saint-Germain 1–0 at Plough Lane with an 87th-minute winner; the men have no news, with Leeds at the Bridge on Sunday morning. Premier League clubs voted to cover their own directors' legal costs if Manchester City's sponsors sue them. And the Utah Jazz, who started the season 1–13, ended the Lakers' nine-game winning streak.

## Chelsea

### Buurman's late corner turns a stodgy night into three Champions League points
She turned and finished from close range on 87 minutes at Plough Lane, a 1–0 win over Paris Saint-Germain and a fifth clean sheet of the league phase. Second straight player-of-the-match award for the 20-year-old. Eleven points from five games keeps Chelsea in the top four, which skips the knockout play-off round.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/buurman-praises-chelseas-determination-after-late-win)

## Premier League

### Clubs vote to indemnify their own board against Manchester City's sponsors
Eighteen clubs backed extending legal cover to chief executive Richard Masters, chair Alison Brittan and three non-executives; City and Nottingham Forest voted against. Etihad and Etisalat are weighing action over September's guilty verdict on financial-rules charges, so City will help fund the defence against their own sponsors. Collective liability could run to hundreds of millions.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cqy4jvdw7jdlo)

### Mudryk blames the Ukraine camp for the meldonium test that cost him 20 months
Chelsea's £89m signing, now on loan at Tottenham, told the YouTube channel Guest of Gordon that meldonium "is a drug you can only get in Eastern Europe" and could only have entered his system on international duty. He served from November 2024 to July 2026 after CAS cut the FA's four-year ban.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cr930l71z5ejo)

## NBA

### The 3–13 Jazz end the Lakers' nine-game run
Keyonte George scored nine of his 30 in the fourth as Utah pulled away for 109–97 in Los Angeles, a night after they beat the Clippers to stop a 12-game losing streak. Luka Dončić had 29 but nine turnovers and 2-of-12 from three; the Lakers shot a season-low 38.2%.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401910017)

### Dort and Wiggins come back to Oklahoma City and win
Traded out in the summer salary clear-out, the pair got video tributes and then 30 points between them in Atlanta's 117–112 win. Onyeka Okongwu had 28 and Jalen Johnson a triple-double. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's 34 wasn't enough for a Thunder side now 9–6, beaten in four of five and missing three starters.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401910014)

## Formula 1

### Cadillac keep Bottas for 2027 rather than hand Herta a seat
The Finn is confirmed alongside Sergio Pérez, whose deal runs to at least 2029. Cadillac have scored no points in a debut season with 14 DNFs, nine of them Bottas', and Pérez leads him 11–9 in qualifying and 6–1 in races. They chose experience over promoting their American reserve.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/analysis-why-cadillac-have-stuck-with-bottas-for-2027.7vPHYNkm1aup0szRFaVYyd)
