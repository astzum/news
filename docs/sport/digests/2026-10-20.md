# Tue 20 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Tottenham beat Coventry 3–1 to get their first win of the season and climb off the bottom. In the States it was all bad news: Philadelphia confirmed blood clots have reached Jalen Hurts's lungs, and Ben Simmons tore an achilles at Kings practice three days before his comeback season started. No F1 since Singapore — Austin is Sunday.

## Premier League

### Maddison, 536 days out, hands Spurs their first win
Not convincing, but theirs. Maddison bent one in from 25 yards on his first start since May 2025, Jack Rudoni levelled for Coventry just after half-time, and Omar Marmoush and substitute Ben Davies settled it. Dejan Kulusevski also came on, his first appearance since a knee injury 17 months ago.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/19/tottenham-coventry-premier-league-match-report)

### Manchester City make it seven from seven
Erling Haaland's double saw off Ipswich 3–2, though Daizen Maeda and a 90th-minute Zian Flemming penalty made it uncomfortable. Enzo Maresca's side lead on 21 points, three clear of Arsenal, who won 1–0 at Nottingham Forest through Noni Madueke. The cost: Haaland and Antoine Semenyo limped off, with Kovacic, Nunes and O'Reilly also hurt.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/17/manchester-city-ipswich-premier-league-match-report)

### Brighton are third, and Diego Gómez scored from 51 metres
Three goals in ten minutes buried Crystal Palace 5–0 — Jaydee Canvot's own goal, then Gómez's lob from just inside the Palace half, the longest-range Premier League goal since Rooney in 2017. A fourth straight win, Fabian Hürzeler's first over Palace at the fifth go, and a division-best +18.
[source](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401879261)

## NBA

### Ben Simmons tears an achilles three days before his opener
The Kings announced it Sunday: a torn right achilles in a live practice drill, surgery to follow. Simmons, 30, had signed a one-year, $3.5m deal on 4 September and had a good camp. ESPN calls it likely season-ending, which would wipe out the Melbourne man's comeback before it started.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50214131/kings-simmons-believed-achilles-tendon-tear)

## NFL

### Jalen Hurts's season is almost certainly over
The Eagles and agent Nicole Lynn said on Monday that the superficial calf clot diagnosed in London is now deep vein thrombosis plus clots in both lungs. He is stable, on blood thinners and barred from contact; Ian Rapoport and Adam Schefter both report a three-month recovery. Philadelphia are 2–4.
[source](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/eagles-and-jalen-hurts-agent-nicole-lynn-provide-an-update-on-qb-jalen-hurts)
