# Sun 08 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's women drew 1–1 with Everton at the Bridge and lost top spot in the WSL to Manchester City, with Lucy Bronze contriving to make both goals. The men were idle — Moisés Caicedo still hasn't trained fully, and Sunderland away is Monday 1am. The big one was at the City Ground, where Liam Delap scored twice to beat Manchester City; Arsenal ground out a 1–0 and are six clear. And four of the top five in the F1 championship are taking grid penalties in Brazil.

## Chelsea

### Bronze made Chelsea's goal, then made Everton's
Katie McCabe's cross, Bronze's cushioned header, Sandy Baltimore nodding it in on 28 minutes. Four minutes later a Yuka Momiki cross looped off Bronze and in. Everton managed no shots on target and still left with a point, so Chelsea drop two behind Manchester City, who visit the Bridge on Saturday.

## Premier League

### Delap scores twice and Manchester City lose a third league game in a row
Jair Cunha nicked it off Josko Gvardiol after six minutes, Forest's first home goal in 510 minutes. Delap then ran straight through Rúben Dias and Marc Guéhi twice, on 18 and 73; Rayan Cherki's half-volley on 52 was City's lot. Forest are out of the bottom three.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/07/nottingham-forest-manchester-city-premier-league-match-report)

### Ødegaard's scruffy tap-in takes Arsenal six clear into the break
Hull sat in a 5–3–2 and had Arsenal pawing at it for 72 minutes until Konstantinos Tzolakis spilled a Noni Madueke shot and the captain pounced — his fifth of the season. Arsenal have matched their own record of 27 points from ten games. Manchester City visit the Emirates next.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/07/arsenal-hull-premier-league-match-report)

### Tottenham are reporting the referee over the tackle that ended Mathys Tel's afternoon
Jayden Bogle caught Tel on the achilles before half-time; a four-minute VAR check graded it reckless rather than serious foul play, and Mike Dean called it "100 per cent a red". Substitute Noah Okafor rifled in Harry Wilson's lay-off on 79 for Leeds' first win in six.
[source](https://www.skysports.com/football/leeds-united-vs-tottenham-hotspur/report/559540) · [Spurs to complain](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c39wz9llg255o)

## Formula 1

### Hamilton and Verstappen start the São Paulo race from the back
Hamilton reported vibrations in final practice, then lost power with smoke in the cockpit, so Ferrari replaced the whole power unit and his ten-place drop became a back-row start. Verstappen took a fresh engine too; Leclerc drops ten, Russell five. Those four sit second to fifth, inside 16 points of each other.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/hamilton-set-to-start-at-back-of-the-grid-after-further-penalty-ahead-of-sao-paulo-grand-prix.5SFs44M6cNSDvUBCWE9FWZ) · [FP3](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fp3-verstappen-fastest-from-antonelli-and-norris-ahead-of-sao-paulo-grand-prix.43ghT9dACzjrhxyjTBgKct)
