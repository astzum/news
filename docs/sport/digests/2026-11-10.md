# Tue 10 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** No football — the international break has started, and Chelsea are fifth with Leeds at home on the 22nd. The club's actual news is that João Pedro's quad injury got out through Fantasy Premier League team selections, and staff have been emailed about it. Elsewhere: Oscar Piastri has split from Mark Webber's management, Manchester United still haven't decided on Michael Carrick, and Austin Reaves put up 43 for a Lakers side that is 9-2.

## Chelsea

### Chelsea email staff after Fantasy Premier League gave João Pedro's injury away
Pedro's absence from the Tottenham defeat was never explained by the club; BBC Sport reports it was a quad problem, and that FPL creators spotted Morgan Rogers and several staff quietly removing him from their teams before kick-off. Alonso, asked about it, said he is "not into the fantasy team thing".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c98rzr51rlvpo)

### Martínez flies to Argentina in the mask, and Anselmino gets a first senior call
Emi Martínez is in Scaloni's squad for Uruguay and Costa Rica despite needing another week or so after jaw surgery. Aaron Anselmino is called up for the first time, with Valentín Barco. Josh Acheampong, dropped at Sunderland with a knock, is fit enough for England Under-21s; Geovany Quenda may debut for Portugal.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/chelsea-international-call-ups-and-schedules-for-november-2026)

## Premier League

### United's board still hasn't decided on Carrick, and will use the break to think
Simon Stone reports the hierarchy wanted the two-week gap to reassess calmly after Sunday's 4-4 draw left United 18th and their worst start in 40 years. Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann were considered last month. Carrick says he won't resign; Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox decide.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cw4g1g7z2mdgo)

## Formula 1

### Piastri parts ways with the Webbers after seven years
A joint statement the morning after Interlagos confirmed Oscar Piastri and Mark and Ann Webber have "mutually agreed to terminate their management agreement", with the Webbers having already scaled back trackside this season. They signed him in 2019 and placed him at McLaren; he moves to a new structure with two races left.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/piastri-and-management-team-mutually-terminate-agreement.a59N6pYIBGSiFmwMY1Xkk)

## NBA

### Reaves scores 43 with Dončić resting and the Lakers win anyway in Detroit
Luka Dončić had averaged 45 points across four straight wins, so this was a test: Reaves went 14 of 27, 4 of 6 from three and 11 of 11 from the line in the 121-116 win, with one turnover in 37 minutes. Walker Kessler had 16 rebounds. Los Angeles are 9-2.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909946)

## NFL

### Jacksonville go all in before Wednesday morning's deadline
The Jaguars traded for safety Jessie Bates III and defensive tackle Davon Godchaux inside two days, and Detroit took Andre Cisco off the Jets to patch a battered secondary. Earlier moves: J.J. McCarthy to the Giants, Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore, Joey Porter Jr. to Dallas.
