# Tue 27 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Kimi Antonelli won the United States Grand Prix from pole by thirteen seconds and still is not champion — he needed Russell ninth or lower and Hamilton outside the top four, and got eighth and fourth, so Mexico City on Sunday night will almost certainly do it. Chelsea had no game: the one piece of news is that Cole Palmer's ban falls on Wednesday's cup tie at Anfield, not on Saturday's league game. Manchester United still had not decided on Michael Carrick by the time Carrington emptied.

## Chelsea

### Palmer's ban lands on the cup tie, so he is free to face Manchester United
The Premier League's own suspension list spells out the quirk: red cards carry across the domestic competitions, so the one match for Saturday's second yellow is served at Anfield on Wednesday rather than in the league. He misses the Carabao Cup fourth round and plays United at the Bridge on Saturday.

## Formula 1

### Antonelli wins Austin by a street and still has to wait for the title
Pole to flag, one stop while nearly everyone else made two — mediums then hards — and 13.078s clear of Leclerc. Ninth win in eighteen races. He is 123 points up with five to go, and only has to avoid losing 23 to Russell or 24 to Hamilton in Mexico.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/formula1/articles/cm20w8154wn0o)

### Leclerc takes second back off Verstappen for Ferrari's second podium in a row
They were wheel to wheel on the first lap, then split by strategy, with a late move putting Leclerc back ahead. Verstappen nursed a Red Bull full of niggles to third, a second clear of Hamilton, and grumbled about it on the radio all afternoon. Norris's ageing tyres cost him a podium: fifth.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-surges-to-dominant-victory-in-united-states-grand-prix-ahead-of-leclerc-and-verstappen.6kmBQv9FNTW7LzkDj8S34j)

## NBA

### Jalen Williams is stretchered off in Oklahoma City and nobody will say how bad it is
The All-Star's lower left leg collapsed under him as he was fouled with 1:14 left of a 108–101 win over the Clippers, his foot bending out sideways. No diagnosis yet; Gilgeous-Alexander was in tears talking about it. The Thunder are 3–0 and play Phoenix this morning regardless.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50283862/thunder-g-jalen-williams-taken-significant-injury)

### Doncic's 52 is the first 50-point game of the season and a piece of history
Eight threes, ten assists and seven rebounds in a 132–123 win at Utah, where the Jazz blew an 18-point lead. It was his fourth 50-and-10 game, behind only James Harden, and he is the second player after Wilt Chamberlain to do it for two different clubs. Reaves added 29.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50282749/luka-doncic-scores-nba-season-high-52-points-lakers-win-vs-jazz)
