# Sun 15 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea's women beat Manchester City 1–0 at the Bridge to go top of the Women's Super League — Sandy Baltimore's stoppage-time screamer, a red card for City's goalkeeper, City's first defeat of the season — though Kadeisha Buchanan and Melvine Malard both came off hurt. The men have nothing to report in the break and are home to Leeds next Sunday. Tijjani Reijnders hit a first-half hat-trick as the Netherlands put seven past Greece, San Antonio won an eleventh straight, and Milwaukee are 1-12.

## Chelsea

### Baltimore's stoppage-time bullet puts Chelsea top of the WSL
Baltimore took Ellie Carpenter's pass inside the box, turned and smashed it into the top corner four minutes into first-half added time. City goalkeeper Ayaka Yamashita was then sent off on 75 for flattening Alyssa Thompson outside her area. It is City's first defeat, and their first blank in 22 games.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/14/chelsea-manchester-city-womens-super-league-match-report)

### Buchanan and Malard both limped out of the win
Kadeisha Buchanan came off after 12 minutes, Melvine Malard after 35. Bompastor said Buchanan took a knock to the head in Friday's training and "was not herself" from the start; Malard felt her hamstring and will be assessed. Chelsea were already without Lauren James and Naomi Girma.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/sonia-bompastor-provides-injury-update-on-kadeisha-buchanan-and-melvine)

## Internationals

### Reijnders' hat-trick leaves Klopp needing a win in Berlin
Reijnders scored on 12, 18 and 33 as the Netherlands led Greece 5–0 at the break in Amsterdam; Jurriën Timber got two and Donyell Malen the seventh. Germany won 3–1 in Belgrade against nine men, but now probably have to beat the Dutch in Berlin on Monday to top Group A2.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/13/nations-league-germany-set-up-netherlands-clash-serbia-greece)

## NBA

### Spurs make it eleven straight by holding Golden State to 85
Stephon Castle scored a season-high 32 on 12 of 18, and Victor Wembanyama had 12 points and 12 rebounds. The Warriors, without Stephen Curry, had two double-figure scorers: L.J. Cryer 20 and Charles Bassey 11. San Antonio are 11–2, the league's best record, and unbeaten in NBA Cup play.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401909302)

### Milwaukee's 1-12 is their worst start since Kareem got hurt
Franz Wagner's 35 points and 12 assists — a rebound short of his first triple-double — sank the Bucks 107–99 in Orlando. That is nine straight defeats, equalling their longest run since January 2014, and per Sportradar the worst start in 52 years, to 1974-75 without an injured Kareem Abdul-Jabbar.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap?gameId=401909297)
