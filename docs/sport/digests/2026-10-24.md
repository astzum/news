# Sat 24 Oct 2026

**Overnight:** Chelsea host Tottenham at 03:30 Sunday and Xabi Alonso will do it without Emi Martínez, who needed surgery on the jaw he broke at Everton. Arsenal fear a grade-two tear has cost them Bukayo Saka for six weeks. And the day's worst news came out of Munich, where Vincent Kompany collapsed watching reserve-team training and was taken to hospital.

## Chelsea

### Martínez is weeks away and Chelsea face Spurs without him
Alonso said the goalkeeper, who had surgery after colliding with Jarrad Branthwaite at Everton, is "a few weeks" off: "Considering it was a bad one, he is doing good." Malo Gusto is back; Pep Chavarría and Emmanuel Emegha are not. Chelsea are fourth on 13, Tottenham 16th on six.
[source](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/xabi-alonso-confirms-chelsea-team-news-for-tottenham)

## Premier League

### Arsenal brace for six weeks without Saka, and Arteta is worried about more than that
A grade-two quad tear is the fear after Wednesday's limp-off in Munich, which would also take out England's Nations League games. Arteta, asked if Saka is still robust enough to start every week, would not say yes: "We really need to understand, whenever he comes back, how we are going to manage his load."
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/oct/23/arsenal-arteta-concerned-saka-durability-six-week-absence)

### Carrick talks for five and a half minutes and won't promise Fernandes a start
The Manchester United manager opened Friday's press conference himself rather than take questions, called the 4–1 at Como "unacceptable" and said "entitlement cannot be at this football club". Asked about dropping the captain who criticised his tactics, he answered only "I'll always do what is best". Bournemouth visit Sunday.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cq78pv0073dlo)

## European football
