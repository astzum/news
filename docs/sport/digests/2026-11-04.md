# Wed 04 Nov 2026

**Overnight:** Everton won 1–0 at St James' Park to go seventh, their second win over Newcastle inside a week, and Newcastle finished with ten men. Chelsea: nothing new out of Cobham, Sunderland away at 1am Monday. The NBA crammed 15 games in before pausing for Election Day — Luka Doncic matched a Damian Lillard record, Memphis lost for the first time, and Indiana turned up with six available players.

## Premier League

### Everton beat Newcastle twice in a week and climb to seventh
Thierno Barry tapped in his eighth of the season on 49 minutes after Tyrique George's low shot squirmed under Lukas Hornicek. Jacob Ramsey collected two yellows in stoppage time and Newcastle finished with ten, still 13th on 11 points after losing to the same opponent in the Carabao Cup last week.
[source](https://www.evertonfc.com/news/2026/november/02/newcastle-report/)

## NBA

### Doncic has now gone five straight games at 35 points with five threes
42 points, 13 assists, seven rebounds and six threes in the Lakers' 138–117 win over Miami. Five consecutive games of 35-plus with five rebounds, five assists and five threes matches Damian Lillard's record, and he is averaging 43.4 across the run. The Lakers are 6–2.
[source](https://www.nba.com/news/starting-5-november-3-2026)

### Indiana had six available players and still nearly beat Atlanta
Eleven Pacers were out injured or rested — about $193m of salary, roughly 93% of the payroll — leaving five starters and one substitute. They lost 108–104, Jay Huff scoring 22 and Braden Smith 19 with 10 assists. Rick Carlisle called it "a perfect storm of things happening at once."
[source](https://www.nba.com/news/5-starters-1-sub-pacers-down-to-6-players-for-game-against-atlanta-because-of-injuries-rest)

### Memphis's unbeaten start ends by a point in overtime
Ty Jerome scored nine in a row inside the last 1:43 to force extra time and finished with 40, then missed a 15-footer at the buzzer as New Orleans won 135–134. Zion Williamson had nine of his 25 in overtime. Houston, at 7–1, now lead the West.
[source](https://www.espn.com/nba/recap/_/gameId/401909914)

## NFL

### Seattle went 1 of 11 on third down and the Bears strolled it
Chicago won 34–17 on Monday night with touchdown drives of 80, 69 and 56 yards. Sam Darnold was sacked three times and threw a tipped fourth-quarter interception, and Seattle had an extra point and a field goal blocked. Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 118 receiving yards were the only bright spot; the Seahawks are 4–4.
[source](https://www.seahawks.com/news/rapid-reactions-seahawks-struggle-to-find-offensive-rhythm-in-34-17-loss-to-bears)
