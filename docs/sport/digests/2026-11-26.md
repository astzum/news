## NBA

## Formula 1

### Williams are £627k over the 2025 cost cap and lose wind tunnel time for it
The FIA's eight-month review cleared nine of eleven teams and all five engine makers. Williams admitted a 0.48% overspend plus a procedural breach, blaming restructuring costs they thought sat outside the cap, and take a fine and a 1.5% cut to wind tunnel and CFD running for 12 months. Alpine: procedural breach only.
[source](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-confirm-williams-minor-overspend-breach-in-2025-cost-cap-review.12rGorQ1pzc6aH0LX2WdLZ)
