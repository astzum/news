# Fri 30 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 25°, mostly clear, 5% chance of rain. The Southwest Metro opened this morning, so the pink replacement buses on the old Bankstown line are finished.

**The three that matter:** Israel's final count leaves Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc short of a majority, with Arab-led parties holding the balance and his party accusing them of stealing the election. At least 69 people died when two trains collided in central Spain, the second crash on that line this year. Sydney's Southwest Metro opened after a two-year shutdown of the Bankstown line.

## World

### Israel's Arab-led parties now hold the balance of power
Final results give Netanyahu's bloc 53 of 120 Knesset seats and Gadi Eisenkot's 48. The Joint List and Ra'am took a record 19 on a surge in Arab turnout, and are Eisenkot's only route to a majority. Likud says voters were bribed; the elections committee asked Shin Bet to examine suspected Qatari and Turkish funding, which Qatar calls "ridiculous".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckdd8qdl9e0eo)

### At least 69 people died in a rear-end train collision in central Spain
A Renfe service hit the back of a halted Iryo train near Urda, Toledo, on Wednesday morning. Transport minister Óscar Puente called such collisions "highly unlikely" and suggested human error. It is Europe's deadliest rail crash since 2013 and the second on that line this year — one in January killed 46. A snap election is due next month.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/29/spain-mourning-period-deadly-train-crash)

### A Russian drone killed 14 people on a platform at Dnipro's main station
Ukraine's rail operator says a jet-powered drone hit the platform on Thursday as a train prepared to leave for Zaporizhzhia; 14 more were hurt, six critically. Strikes on Chernihiv and Kremenchuk killed five others. Zelenskyy linked the attacks to recent sanctions relief on Russian oligarchs and energy. Ukraine sent 613 drones at Moscow while it hosted African leaders.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/russia-says-more-than-600-drones-launched-towards-moscow-overnight)

### China expelled a record 33 officials from the Communist Party at its fifth plenum
Only 279 of the 376 Central Committee members named in 2022 attended — more than a quarter absent, the most empty chairs in modern Chinese history. Those expelled include Zhang Youxia, formerly second-in-command of the military. The plenum set up the 2027 party congress, where Xi Jinping is expected to take a fourth term.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/30/china-expels-record-number-communist-party-xi-jinping-purges-officials-fifth-plenum)

### Binance says US$6.3 billion was taken in the largest crypto hack recorded
The exchange says attackers may have compromised a staff member's laptop and drained internet-connected "hot wallets" across 30 to 40 tokens. About US$3 billion has been frozen, and Binance says customer funds are safe and it will cover losses. The previous record was US$1.5 billion taken from Bybit in 2025 by North Korea's Lazarus group.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-30/largest-cryptocurrency-hack-billions-stolen-binance/107327732)

## Australia

### Inflation accelerated to 4.3%, with an RBA rate decision on Tuesday
The ABS says the CPI rose 4.3% in the year to September, up from 4.0% in the year to August, and trimmed-mean inflation reached 3.6%. The Monetary Policy Board meets 2–3 November and announces at 2.30pm Tuesday. It last moved on 30 September, lifting the cash rate a quarter point to 4.60%.
[source](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/consumer-price-index-australia/latest-release)

## Sydney

### The Southwest Metro opened, ending two years of replacement buses
The first service left Bankstown at 4.31am and more than 18,000 people had boarded at the new stations by 10am. Trains run every four minutes in weekday peak, five off-peak and ten on weekends. Marrickville to Gadigal is now 12 minutes. Converting the 130-year-old T3 line took a year longer than forecast; the line cost just under $23 billion.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-30/nsw-sydney-metro-southwest-line-opens-disruption/107310342)

### Eleven more housing projects were declared state significant, ten of them in Sydney
They could yield more than 4,000 homes if lodged and approved. That is the pipeline's problem: of 509 proposals in the Housing Delivery Authority fast-track scheme, covering 158,000 potential homes, 26 have been approved and construction has started at 11 sites. Thirty-two declarations have been revoked for missed timelines or withdrawal.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/more-than-4000-homes-declared-state-significant)
