## World

### The ANC fell below a third of the vote in South Africa's local elections
With more than 80% of ballots counted, the party of Nelson Mandela was on 31.8%, almost 14 points below its 2021 local result, and is projected to lose its place as the largest party in Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal. The Democratic Alliance had 24.1%, Jacob Zuma's MK party 9.3%. Turnout rose to 54.5%. Final results Sunday.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/06/anc-faces-defeat-south-african-local-elections)

### AI-linked shares fell sharply, then partly recovered
The Nasdaq dropped 5.5% on Thursday — Nvidia fell more than 9%, AMD and Micron more than 17% — then rebounded 1.4% on Friday after US payrolls came in at 165,000 for September. Asia and Europe fared worse, with South Korea's Kospi down 6%. Investors partly blamed the Democrats' midterm win for the prospect of slower AI development.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c35ynnpzdq51o)

### Trump is handing his White House to the architect of its immigration crackdown
Stephen Miller, 41, replaces Susie Wiles as chief of staff on Monday, Trump said in a social media post. Wiles moves to his "political operation"; she had stepped back in March after a breast cancer diagnosis. The switch follows the Republicans' midterm losses. Miller was Trump's chief immigration adviser in his first term.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-07/donald-trump-cos-susie-wiles-to-be-replaced-by-stephen-miller/107358840)

### The EU says TikTok's "For You" feed breaches the Digital Services Act
Preliminary findings published Friday say TikTok did not adequately assess how its recommender pulls users into "rabbit holes" of content about sadness, isolation and body image, and that its existing fixes, including the "not interested" button, do not work. TikTok can now reply; a final non-compliance decision could carry a fine of up to 6% of global turnover.
[source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-you-feed-breach-digital-services-act-harmful-rabbit-holes)

### California commuted the death sentences of all 548 people on its death row
Governor Gavin Newsom converted them to life without parole, saying "state-sanctioned killing is incompatible with California's values". The state has not executed anyone since 2006 and Newsom halted executions in 2019, so the move is largely symbolic, though experts say it could save millions a year in court costs. He leaves office in January.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrkyyd2j8mvo)

### Taiwan is closing its Papua New Guinea office and cutting its gas purchases
Taipei said on Friday that Port Moresby had repeated demands that staff leave, and blamed Chinese pressure. It will stop signing new spot LNG contracts — Taiwanese purchases accounted for a third of PNG's exports — and end medical missions and scholarships. Taiwan holds formal ties with 12 countries, three of them Pacific states.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/taiwan-to-close-representative-office-in-papua-new-guinea-blames-china)

## Sydney

### The City of Sydney wants more than 5000 shared e-bikes off its streets
A council plan would cap shared bikes at 7000 across the local government area, down from about 12,000, and cut operators from three to two. Almost 700,000 shared-bike trips were taken there last month. New NSW laws take full effect in March, requiring Transport for NSW and council approval to deploy bikes, with penalties up to $55,000.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/revealed-the-plan-to-strip-5000-e-bikes-from-sydney-s-streets-20261106-p61c4k.html)

### NSW Labor says it will let developers pay council contributions later
The pre-election pledge would defer local infrastructure contributions from the construction certificate stage to the occupation certificate, cutting upfront costs on housing projects. As an immediate step, the planning minister will direct councils to spend more than $900 million collected under repealed plans; councils hold $4.6 billion in all. Minor fitouts and signage would also skip full development applications.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/re-elected-minns-labor-government-to-boost-productivity-and-deliver-more-homes-and-jobs)
