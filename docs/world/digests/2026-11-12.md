# Thu 12 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 24° / 18°, partly cloudy, 10% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Russia and Ukraine will exchange prisoners after their first talks in nine months, but nothing moved on a ceasefire. Indonesia's vice-president has resigned after a court found he did not meet the educational requirement for the job. New home lending in Australia fell at the fastest rate on record, led by investors leaving the market.

## World

### Russia and Ukraine agreed a large prisoner exchange, and little else
Two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, the first meeting between the sides in nine months, ended with both delegations confirming a planned swap of prisoners of war. US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff travel to Moscow on Thursday to meet Vladimir Putin, then to Dublin to meet Volodymyr Zelenskyy.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-12/ukraine-russia-agree-prisoner-swap-abu-dhabi-talks/107375462)

### Indonesia's vice-president resigned after a court voided his school credentials
Gibran Rakabuming Raka submitted his resignation to President Prabowo Subianto on Wednesday, an aide told the BBC. The Supreme Court had found no evidence he completed senior secondary school, which the constitution requires, and more than 100 lawmakers backed a move towards impeachment. Gibran is the son of former president Joko Widodo.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmvgxgz87qqro)

### US prosecutors charged three Iran-linked men over 18 attacks in Europe
The Justice Department says they operated under the name Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamiya, directing arsons, explosives and a near-fatal stabbing in London, Amsterdam and Paris this year, some over FaceTime, and orchestrated September's incident at RAF Fairford. Iran denies any involvement. Two of the three are not in custody.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3rryrryk59zo)

### Millions joined Spain's first general strike in 14 years
Tenants' associations called the 24-hour stoppage over housing costs and unions later backed it, reporting 90% participation in the automotive and port sectors but uneven turnout nationally. The tenants' confederation said four million had taken part by midday. Officials reported more than 50 arrests. A general election follows on 29 November.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/11/spain-general-strike-housing-costs)

### Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient and started a 42-day countdown
The country can be declared free of the disease on 23 December if no new case appears. Nine people were infected, all traced to one traveller from the Democratic Republic of the Congo; two died. The DRC outbreak itself continues, with 11,042 infections and 5,387 deaths reported by its government.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/11/kenya-discharges-last-ebola-patient)

## Australia

### New home lending fell at a record rate in the September quarter
The number of new loan commitments for dwellings dropped 15.0% and their value 15.5%, the Bureau of Statistics said. Investor lending fell 30.6% by number and 32.3% by value, both record falls; owner-occupier lending fell about 5%. The bureau cited high rates, tax changes and expectations of lower house prices.
[source](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/finance/lending-indicators/latest-release)

## Sydney

### Bus drivers will stop work twice during Friday's peaks
About 2,000 drivers in the Rail Tram and Bus Union will strike from 6.30am to 7.30am and 5pm to 6pm in a pay dispute with operators Keolis, Transdev John Holland and Busways. The eastern suburbs, northern beaches, lower north shore and inner north-west are affected. Metro M1 services start four-minute frequencies an hour early.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/sydney-commuters-warned-of-major-disruptions-to-buses-on-friday-20261111-p61dbj.html)

### NSW changed how adult sexual assault trials are run
Recorded police interviews can now serve as a complainant's evidence in chief, which previously applied only to children under 16 and people with a cognitive impairment. Ground rules hearings, which set limits on questioning before a complainant testifies, will be piloted at Parramatta, Wollongong and Dubbo District Courts from 2027.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/reforms-to-protect-sexual-assault-complainants-pass-nsw-parliament)
