# Mon 26 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 22° / 16°, a shower or two, 50% chance of rain. BOM has a marine wind warning out for NSW coastal waters through Monday.

**The three that matter:** Israel votes on Tuesday with all West Bank land crossings closed and an army operation running in Hebron. Elon Musk said SpaceX restricted Ukraine's Starlink service, which is back to normal speeds after more than a week. Serbia and Brazil both voted on Sunday, and neither had a result by 7am Sydney time.

## World

### Israel will close all West Bank crossings for Tuesday's election
Israel's military says all land crossings with the occupied West Bank will shut on election day, and Palestinian workers were barred from settlements from Sunday. The closures follow an operation launched after gunmen killed two Israeli soldiers near Hebron on Friday. Hebron's mayor says 2,200 people have been forced from their homes; Defence Minister Israel Katz confirmed the figure.
[source](https://www.dw.com/en/israel-to-close-all-west-bank-land-crossings-on-election-day/a-79755010)

### Musk says SpaceX restricted Ukraine's Starlink, now back to full speed
He wrote on X that Ukraine's military had violated Starlink's terms by using it for offensive drone strikes and that "loophole was closed last week". Kyiv says the slowdown hampered drone defence for over a week. Presidential adviser Serhii Beskrestnov called the post "very strange", saying commercial Starlink is capped at 70-90km/h in Ukraine and blocked over Russia.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvx28ljp8n4xo)

### María Corina Machado has returned to Venezuela after 10 months away
The Nobel laureate posted "I am here. With you" on Sunday and appeared at a church in Lara state. Acting president Delcy Rodríguez, installed with US backing after the American military removed Nicolás Maduro in January, has not called an election despite promising one at the UN in September. Thousands protested this week demanding a vote.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/venezuelas-opposition-leader-maria-corina-machado-returns-from-exile)

### Serbia's election drew its highest turnout since Yugoslavia broke up
Monitors CRTA and CeSID/Ipsos both projected turnout near 68% at 7pm, 13 to 14 points above the 2023 election and close to the 71.4% record set in 1990. The EU's ambassador said the bloc was "deeply concerned about reported violent incidents". Counting began after polls closed at 8pm, with first projections due late Sunday.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/oct/25/europe-serbia-elections-aleksander-vucic-student-list-protests-bulgaria-latest-news-updates)

### At least 48 people were killed in a raid on a north-east Nigerian village
Gunmen on about 50 motorcycles attacked Binnari in Taraba state after Friday midday prayers, looting shops and burning houses over roughly four hours, police and a community leader said. Police put the toll at 48 after more bodies were found on Sunday; Amnesty International says it has confirmed 52. No group has claimed the attack.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq1kdlm1ze7do)

## Australia

### 3.6 million Australian households went short of food in the past year
Foodbank's annual report puts food insecurity at one in three households, about 100,000 more than last year, and says it is now entrenched rather than a shock effect. Cost of living was named as the cause by 92% of affected households. Renters, single-parent and First Nations households, and younger Australians, are hit hardest.
[source](https://reports.foodbank.org.au/foodbank-hunger-report-2026/)

## Sydney

### Western Sydney International flew its first commercial passengers
Jetstar's JQ362 left Badgerys Creek for the Gold Coast just before 11.30am Sunday, the first of three domestic services. Air New Zealand's NZ166 to Auckland is due to depart at 8.55am Monday, the first international flight. The airport is curfew-free and reached toll-free via the M12; the St Marys light rail is not due until late next year.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-26/passengers-praise-western-sydney-airport-first-flights/107303722)

### The NSW childcare regulator suspended 53 staff in its first year
Marking a year since its child-safety laws passed, the government said the Early Learning Commission has suspended 53 educators, staff or volunteers, cancelled eight services, suspended 10 more and imposed $324,290 in fines. It made 8,776 regulatory visits, 71% of them unannounced, and took 876 compliance actions, up 108% on 2025.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/tougher-child-safety-laws-mark-one-year)
