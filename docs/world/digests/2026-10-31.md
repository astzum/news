# Sat 31 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 28° / 17°, showers and a possible storm, 80% chance of rain. No BOM warnings are current.

**The three that matter:** Hundreds of people were bused out of Russian-occupied Oleshky in the first joint Russian-Ukrainian humanitarian operation of the war. Hong Kong's inquiry into the fire that killed 168 people found failings at four government departments, and the housing secretary is out. US tech stocks fell for a second day as investors looked at what the big AI spenders are earning back.

## World

### Hundreds left Oleshky in the war's first joint humanitarian convoy
Twenty-five buses and ambulances entered the Russian-held city opposite Kherson on Friday, giving up to 750 people a four-hour window to leave for Russia or Ukraine. A ceasefire across the Kherson front, in force since Tuesday, let the road be demined. Both sides' human rights ombudsmen travelled in together. Several hundred residents stayed.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60me298k1yeo)

### Hong Kong's fire inquiry faulted four government departments
Justice David Lok's committee found substituted non-fire-retardant scaffolding nets and absent contractor supervision spread the November 2025 Wang Fuk Court blaze, which killed 168, and identified "deficiencies and failings" at four regulators. John Lee apologised, recommended the housing secretary's removal after she resigned, and ordered a disciplinary investigation into individual officials.
[source](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202610/29/P2026102900551.htm)

### Christine Lagarde will leave the ECB nine months early
She told the Governing Council on Thursday she steps down on 7 February 2027; her term ran to October. No reason was given, and the European Council began the search for a successor the same day. At that meeting the bank held its deposit rate at 2.50%, warning a longer energy shock will raise inflation and cut growth.
[source](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr261029~abbb6dc64b.en.html)

### Canada and the EU launched an "Alliance for the Future"
Mark Carney and Ursula von der Leyen closed a Montreal summit on Friday with a partnership open to other countries, promising a more integrated market and fewer trade and travel barriers. Canada will contribute to a €90 billion loan for Ukraine and joins Europe's supercomputing network. Associate membership is deferred to a 2027 summit.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/30/canada-european-union-alliance)

### US tech stocks fell for a second day as AI spending outran earnings
The Nasdaq dropped more than 3.5% on Friday and Intel fell as much as 30% on weak PC demand. Alphabet had lost over $500 billion of value on Thursday after telling investors its own AI search summaries were cutting into search ad revenue. Five big tech firms are committed to spending $800.6 billion this year.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvwy72e0n72yo)

## Australia

### Queensland's Land Court ruled for a $1 billion coal mine
It backed Whitehaven's Winchester South project near Moranbah after a seven-week hearing. Judge Nicholas Loos called its 557 million tonnes of lifetime emissions "an extraordinary amount" and the conservation groups' case "eloquent and powerful", but found the consequences would come with or without the mine. State and federal approvals are still needed.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-30/whitehaven-coalmine-expansion-approval-qld-land-court/107328464)

## Sydney

### Powerhouse Parramatta opens next Saturday, with a Treasury review running
Western Sydney's first state cultural institution, a $915 million complex on the river, opens on 7 November with five exhibitions, most of them free. Its long-serving director, Lisa Havilah, was stood aside in late August pending an investigation into governance and procurement, and NSW Treasury is reviewing the museum's ongoing operating costs.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/oct/31/parramatta-powerhouse-museum-opening-western-sydney-ntwnfb)

### NSW renters can now move a bond between properties for $25
Fair Trading says 2,700 households have transferred more than $7 million since Smart Rental Bonds went statewide last month, instead of finding a second bond up front. Renters pay the difference if the new bond is higher. Sydney gets about 39,000 new rental listings over spring, its busiest season.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/stay-cost-savvy-spring-rental-season)
