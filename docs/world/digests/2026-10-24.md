# Sat 24 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 26° / 17°, partly cloudy, 20% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Israel has besieged Hebron and begun demolitions after two soldiers were killed, and Netanyahu says plans exist for the largest West Bank operation since 1967. Eight European and Middle Eastern foreign ministers meeting in Istanbul urged the US and Israel not to restart strikes on Iran, as Brent crude passed $117. NSW parliament passed the law creating the Great Koala National Park.

## World

### Israel has besieged Hebron and started demolitions after two soldiers were killed
Gunmen killed two Israeli soldiers and injured three at a checkpoint on Friday; both attackers died in a 20-minute gunfight. Netanyahu ordered a siege of Hebron and Halhul and the forced removal of the gunmen's home neighbourhoods. Israel's high court temporarily halted the first demolitions. Ministers campaigning before Tuesday's election invoked the Jenin camp model; Hebron has about 250,000 residents.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/23/netanyahu-orders-very-extensive-operation-west-bank-two-israeli-soldiers-killed)

### Eight countries formed an anti-war bloc to argue against new strikes on Iran
Foreign ministers from Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Pakistan, France, Germany, Italy and the UK met in Istanbul and said a fresh US-Israeli attack would spread the war. They also demanded the Houthis stop attacking Saudi Arabia and that the strait of Hormuz reopen. Brent passed $117 after Tehran threatened Gulf refineries, undersea cables and datacentres.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/23/european-middle-eastern-ministers-summit-iran-us-israel)

### A magnitude-7 earthquake in northern Tibet killed at least 16 people
The quake hit Baqên County in Nagqu on Thursday night, 400km north-east of Lhasa, at an altitude of about 4,800m. State media report 339 homes collapsed, dozens injured and more than 12,400 people relocated; Beijing has allocated 50 million yuan for relief. Rockfalls closed roads and over 400 aftershocks have been recorded. China restricts foreign media access to the region.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck5ynd1z6e83o)

### Russia and Ukraine agreed a local ceasefire around the besieged city of Oleshky
The White House says Melania Trump brokered it; both militaries said they would stop firing on the Kherson axis early Tuesday, while the White House put the truce's start at 29 October. Ukraine estimates 6,000 people remain, including 200 children, from a pre-war 24,000. Zelenskyy also said Starlink and Starshield limits remain, contradicting Trump.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-24/ukraine-latest/107304160)

### Macron called a shooting outside Paris a racist terrorist attack
A 39-year-old man fired from his car over about 15 minutes in Trappes and La Verrière on Friday, killing one and wounding five, including a 12-year-old girl in a serious condition. He was arrested. The anti-terrorist prosecutor is treating it as murder linked to a terrorist enterprise. It lands as France bans weekend protests in several cities.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6ev38dj1g8zo)

## Australia

### The Great Koala National Park has passed into law
The bill adds 178,000 hectares of state forest and Crown land on the NSW Mid North Coast to the national parks estate, home to about 12,000 koalas and more than 100 other threatened species, making a reserve of over 475,000 hectares. It opens before year's end. Forestry Corporation staff get redundancy or transfers into new parks roles.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/great-koala-national-park-created)

## Sydney

### NSW has legislated changes to takeaway cups and a list of plastic phase-outs
From 2028 single-use coffee cups must be recyclable, cafes must offer a mug or glass for dining in and accept clean keep cups. Cups are not banned. Bread tags, balloon sticks, microbeads and lollipop sticks are being phased out; produce stickers must be compostable by 2030. NSW sends more than 800,000 tonnes of plastic to landfill a year.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/practical-reforms-to-reduce-plastics-and-microplastics)

### NSW MPs say a parliamentary security briefing failed to answer their questions
Liberal MP Alister Henskels said some MPs were in tears at Thursday's police and parliamentary briefing "because the answers were so inadequate", and others called it box-ticking. It followed a protester reaching the chamber floor last week and the attack on federal MP Helen Haines. Parliament says extra special constables, new checkpoints and screening are coming.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-23/nsw-mp-security-inadequate-after-parliament-safety-briefing/107299022)
