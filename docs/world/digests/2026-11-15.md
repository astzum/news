# Sun 15 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 18°, showers, 90% chance of rain, with a chance of a thunderstorm this afternoon and early evening. No BOM warnings current for Sydney.

**The three that matter:** A botched copper-to-fibre upgrade by an FAA contractor shut US airspace for more than four hours on Friday, the longest nationwide ground stop since 2001, and the disruption will run for days. US negotiators asked Ukraine whether it would leave Donbas in exchange for NATO membership, the first time the two have been linked. Trump, Putin and Xi will hold a three-way meeting in China late next week.

## World

### A copper wire upgrade shut all US departures for more than four hours
Transport Secretary Sean Duffy said a contractor, L3Harris, was switching an old copper line to fibre when a router began flooding air traffic control systems and broke communications with aircraft. More than 3,500 US flights were cancelled on Saturday. Duffy said there is no evidence of a cyber-attack and asked Congress for $30.1 billion in upgrades.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6wy9lynq4qno)

### US negotiators asked whether Ukraine would leave Donbas to join NATO
Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the Americans put the question during this week's Abu Dhabi talks, the first time membership has been tied to territorial concessions. He said Kyiv is not ready to concede land, but would put a comprehensive package including security guarantees to a referendum. Putin has long called Ukrainian NATO membership a red line.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-15/us-idea-of-ukraine-ceding-donbas-to-join-nato-zelenskyy/107386558)

### Trump, Putin and Xi will meet together at the APEC summit in Shenzhen
The White House said the trilateral will close Trump's visit late next week, after a bilateral with Xi. Spokeswoman Olivia Wales said reducing nuclear weapons is on the agenda; Ukraine is not. Russia has confirmed, China has not. The last US-Russia arms control treaty expired in February. A senior US official said no agreement is expected.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckz74jzpy18do)

### Italy fined Meta €805m over Facebook's access to users' photos
Garante, the data protection authority, said the Facebook Android app read photos, videos and metadata from Italian users' phones without specific consent or notice, through a gallery-suggestions feature that was on by default. It said up to 20 million people in Italy were affected. It is by far the largest fine the Italian regulator has imposed.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/14/italy-fines-meta-880m-over-photo-privacy)

### Chile's government reversed planned fuel price rises after truckers blockaded highways
Truckers' unions shut two major motorways this week; on Friday the government sent congress a bill undoing the increases and the strike ended. President José Antonio Kast, eight months into office and polling at 24% approval, had declined in July to use a state anti-inflation tool as prices rose. Protests over living costs continue.
[source](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/14/nx-s1-6040144/chile-protests-fuel-kast-students)

## Sydney

### A confidential survey found a derelict ship in the harbour could sink at any time
Marine surveyors warned early last year that the MV Cape Don, moored at Balls Head in Waverton, is "severely deteriorated", with hull holes and grossly inadequate bilge pumping, and that failure of a port-side hull plate is an "extremely high" risk. NSW Maritime says removal is unlikely before next year, at a taxpayer cost of $15–20 million.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/secret-report-finds-2000-tonne-ship-poses-severe-environmental-threat-20261115-p61dzn.html)

### NSW Labor promised 100 government-run long-day childcare centres
Premier Chris Minns said they would be built on existing public primary school sites in "childcare deserts", many in Western Sydney, if Labor wins the March state election. They would be the first publicly owned long-day centres in NSW, with 5,000 places for about 8,000 families, on top of 100 preschools due to open from January.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-14/nsw-long-day-childcare-centres-minns-government-election-promise/107385858)

### Lakemba Mosque is being assessed for the NSW State Heritage Register
The Heritage Council decided this month that the Wangee Road mosque may meet the threshold for state significance, and will run public consultation before Heritage Minister Penny Sharpe decides early next year. It would be the first mosque in Sydney on the register and the second in NSW, after the 1887 Afghan Mosque in Broken Hill.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/lakemba-mosque-poised-to-join-qvb-and-parliament-house-with-top-heritage-listing-20261114-p61daf.html)
