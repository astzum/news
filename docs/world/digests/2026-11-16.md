# Mon 16 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 21° / 16°, showers, 90% chance of rain, with southerly winds 25 to 35 km/h easing late. No BOM warnings current for Sydney.

**The three that matter:** Iran fired drones at a US radar site in Bahrain for the second time in three days, and the Revolutionary Guard says the Strait of Hormuz is closed. Yemeni government forces abandoned positions on the Bab al-Mandeb strait to defend Aden, opening the way for a Houthi push on the interim capital. On Thursday the International Court of Justice will say whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — the first time the court has ruled on state responsibility for genocide.

## World

### Iran launched a second drone attack on Bahrain in three days
Sirens sounded across Bahrain on Sunday and the interior ministry told people to shelter. The Revolutionary Guard said it targeted a US air defence radar; Bahrain said its defences intercepted the drones and held Iran "fully responsible". The US denied Iran's claim to have hit a radar on Friday. Trump said last week he was no longer seeking a deal.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqn4e2py48kwo)

### Yemeni government forces gave up Bab al-Mandeb to defend Aden
A spokesman for the Saudi-backed Southern Armed Forces said on Sunday they are redeploying from Dhubab airport and the area around the strait to protect the interim capital, and to break the Houthi siege of Taiz. Houthi fighters already hold highlands north of Aden. The Saudi-led coalition says it shot down two missiles aimed at Riyadh.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/yemeni-government-forces-redeploy-from-bab-al-mandeb-to-defend-aden)

### Estonia will close all three of its border crossings with Russia
Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna announced the closure of Narva, Luhamaa and Koidula after eight people were detained over an attempted arson attack on Threod Systems, a drone maker supplying Ukraine. Estonian authorities say Russia's GRU ordered it; Moscow has not commented. Tsahkna said the closure will also curb sanctions circumvention, but not how long it will last.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/estonia-to-close-border-crossings-with-russia-after-attack-on-drone-maker)

### About 350,000 Czechs rallied in Prague against a public media funding bill
Organisers put that figure on Sunday's crowd at Letná Plain, one of the largest gatherings in Czech history. The bill would scrap the licence fees funding Czech TV and Czech Radio and pay for them from the state budget, which protesters say hands Andrej Babiš's coalition leverage over newsrooms. Weeks of protests and a broadcast staff strike preceded it.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/15/czechia-protest-prague-media-freedom)

### The ICJ will rule on Thursday whether Myanmar committed genocide
The court will deliver judgment in The Gambia v Myanmar at 10am on 19 November, seven years after the case was filed over the military campaign that drove hundreds of thousands of Rohingya into Bangladesh. The ICJ has never found a state responsible for genocide; 11 states have intervened, and a separate case against Israel is pending.
[source](https://www.icj-cij.org/case/178)

## Australia

### The illicit tobacco market shrank for the first time in five years
The Illicit Tobacco and E-cigarette Commissioner estimates the black market fell from last year's peak of 5,972 tonnes to about 5,893, and possibly as low as 4,453, after five years of 22.6% average annual growth. The government credits enforcement: 1,300 shops closed since October 2025. The Coalition and One Nation want excise cut by 80% and 75%.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-15/illicit-tobacco-market-shrinks/107387590)

## Sydney

### The outgoing casino commissioner says NSW has "got nowhere" on pokies laundering
Philip Crawford, inaugural chief commissioner of the Independent Casino Commission, retires next week and told the Herald the government had bowed to industry pressure. Minns has refused to adopt the mandatory cashless gaming cards that Project Islington recommended; August's reforms rely on facial recognition and optional account-based play. NSW is on track to pass $10 billion in machine losses.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/there-s-no-bravery-in-government-nsw-casino-watchdog-lashes-labor-s-pokie-failures-20261116-p61df9.html)

### A researcher warns the new Western Sydney airport will make hot suburbs hotter
Sebastian Pfautsch of Western Sydney University says the 1,780-hectare site at Badgerys Creek will absorb and re-radiate heat, and weaken the southerly flow that cools Penrith, St Marys and Emu Plains on extreme days. ABC meteorologist Tom Saunders puts the airport's mean summer maximum near 30°C against 26.7°C at Sydney Airport. WSI says climate risks were mitigated to 2090.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-16/nsw-wsi-airport-heat-impact-summer-western-sydney/107297220)
