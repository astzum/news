# Thu 19 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 26° / 18°, mostly sunny, 5% chance of rain, north-easterly winds 25 to 35 km/h. No BOM warnings current for Sydney.

**The three that matter:** Iran attacked Kuwait for the first time in two months and Bahrain for the fourth time in six days, after US aircraft struck a missile site on an Iranian island in the Strait of Hormuz. Democrats won the last undecided Senate race, taking control of both houses of Congress for the rest of Donald Trump's term. Israeli tanks pushed into central Gaza in the first ground operation since the ceasefire was signed.

## World

### Iran attacked Kuwait and Bahrain after a US strike on Qeshm Island
Kuwait's military said it engaged drones overnight and reported no damage; Iran's army said it targeted Patriot batteries at Ali Al Salem Air Base. Bahrain said it destroyed several Iranian attacks, its fourth in six days. US officials said warplanes had struck a missile launch site on Qeshm, in the Strait of Hormuz. Qatar called the exchange a dangerous escalation.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw4gn9jeqjq7o)

### Israeli forces began a ground operation in central Gaza
Hospital officials said three Palestinians were killed as tanks moved near Salah al-Din Street, east of the Nuseirat camp — the first broad ground push since the ceasefire was signed over a year ago. The army said it was eliminating a threat to troops near the Yellow Line. Al Jazeera reported no evacuation order preceded it; residents fled anyway.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/18/palestinians-flee-refugee-camp-as-israel-begins-operation-in-central-gaza)

### Democrats won Alaska's Senate seat, completing control of Congress
Mary Peltola unseated Republican Dan Sullivan two weeks after counting began, the ninth seat Democrats flipped and their 56th. With the House already won, the party controls both chambers for Trump's final two years. Peltola is the first Alaska Native senator. Her campaign faces federal and state investigation over an alleged spoiler-candidate scheme she denies knowing about.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqev3v2ygzlvo)

### South Korea banned devices from its university exam after an AI cheating case
More than half a million candidates sit the eight-hour suneung today at 1,312 centres. The top scorer in this year's tax accountancy exam, who got one of 160 questions wrong, admitted on Sunday to using a hidden camera and AI. Invigilators will collect phones and inspect glasses, watches and collars; an unsilenceable alert goes to every phone nationwide.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/18/south-korea-university-entrace-exams-ai-cheating-measures)

### The EU said it will not discuss long-term UK ties until Britain decides on Brexit
Diplomats told the Guardian that piecemeal "reset" summits are finished and Brussels wants to know whether the UK intends to reverse its exit before negotiating further. A joint statement at Friday's summit is still expected to open talks on energy markets, migration and defence. Andy Burnham sets out his next steps on Monday, including a "national conversation".
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/nov/18/eu-uk-reset-summit-talks-burnham-brexit)

## Australia

### A study found regeneration projects behind carbon credits have little forest
Academics led by Andrew Macintosh, the scheme's former integrity chair, assessed 50 human-induced regeneration projects and found average forest cover of 37% where 100% was assumed. Fossil fuel firms buy 48% of these credits to meet safeguard mechanism obligations; emissions under that scheme fell 0.4% over two years. Project managers reject the findings.
[Australian Conservation Foundation](https://www.acf.org.au/news/invisible-forests-study-reveals-carbon-credit-scam)

## Sydney

### Domain forecasts another 10% fall in Sydney house prices before a rebound
The report released Thursday has capital city prices dropping 10 to 13% by mid-2027, with Sydney stabilising in the second half of the year and then recovering faster than any other capital — a median moving from $1.6m to $1.7m over the year to December 2027. Domain blames borrowing costs, weak confidence and this year's investment tax changes.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/nsw-sydney-house-prices-forecast-rebound/107399420)

### Low-rise apartments have fallen from 18% of Sydney approvals to 1%
A Committee for Sydney report argues three- and four-storey walk-ups are priced out by building rules that treat them like towers. Its planning director, Estelle Grech, said rezoning will not produce homes nobody can afford to build. The architect Philip Thalis said a low-rise block that once needed six to eight consultants now needs 20 to 25.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/why-sydney-s-popular-three-storey-walk-ups-are-facing-extinction-20261119-p61er1.html)
