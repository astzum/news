# Tue 10 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 12°, sunny, 5% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** France says Iran's Revolutionary Guards were behind last month's cyber-attack that cooked the contents of hundreds of supermarket freezers, and has promised Europe-wide sanctions. Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation into chief executive Jensen Huang after reports he was targeted by Chinese intelligence, and about $250 billion came off the company's value. NSW public primary schools will drop bring-your-own-device requirements from 2027, so parents will not have to buy laptops.

## World

### France says Iran's Revolutionary Guards hacked its supermarket freezers
Foreign minister Jean-Noël Barrot said the IRGC was responsible for the 11–12 October attacks, which overrode thermostats in as many as 700 French stores and pushed temperatures above 50C. Paris prosecutor Laure Beccuau has opened an investigation into the IRGC-linked Shahid Kaveh unit, citing Farsi use and Iranian IP addresses. Barrot promised Europe-wide sanctions and summoned Iran's ambassador.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/09/france-blames-irans-revolutionary-guards-for-cyber-attacks-on-supermarket-fridges)

### Nvidia's board is investigating its own chief executive
Directors ordered an independent review "in light of recent reporting" that Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The New York Times reported US officials believe Alice Huang, a Singapore-based executive whose firm dealt with Nvidia, spied for Beijing. Huang admitted an affair; China called the claims "groundless". Shares fell about 6%, cutting roughly $250 billion.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6zxjx01vyleo)

### COP31's president told delegates the 1.5C limit will be overshot
Opening the Antalya talks, Australia's Chris Bowen said the Paris target would be passed but that "we can return" to it. The summit's centrepiece is a new global electrification goal. UN climate chief Simon Stiell said fossil fuel price chaos was "spreading inflation like a cancer". A leaders' summit runs Wednesday and Thursday.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/nov/09/high-fossil-fuel-prices-spread-inflation-like-cancer-cop31egotiator-tells-cop31)

### Novaya Gazeta has closed its Moscow newsroom
Russia's best-known investigative paper held its last editorial meeting on Monday, two months after being designated a "foreign agent" — a label that stopped banks processing the reader donations it lived on. It was co-founded with Mikhail Gorbachev's Nobel Prize money. The Kremlin called the closure part of "a normal cycle of change".
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/russian-investigative-newspaper-novaya-gazeta-closes-citing-govt-clampdown)

### Fighting on Ethiopia's border has pushed more than 50,000 people into Kenya
The Kenya Red Cross's regional head says displacement around Moyale has risen from about 36,000 in a late-October government assessment to above 50,000; no final figure is confirmed. The clashes follow a dispute between Ethiopia's Oromia and Somali regional administrations that escalated in mid-October. Kenya has no contingency food stocks and has not set up a camp.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/more-than-50000-displaced-by-deadly-ethiopia-border-clashes)

## Sydney

### NSW public primary schools will stop requiring students to bring their own devices
From 2027, no public primary school can make parents buy a laptop or tablet; high school schemes become explicitly voluntary. New guidelines cap device learning at about 15 minutes a day for K–2 and 45 minutes for Years 5–6, recommended from Term 1 2027 and mandatory in 2028. No screen-based homework before Year 5.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/turning-page-on-screens-class-as-minns-labor-government-ends-bring-your-own-device-requirements-for-public-primary-schools)

### ASIC has opened a formal investigation into the collapsed Bathla Group
The regulator confirmed it is examining the conduct of the Sydney developer's affairs, without saying what. Bathla entered voluntary administration on 25 August owing at least $3.4 billion across more than 500 companies, halting about 2,000 homes under construction with 13,000 more planned. Creditors now wait until September 2027 for their second meeting.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-10/asic-launches-investigation-into-bathla-group/107365198)

### The state will take single ownership of the Cooks River
Boundaries never caught up with the river's realignment between 1946 and 1963, leaving it split between owners and stalling repairs. It will now become a Crown waterway in state hands, with banks kept as council-managed Crown land. About 200 metres of exclusion fencing is up along 1.4km of failing steel piling; a management plan goes on exhibition early 2027.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/minns-labor-government-to-take-action-on-cooks-river)
