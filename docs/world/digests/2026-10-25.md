# Sun 25 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 30° / 19°, showers and a possible storm, 80% chance of rain. BOM has a marine wind warning out for NSW coastal waters through Monday.

**The three that matter:** Brazil votes today in a presidential runoff that polls put level between Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro, after Bolsonaro won the first round. Serbians vote in a snap parliamentary election where a student movement is the first serious threat to Aleksandar Vučić's party since 2012. The Houthis say they struck airports in Riyadh and Dammam and Saudi oil infrastructure, widening the conflict that reignited with the Iran war.

## World

### Brazil votes today in a runoff the polls put level
Nearly 159 million Brazilians choose between President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, 80, and Flávio Bolsonaro, who won the 4 October first round 47% to 45%. No first-round loser has come back since democracy returned in the 1980s. Flávio's father Jair is serving 27 years for plotting a coup, and his possible release is an election issue.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/24/lula-da-silva-supporters-rally-eve-brazil-election-runoff-bolsonaro)

### Serbians vote in the first election to seriously threaten Vučić since 2012
A Student List of 250 candidates, picked by university assemblies and with no named leader, is polling level with Aleksandar Vučić's Serbian Progressive Party. The movement grew out of two years of anti-corruption protests after 16 people died when a railway station canopy collapsed in Novi Sad. Vučić stood down as president to run for prime minister.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm1dwz311476o)

### The Houthis say they attacked Saudi airports and oil infrastructure
Spokesman Yahya Saree said the group hit Riyadh's King Khalid and Dammam's King Fahd airports, an air base at Khamis Mushait, the Rabigh refinery and the East-West pipeline. The Saudi-led coalition said it responded to falling debris near oil sites but did not confirm the claims. The Houthis accuse Riyadh of strikes in Yemen that killed more than a dozen.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-25/houthis-claim-attacks-on-saudi-airports-oil-pipelines/107305824)

### At least 400,000 marched across France as Macron's protest restrictions held
Government figures put Saturday's turnout at 400,000 nationwide and 110,000 in Paris; unions say double that. The interior ministry reported almost 1,000 arrests, with more than 70,000 police deployed. Marches backing school students' demands for teachers and building repairs were largely peaceful until masked groups clashed with police. Last Saturday brought at least 2,700 arrests.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/24/hundreds-of-thousands-march-across-france-in-protests-over-schools-and-policing)

### Gunmen killed nine people at a church funeral in Jordan
Three attackers fired for several minutes at St George's Greek Orthodox church in Hamoud, Karak governorate, on Friday, killing nine and wounding 40. No group has claimed responsibility and the manhunt continues; ministers called it extremist terrorism but say links to known groups are unclear. Jordan declared three days of mourning. Attacks on its Christian minority are rare.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/24/jordans-king-abdullah-orders-firm-response-after-deadly-church-attack)

## Sydney

### NSW has scrapped next month's T4, T8 and South Coast timetable changes
Premier Chris Minns cancelled the 15 November timetable on Saturday, saying "the vast majority of people do not want" it. It would have lifted T4 peak frequency to a train every 7.5 minutes and added 200 weekly services, but dropped Wolli Creek and Sydenham stops, forcing extra changes for airport and metro trips. Reliability comes first, the transport minister said.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-24/nsw-government-backflip-t4-t8-south-coast-rail-timetable-changes/107304442)

### Submissions close Wednesday on rezoning that would reshape the inner west
Inner West Council's second-stage Fairer Future plan covers sites near light rail stops and main streets in Ashfield, Dulwich Hill, Marrickville, Rozelle, Balmain, Lilyfield and Newtown. Mayor Darcy Byrne has conceded the proposed 20-storey blocks in Lilyfield are "over-the-top" and will be revisited. The exhibition period ends 28 October. Stage one targeted more than 30,000 dwellings.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/a-sydney-council-wants-to-add-thousands-of-homes-residents-oppose-such-radical-change-20261023-p613m4.html)
