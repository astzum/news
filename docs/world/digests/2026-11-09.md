# Mon 09 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 21° / 12°, partly cloudy, 40% chance of rain. A marine wind warning is current for the NSW coast.

**The three that matter:** The UN climate summit opens today in Turkey, with Australia presiding over the negotiations and the United States absent. European officials will join this week's US-brokered Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi after the Kremlin said they had no place there. The corporate regulator found unlicensed telemarketers pushed Australians to shift about $678 million, mostly superannuation, into riskier schemes.

## World

### COP31 opens in Antalya with Australia running the negotiations
Delegates from close to 190 countries meet in Turkey from today until 20 November. Turkey hosts, but Australia presides over the formal talks under a compromise struck after both countries bid for the job. Climate minister Chris Bowen says he wants an outcome "that people can see is real". The US, having left the Paris agreement, is absent.
[source](https://www.dw.com/en/as-cop31-antalya-kicks-off-can-world-move-beyond-big-promises/a-79661366) · [source](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/nov/08/cop31-climate-summit-deliver-progress-real-host-says)

### Europe will join the Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi
France's foreign minister said on Sunday that European officials will attend Tuesday and Wednesday's US-brokered meeting, days after the Kremlin ruled out any European role. It is the fourth round under Trump and the first since February. Zelenskyy said he has not been shown the agreement Trump has referred to, and likened it to Yalta.
[source](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/08/nx-s1-6034014/europe-join-us-brokered-ukraine-talks-abu-dhabi-russia)

### The US struck a cargo ship that had visited an Iranian port
US Central Command said an aircraft hit the stern of the M/V Huda with precision munitions after its crew ignored repeated warnings and tried to leave regional waters; the UAE said 22 people were rescued. The blockade is meant to force Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, which carried a fifth of world oil and gas before February.
[source](https://www.dw.com/en/iran-us-strikes-ship-after-hormuz-blockade-breach/a-79890434)

### Saudi strikes killed 33 in Yemen, and Houthi missiles reached Riyadh
Houthi-run media said Saturday's strikes on al-Shariyah, east of Sanaa, killed 33 people including women and children; the Saudi-led coalition has not commented. Saudi Arabia says it intercepted missiles over Riyadh on Sunday, with falling debris damaging an Aramco building. The UN counts more than 1,600 dead and 300,000 displaced since the fighting resumed.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c65yn1v99ypmo)

### Iran executed three men over January's protests
State media said the three were hanged after the Supreme Court upheld convictions for arson and armed attacks in Karaj on behalf of Israel and the US. The UN human rights chief said in August that at least 56 people had been executed on national security charges since 19 March, 27 tied to the protests.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckly0ex633ydo)

## Australia

### Telemarketers moved $678 million of savings into riskier schemes
ASIC's review of 15 licensees from January 2025 to June 2026 found unlicensed lead generators brought in 7,639 clients, about 35% of all new clients, and $678 million switched or invested — roughly 90% of it superannuation. Clients paid $9.8 million in up-front fees. Cold-call switching is due to be banned under announced reforms.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-09/asic-warns-firms-using-lead-generators-to-push-super-switching/107356376)

## Sydney

### The Cooks River AI data centre has been dropped
Stockland and John Boyd Properties have withdrawn the data centre from their $3.5 billion logistics plan for the former Kogarah Golf Club site at Arncliffe, after a residents' campaign over the river's ecology and the project's power and water use. The rest of the precinct proceeds, with a foreshore link and a 14-hectare park.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-08/sydney-ai-data-centre-scrapped-after-community-backlash/107360592)

### Victoria Barracks will stay in public hands
The federal government pulled four heritage defence sites from its $3 billion selloff: Victoria Barracks in Sydney and Melbourne, Fort Queenscliff and RAAF Base Point Cook. Defence will still move out, with the historic parts kept for public use and some commercial use allowed. Veterans, John Howard and Paul Keating had opposed the sale.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/08/victoria-barracks-sydney-and-melbourne-remain-in-public-hands-backtrack-defence-property-sale-albanese-marles)
