# Wed 21 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 24° / 15°, shower or two, 60% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Drone strikes attributed to Sudan's army killed around 300 people in two days, including mourners travelling to a funeral. Iran has hit at least 15 container ships in the Strait of Hormuz in ten days, pushing Brent crude close to $108 a barrel. Volodymyr Zelenskyy says the United States has restored advance warnings of Russian missile strikes after talks in Oslo.

## World

### Sudanese army drones killed hundreds in Kordofan and Abyei
A military source told the BBC the army hit a market in Abyei on Monday, calling it an RSF fuel site; the region's information minister put the dead at 153. The Sudan War Observatory counted 150 killed in West Kordofan on Sunday, including mourners on a truck. Earlier reports gave lower tolls. South Sudan has summoned Sudan's ambassador.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr3el70jz1nqo)

### Iran's attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz have pushed oil near $108
At least 15 vessels have been hit in ten days — by drones, by mines, and in one case by a rocket-propelled grenade fired from a small boat near Saudi Arabia's Jubail. Brent traded close to $108 a barrel on Monday. The attacks appear to have firmed Tehran's disputed control of the waterway.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/19/strait-of-hormuz-spilt-cargo-iran-attacks-container-ships)

### A Houthi strike on a Yemeni training camp killed 73, and Britain is sending jets to Saudi Arabia
Shabwa province first reported 53 dead at the al-Alam security forces camp, then raised it to 73. The Houthis said they targeted Saudi-run forces. Britain will fly RAF Typhoons defensively over Saudi cities and infrastructure, with no strikes inside Yemen. The Houthis have also re-encircled Taiz; the WFP says 800,000 people, mostly there, are cut off from aid.
[Yemen](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/20/yemens-taiz-faces-deadly-siege-as-shabwah-death-toll-rises) · [the camp strike](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv0ld6lrr2zeo)

### Albertans voted about three to one to stay in Canada
CBC projects roughly 75-25 against starting the legal process for a binding separation referendum. Prime Minister Mark Carney and Premier Danielle Smith, who opposed separation, both welcomed the result. The nine other ballot questions, on immigration and constitutional change, are closer and mostly tracking yes; Smith said she needed those to bargain with Ottawa.
[source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/livestory/alberta-referendum-results-live-updates-separatism-october-19-2026-9.7387777)

### Washington has restored missile warnings to Ukraine, Zelenskyy says
After meeting Steve Witkoff in Oslo, Zelenskyy said advance warnings of ballistic strikes, apparently suspended last week, are working again. He offered to halt long-range attacks on Russian refineries if the US releases Patriot interceptors that Europe has paid for and the Pentagon is holding. Marco Rubio denies anything is being deliberately withheld.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/20/us-has-resumed-sharing-intelligence-with-ukraine-about-russian-strikes-says-zelenskyy)

## Australia

### Helen Haines has lost a leg and her alleged attacker faces three charges
Victoria Police charged Brendan Nugent, 43, with dangerous driving causing serious injury, intentionally causing serious injury, and the federal offence of causing harm to a Commonwealth official, which carries ten years. Police will allege the incident was targeted. Haines, 65, was pinned between two cars; doctors are trying to save her other leg.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-20/brendan-nugent-charged-over-attack-against-helen-haines/107288126)

## Sydney

### NSW will legislate around a High Court ruling against a Hunter coalmine
The court found planning authorities failed to consider conditions limiting greenhouse emissions, including Scope 3, from Mount Pleasant's expansion to 21 million tonnes a year. Chris Minns says the government will underwrite the higher output anyway. Former Supreme Court judge Anthony Whealy called that reprehensible and wants an independent review; the mine is separately under investigation over production limits.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-21/probe-into-nsw-coalmine-at-the-centre-of-climate-court-battle/107268146)

### Inner West buses get 190 extra weekly trips to feed the Southwest Metro
Route 413, Campsie to the city via Summer Hill and Annandale, gains 173 trips a week; off-peak and weekend services move from every 30 minutes to every 20, and Sunday buses run until about midnight. Route 418, Burwood to Sydenham, gains 17 and will run hourly through Friday and Saturday nights.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/inner-west-gets-190-new-bus-trips-to-complement-southwest-metro)
