# Wed 28 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 14°, partly cloudy, 10% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Israeli exit polls point to deadlock, with neither Benjamin Netanyahu nor Gadi Eisenkot taking a majority on the highest turnout in 50 years. A truce is holding in the besieged Ukrainian city of Oleshky, with an evacuation planned for Friday. About $2.4 trillion has come off China's stock markets since May as profits from its AI build-out fail to arrive.

## World

### Israel's exit polls project no outright winner
Turnout reached 66.7% by 6pm, against 57.7% at the same point in 2022, and was described as the highest in 50 years. After polls closed at 10pm, TV exit polls gave neither Netanyahu's coalition nor Gadi Eisenkot's opposition alliance a clear majority. Official results take days; forming a government can take weeks.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/10/27/israel-election-results-live-exit-polls-soon-as-netanyahu-faces-tight-race)

### A truce has begun in the Russian-occupied Ukrainian city of Oleshky
Ukrainian officials and Russia's defence ministry both say the limited ceasefire is being respected. It runs to late on 1 November and is meant to let aid in and allow an evacuation on Friday of up to 6,000 people, including 200 children, from a city with no power, gas or water for three years.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck20wjnv3w0jo)

### Jordan bombed southern Syria and Syria shut their only crossing
Jordan's military says its jets destroyed "terrorist sites" in retaliation for the Karak church shooting that killed nine, and that it coordinated with Damascus. Syria's foreign ministry says there was no prior coordination and that seven people died, three of them children. Syrian customs closed the Nassib crossing to all traffic.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/27/why-jordan-has-struck-syria-what-we-know)

### About $2.4 trillion has been wiped off China's stock markets since May
Shanghai's STAR 50 index of technology companies is down 40% since June, on Bloomberg's figures, with other major indexes off about 20%. Goldman Sachs puts AI stocks at over 40% of Chinese and Hong Kong market value, so thin profits and US bans on Chinese technology drag the whole market.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/27/chinese-markets-lose-trillions-ai-race-beijing)

### The US, UK, Canada, Norway and EU states warn Sudan is heading into famine
Their joint ministerial statement, dated 23 October, says 19.5 million people — 41% of the population — face high levels of acute food insecurity, five million of them at emergency levels, and that the projected 825,000 children with severe acute malnutrition could be exceeded. They want air access into Darfur.
[source](https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/joint-ministerial-statement-risk-famine-sudan-2026-10-23_en)

## Australia

### Centrelink's automated compliance system has restarted
The mutual-obligation framework was paused in July 2024 after the Commonwealth Ombudsman found 964 payment cancellations between April 2022 and July 2024 were unlawful. The department says IT and training fixes are done; advocates say the underlying problems are not. It is inviting 50,652 people to seek compensation, having paid $870,000 to more than 600.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-28/centrelink-jobseeker-compliance-regime-restarts/107313184)

## Sydney

### NSW Labor has promised $6.8 billion for the South Coast line if re-elected
The plan would electrify Kiama to Bomaderry, add tracks between Wollongong and Kiama, and quadruple the Hurstville–Sutherland section that T4 Illawarra passengers share with freight. Detailed planning starts in 2027, with construction finishing in the mid-to-late 2030s. A separate $621 million of track, power and drainage work is already underway.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/south-coast-rail-line-to-be-electrified-and-duplicated-largest-investment-rail-infrastructure-generations)

### NRMA Insurance was fined for overcharging NSW drivers on green slips
It applied the wrong geographic risk rating when customers changed address, overcharging an estimated 41,564 policies by about $12.17 million. The State Insurance Regulatory Authority imposed a $479,500 penalty for that and seven other breaches. NRMA Insurance, which found the error itself, is repaying affected customers with interest.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-28/sira-fines-nrma-insurance-for-overcharging-customers-green-slips/107315524)
