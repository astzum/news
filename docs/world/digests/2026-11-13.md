# Fri 13 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 18°, shower or two, 70% chance of rain, with a possible thunderstorm late in the day. Bus drivers across the eastern suburbs, northern beaches, lower north shore and inner north-west stop work 6.30–7.30am and 5–6pm today.

**The three that matter:** Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's chief executive, effective immediately, days after the board opened an independent investigation into him. Vladimir Putin told US envoys in Moscow that European troops in Ukraine after any ceasefire would be legitimate targets. A Democrat has won Mississippi's Senate seat, taking the party to 55 of 100.

## World

### Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive
The board said he quit as president, CEO and director on Thursday. Chief financial officer Colette Kress becomes interim chief executive while a search runs, and Harvey Jones becomes the company's first board chair. Two days earlier the independent directors hired outside counsel to investigate Huang, who has acknowledged an affair with a Chinese business associate.
[source](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)

### Putin ruled out European troops in Ukraine as US envoys briefed him
Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff met him for about two hours after two days of talks in Abu Dhabi, then flew to Dublin to see Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Putin said post-ceasefire European deployments would be "absolutely unacceptable" and treated as targets, and that a deal must cover all four regions Russia annexed in 2022.
[source](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6038809/us-envoys-meet-putin-russia-ukraine-war-talks)

### An Israeli strike hit the surgical wing of Gaza's Nasser Hospital
Six people died, including a general surgeon and a 13-year-old boy, and at least 28 were wounded, Gaza's health ministry said. The Israeli military said it was targeting a Hamas commander and other fighters; the commander it named was not among the dead. Nasser is one of the few hospitals still operating in Gaza.
[source](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6038339/israeli-strike-gaza-hospital)

### A Democrat won Mississippi's Senate seat, taking the party to 55 of 100
Scott Colom beat Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith 49.2% to 48.7%, a margin of fewer than 5,000 votes in a state Trump carried by more than 22 points in 2024. He is the first Black senator from Mississippi since Blanche Bruce left office in 1881. An Alaska race is still uncalled.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw7vppyr80eeo)

### MSF says Ebola money is not reaching DR Congo's front line
The charity reports unpaid and untrained responders, shortages of supplies, ambulances out of fuel and surveillance teams that "exist on paper". Africa CDC said last month that $700m of $962m disbursed was unaccounted for. More than 5,400 people have died since May, the second-deadliest Ebola outbreak recorded.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx98757ymm6zo)

## Australia

### A federal centre for online counterterrorism opened in Sydney
The $76 million Counter Terrorism Online Centre, funded in the May budget, puts the AFP and ASIO together to identify and disrupt radicalisation before it leads to violence. Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke said extremists are recruiting through gaming platforms such as Discord and Roblox. Children are 40% of this year's counterterrorism caseload.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-13/sydney-counterterrorism-hub-targets-youth-extremism/107380242)

## Sydney

### The airport metro line still has no opening date
A two-year dispute between the government and Parklife Metro, the Webuild-led consortium building and operating the $12 billion-plus line, remains unresolved, and the 23-kilometre line may not open until well into 2028. Transport Minister John Graham said his direction was to protect taxpayer money. Free buses run between the airport and St Marys.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/new-airport-flyers-face-long-wait-for-metro-train-line-s-opening-due-to-dispute-20261113-p61civ.html)

### NSW will put full-time security in 27 hospitals
The government is recruiting 129 extra full-time-equivalent security staff as part of a $113 million package it says follows work with unions and the health workforce. It has added 180 hospital security staff since 2023, a 13 per cent increase.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/129-additional-frontline-security-staff-for-27-nsw-hospitals)
