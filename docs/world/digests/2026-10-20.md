# Tue 20 Oct 2026

## World

### France imposes partial curfews after its most violent protests in years
Interior minister Laurent Nuñez conceded police "lost control" on Saturday, when 500,000 protested against an expected 200,000. Justice minister Gérald Darmanin put arrests at 2,513, with roughly 2,000 shops looted and nearly 1,000 police injured. Curfews now cover parts of Paris and four northern cities; unions have called fresh strikes for 5 November.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/19/french-police-lost-control-during-riots-concedes-interior-minister)

### Trump suspends Russia sanctions as Ukraine says US warnings are failing
Trump blocked sanctions Congress passed in September two days before they took effect; Marco Rubio called it a 60-day review of their economic impact. A Zelensky adviser, Serhii Beskrestnov, told the BBC that incomplete US missile warnings have cost lives and that Starlink restrictions continue. The White House denies changing intelligence sharing.

### China's economy grew 4.5 per cent in the third quarter on export strength
September exports rose 29.6 per cent year-on-year in yuan terms and industrial output 5.5 per cent. Domestic demand did not follow: retail sales rose 1.1 per cent, fixed-asset investment fell 7.2 per cent and property investment 20.6 per cent. Nine-month growth of 4.6 per cent keeps Beijing on target without new stimulus.
[source](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202610/t20261019_1965619.html)

### The World Food Programme has cut Gaza food aid for want of funding
The agency says it is now reaching 1.1 million people, down from 1.6 million in September, halving rations to one food parcel per family. Daily hot meals fall from 250,000 to 150,000. WFP says its 2026 Palestine funding is less than half earlier years' and it needs $450 million through March.
[source](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/10/1168622)

### Albertans are voting on whether to open a path out of Canada
The tenth question on the ballot asks whether the province should begin the constitutional process for a binding separation referendum. Elections Alberta says 1.3 million people voted early, in a province of about five million. Polls close 1pm Sydney time; counting is by hand and the separation question goes first.
[source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/calgary/alberta-referendum-where-when-to-vote-9.7384053)

## Australia

### Independent MP Helen Haines is critically injured after being struck by a car
Haines was hit outside her Wangaratta electorate office and is in a Melbourne hospital. A 43-year-old man was arrested at the scene; counter-terrorism police are investigating. Anthony Albanese called it a "deliberate act"; Victoria Police say intent is yet to be determined. The health minister said credible threats against MPs now exceed 1,000, double five years ago.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-20/live-blog-victoria-indi-mp-helen-haines-attacked-wangaratta/107285252)

## Sydney

### Western Sydney International opens to passengers on Sunday without its rail link
The curfew-free airport takes its first flights on 25 October, ending Kingsford Smith's monopoly in the Sydney basin. The 23km metro line to St Marys is delayed to at least December 2027, so public transport means free shuttle buses every 30 minutes, about half an hour each way. Long-stay parking starts at $15 a day.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/traveller-s-guide-to-sydney-s-new-24-hour-international-airport-20261020-p5zxzy.html)

### NSW Health warns of a sharp rise in hospital visits linked to unapproved peptides
Emergency departments recorded 1,180 presentations associated with peptide use between July 2025 and September 2026, rising from five a month to more than 217 in September. Products sold online as BPC-157, TB-500 or retatrutide are not approved in Australia for any purpose; clinicians have reported kidney and liver injury.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/nsw-health-warns-of-surge-hospital-presentations-linked-to-peptide-use)
