# Fri 23 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 17°, shower or two, 70% chance of rain. Maroubra and Coogee beaches are closed until further notice after a whale carcass washed into Lurline Bay, with warnings of increased shark activity.

**The three that matter:** Brent crude hit a five-month high after Houthi attacks inside Saudi Arabia and strikes on tankers near the strait of Hormuz. Jim Chalmers says December's midyear budget update will downgrade growth and raise unemployment, with markets now pricing a rate rise in under two weeks. Brazil's presidential run-off is on Sunday and both campaigns are in court accusing each other, and Meta, of tilting the vote.

## World

### Oil hit a five-month high after attacks on Saudi targets and Gulf shipping
Brent rose 3% on Thursday to $112 a barrel, its highest since 18 May. Houthi forces struck a civilian airport in Dammam, killing two, and a Saudi oilfield. Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it hit four vessels doing "illegal" oil transfers and that three more struck mines near Hormuz. The US has ordered some diplomats out of Saudi Arabia.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/22/oil-prices-jump-five-month-high-threats-supply)

### France has banned Saturday's protests in Marseille and Lyon
Macron said only authorised union marches may proceed; elsewhere they will be banned "wherever possible". Paris extends its overnight curfew around Place de la République and Les Halles to Monday, and the Louvre, Orsay and Picasso museums shut on Saturday. The justice minister said 2,700 people have been arrested since last weekend's riots and 217 jailed in fast-track trials.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvde8g40xx42o)

### Brazil's run-off has gone to court over Meta's handling of the campaigns
Lula's campaign asked the electoral court to disqualify Flávio Bolsonaro, alleging Meta throttled content to favour him; Bolsonaro filed a counter-petition over mass WhatsApp messaging of government programmes. UN experts said Meta must ensure a level playing field. Meta denies treating the sides unequally. Bolsonaro narrowly led the 4 October first round and polls show Sunday close.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6pwgve2lk5go)

### The WHO says the Ebola outbreak now spans three countries
Nigeria's first case, confirmed Tuesday, makes it the fourth African country hit by the Bundibugyo strain that emerged in DR Congo in mid-May and has killed more than 4,000 people there. The WHO's Africa director called it a "more complex outbreak landscape". The source of the Nigerian infection is still unknown; Uganda rejects suggestions it was infected there.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/22/who-warns-more-complex-ebola-outbreak-now-present-in-3-african-countries)

## Australia

### Snowy 2.0 will cost up to $25 billion and finish in late 2029
Snowy Hydro published its contractor's reassessment: a $22.2b target with an allowance up to $25b, against the $12b set at the 2023 reset and the $2b first budgeted in 2017 for a 2021 start. Tunnelling is 65% complete. The company says most of it is loans Snowy must repay, not grants.
[source](https://www.snowyhydro.com.au/news/snowy-2-0-cost-reassessment-complete/)

### Treasury will downgrade growth and lift unemployment in December
Chalmers said in a speech this morning that Treasury is revising all its midyear numbers, and that higher rates and inflation are "likely to mean softer growth and slightly higher unemployment". Unemployment reached 4.7% in September, a near five-year high. ANZ puts the chance of a Melbourne Cup day rate rise at close to 60%.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/22/jim-chalmers-economy-speech-rising-unemployment-inflation)

## Sydney

### Western Sydney International opens to passengers on Sunday
The first commercial flight, a Jetstar service, leaves on Sunday, with seven airlines signed up. The airport is named for Nancy-Bird Walton, the first Australian woman to hold a commercial pilot's licence, and a tribute to her sits above the domestic gates. It opens on a promise to ease congestion at Kingsford Smith and add competition on fares.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-23/nancy-bird-walton-western-sydney-international-airport/107289382)

### NSW has taken some government websites offline to patch a PHP flaw
Cyber Security NSW is working with agencies to apply updates after a global vulnerability in the PHP scripting language, and some sites are down as a precaution. No NSW agency has reported a data breach. The minister urged businesses and councils to follow Australian Cyber Security Centre guidance and patch their own systems.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/update-on-php-response)
