# Sun 22 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 31° / 21°, partly cloudy with a slight chance of an afternoon shower, 20% chance of rain. BOM has a severe heatwave warning covering the Sydney metropolitan district, and says conditions peak today and ease on Monday behind a southerly change.

**The three that matter:** Two tankers were struck in the Strait of Hormuz, part of a sharp rise in attacks as Iran asserts control over a waterway that carried nearly a fifth of the world's crude. The COP31 climate summit in Antalya ran more than 36 hours past its deadline, deadlocked over whether the final text mentions fossil fuels at all. UN talks on a binding treaty for autonomous weapons collapsed in Geneva after all-night negotiations.

## World

### Two tankers were struck in the Strait of Hormuz
UKMTO, Britain's shipping-threat monitor, reported both hits near Khasab, Oman, on Saturday, with no casualties. Iran's navy said separately that a tanker caught fire after hitting a mine in a restricted area; that account is unverified. Al Jazeera's Tehran correspondent counted about 25 incidents in a week. The strait carried nearly a fifth of world crude before the war.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/tankers-hit-in-strait-of-hormuz-as-iran-tightens-grip-on-shipping)

### COP31 ran more than 36 hours past its deadline without a deal
The EU, Pacific island states and Panama are refusing a draft that drops any mention of cutting fossil fuel demand; the Africa Group wants adaptation money, and a $120bn-by-2035 figure was removed. Australia's Chris Bowen, leading negotiations, said the summit is about implementation. Delegates rebooked flights to Monday. A COP has been suspended without a deal only once since 1995.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6ly5w40eg07o)

### UN talks on a binding treaty for autonomous weapons collapsed
After all-night negotiations, the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons adopted only a mandate to "elaborate by consensus" an instrument that may carry no legal obligations. Diplomats said Russia filibustered; the US, UK, France and Germany also resisted binding rules. More than 100 states wanted a treaty. The forum requires unanimity among its 128 parties.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/international-talks-control-ai-autonomous-weapons-collapse)

### A Russian cargo ship was hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland
The Sparta II, bound for Kaliningrad and under US sanctions since 2022, caught fire and drifted into Finnish waters. Finland's defence forces said Ukraine was likely responsible, and a Russian helicopter is suspected of then violating Finnish airspace. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said war "is spilling over into our immediate vicinity", while adding Ukraine may fight back.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj752ddve27ko)

## Australia

### David Jones issued redundancy notices to 1,689 staff
Employment ends on 30 December, with about 10% offered redeployment, after the chain entered voluntary administration and receivership this month and said it would shut 19 stores in Australia and New Zealand. Seven NSW closures account for 641 of the jobs. The retail union says one member already cannot access accrued entitlements.
[SBS News](https://www.sbs.com.au/news/article/nearly-1-700-david-jones-workers-to-lose-their-jobs-after-christmas/3dy8d04v9)

## Sydney

### Victoria Pass is on track to reopen between April and June
Contractors have finished driving 54 piles for a new bridge over Mitchell's Causeway, the convict-built stone structure whose cracking shut the Great Western Highway in early March. Up to 12,000 vehicles a day have been detouring via Darling Causeway and Chifley Road, adding 25 minutes. Transport for NSW is urging holiday drivers to learn the detour now.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/crucial-work-drives-hope-for-motorists-disrupted-by-shut-blue-mountains-highway-20261122-p61fyw.html)

### A Darlinghurst restaurant strip will close to cars for a month from 4 January
Stanley Street between Crown and Riley streets becomes a pedestrian plaza with outdoor dining and seating until 5 February, a trial the traders behind it hope becomes permanent. The 378 bus will be rerouted along William Street, which some residents object to. Stanley Street is one of the few inner-city strips with falling foot traffic.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/piazza-feel-the-plan-to-make-sydney-s-original-little-italy-car-free-over-summer-20261121-p61d0o.html)

### The NSW government finalised a rezoning for 850 homes at Beverly Hills station
Buildings of 15 to 22 storeys are allowed on about 1.7 hectares around King Georges Road, with roughly 190 retail and commercial jobs and new public spaces. The government said more than half of 500 submissions supported it, and blamed Georges River Council for sitting on an unfunded master plan dating to 2018.
[NSW Government](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/beverly-hills-renewal-to-finally-unlock-hundreds-of-homes-and-jobs)
