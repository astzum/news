# Sat 21 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 29° / 19°, possible shower with a chance of a thunderstorm in the afternoon, 50% chance of rain. No BOM warnings current for Sydney.

**The three that matter:** A Russian cruise missile landed in south-eastern Poland during one of the war's largest attacks on Ukraine, near where eleven countries were holding a military exercise. The UK and EU signed their biggest post-Brexit agreement, scrapping most checks on British food exports and opening 100,000 youth visa places each way. Anthropic's shares fell back towards their listing price as an AI sell-off pushed Wall Street into correction territory.

## World

### A Russian cruise missile landed in Poland during a Western military exercise
Poland says three objects crossed its airspace on Friday morning: a Kh-101 that hit farmland at Nowy Lipowiec, 50km from Ukraine, another suspected missile and a jet drone. Nobody was hurt. Donald Tusk said it was no coincidence they fell near the Koalicja 2026 drill. NATO scrambled jets; Russia has not commented.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqx2x9dklxnro)

### The UK and EU agreed their biggest deal since Brexit
From 2028 most certificates and border checks on food and animal products go, with Britain aligning to EU food-safety rules, and the two emissions-trading systems will link. Up to 100,000 under-30s from each side can live and work in the other for three years. Britain rejoins Erasmus+. No deal yet on EU students' tuition fees.
[Deutsche Welle](https://www.dw.com/en/eu-and-uk-strike-landmark-deal-to-strengthen-ties-after-brexit/a-80001076)

### Cost-of-living protests ran in 25 European countries on the same day
Italy's largest union, CGIL, struck, and tens of thousands marched in more than 100 Italian cities. "Gen Z regelt" organised protests in 73 German towns, with about 4,000 at Berlin's Alexanderplatz. Croatian students boycotted classes; police used pepper spray in Vienna. France's interior ministry counted 146,000 protesters there on Tuesday.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/protests-held-across-europe-over-wages-services-high-living-costs)

### Saudi Arabia and the Houthis started striking each other's ports
The Saudi-led coalition told civilians to leave Yemen's Hodeidah port, then said it had hit capabilities there and missile sites in Sanaa. The Houthis, a day after striking Riyadh's tallest tower, told shipping to leave Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports and warned airlines out of Saudi airspace. Al Jazeera's Sanaa correspondent called it the war's heaviest bombardment.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/saudi-coalition-and-houthis-exchange-threats-to-bomb-key-ports)

### Anthropic's shares fell back near their IPO price as tech stocks sold off
The S&P 500 was down about 2.8% and the Nasdaq more than 4% by mid-afternoon in New York, with both main indexes touching correction territory. Anthropic, which raised a record $81bn on Thursday, dropped as much as 12% to about $85 against an $83 offer price. Nvidia fell more than 11%; Germany's DAX closed down 2.6%.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6808ym10vzgo)

## Australia

### Australia and China agreed to hold talks on artificial intelligence
Albanese and Premier Li Qiang set up an AI dialogue in Beijing on Friday, days after Australia signed a US-led declaration on cutting reliance on China for AI supply chains. Xi told Albanese that morning there had been a "complete turnaround" in relations despite continuing "differences". Albanese lands home after 11 days at COP31, ASEAN and APEC.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-21/australia-strikes-deal-on-ai-talks-with-china-after-apec/107411592)

## Sydney

### A man was charged with terrorism offences after raids across south-west Sydney
Police will allege Nowroz Amin, 35, researched how to build a vehicle-borne explosive device. Arrested at Ingleburn, he is charged with making a document connected with terrorism and possessing violent extremist material. The AFP says he acted alone, no target was identified and he lacked the capability to attack. Bail was refused.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-20/sydney-counter-terror-raids-campbelltown/107407568)

### NSW Health warned that vapes containing an anaesthetic are circulating
Three people lost consciousness and were hospitalised after using non-nicotine refillable vapes; blood and urine tests found etomidate, a fast-acting general anaesthetic not registered for medical use in Australia. Products sold as space pods, kpods or zombie vapes may also contain ketamine, THC or opioids. Victoria found etomidate in an illicit vape four months ago.
[Guardian Australia](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/20/vape-users-hospitalised-after-losing-consciousness-prompting-public-health-warning-in-new-south-wales)
