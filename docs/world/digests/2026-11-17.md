# Tue 17 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 15°, partly cloudy, 20% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands will open a joint deportation centre in Rwanda next year. Russia says it shot down more than 2,000 Ukrainian drones in a day, one of which hit the Crimea bridge. COP31's first draft text calls for an immediate transition away from fossil fuels, and has four days to survive negotiation.

## World

### Five EU states will send rejected migrants to Rwanda
Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands plan a "return hub" open in 2027 for people who have exhausted appeals against deportation. Unlike the scrapped UK scheme, asylum claims will not be processed there. The Dutch government says only single men go in the first phase. Human rights bodies say it may breach migrants' rights.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw987343352go)

### Ukraine's largest drone attack of the war hit the Crimea bridge
Russia's military says it destroyed 2,049 drones and four Flamingo missiles over 24 hours, about 600 of them near Moscow. Drones struck the Kerch bridge and closed traffic; Russian pro-war commentators said falling debris, not a direct hit, caused the damage. Kyiv had a record 17 air raid alerts, seven dead and blackouts for 50,000.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckx27v6dpjz0o)

### COP31's draft deal asks countries to quit fossil fuels immediately
The Antalya summit produced an 18-page text with 144 items on Monday, earlier than expected with four days of talks left. It includes a call to "immediately transition away from fossil fuels" and a 35% electrification target by 2035. Australia's Chris Bowen, presiding, called it an attempt to draw out where countries actually disagree.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/nov/16/cop31-draft-text-draw-out-parties-find-agreement-president-talks-says)

### Iraq ordered troops into a stronghold of Kataib Hezbollah
Baghdad gave the Iran-backed group until Friday to leave Jurf al-Nasr, south of the capital, and told security forces to stop oil tankers leaving the area. It follows an investigation into illicit refining and smuggling, and US and Gulf pressure to curb Tehran's influence. The group denies the allegations.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/iran-deploys-army-to-stronghold-of-iran-backed-kataib-hezbollah)

### UN talks on autonomous weapons opened in Geneva
The Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons review meets once every five years, and campaigners call this the last realistic chance to set rules before weapons can select and kill targets without a human. The US, Russia and Israel resist legally binding limits. The UN secretary general, the Red Cross and the Pope back them.
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/16/nx-s1-6042548/un-killer-robots-ai-weapons-drones)

## Australia

### Australia joined a US-led pact on AI supply chains
Ten countries including Japan, India and the UK signed the San Francisco Declaration, committing to map vulnerable supply chains for critical minerals, energy, data centres and transport, and to guard sensitive technology against theft and interference. It is Washington's counter to China's own offers across South-East Asia. Albanese meets Trump at the Quad leaders' meeting in the Philippines.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-17/australia-signs-us-led-ai-supply-chain-agreement/107392252)

## Sydney

### NSW shelved its underquoting crackdown the day before it started
Rules due Tuesday would have banned agents from advertising below a written offer the seller had already rejected, with fines up to $110,000 or triple commission. Chris Minns paused them for up to a year after agents in his Kogarah electorate said they misfire in a falling market. Sydney values are down 10.1% from their 2026 peak.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/16/nsw-halts-new-law-to-crack-down-on-underquoting-by-real-estate-agents-at-11th-hour)

### Almost one in 10 NSW emergency patients felt threatened in the department
The Bureau of Health Information's 2024-25 survey asked 23,621 patients whether other patients or visitors made them feel unsafe; about 9% said yes. At Royal Prince Alfred and Westmead it was 19%, and 18% at Liverpool and Auburn. Women and patients aged 18 to 34 reported it most often.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/revealed-the-sydney-hospitals-where-one-in-five-patients-don-t-feel-safe-20261116-p61d6s.html)
