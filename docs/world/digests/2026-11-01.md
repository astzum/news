# Sun 01 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 19°, a shower or two, 50% chance of rain. No BOM warnings are current.

**The three that matter:** Iran's supreme leader has ordered his commanders to answer any new attack with a strike anywhere, as reports from Washington say Gulf allies were warned to expect a US-Israeli assault days after Tuesday's midterms. More than 5,000 people have now died in DR Congo's Ebola outbreak, the second largest on record. A NSW police unit carrying military rifles starts patrolling Sydney today.

## World

### Iran ordered its military to answer any new attack without geographic limits
Mojtaba Khamenei told his commanders to meet any attack with an "immediate, impactful, and geographically unrestricted response", and to widen the target list to US and Israeli assets. Reports from Washington say Gulf allies were warned to expect an assault as early as 4 November, after the US midterms. Qatar is mediating in Doha; no US officials attended.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-31/attacks-on-iran-should-be-met-without-restraint-khamenei-orders/107332392)

### More than 5,000 people have died in DR Congo's Ebola outbreak
Government data puts confirmed cases above 10,300 across eight of 26 provinces, with a 48.6% fatality rate. It began in May and is now the second-largest outbreak on record. The epicentre is Ituri, a conflict zone, and there is no vaccine or treatment for the Bundibugyo strain. Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda have cases; health workers have been attacked.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj5ynryyep2yo)

### Riot police were deployed at Poland's Constitutional Court
Justice minister Waldemar Żurek requested that the court's president and vice-president be stripped of immunity and charged over refusing to let five parliament-elected judges sit. The court called the move "a coup d'etat". President Karol Nawrocki turned his plane around mid-flight to Senegal to intervene. A judge he has refused to swear in now has temporary charge.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crz9824d5e88o)

### COP17 agreed to scale up nature protection, but not who pays for it
Governments in Yerevan agreed on Saturday, after an all-night session, to lift conservation policies, and for the first time invited large emerging economies including China and Gulf states to volunteer as donors. Negotiators said anything stronger on widening the donor base would have collapsed the deal. A UN review found the world off track on 22 of 23 targets.
[source](https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/10/31/cop17-delivers-fragile-deal-scaling-up-biodiversity-conservation-armenia-cbd/)

### The US cancelled the G20 family photo after a third of countries refused to appear with Lavrov
Marco Rubio dropped the opening photograph at the foreign ministers' meeting in Atlanta after EU states, the UK and Australia said they would boycott or walk out rather than stand beside Russia's foreign minister. Penny Wong condemned the invasion of Ukraine in front of him. Washington has invited Putin to the leaders' summit at a Trump golf club.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/31/us-cancels-g20-family-photo-after-australia-uk-and-others-refuse-to-appear-with-russia)

## Australia

### The cyclone season opened with modelling pointing to one of the quietest on record
ECMWF's ensemble suggests five to eight named storms in the Australian region this season, against a long-term average of 11, consistent with past El Niño years. BOM no longer issues an official seasonal outlook. Since 1970 three or four systems a year have crossed the coast at cyclone strength, usually not before late December.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-01/bom-australia-cyclone-season-predicted-quiet/107329238)

## Sydney

### Police carrying military rifles start patrolling Sydney today
The Armed Response Command, promised after the Bondi attack, puts about 200 specialist officers in tourist areas, crowded places, major events and places of worship, "highly visible" and around the clock. Jewish groups welcome it. Policing researchers warn of mission creep, of attacks shifting to softer targets, and say long-arm use needs tight rules.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/01/nsw-police-armed-response-command-rifles-ntwnfb)

### Getting to Western Sydney Airport still means a bus
The metro line to the new airport is not due before December 2027, so the Herald's reporters tested the alternatives from Parramatta, Penrith and Campbelltown. The advertised $25 Parramatta "super shuttle" runs twice a day; the practical route is a train to St Marys, then a free shuttle from a stop whose shelter was still being built.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/western-sydney-airport-is-made-for-western-sydney-so-how-does-travel-there-stack-up-we-tested-it-20261031-p619km.html)
