# Sat 14 Nov 2026

## World

### Iran says it struck a US radar in Bahrain; US Central Command denies it was hit
The Revolutionary Guard says it destroyed a US early-warning radar, calling it retaliation for a US airstrike on a communications tower at Sirik overlooking the Strait of Hormuz. It is Iran's first direct attack on a US installation in months. US Central Command says the radar was not destroyed and that it intercepted two Iranian drones.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/13/iran-us-radar-bahrain-sirik-hormuz)

### Europe will send diplomats to the next round of Ukraine talks
European Council president Antonio Costa said after a Dublin summit that the EU plus the UK, France, Germany, Italy and Poland will be represented. The Kremlin says no decision has been made and does not want European states at the table. Chancellor Merz asked Washington for "no surprises" before Trump's expected meeting with Putin in China next week.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/europe-seeks-seat-at-ukraine-peace-talks-but-lacks-unified-voice)

### Germany's parliament voted to let its intelligence services act, not just gather information
The Bundestag passed the overhaul 309 to 190, with 71 abstentions. The BND may now run covert operations abroad, including sabotage; the domestic BfV may disable weapons, block data flows and enter homes covertly with prior authorisation. The government cites Russian, Chinese and Iranian activity; the Left Party says it creates a secret police.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6jre3z03w4go)

### Türkiye and Qatar signed a mutual defence agreement
Erdoğan and Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani signed it in Istanbul, one of five agreements at the 12th Türkiye-Qatar strategic committee. Qatar hosts the largest US air base in the region, and Gulf governments have openly questioned the value of American security guarantees since the Iran war began.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/turkiye-and-qatar-sign-mutual-defence-agreement)

### The US treasury secretary blamed Commerce for Nvidia chips reaching China
Scott Bessent told CNBC that several agencies wanted the firm Megaspeed on the export blacklist "for months" but "Commerce has the pen". Commerce secretary Howard Lutnick will testify to the Senate Banking Committee next month. The fallout from Jensen Huang's ouster is now a fight inside Trump's cabinet over who let advanced chips leak.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqvg2d1eg7zro)

## Australia

### Australia will sign a defence cooperation deal with the Philippines on Sunday
Signing is set for the ASEAN summit in Manila. The ABC reports it expands reciprocal military access and information sharing and builds up the Philippine navy and coastguard. It formalises Australia's role in the four-country grouping with the US and Japan that is pushing back on China's claims in the South China Sea.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-14/major-defence-deal-astralia-philippines-clashes-china/107381694)

## Sydney

### Former Pittwater MP Rory Amon was found guilty of raping a 13-year-old boy
A NSW Supreme Court jury deliberated about four hours over the 2017 offence, which followed a meeting arranged on a dating app. Amon argued he believed the boy was 17. This was a retrial on two counts after a jury was hung on them in March; he was acquitted of eight others. Sentencing is later this month.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-13/ex-nsw-liberal-mp-rory-amon-guilty-sexual-assault-13yo-boy/107384336)

### David Jones will close seven NSW stores
Macquarie Centre, Macarthur Square, Parramatta, Hornsby, Warringah Mall, Green Hills and Wollongong Central are going, part of 19 nationally. Staff were told on Wednesday, a week after the chain collapsed into administration when talks with Westfield operator Scentre Group broke down. Thousands of jobs end after the Christmas trading period, and centre owners have no replacement anchor tenants.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/david-jones-will-close-seven-stores-across-nsw-what-will-replace-them-20261113-p61dqi.html)
