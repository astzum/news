# Thu 05 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 17°, partly cloudy, 20% chance of rain. Bus drivers across the eastern suburbs, northern beaches, lower north shore and inner north-west expand work bans today, so some services may be late or cancelled.

**The three that matter:** Democrats have won both houses of the US Congress, taking control for the rest of Donald Trump's term. Trump says the war with Iran will end "when I say it ends", while conceding it cost Republicans votes. David Jones is expected to appoint voluntary administrators this morning after rent talks with Westfield's owner collapsed.

## World

### Democrats won control of both houses of the US Congress
Projected results hand the party the Senate and the House for the rest of Trump's term. Democrats took a Texas Senate seat and the governorship, and Kansas elected its first Democratic senator in nearly a century. Hakeem Jeffries is in line to become Speaker. Trump said he would accept the results.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmr4y92rgd9po)

### Trump says the war with Iran will end "when I say it ends"
He told CBS the conflict runs on his terms, and told Fox the war and petrol prices hurt Republican candidates. Iran's supreme leader Mojtaba Khamenei said a resumption would have "much wider dimensions". The Revolutionary Guard said four tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz; BBC Verify found no evidence for that.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6d934npl9y8o)

### The WHO cleared trials of two vaccines built for the Ebola strain spreading in DR Congo
The approved Ervebo shot targets the Zaire strain, not the Bundibugyo virus behind an outbreak Africa CDC puts at 10,444 confirmed cases, with a 50% fatality rate and more than 5,300 deaths since May. The Oxford, Serum Institute and Moderna candidates report in early 2027.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/who-approves-trials-for-two-new-ebola-vaccines)

### Hackers raised the temperature inside three French blood stores
France's blood service says defrost thresholds were pushed to "very high levels" overnight on Monday, and alarms let technicians save the stock; it supplies more than 1,900 hospitals. This is the first medical target in a fridge-hacking wave across a dozen European countries. Nobody has claimed responsibility and the EU is coordinating a response.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/04/france-national-blood-service-hack-cyber-attack-europe)

## Australia

### David Jones is expected to appoint voluntary administrators today
Talks with Westfield owner Scentre, landlord to 17 of its stores, collapsed after the 188-year-old retailer stopped paying rent in October and each side rejected the other's restructuring offer. The board met last night, with McGrathNicol expected. MST Marquee analysis flags closures including Westfield Parramatta and Warringah Mall.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/business/companies/talks-on-edge-david-jones-stopped-paying-rent-on-major-stores-20261104-p61bbc.html)

### Scanlon's social cohesion index held at 78, but pessimism about the country rose
The annual survey of more than 10,000 people found 48% pessimistic about Australia's future, up from 43% last year, and 62% saying cohesion has declined. Support for immigration and multiculturalism stayed high. Respondents worried more about AI than climate change or war, and hardship tracked with switching to One Nation.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/04/social-cohesion-report-australia-cost-of-living-economy-immigration-multiculturalism-ai-ntwnfb)

## Sydney

### Bus drivers widen their work bans from today
About 2,000 drivers will refuse to take buses out of depots without full fuel or charge, after Wednesday's ban on driving buses showing dashboard warning lights. Transport for NSW warns of delays and cancellations and told HSC students to allow extra time. The pay dispute with Keolis, Transdev John Holland and Busways is nine months old.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/warning-of-cancelled-delayed-sydney-bus-services-from-industrial-action-20261104-p61azp.html)

### A claimed theft of NSW Health documents is being treated as a major incident
A hacker calling themselves bytetobreach says they took about 70GB, roughly 100,000 documents, through a misconfigured subdomain before crashing the server. Cyber Security NSW says an isolated server was breached and no patient data has been affected. The police cybercrime squad is investigating. The hacker's claim is unverified.
[source](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14396-breached-nsw-police-investigating-major-cyber-security-incident-impacting-nsw-health)
