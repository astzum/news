# Tue 27 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 24° / 14°, a shower or two, 70% chance of rain. All southbound lanes of the M7 were closed at Horsley Park early this morning after a crash.

**The three that matter:** Lula won a fourth term in Brazil, the first president in 40 years to win after losing the first round. Serbia's student protest movement won a parliamentary landslide and Aleksandar Vučić conceded. Israel votes today, with its military publicly denying it is being pushed to strike Iran for political reasons.

## World

### Lula won a fourth term after losing Brazil's first round
He took 51.79% in Sunday's runoff, 4.4 million votes ahead of Flávio Bolsonaro, and is the first president since 1985 to win after trailing in round one. Bolsonaro refuses to recognise the result and wants it annulled over a two-hour voting extension both campaigns had requested. São Paulo's exchange opened 15% down.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckm23y3mg1yyo)

### Serbia's student movement won a landslide and Vučić conceded
The Student List, formed after the Novi Sad station canopy collapse that killed 16, took about 53% to the Serbian Progressive Party's 39% — 141 of 250 seats — on CeSID projections. It is deliberately leaderless and has named no prime minister or cabinet. Vučić conceded late Sunday, ending 14 years in power.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/26/serbia-student-list-preparing-for-talks-on-forming-government-despite-no-clear-leader)

### Israel's military denies being pushed to strike Iran before today's vote
Channel 12 reported that IDF chief of staff Eyal Zamir was at odds with Netanyahu over attacking Iran during the campaign; CNN reported Zamir told his US counterpart that strikes would delay the election. The IDF said its decisions rest "solely on security, professional, objective and national considerations". Defence Minister Israel Katz rejected the reports.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-27/israel-election-military-netanyahu-feud/107312270)

### Algeria will deploy troops abroad for the first time since 1973
148 of Algeria's 174 senators backed sending forces to Niger on Sunday, AFP reported, and the lower house followed. The troops are to protect energy sites in northern Niger, where Algeria plans a gas pipeline, as Niger's junta fights groups tied to al-Qaeda and Islamic State. Numbers and timing are not set.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4g14n1e5wko)

## Australia

### Sunscreen makers would have to hand SPF test results to the regulator
The TGA's proposals, released today, would require brands to test the finished product rather than the base formula, retest it over its shelf life, and give results to the TGA confidentially before sale. It follows Choice testing in which 16 of 20 sunscreens missed their SPF claims. The TGA will not routinely check the data pre-market.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-27/tga-to-overhaul-spf-testing-for-australian-sunscreen-brands/107308980)

## Sydney

### NSW estimates 20,000 endoscopies are overdue and has added $17 million
Wait times were never centrally monitored; NSW Health collected the data after raising the issue with clinicians and patients, and says the estimate is still being validated. The money goes to the most urgent cases first, some already underway. NSW does about 350,000 endoscopies a year, 160,000 of them public.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/nsw-government-to-expedite-overdue-endoscopies)

### Gun buyback bookings open today, with surrenders from 2 November
Eligible NSW licence holders must book through the Gun Safe portal; walk-ins will not be accepted. Six stations take firearms from 2 November — Sutherland, Penrith, Narellan, Grafton, Griffith and Dubbo — with 34 more from 1 December. About 18% of licence holders qualify and up to 274,000 firearms could be returned.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/national-gun-buyback-to-begin-nsw-on-november-2-bookings-to-surrender-open-today)

### Abbotsford ferry wharf is closed until 18 December
Transport for NSW shut it last week on about two weeks' notice to replace the gangway and make repairs, directing passengers to buses on Great North Road and a shuttle. The Sydney Rowing Club next door says customers have already cancelled bookings in what is normally its busiest trading period.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/the-riverside-venue-left-high-and-dry-by-sydney-ferry-wharf-shut-down-20261027-p618er.html)
