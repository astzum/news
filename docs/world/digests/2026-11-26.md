# Thu 26 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 26° / 18°, partly cloudy, slight chance of a shower, 20% chance of rain. No current BOM warnings.

**The three that matter:** The US has proposed new tariffs on 14 of its biggest trading partners, including China and the EU. Australian annual inflation jumped to 4.9%, putting a rate cut out of reach. Regional and interstate trains out of Sydney stop for a week from Friday.

## World

### The US proposed new tariffs on 14 trading partners under Section 301
The Trade Representative's office named China, the EU, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and eight others after an eight-month inquiry into "structural excess capacity". China's proposed 20% rate sits atop first-term duties; negotiated caps hold the EU, Japan and Korea at 15%. The duties start next year, and outlets differ on headline rates until the Federal Register text appears.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/trump-announces-new-trade-tariffs-which-countries-will-they-target)

### Leaked Russian government records put military deaths in Ukraine near 490,000
BBC Russian and Mediazona analysed 11 million Rosstat death records obtained in a hack and counted 490,334 soldiers dead since 2022. Rosstat confirmed the breach but denies protected data leaked; the Kremlin says only the defence ministry may report losses, and it has not done so for years. Experts put the true toll higher.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmx27g533zwyo)

### A Brooklyn jury convicted Huawei of trade secret theft but rejected racketeering
Jurors found the company guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including conspiracy to steal trade secrets, bank fraud and conspiracy to violate US sanctions, after a two-month trial. The acquittal on racketeering means the jury did not accept the 2019 framing of Huawei itself as a criminal enterprise. Huawei says it will pursue further legal action.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmy49ngydqzpo)

### Netanyahu's judges again told prosecutors to reconsider the bribery charge
The Jerusalem District Court panel summoned the attorney general and said conviction in Case 4000 is unlikely — advice it first gave three years ago, but now after the cross-examination that was the state's last chance to prove bribery. The judges also queried a second case and urged a plea deal in a third.
[source](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rje8rbxxfx)

### Iraq's top court gave Baghdad authority over Kurdish border crossings and oilfields
The Federal Supreme Court ruled that federal forces may deploy to all international borders and that the oil ministry can audit fields in the Kurdish region, a day after the prime minister ordered a takeover to curb smuggling. Erbil calls it an assault on its constitutional rights. No timetable was given.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/iraqs-top-court-rules-government-can-take-over-kurdish-borders-oil-fields)

## Australia

### Annual inflation rose to 4.9% in October
The ABS monthly CPI was up from 4.3% in September, and trimmed mean inflation rose to 3.8% from 3.6%. Both sit above the Reserve Bank's 2–3% target band, which puts a near-term rate cut out of reach and revives talk of a rise. Prices rose 0.5% in the month, 0.7% seasonally adjusted.
[source](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/consumer-price-index-australia/oct-2026)

## Sydney

### Regional and interstate trains out of Sydney stop for a week from Friday
Rail, Tram and Bus Union and ASU members will strike after six months of bargaining with the Australian Rail Track Corporation. XPT services to Melbourne and Brisbane, regional routes and the Hunter and Southern Highlands lines are affected, along with pre-Christmas freight. ARTC is seeking to block the action at a hearing today.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-25/train-disruptions-nsw-strike-planned/107425948)

### ICAC found Corrective Services NSW let sexual misconduct continue for decades
Operation Targo, a three-year investigation, found cultural failures, executive dereliction and cronyism allowed one guard to repeatedly rape female colleagues while complaints went nowhere. Commissioner Helen Murrell SC said ICAC twice urged Premier Chris Minns to call a public inquiry and he declined. The agency runs 34 prisons and employs about 11,000 people.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/prison-service-let-rapists-roam-free-bombshell-corruption-report-finds-20261125-p61h4z.html)
