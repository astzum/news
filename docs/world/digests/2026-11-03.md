# Tue 03 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 26° / 18°, showers and a likely thunderstorm, 90% chance of rain. BOM says the storm could be severe, with damaging winds, large hail and heavy rain that may cause flash flooding; no warning is current yet.

**The three that matter:** The Reserve Bank decides on interest rates at 2.30pm today and a fifth rise is widely expected, which would put the cash rate at its highest since 2008. Iran says intensive indirect talks with Washington are underway and oil fell more than 6%, though Donald Trump is still threatening strikes after this week's midterms. Americans vote today in elections Democrats expect to win at least partial control of Congress.

## World

### Russian bombs killed 24 people at a Ukrainian aid distribution point
At least nine 3,000kg aerial bombs hit a school used for aid distribution in Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia, then the spot where people sheltered, regional head Mykhailo Semikin said. Ukraine's emergencies service confirmed 24 dead, including two rescuers and four police officers. Russia's military has not commented. Zelenskyy called it proof Russia "does not want any de-escalation".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cjy56dndlyz0o)

### Iran says talks with the US on reopening the strait of Hormuz are now intensive
Foreign minister Abbas Araghchi said consultations are "very intensive", with Qatar and Pakistan mediating, and Iran repeated it could reopen the strait within two days if Washington ends the war, unfreezes assets and lifts oil sanctions. Oil prices fell more than 6%. Trump told a rally the US may still "give them the hammer".
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-03/iran-negotiations-us-intensive-war/107339208)

### The US administration asked the Supreme Court to set aside 20,000 California ballots
The Justice Department's emergency filing, a day before election day, asks the court to quarantine mail ballots from voters on a Homeland Security list it says are not citizens, until citizenship is confirmed. A district judge and an appeals court both refused. California says the list is unreliable and the ballots can no longer be separated.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/trump-asks-supreme-court-to-block-20000-california-ballots-before-midterms)

### Shop fridges in seven European countries were hacked overnight and heated to 50°C
Retailers in Belgium, Estonia, Ireland, Finland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands and Sweden reported refrigeration failures; Ahold Delhaize, Carrefour Belgium, SuperValu and an Ikea store said it was an attack. It repeats last month's French hack of up to 700 supermarkets. EU food commissioner Christophe Hansen called it a deliberate attempt to disrupt the food supply. Nobody has claimed responsibility.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/02/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-hit-by-second-suspected-cyber-attack)

## Australia

### The RBA decides on rates this afternoon, with a fifth rise expected
A hike would take the cash rate to its highest since 2008. CBA now sees growth slowing to 1% by the middle of next year, half its earlier estimate, blaming oil prices and falling house prices; NAB forecasts unemployment rising from 4.7% to 5.4%. AMP's Shane Oliver puts the recession risk at 45% if the RBA moves.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/02/australia-economy-fifth-interest-rate-hike-looms)

### The High Court starts hearing the challenge to the under-16s social media ban today
Reddit and the Digital Freedom Project argue the ban, in force since December, unreasonably burdens the implied freedom of political communication, and that children evade age checks anyway. Nearly 5 million accounts have been blocked across ten platforms. The Commonwealth says the burden is modest and that the freedom protects only eligible voters. Hearings run two days.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-03/high-court-challenge-to-under-16s-social-media-ban/107336886)

## Sydney

### The Warringah Freeway upgrade finishes within weeks after five years of lane changes
Two switches are left: the reversible lane between Lavender and Ernest streets becomes permanently northbound by early December, then a new High Street on-ramp opens about a week later. Roads minister Jenny Aitchison said 400,000 people use the corridor daily, after 300 lane switches. Budget papers show $2.346bn spent to June, against a $2bn estimate last year.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/after-five-years-motorist-misery-from-sydney-motorway-project-set-to-end-20261102-p61aiv.html)

### NSW ends "price on inquiry" in property advertising from 17 November
Agents must publish a price or range, update online listings within one business day when a guide no longer complies, base estimates on the three most comparable sales, and keep records of how they got there. Penalties for underquoting rise to $110,000 or three times the commission. A statement of information explaining the estimate follows on 8 February 2027.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/start-dates-set-for-minns-labor-governments-new-underquoting-laws)
