# Thu 22 Oct 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 17°, showers, 95% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Africa's health agency says the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak is now the most geographically extensive on record, and it has reached Abuja. Russia has agreed to a three-way technical meeting with Ukraine and the United States, possibly in Abu Dhabi on 10 November. House prices fell in every Australian capital in the September quarter, the first time in 15 years.

## World

### Russia has agreed to a three-way meeting with Ukraine and the US
Putin's aide Ushakov said Moscow is ready "in principle" after Steve Witkoff's St Petersburg visit, and floated 10 November in Abu Dhabi. A US official said a technical-level trilateral was accepted by Kyiv on Tuesday and Moscow on Wednesday. Zelensky says he would take a freeze along the current front line. Putin freed American Stephen Hubbard without a swap.

### Nigeria has recorded its first Ebola case of this outbreak
Africa CDC's Jean Kaseya calls the Bundibugyo outbreak, which began in Congo in May, the most geographically extensive in history: more than 9,500 infected and 4,600 dead, with a fatality rate near 50%. Kenya has nine cases. Abuja is tracing 44 contacts of a woman admitted on 11 October; Nigerian and Ugandan officials give different accounts of her travel.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/21/nigeria-first-ebola-case-africa-outbreak-latest)

### A New Mexico judge has ordered Meta to pay $25bn over Facebook data
Judge Francis Mathew set the maximum $5,000 for each of the 43 million violations of the state's Unfair Practices Act a Santa Fe jury found in September, calculated $219.5bn, then cut it to $25bn, with 15% annual interest. The case dates to Cambridge Analytica's harvesting of 87 million users' data. Meta says it will win on appeal.
[source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/meta-new-mexico-cambridge-analytica-penalty.html)

### France has secured Gulf diesel covering a tenth of its consumption
Macron flew to Riyadh and met Mohammed bin Salman, the first Western leader in Saudi Arabia since the Houthis escalated attacks this month. The Élysée says the kingdom will supply extra diesel for as long as needed; with UAE and Bahrain pledges France now has 295,000 tonnes a month, just over 10% of consumption. Petrol prices helped drive the riots.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/21/macron-makes-surprise-saudi-visit-as-france-secures-extra-diesel-supplies)

### Albania's anti-government protests have run for a week
Student-led demonstrations over university conditions and corruption produced official figures of 295 injured, 186 of them police, before Monday. A police station and government buildings in Tirana have been attacked. Prime Minister Edi Rama, in power since 2013, refuses to resign and blames protesters "radicalised by dark forces in Europe and the US".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmy4xjlpqgd3o)

## Australia

### House prices fell in every capital city for the first time in 15 years
Domain's September quarter data shows a 4.7% fall in combined capital house prices to $1,226,566, the steepest quarterly drop on record. Perth fell 7.4%, Sydney 4.7%, Melbourne 3.3%. Units fell 3.5%, a second straight quarterly decline. Experts quoted by the ABC put it down to rate rises and weaker confidence.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-22/domain-house-price-september-quarter/107289400)

## Sydney

### The Southwest Metro opens on Friday 30 October
The first service leaves at 4:31am, reopening the Sydenham-to-Bankstown corridor after the T3 line closed in September 2024 for conversion. Replacement buses roughly doubled travel times for two years; the work was meant to take 12 months and slipped on industrial action and complexity. It completes the 66km M1 from Tullawong to Bankstown.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-22/nsw-south-west-metro-opening-date-october-30/107294482)

### Almost 300 NSW GPs can now diagnose ADHD and start medication
They have finished the state's endorsed prescriber training, with another 300 starting next month; rural and regional GPs were prioritised. An earlier stage trained 1,675 GPs to continue existing prescriptions. The $11.7m package sits in the 2026-27 budget, and prescriber locations go on Healthdirect next month.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/nearly-300-gps-trained-to-safely-diagnose-and-treat-adhd)
