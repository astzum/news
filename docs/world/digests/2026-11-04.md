# Wed 04 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 17°, a shower or two, 50% chance of rain, with a chance of a thunderstorm this evening. No BOM warnings are current.

**The three that matter:** The Reserve Bank raised the cash rate to 4.85% and no longer expects inflation back inside its target band until late 2027. Donald Trump said the US will end about $4bn a year in military aid to Israel at the end of this year. Americans are voting in the midterms, with polls closing from late this morning Sydney time and control of both houses at stake.

## World

### The US struck Iran's Qeshm Island hours after Vance said a deal was close
US Central Command confirmed limited strikes late Monday on missile batteries it said had been used against US aircraft. Iran's deputy foreign minister, Kazem Gharibabadi, said "we are still in a state of war". Mediators believe it will be clear within two days whether the indirect talks can continue; Iran's security council secretary called Washington's answer to its conditions unconvincing.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/03/us-strikes-irans-qeshm-island)

### Trump says the US will end military aid to Israel at the end of this year
He told Fox News on election morning that the roughly $4bn would stop because Israel "doesn't need it", and would be replaced by arms sales. Netanyahu had proposed tapering aid over a decade. Gadi Eisenkot, who is trying to replace him after last week's election, said learning of it through the media showed Netanyahu's conduct was a danger.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/03/donald-trump-usa-end-military-aid-to-israel-end-year-midterms)

### Trump moved to strip South Africa and Rwanda of duty-free US trade access
He notified Congress that both lose African Growth and Opportunity Act eligibility from 1 January 2027 after an annual review. Trade representative Jamieson Greer cited barriers to US trade and national security concerns; the letter to Cyril Ramaphosa also raised South Africa's ties to governments Washington distrusts. Pretoria says exports under the scheme fell to $1.7bn in 2025.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9j346xg0mkvo)

### Britain and France will rehearse a Ukraine deployment with 2,800 troops in Poland
Exercise Koalicja, from 9 to 22 November, tests how 12 European countries would move, supply and command a reassurance force if the fighting stops. Turkey will lead a maritime mission aimed at clearing Black Sea mines. The force would not fight or monitor a ceasefire, and the US is not contributing. Russia calls such troops legitimate targets.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm3wv085ne6po)

### Greenland's parliament ratified the Arctic security deal with the US and Denmark
The vote was 16–0 with seven abstentions, on an agreement signed in September by Trump, Mette Frederiksen and Jens-Frederik Nielsen. It allows more US bases and an expanded Pituffik Space Base, keeps Greenland under Danish control, and would still apply if the island became independent. Danish MPs debate it this month; the US Senate need not approve it.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/greenland-parliament-approves-security-deal-with-us-denmark)

## Australia

### The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% on a 6–3 vote
The board said inflation is broadly based and revised its forecasts materially higher, citing fuel and commodity prices from the Middle East conflict, tighter domestic capacity and AI-driven technology prices. It now expects inflation to reach the top of the 2–3% band only in late 2027, three quarters later than forecast in August. Three members wanted no change.
[source](https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2026/mr-26-30.html)

## Sydney

### Powerhouse Parramatta opens on Saturday
The $1bn museum is the largest cultural building project in Australia since the Opera House: 30,000 square metres, six storeys, about 3,000 objects across five exhibitions. General admission is free, though the aerospace show Task Eternal costs $20 to $30. A 23-day, $4.5m opening festival follows. Opening-weekend general admission tickets have sold out.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/visitor-s-guide-to-the-new-powerhouse-parramatta-20261103-p618jb.html)

### Six NSW Liberal MPs will not contest the March election
Mark Coure, the shadow roads minister, said on Tuesday he is leaving the southern Sydney seat of Oatley — the third frontbencher to go in under three months, after Alister Henskens and James Griffin. Backbenchers Mark Taylor, Ray Williams and Tanya Davies are also out. Opposition leader Kellie Sloane says she is increasingly confident of winning.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-04/nsw-election-analysis-liberal-mp-exodus-amid-one-nation-surge/107342536)
