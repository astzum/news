## World

### The ICJ found Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
Judges voted nine to six that the 2017 military crackdown in Rakhine was carried out with intent to destroy the Muslim minority — the first such finding against a state in the court's 80-year history. The court estimated about 10,000 people were killed and 392 villages burned in whole or part, and rejected Myanmar's argument that this was counterinsurgency.
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/19/g-s1-154340/un-court-myanmar-genocide-rohingya)

### Trump, Xi and Putin signed a declaration that did not mention Ukraine or Iran
Their 80-minute meeting in Shenzhen produced a statement on UN reform, increasing oil supply, and the line that "a nuclear war cannot be won and must not be fought", already standard P5 language. It also said the International Criminal Court "must not abuse" its jurisdiction; Putin is subject to an ICC arrest warrant. Analysts called the substance thin.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/19/china-russia-us-agree-peace-statement-no-mention-ukraine-iran-wars)

### Europe said it is ready for talks with Russia, and Putin invited it to Moscow
At the Coalition of the Willing summit in Berlin, Friedrich Merz called Moscow's demands unrealistic but said Europe would enter talks on European security after a Ukraine peace deal. Jared Kushner said Russia was now "comfortable" with Article Five-style guarantees for Kyiv. Zelenskyy was unconvinced: "Putin has always said no."
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckzd9nln5e6go)

### The US and Venezuela restored military ties, 11 months after Maduro's capture
Pete Hegseth signed a cooperation agreement in Caracas with the interim government of Delcy Rodríguez, Maduro's former vice-president. Details were not published; Rodríguez cited training, equipment and technology against "shared threats". Hugo Chávez ended military co-operation in 2005. Maduro is in custody in New York on drug-trafficking charges he denies.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmwy8pyqlq5vo)

### Iraq settled its standoff with Kataib Hezbollah by sharing control of its stronghold
Rather than clearing the Iran-backed group out of Jurf al-Sakhar by Friday's deadline, Baghdad will open a joint operations centre there: an army brigade, federal police, an energy police regiment guarding oil pipelines, and two Popular Mobilisation Forces brigades that include Kataib Hezbollah fighters. Iran's Quds Force commander visited Iraq this week to press for a settlement.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/iraq-reaches-compromise-with-kataib-hezbollah-over-armed-group-stronghold)

## Australia

### A storm toppled transmission towers and cut power across the Eyre Peninsula
About 24,000 South Australian properties lost power on Thursday after two towers came down and a third was damaged; more than 10,000 were still out on Friday morning. The premier said the main line may take days. The state has ordered AEMO to find spare generation. Schools are closed and eight hospitals are running on generators mid-harvest.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-20/impact-of-widespread-power-supply-cut-across-eyre-peninsula/107406428)

## Sydney

### Mark Speakman is the eighth of 24 NSW Liberal MPs to quit before March
The former opposition leader told parliament on its final sitting day he will not recontest Cronulla, held since 2011, and warned his party against becoming "Labor lite" or "One Nation lite". Resolve polling for the Herald has the Coalition on 23%, Labor 30% and One Nation 26%. Kellie Sloane has ruled out governing with One Nation.
[Guardian Australia](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/19/mark-speakman-resigns-and-says-liberals-face-existential-threat-as-nsw-exodus-continues)

### The new Bankstown metro section is carrying 18% fewer passengers than forecast
Weekday trips through the south-west stations averaged 7,730 in early November, the first demand figures since the section opened on 30 October. Hurlstone Park and Punchbowl ran 40% below forecast, Belmore 34%, though the morning peak beat the old T3 line by 10%. The final 13 kilometres cost $10 billion.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/it-s-a-fizzer-newest-section-of-sydney-s-30b-metro-misses-target-20261120-p61c21.html)
