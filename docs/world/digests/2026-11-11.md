# Wed 11 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 26° / 16°, shower or two and a possible storm, 60% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** Russia, Ukraine and the United States are negotiating face to face for the first time since February. Spain stops work for a day over housing and prices, three weeks before an election. Canberra will legislate binding safety duties on frontier AI companies and create a regulator to enforce them.

## World

### Ukraine, Russia and the US held their first substantive talks since February
Officials met for four hours in Abu Dhabi and resume on Wednesday. Zelenskyy, who was not there, called the day "constructive and fairly substantive" and said security guarantees were discussed in detail. US officials say they want a "final end" to the war and are drafting a long-term security arrangement between Russia and Europe.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/10/us-officials-russia-ukraine-war-abu-dhabi-talks)

### Spain's first general strike since 2012 starts today
The CCOO and UGT unions called the 24-hour walkout over wages, housing and prices. Transport, schools and health services drop to minimum staffing, and flight disruption is expected. The Bank of Spain estimates the country is 750,000 homes short. A snap election follows on 29 November.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/10/spain-braces-for-nationwide-walkout-as-anger-over-housing-costs-boils-over)

### The Privy Council struck down Trinidad and Tobago's laws against gay sex
Britain's Judicial Committee, still the islands' final court of appeal, unanimously held that the 1986 Sexual Offences Act provisions were not reasonably justifiable. It is the committee's first ruling of this kind, and campaigners expect it to be cited in challenges to similar colonial-era laws elsewhere in the Commonwealth.
[source](https://www.jcpc.uk/cases/jcpc-2025-0095)

### Trump's Board of Peace called Israel's response to Gaza's floods insufficient
Rain flooded tens of thousands of tents, and four people died on Sunday when a sand embankment collapsed. The US-backed body overseeing the Gaza plan said delays in approving shelter and sanitation supplies worsened a foreseeable crisis. Israel's aid coordination agency says it has admitted tents and winter supplies, and that reconstruction awaits Hamas disarmament.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw4g1g4ll5m1o)

### Malaysia moved Najib Razak from prison to house arrest
The former prime minister, jailed over the 1MDB fund from which investigators say $4.5bn was siphoned, went home to Kuala Lumpur on Tuesday night after four years inside. His mansion has been declared a federal prison. Critics say Malaysian law makes no provision for house arrest and that the move undercuts anti-corruption efforts.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxnv0v4yln7do)

## Australia

### Frontier AI companies operating here will face enforceable safety obligations
The government will legislate six duties, including rapid notification of incidents, independent evaluation of models and published safety frameworks, policed by a new AI safety regulator able to impose significant penalties. It follows a rapid review of September's OpenAI agent breach of a Medicare statistics portal, whose 18 recommendations the government accepted.
[source](https://www.pm.gov.au/media/new-australian-ai-safety-and-security-protections)

## Sydney

### Mental health patients waited up to six days in Westmead's emergency department
A doctor at the western Sydney hospital, who asked not to be named, called the situation unprecedented and potentially dangerous. The ABC confirmed 12 involuntarily held patients with acute mental illness had been in the department more than 48 hours on Monday, seven of them beyond 100 hours. The health district cited high demand and apologised.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-10/sydney-westmead-hospital-mental-health-emergency-wait-times/107365646)

### Sydney's eastern cliffs will stop discharging untreated wastewater
Sydney Water has started the final stage of work to divert the Vaucluse, Diamond Bay and Parsley Bay outfalls to Bondi for treatment, through a new underground pumping station at Dover Heights. The government says the untreated discharges end when the project finishes in mid-2028.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/vaucloos-gets-a-much-needed-flush-as-nsw-government-cleans-up-iconic-coastline)
