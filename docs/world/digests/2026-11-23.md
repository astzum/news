# Mon 23 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 32° / 23°, showers with a thunderstorm likely this afternoon and evening, 70% chance of rain. BOM says the storm may be severe, with damaging winds, large hail and heavy rain that could cause flash flooding; the heatwave warning for the metropolitan district has been cancelled.

**The three that matter:** COP31 ended in Antalya without a deal, the first time a UN climate summit's main political agreement has collapsed since Copenhagen in 2009. The EU and Indonesia signed a trade and investment pact that scraps almost all Indonesian tariffs on European goods. Thirty-six workers are missing after a fire at a confectionery factory west of Jakarta.

## World

### COP31 ended without a deal on fossil fuels
Negotiations in Antalya ran into Sunday, the longest UN climate conference on record. The EU, Pacific island states, Panama and the Marshall Islands rejected a final text that never named coal, oil or gas; a first draft mentioned fossil fuels six times. Poorer countries objected to the lack of firm adaptation finance.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqwy06zrd26lo)

### The EU and Indonesia signed a trade and investment pact
Signed on Saturday, the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement removes 98.5% of Indonesian tariffs on EU goods, including 50% duties on cars, and the Commission estimates EU exporters will save over €650 million a year in customs. It opens a market of 283 million people and still needs European Parliament consent.
[European Commission](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/indonesia/eu-indonesia-agreements/factsheets/factsheet-eu-indonesia-comprehensive-economic-partnership-agreement-main-benefits-0_en)

### A Russian navy corvette was sunk in Benghazi harbour
Libya's eastern government said a suspected sea-drone attack hit the ship at dawn on Sunday. Satellite imagery from Vantor showed the Merkury capsized, only its bow above water. Local sources told Al Jazeera it carried weapons and drones, and that Libyans and Russians died, a toll nobody has confirmed. No one claimed the attack.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/suspected-drone-strike-hits-russian-vessel-in-libya)

### Thirty-six workers are missing after an Indonesian factory fire
Fire broke out around 4.30pm Saturday at the Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery plant in Cikande, Banten province, about 50km west of Jakarta. A rescue official said three people died and four were injured; the Banten governor estimated 60 were inside. Firefighters said they arrived to find the building already engulfed.
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/22/fire-confectionary-factory-indonesia-jakarta)

## Australia

### Small businesses will be exempt from Australia's digital duty of care
Communications Minister Anika Wells confirmed the carve-out after business groups said cafés and bookkeepers could not tell whether their websites fell under a regime carrying penalties up to $109.2 million. The bill requires platforms to reduce harm before it occurs and lets users opt out of algorithmic feeds. The Greens are trying to strike a deal this week.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-23/federal-politics-live-blog-parliament-nov-23/107415266)

## Sydney

### A second fire ant nest was found in Sydney, six kilometres from the first
The nest at Auburn sits outside the biosecurity control zone declared around Enfield's Intermodal Logistics Centre, where red imported fire ants turned up in September. It has been destroyed, but the NSW government said experts believe it may have been there about 12 months. Agriculture Minister Tara Moriarty said moving materials is the main way the ants spread.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/second-fire-ants-nest-discovered-in-sydney-may-have-been-there-for-a-year-20261123-p61gch.html)

### A rail worker came within three seconds of being hit by a train at Seven Hills
A Sydney Trains investigation, surfacing in a dispute with unions over expanded duties for infrastructure workers, found three signal electricians and a protection officer crossed live tracks on 4 March as a Lithgow-bound service approached at 95km/h. The driver braked and sounded the horn for five seconds; the train halted 180 metres past the platforms.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/worker-three-seconds-from-being-hit-by-sydney-train-travelling-at-95km-h-20261123-p61c29.html)

### The NSW Tenants' Union says $200 million a year in bond interest is going unused
Chief executive Leo Patterson Ross said the $2.3 billion held in NSW rental bonds could return that much annually if invested through the government's centralised fund, and should pay for enforcement. The $8.4 million Rental Taskforce, set up in 2025 to police landlords and agents under the new rental laws, needs more money, he said.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-23/more-funding-needed-to-equip-nsw-rental-taskforce-says-union/106973144)
