# Mon 02 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 17°, a shower or two, 70% chance of rain, with a possible thunderstorm this afternoon. No BOM warnings are current.

**The three that matter:** Qatar is trying to close a deal that would reopen the strait of Hormuz, and Washington has not yet answered Iran's offer. Ukraine has resumed drone strikes on Russian oil refineries after refusing a White House request to pause them. Australian home values fell for a seventh month and are now dropping faster than in any downturn in 40 years.

## World

### Qatar is trying to broker a deal that would reopen the strait of Hormuz
Indirect US–Iran talks in Doha ran into a third day, centred on releasing more than $6bn in frozen Iranian assets. Iran says it will reopen the strait two days after Washington lifts oil sanctions and the shipping blockade. The UAE's envoy said Gulf states are "braced for the worst". A deputy speaker of Iran's parliament ruled out nuclear talks.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/01/negotiators-braced-for-worst-over-us-iran-talks-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz)

### Ukraine resumed strikes on Russian oil refineries after refusing a US request to stop
Russian state media said drones set fire to the Kstovo refinery in Nizhny Novgorod overnight, after a strike on a Rosneft refinery in Samara on Friday. Ukrainian officials told the Kyiv Independent they never agreed to Jared Kushner's demand for a 30-day pause. Trump has blamed Zelenskyy for higher global fuel prices. The Oleshky ceasefire ended Sunday night.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/01/ukraine-defies-trump-resumes-attacks-russian-oil)

### The UN's 35-year Western Sahara mission has lapsed
The Security Council failed to adopt two drafts before MINURSO's mandate expired on 31 October. A US text backing self-determination, which dropped "referendum" from the mission's name, won 11 votes but was vetoed by Russia; Russia's one-year rollover drew three. Created in 1991, the mission never held the vote it was named for.
[source](https://press.un.org/en/2026/sc16498)

### Germany's cost-of-living protests turned violent in Frankfurt
Marches in dozens of cities organised by Gen Z regelt — against school underfunding, living costs and military service — were followed by looting on Frankfurt's main shopping street and clashes in Berlin, Cologne and Hamburg. Berlin police reported 27 officers injured. Hesse's interior minister blamed about 100 identified left-wing extremists. Federal minister Alexander Dobrindt wants "harsh punishments".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqj34gnd3n30o)

### India's opposition rallied against a voter-roll revision that has cut 130 million names
Tens of thousands filled Ramlila Maidan in Delhi to demand the chief election commissioner resign. The Election Commission says the Special Intensive Revision removes duplicates and people who have died or moved, and that wrongly excluded voters can be restored. The INDIA bloc says it strips legitimate voters to help the BJP, which called the rally a conspiracy.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60qk92dvlwgo)

## Australia

### Home values fell for a seventh month, and the downturn is now the fastest in 40 years
Cotality's index dropped 1.2% in October and is 6.7% below March's peak, about $63,400 off the median dwelling, now $884,855. Capital city values are down 8.1% in seven months, just short of the 2017–19 record of 8.2%. Sales over three months are 23% below a year ago. Research head Gerard Burg expects more falls.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-02/house-prices-fall-in-october-biggest-downturn-40-years-cotality/107325214)

## Sydney

### NSW gun owners can start handing in firearms today
The 12-month national buyback, announced by Anthony Albanese after the Bondi Beach attack, opens at NSW police stations. Only NSW and the ACT have joined. New ownership caps — four firearms for recreational hunters, 10 for farmers and sporting shooters — are not yet enforceable on existing owners, because the government is still writing the regulations and possible exemptions.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-02/what-to-expect-nsw-gun-buyback-begins-for-one-year/107330518)

### Post-Bondi policing in NSW has cost $53.7 million in ten months
FOI data obtained by the Herald shows Operation Shelter's cost rose from $4.1 million when it was set up two years ago to $41.8 million in 2025–26, and to $53.7 million since the December attack. Nearly 97% went on police shifts, overtime and allowances. The Armed Response Command replacing it cost $94.3 million to establish.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/mammoth-cost-of-post-bondi-policing-revealed-20261102-p619xu.html)
