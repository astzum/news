# Wed 25 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 23° / 17°, cloudy, slight chance of a shower, 20% chance of rain.

**The three that matter:** German media report that Russia is preparing to put a nuclear-armed satellite in orbit, and Nato's secretary general says the alliance will defend itself in space. An Israeli drone strike killed the head of Hamas's internal security service in Gaza City. ASIC published 35 allegations of misconduct inside the big four accounting firms' audit practices.

## World

### Nato said it will defend against threats in space after reports of a Russian nuclear satellite
German public broadcasters NDR and WDR and the Süddeutsche Zeitung, citing anonymous military and intelligence sources, reported Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite within 60 days. Mark Rutte said in Berlin the alliance would stay "safe when it comes to the space element". The Kremlin dismissed the reports; Chancellor Merz neither confirmed nor denied them.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9x2er1ry2mzo)

### An Israeli strike killed the head of Hamas's internal security service
Naeem Abu Nahel, in the job since September, was hit by a drone in western Gaza City, Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry said. Israel's prime minister and defence minister confirmed the strike. Health officials said six others died in two earlier strikes, two of them police officers. Israel said it struck "military operatives".
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6rrg7ggq190o)

### Landmine casualties reached their highest level since 2020
Landmine Monitor 2026 recorded 6,929 people killed or wounded by mines and explosive remnants of war in 2025, 10% more than 2024. Civilians were 84% of casualties whose status was known, and children half of those civilians. Myanmar (1,891) and Syria (1,602) led; Syria's total rose as displaced people returned home.
[source](https://the-monitor.org/online-reader/landmine-monitor-2026)

### China opened a corruption investigation into Shanghai's party chief
Chen Jining, 62, is suspected of "severe violations of discipline and laws", Xinhua said — the usual euphemism for corruption. He is the fourth member of the 24-seat Politburo to be investigated since last year, and three have already been removed this term, including two senior figures from the body that runs the armed forces.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/xis-anti-corruption-campaign-targets-shanghai-party-chief-chen-jining)

### The EU will cap tariff-free imports of hybrid cars from 1 December
The European Commission set temporary 200-day quotas while it investigates plug-in and battery hybrid imports. China's tariff-free allowance is about 191,000 vehicles over the period, or 350,000 a year; Japan gets 310,000, Turkey 230,000 and South Korea 105,000, and British-built cars are also covered. The aim is to cut the bloc's trade deficit with China.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/24/eu-cap-chinese-hybrid-ev-imports-tariff-uk)

## Australia

### ASIC found 35 allegations of misconduct in big four audits
Staff at Deloitte, EY, PwC and KPMG alleged falsified work, concealed concerns and possible manipulation of audit findings in work since 2023, the regulator said. It did not say which allegation belonged to which firm, and the firms declined to clarify. ASIC urged sweeping reform as the government weighs new rules.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/24/auditors-accused-of-falsifying-work-as-asic-publishes-shameful-list-of-failures-ntwnfb)

## Sydney

### Sydney Trains is paying extra penalty rates because the new timetable was scrapped
Internal memos reported by the Herald say drivers and guards can claim higher rates from 15 November to 23 January, including for hours they would have worked under the abandoned roster. Chris Minns dropped the timetable last month after a backlash over longer journey times. Sydney Trains would not say what this costs.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/the-cost-to-taxpayers-of-minns-timetable-backflip-extra-pay-for-train-drivers-20261125-p61gk7.html)

### More than 1,500 guns have been handed in under the national buyback in NSW
The government says 1,552 firearms and 101kg of ammunition were surrendered in the scheme's first three weeks, with applications lodged covering 6,646 more. Thirty-four extra police stations start taking bookings from 1 December. The buyback follows the Bondi terror attack and an interim recommendation of the antisemitism royal commission.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/hundreds-of-guns-returned-more-bookings-open-to-participate-national-gun-buyback-nsw)
