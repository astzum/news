# Sun 08 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 19° / 11°, a shower or two, becoming windy, 70% chance of rain. A marine wind warning is current for the NSW coast. Transport for NSW's bus industrial action notice is still in force for the eastern suburbs, northern beaches, lower north shore and inner north-west, so some services may be late or cancelled.

**The three that matter:** Israel's president has given Benjamin Netanyahu's main rival, Gadi Eisenkot, the first chance to form a government. New Zealand will not know the shape of its next government for about three weeks after an election decided by fractions of a per cent. Russia hit Ukraine's power grid with 172 drones and 36 ballistic missiles days before peace talks open in Abu Dhabi.

## World

### Herzog gave Netanyahu's main rival the first chance to form a government
Most parties in the new Knesset recommended Gadi Eisenkot, the former military chief who leads the centre-right Yashar party. Herzog will make the offer formally at 2am AEDT Monday, starting a 28-day clock. Eisenkot's numbers depend on either the Arab-Israeli parties, which hold a record 19 seats, or the ultra-Orthodox parties from Netanyahu's old coalition.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-08/israel-president-prime-minister-government-picked-eisenkot/107360472)

### New Zealand's election is too close to call for another three weeks
National (42 seats), Act (13) and New Zealand First (12) hold a one-seat majority on preliminary counts, but official results are not due until 27 November. Special votes, 21% of the total in 2023 and historically favourable to the left, are still to be counted. National finished 0.02% ahead of Labour on the party vote.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/07/new-zealand-election-too-close-to-call-vote-count)

### A gunman killed six people in Krakow's main square
Police say a 25-year-old Polish man fired at random from a car in the Main Market Square on Saturday afternoon and was shot and arrested within three minutes. Four women and two men died, including a foreign national; 16 people were hospitalised, among them two officers, one critical. A minister said early investigations point to far-right motivation.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmd7q15drwgeo)

### Russia struck Ukraine's grid days before talks in Abu Dhabi
Kyiv faced blackouts and water shortages after strikes on substations in five regions, including one linking two nuclear plants to the capital. Five people were injured. Russian, Ukrainian and US negotiators meet Tuesday and Wednesday; Zelenskyy called Jared Kushner's framework "Yalta 2.0" and asked the new US Congress for more support.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/07/blackout-water-shortage-kyiv-russia-strike-ukraine-energy-grid)

### Paraguay's president was impeached, then charged, over a bank collapse
Santiago Peña was charged on Saturday with breach of trust and administration for personal gain after regulators seized Ueno, a digital bank that grew partly on more than $800 million in public funds. Nearly three million people cannot reach their savings. The central bank governor he appointed was also charged; prosecutors are seeking pre-trial detention for both.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/7/paraguays-ousted-president-charged-over-banking-scandal)

## Sydney

### Powerhouse Parramatta opened to the public on Saturday
Thousands went through the roughly $1 billion museum on its first day, across five launch exhibitions that include a First Nations-led show on water with an ankle-deep wading pool on the third floor. It is Western Sydney's first major museum, and it arrives after years of delays and cost blowouts.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/instantly-iconic-the-people-s-verdict-of-sydney-s-new-powerhouse-20261107-p61c95.html)

### A forensic psychiatrist says Corrections should not run inmate mental health care
Kerri Eagle told the ABC that Justice Health should take the lead once a prisoner in crisis is identified, rather than prison officers, who currently write the support plans deciding how at-risk inmates are housed. An ABC investigation found dozens of NSW coronial findings since 2015 identifying failures in that care. Corrections says progress needs a whole-of-government response.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-08/nsw-prisons-failing-inmates-on-mental-health-says-psychiatrist/107302582)
