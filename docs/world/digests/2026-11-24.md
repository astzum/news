## World

### Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang after input from the US government
The board voted unanimously 11 days after Huang resigned over reports he had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. It said it received "input from senior levels of the United States government" and is confident there is no national security risk. An independent committee's investigation continues. Trump said he convinced the board; Nvidia shares rose about 31%.

### Zelensky said Ukraine sank a Russian corvette at a Libyan naval base
Seven maritime drones hit the Merkury, commissioned in 2023, about 2,400km from the front line, he said on X. Nobody had claimed the attack when Libya's eastern government reported it on Sunday. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov declined to comment, saying the defence ministry would speak "if deemed to be appropriate".
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/23/g-s1-155017/ukraine-attack-russian-warship-libya)

### Russia told Estonia it is "on the verge of triggering open war"
Moscow's foreign ministry summoned Estonia's chargé d'affaires to protest the closure of border crossings from 1 December, and accused Tallinn of letting Ukrainian drones cross its airspace, which Estonia denies. Estonia, a NATO and EU member, ordered the six-month closure after alleging Russian services staged an arson attempt on a defence company.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/russia-threatens-hostile-estonia-with-open-war)

### Denmark's top court ruled "ghetto law" evictions were not discriminatory
Seven families lost their appeal over leases terminated in Helsingor in 2022 under a 2018 law covering estates where more than half of residents are "non-Western". The Supreme Court held that category is a neutral criterion, not one of ethnic origin. The law requires 60% of such homes to be let at market rates by 2030.
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/danish-top-courts-ghetto-law-ruling-supports-hardline-immigration-policy)

### India pledged $1.6bn to Nepal on Prime Minister Balen Shah's first Delhi visit
Most is a line of credit for energy projects, with about $400m in grants for reconstruction after September's floods, which killed more than 1,400 people. Nepal puts rebuilding at $4.8bn. The two also signed agreements on trade, hydropower, transport and education, and opened a cross-border train service.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwx2ervemj4ko)

## Australia

### Treasury now expects unemployment to peak above 5%
Jim Chalmers told parliament that if the mid-year forecasts were updated today they would show softer growth, higher inflation and higher unemployment: inflation in the mid-3s through the middle of next year, and growth well below 2% for two years, down from 2.3%. Neither Treasury nor the RBA forecasts a contraction. The update lands next month.
[Treasury](https://ministers.treasury.gov.au/ministers/jim-chalmers-2022/speeches/ministerial-statement-economy-parliament-house-canberra)

## Sydney

### NSW Labor wants developers to pay infrastructure levies later, and councils are resisting
Contributions for parks, roads, drainage and libraries are now paid before construction starts; Labor's election promise would defer them until the occupancy certificate. One expert put the cost to councils at $850 million. The City of Sydney and western Sydney councils object; developers say it will speed up housing. The Coalition has promised a similar change.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/more-box-hills-councils-warn-levy-changes-would-create-suburbs-without-footpaths-20261123-p61f4n.html)

### More than 91,000 NSW child protection reports last year involved family violence
The state's Advocate for Children and Young People tabled the 2025-26 figure in parliament on Friday. It counts calls to the Child Protection Helpline rather than individual children. More than 12,000 children were recorded by police as victims of domestic assault in the year to March, with four child deaths. The report makes nine recommendations.
[ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-24/91000-child-protection-reports-domestic-violence-nsw-in-a-year/107404086)
