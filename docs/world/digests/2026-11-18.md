# Wed 18 Nov 2026

**Sydney today:** 25° / 17°, partly cloudy, no rain expected, easterly winds 15 to 25 km/h in the middle of the day. No BOM warnings current for Sydney.

**The three that matter:** Ukraine brought down a section of the Crimean Bridge, Putin's road and rail link to the occupied peninsula, days before he meets Trump and Xi in Shenzhen. The United States, Mexico and Bolivia have lost their measles elimination status after a year of uninterrupted transmission. Three of nine Reserve Bank board members voted against this month's rate rise, warning the economy may be close to a sharper downturn.

## World

### A section of the Crimean Bridge collapsed after a Ukrainian strike
Zelenskyy said the overnight attack, using cruise missiles and drones, was a seven-stage operation weeks in the making; images released by Ukrainian officials show an arched span in the water. The Kremlin said it would rebuild "better than before". Russia struck Kyiv the same night, closing two Dnipro crossings. The $3.6bn bridge carries road and rail traffic towards the front.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw772jk4rvmxo)

### Trump, Xi and Putin will meet on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen
Trump landed in Manila on Tuesday, met the other Quad leaders, and flies to China for the Asia-Pacific summit, where Xi will host him and Putin for talks the BBC reports will cover nuclear weapons. Trump said he would take a separate one-on-one with Putin "if it's available". The three have not met together before.
[BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crq5vlxxx32no)

### The US, Mexico and Bolivia lost measles elimination status
PAHO's regional review commission found the same measles lineage had circulated uninterrupted for 12 months or more in each country. The Americas have recorded 58,213 confirmed cases and 82 deaths to 13 November, against 12,806 cases in the same period last year. All three must now submit corrective action plans.
[Pan American Health Organization](https://www.paho.org/en/news/17-11-2026-paho-urges-countries-step-efforts-stop-measles-outbreaks-and-move-towards)

### The UK sanctioned eight firms building Israeli settlements
Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband's first package since September's policy shift targets construction and property developers in the occupied West Bank, plus Tzav 9, an activist group the UK says has blockaded aid into Gaza. It lands two days before bids close on the E1 project east of Jerusalem. Israel called it a "hostile act" and promised countermeasures.
[UK Foreign Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-cracks-down-on-companies-building-settlements-in-the-west-bank-and-groups-blocking-aid-access-into-gaza)

### A third US aircraft carrier arrived in the Middle East
Central Command said the USS Theodore Roosevelt reached the region on Monday, joining the George HW Bush and George Washington, 13 destroyers, two cruisers and a littoral combat ship enforcing a blockade of Iranian shipping. The Iran war is in its ninth month and stalemated; Trump has alternated between threatening fresh strikes and calling it "effectively over".
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/16/aircraft-middle-east-trump-military)

## Australia

### Three of nine RBA board members voted against this month's rate rise
Minutes released Tuesday show the cash rate went to 4.85% on a six-to-three vote. The minority wanted more time to judge how 100 basis points of tightening already delivered will land, and said the economy might be near "a materially sharper downturn", citing the half-point rise in unemployment over six months.
[Reserve Bank of Australia](https://www.rba.gov.au/monetary-policy/rba-board-minutes/2026/2026-11-03.html)

## Sydney

### NSW rezoned Orchard Hills for up to 5,900 homes
The finalised Stage 1 plan covers 155 hectares around the future metro station on the Western Sydney Airport line: a town centre, more than 20 hectares of parks, building heights from two to 25 storeys, and an affordable housing contribution of up to 3%. The 2024 draft proposed 11,600 homes before feedback halved the area.
[NSW Government](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/homes-jobs-greenlit-orchard-hills)

### A third Sydney officer was charged over an e-bike collision
A senior constable was charged Tuesday with dangerous driving occasioning death over the March 2025 death of a 48-year-old rider hit by a police car at Waterloo. Two officers were charged last week over a Broadway crash. The Police Association has since directed members not to pursue e-bike or motorcycle riders.
[Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/sydney-constable-charged-over-fatal-e-bike-crash-days-after-union-urged-police-to-stop-pursuits-20261117-p61f0a.html)
