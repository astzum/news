# Fri 06 Nov 2026

## World

### Arab-led parties put Eisenkot within reach of Israel's premiership
The Joint List's 11 MPs told President Isaac Herzog on Thursday they would recommend Gadi Eisenkot be tasked with forming a government, after Ra'am's eight did the same. That takes him to about 67 of 120 Knesset recommendations. Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 seats but lost four ultra-Orthodox votes. Herzog decides within a week.
[source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/joint-list-says-it-will-back-eisenkot-to-form-israels-next-government)

### New Zealanders vote tomorrow, with Māori rights on the ballot
Christopher Luxon's coalition of National, ACT and NZ First has rolled back policies giving Māori — about 18% of the population — special status in public life. ACT wants the Waitangi Tribunal abolished once historical claims are settled, and Luxon said on Monday its role "could be" ended if the coalition is returned.
[source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-06/new-zealand-election-2026-treaty-of-waitangi-and-maori-rights/107346020)

### Hong Kong jailed Joshua Wong for 10 years for foreign collusion
High court judge William Tam called the 30-year-old's conduct "grave in nature": urging foreign powers to sanction China alongside exiled activist Nathan Law. Wong pleaded guilty in September. He is already serving four years and eight months and was due out in January 2027, and is the only person prosecuted twice under the 2020 national security law.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/05/hong-kong-joshua-wong-sentenced-national-security-case)

### Paraguay's president resigned after a bank collapse froze millions of accounts
Santiago Peña quit on Thursday after the lower house voted to pursue an impeachment trial. Ueno Bank, whose owner he held shares in until last year, entered insolvency this week; it says it had three million customers, roughly 60% of Paraguayan adults, and held pensions and public funds. Peña denies impropriety.
[source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6p9g39vln3yo)

### Hoax bomb threats disrupted at least 25 European flights in one day
Thursday's warnings hit Ryanair, Aer Lingus, Wizz Air and others across 16 countries; all landed safely and searches found nothing. At least 33 flights have been affected this week. Emails to Rome and Sofia airports were signed by a Russian far-right group, but investigators say there is still no evidence of a state actor.
[source](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/05/bomb-threats-disrupt-flights-across-europe-and-renew-hybrid-warfare-fears)

## Australia

### A Catholic operator will cut IVF and assisted dying at 16 hospitals it is buying
Calvary Health Care says its code of ethical standards will apply across the hospitals it takes over from the collapsed Healthscope by month's end, also ending elective terminations, gender reassignment surgery and some contraception. At Newcastle Private, the only private maternity hospital between Sydney and Queensland, the ban starts 1 December. NSW Health is reviewing the licence transfer.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/national/nsw/outrageous-women-s-health-services-to-be-cut-incoming-catholic-owner-of-nsw-hospital-confirms-20261105-p61bp1.html)

## Sydney

### A parliamentary inquiry told the state to stop data centres clustering in suburbs
Clusters in Lane Cove and Ryde are landing where spare capacity happens to exist, not where planning intends, the committee found, making 23 recommendations including precinct-based siting. It called the state's approach "entirely backwards": fast-track assessment arrived a year before guidelines, and the Investment Delivery Authority has endorsed at least 15 projects worth $51.89 billion.
[source](https://www.smh.com.au/politics/nsw/tech-bro-concierge-service-state-pressured-to-crack-down-on-data-centre-clusters-20261105-p61bmn.html)

### Nitrous oxide sale restrictions took effect across NSW on Thursday
Retailers, wholesalers and online sellers now face rules on supplying the gas sold recreationally as "nangs". NSW Health can issue warnings, seize stock and impose financial penalties. Food preparation, medical, automotive and industrial supply is unchanged. The government cites severe vitamin B12 deficiency and permanent neurological injury among the harms.
[source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/nitrous-oxide-rules-take-effect)
