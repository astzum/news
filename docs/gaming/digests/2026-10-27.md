# Tue 27 Oct 2026

### Sucker Punch has laid off 24 people a year after Ghost of Yotei
Aftermath saw the staff email: leadership cited "the challenges in the market" and the team needs of an unannounced next project. The studio runs under 200 people, so that is more than a tenth of it. Yotei was Sony's best-selling PS5 game of 2026. Earlier reports said 26; Aftermath has corrected it to 24.
[Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/sucker-punch-layoffs-sony-ghost-of-yotei/)

### Arkane Lyon has bought one more month to find a buyer
Microsoft pushed the French consultation deadline from 13 November into December after the unions asked; 217 jobs hang on it. Le Monde reports "several forms of takeover are being considered", and an internal document schedules the wind-up to begin late 2026 and run into Q1 2027. Microsoft says it wants a partner to finish Marvel's Blade.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/microsoft-extends-consultation-period-for-arkane-lyon-as-it-continues-development-on-marvels-blade)

### Phantom Blade Zero reviewed at 90, with one loud dissent
OpenCritic has it at 90 from 45 critics, 95% recommending, three days before Thursday's PC and PS5 launch. IGN gave it 9/10 for "limitless swagger". Eurogamer's Emma Kent went 3/5, saying balance and quality problems "dim its shine".
[OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/19914/phantom-blade-zero)

### Sweeney says Steam's AI labels exist to drive cancel campaigns
Epic's CEO argues that disclosing generative AI is "forcing developers to write a political statement to be used against them by the hate mob they're helping organize", and no different from making studios list their voting history. His test: should Valve force devs to declare a party? He called blocking AI adoption "a political act".
[Tim Sweeney](https://x.com/TimSweeneyEpic/status/2113639717690638483)

### 227,000 people want Mass Effect saved, and they rented Times Square
The #SaveMassEffect petition opened hours after EA announced BioWare's closure and asks EA either to fund the series properly or license it to "another studio that shares the passion". It never names BioWare. On Monday the organisers ran a 15-second spot at 1560 Broadway, hourly at 35 past.
[change.org](https://www.change.org/p/savemasseffect) · [We are Mass Effect](https://x.com/WeAreMassEffect/status/2114730231214883014)
