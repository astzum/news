# Thu 12 Nov 2026

### Sony cancelled Fairgames after a playtest told it what Concord already had
Executives Sean Decker and John Doyle told Haven staff that retention "fell short of the goals we had set", citing "challenging market conditions in the PvP shooter genre", in an email reviewed by Bloomberg. Sony confirmed no job cuts — Haven shifts to supporting other PlayStation Studios teams. Announced in 2023, the heist shooter was never shown publicly again.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/playstation-cancels-live-service-shooter-fairgames) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/fairgames-playstation-heist-shooter-live-service-cancelled-haven)

### Fortis Games has shut down after its casino backer pulled funding
Up to 440 jobs go at the San Francisco mobile publisher, which Las Vegas Sands financed in full. Co-founder Steve Chiang says the team "weren't able to achieve the goals we set for ourselves". Founded in 2022, Fortis bought three studios and never got a game to global launch; Twilight Towers closes on 16 November.
[PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/fortis-games-shuts-down-as-up-to-440-jobs-are-put-at-risk/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/mobile-publisher-fortis-games-shuts-down-up-to-440-workers-affected)

### Ocarina of Time's remake sold 5.2m in four days
Nintendo's own figure covers 5 to 9 November and includes digital, making it the fastest-selling Nintendo-published Switch 2 game worldwide and across the Americas. Chart trackers will report lower totals, because Nintendo doesn't give them digital splits. A 1998 remake is comfortably the biggest launch the new console has had.
[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-for-nintendo-switch-2-sells-over-5-2-million-units-worldwide-in-first-four-days/)

### PS5 passed 100 million consoles shipped on 10 November
Sony Interactive's own site now carries the number, up from 97.9m at 30 September, alongside 127 million monthly active users. It took nearly six years; PS4 managed it in five years and seven months, at a considerably lower price. Seven consoles have ever reached 100m. GTA 6 arrives on this one next Thursday.
[Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://sonyinteractive.com/en/our-company/)

### Untitled Goose Game's studio disclosed LLM use in Big Walk, and apologised for it
House House told Aftermath that LLMs were used "sparingly" late in development to fix a Unity shadow-culling bug, touching "well under 0.1%" of the code. The disclosure came after 2027 IGF jurors said they would not vote for any game involving generative AI. Shipping without an internal AI policy was "a failure", the studio says.
[Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/big-walk-ai-igf-jurors-controversy/) · [RPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/big-walk-developer-house-house-reveal-genai-use-in-final-stretch-of-development-only-revealing-it-now-as-it-has-become-a-matter-of-public-interest)
