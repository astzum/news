# Sat 21 Nov 2026

### Take-Two says GTA 6 made $3.79 billion on day one
That is the publisher's own estimate of retail sales through the end of Thursday, on top of 32.5m units sold before launch — a bigger opening than any film has managed. No PC date yet, so the figure comes from two consoles alone. For scale, GTA 5 has sold in nearly 235m copies since 2013.
[Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-earns-3-79-billion-in-one-day-as-the-biggest-entertainment-launch-in-history/)

### Valve has shut the Steam Machine 2TB waitlist and won't name a future price
All reservations are filled and the 512GB model is add-to-cart for the first time, but 2TB demand now exceeds what Valve can build, so that page flips to out-of-stock rather than growing the queue. Memory and storage costs are climbing "at unprecedented levels": Valve doesn't know how many it can make, or at what price. Update in January.
[Valve](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/steam_hardware/announcements/detail/687522235410285039) · [RPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/valve-are-pausing-steam-machine-2tb-orders-over-supply-constraints-though-the-512gb-model-is-free-of-its-waitlist)

### The EU's draft Digital Fairness Act would put paid loot boxes off-limits to under-18s
The Commission's 11 November proposal bans paid variable-reward systems and pay-to-win mechanics for minors, gives every player a switch to disable loot boxes, and requires virtual-currency prices to be shown in real money as they are spent. Lawyers reading the draft think "pay-to-progress" is loose enough to catch paid DLC. Parliament and Council still have to agree it.
[Commission proposal](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/0575d59f-554f-4a9e-b54b-e6b717175809_en?filename=Proposal+for+a+Digital+Fairness+Act.pdf) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/how-the-eus-digital-fairness-act-will-impact-game-design-and-why-it-hits-european-indies-particularly-hard)

### Embark apologised for dubbing its own esports casters out with the game's AI voices
A highlight reel from The Finals' Grand Major APAC qualifiers carried in-game AI commentators Scotty and June over matches human casters had actually called. The video was pulled; Embark's Oscar Lundberg said the talent's "work deserved to be included and highlighted, and it wasn't". Caster Proto: "we get replaced by two AI casters who even quote us".
[Embark](https://x.com/Schnobben/status/2122641483165557158) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/major-esports-tournament-replaced-caster-commentary-with-ai-voices-and-it-went-over-very-poorly-2000758538)

### Blizzard says WoW: Forever is its busiest Warcraft in 15 years, on a metric it won't publish
Holly Longdale posted that more people are playing weekly than at any point in WoW in 15 years. Fifteen years back is October 2010, when Activision last announced a subscriber peak of 12m; it stopped reporting subscriber counts in 2015. Treat this as a direction of travel, not a number.
[Blizzard](https://x.com/TheWindstalker/status/2122843024791314913) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/wow-forever-weekly-players-15-years)
