# Sun 22 Nov 2026

### Circana says 61% of active US PS5s were playing GTA 6 on launch day
Its Player Engagement Tracker puts the game in front of 61% of US PS5 players and 56% of Xbox players globally who were on at all on 19 November, with PS5 players averaging 513 minutes each — 8.5 hours in a day. Mat Piscatella was clear that is a share of consoles actually played, not of the install base.
[Mat Piscatella / Circana](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2r4ylvpik22)

### GTA 6 ties the highest Metascore ever recorded while its user score sits at 5.4
Metacritic has it at 99 from 158 critics, level with Ocarina of Time's 1998 record. The user score is "mixed or average", with complaints running from the 30fps console cap and a story centred on Jason and Lucia's relationship to the usual "DEI" grievance. Review bombing, but not only review bombing.
[Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-review-bombed-metacritic-user-score)

### Ubisoft has cut another 166 jobs, in Montreal and Quebec City
Mostly production and support roles, under 4% of the two studios' headcount, on projects recently cancelled or changed — including some at Vantage Studios, the Tencent joint venture that holds Assassin's Creed, Far Cry and Rainbow Six. This follows January's "final" round of cuts, and the closures of Winnipeg, RedLynx, Belgrade and Halifax.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/ubisoft-lays-off-166-staff-in-montreal-and-quebec-studios)

### GTA 6 runs on a 2013 Xbox One, on a metered clock
Microsoft and Rockstar enabled cloud streaming on last-gen Xboxes, and Stephen Totilo got over an hour out of a console discontinued in 2020, with one four-second freeze. The catch landed this month: Game Pass Ultimate now includes 15 cloud hours a month, Premium 10, Essential 5. Circana's 8.5-hour launch-day average would eat most of an Ultimate month.
[Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/gta-vi-xbox-cloud-launch-sales) · [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/09/03/xbox-cloud-gaming-changes/)
