# Mon 02 Nov 2026

### Call of Duty on Switch 2 costs you 32ms of reaction time
Digital Foundry measured multiplayer latency at 81ms docked and around 100ms handheld — 83ms at best, 125ms at worst — against 49ms on PS5, both on wireless pads. Crossplay means that gap is against the same opponents. The port is otherwise fine: 60fps most of the time, DLSS targeting 900p docked and dipping to 450p.
[Eurogamer/Digital Foundry](https://www.eurogamer.net/modern-warfare-4-switch-2-input-lag-digital-foundry)

### itch.io is taking twice as many new game pages a day as it did in August
The storefront posted the graph itself, with one follow-up line: "The number of downloads and purchases has *not* doubled." It did not quantify how much of the surge is generative-AI output, and announced no policy change — the replies are developers asking it to ban the stuff outright.
[itch.io](https://bsky.app/profile/itch.io/post/3mz7vswlnbs23)

### Chronicles: Medieval's studio says its funding is gone and it may close
Raw Power Games told players on Steam that "the necessary funding behind Raw Power Games is no longer in place", without saying whose money it was or why it went. The Copenhagen team's medieval sandbox, with Lars Mikkelsen narrating, was due in early access in early 2027. It says talks are under way.
[Raw Power Games](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2231020/view/1847920302238569)

### Skyblivion's legal threat is coming from a former volunteer, not Bethesda
The Oblivion fan remake says a departed contributor objected to the team decompiling parts of their work after repo access was revoked, then demanded the real names and home addresses of volunteer leads. The team removed the code and rebuilt the system anyway; it says the threats continued. No filed suit is confirmed.
[Skyblivion](https://x.com/TESRSkyblivion/status/2116315667078996211)

### Every Xbox owner gets five hours of Crimson Desert for nothing
Pearl Abyss has put a five-hour trial of the Enhanced edition on Xbox from 29 October to 7 January with no subscription required, progress carrying into a purchase. On PS5 it is a PlayStation Plus Deluxe perk instead. It arrived quietly alongside the Charting the Unknown expansion.
[Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/29/crimson-desert-charting-the-unknown/) · [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-au/games/crimson-desert/)
