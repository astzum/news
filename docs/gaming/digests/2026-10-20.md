# Tue 20 Oct 2026

### Activision's QA testers have authorised a strike a week out from Modern Warfare 4
Nearly 600 testers unionised in March 2024 and still have no first contract. They voted on 15 October to authorise a walkout over wages, AI guardrails, contractor use and disability accommodations. Activision says it has bargained in good faith and has offered new dates on 21 and 22 October. MW4 launches worldwide on the 23rd.
[CWA](https://cwa-union.org/news/releases/activision-quality-assurance-workers-vote-authorize-strike) · [Activision's response](https://kotaku.com/modern-warfare-4-testers-vote-to-authorize-union-strike-as-contract-negotiations-stall-it-is-time-to-stop-playing-games-2000746427)

### Modern Warfare 4's campaign is the best-received Call of Duty in years
Steam has it at 93% positive from 4,312 reviews, against 38% for last year's Black Ops 7. Caveats: that is pre-order buyers who paid for Friday's campaign-only early access, critics have not reviewed it at scale yet, and multiplayer — where sentiment usually turns — does not open until the 23rd.
[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4435490/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-steam-review-rating)

### PEGI is retroactively bumping loot box games to a 16 rating after all
When the "paid random items" criteria landed in June, they applied only to newly submitted games. The UK's Games Rating Authority has now started re-rating existing digital-only titles currently at PEGI 3, 7 or 12, rolling out through October. The ESRB declined to match the change in March, so European and US ratings will diverge.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/pegi-begins-moving-existing-digital-only-games-with-loot-boxes-up-to-a-16-rating)

### Samson studio Liquid Swords is gone six months after its debut
The Swedish studio filed for bankruptcy after staff went two months unpaid and petitioned for it themselves; all 27 employees are dismissed, with salaries covered by the state wage guarantee. The administrator's sale document puts Samson at 151,900 copies and SEK 24.1m since April. Bids for the IP close on 21 October.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/samson-studio-liquid-swords-files-for-bankruptcy-staff-reportedly-unpaid-for-two-months)

### Steam Next Fest is live with roughly double the demos of five months ago
PC Gamer counted 8,011 entries on the preview page — roughly twice June's Next Fest, and eight times the June 2022 one. The volume is the story: one dev has pulled its demo entirely, fearing a generative-AI clone ships first, and another says a copycat lifted 13 of its classes verbatim.
[PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/sale/nextfest)
