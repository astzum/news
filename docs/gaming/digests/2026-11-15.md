# Sun 15 Nov 2026

### ARC Raiders is past 17 million copies, and Nexon has quietly killed six projects
Nexon's Q3 release puts Embark's extraction shooter over 17 million units and revenue at ¥138.4bn, up 17% year on year, with the MapleStory franchise up 72%. Buried in the same release: six projects discontinued in recent months, budgets and teams "redeployed to games with greater commercial potential", and AI being accelerated across development, localisation and code review.
[Nexon](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Nexon-Releases-Earnings-for-Third-Quarter-2026) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/maplestory-franchise-q3-revenue-rises-72-drives-overall-growth-for-nexon)

### Some GTA 6 physical boxes are turning up with no code to redeem
Rockstar went disc-free: the physical edition is a download code printed inside a translucent case. Since retailers started handing over pre-orders on 12 November, buyers on both PS5 and Xbox report opening sealed boxes to find a blank grey strip where the code should be. Rockstar hasn't commented. Launch is Thursday.
[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-no-code-physical-edition/)

### Pioner is shutting down less than a year after early access
GFAGames says it ran out of money: talks with partners failed, and "the studio no longer has sufficient funds of its own to continue development and keep the servers running". The Stalker-meets-Tarkov survival shooter peaked at 1,686 concurrent Steam players after December 2025's launch and was down to dozens by September. No shutdown date, nothing on refunds.
[PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/a-survival-game-we-once-called-stalker-and-tarkovs-alien-baby-is-closing-down-less-than-a-year-after-launch/)

### Humble Bundle's redesign dropped two-factor authentication
Users noticed the settings page no longer offers a way to set up 2FA. Kotaku found you can also change the password without entering the old one, and that an email change verifies to the new address, not the old. Owner Ziff Davis laid off most of Humble's engineers in the summer. Some users say 2FA has since returned.
[Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/humble-bundle-redesign-ziff-davis/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1032659/humble-bundle-redesign-two-factor-verification-security-settings)

### Black Forest Games has bought itself out of THQ Nordic
The Destroy All Humans studio has separated from Embracer's THQ Nordic via a management buyout and co-founded Third Signal, a group to back small teams making premium PC and console games. It reportedly halved its staff in 2024. CEO Andreas Tobler: "Some of the best games of the last decade came from teams of 50, not 500."
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/black-forest-games-spins-out-from-thq-nordic-co-founds-indie-studio-group-third-signal)
