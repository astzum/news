# Fri 23 Oct 2026

### An Australian court has ordered Apple to open iOS, and Fortnite is back today
Justice Jonathan Beach found Apple's restrictions on rival app stores and payments breached competition law. Epic says the game returns to the Australian App Store immediately. The structural changes land in March 2027: third-party stores, downloads direct from developers, alternative payments, and no Apple fee on purchases made outside the App Store. Apple will seek a stay and appeal.
[Epic Games](https://x.com/EpicNewsroom/status/2113073010554916912) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/fortnite-returns-to-ios-in-australia-after-court-rules-apple-violated-competition-law)

### A Capcom ID breach exposed 11.7 million email addresses
Capcom noticed suspicious activity on 19 October. The 11,774,247 affected records also carry account metadata — creation date, last login, login count, last-login IP. Separately, 43 accounts had date of birth, gender, country and nickname accessed, and 196 had passwords changed without authorisation. No card data, and games still work. Capcom is warning about impersonation emails.
[Capcom](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e261022.html)

### Xbox console sales just hit an all-time September low in the US
Circana has Marvel's Wolverine as the month's best-selling game and PS5 leading both units and dollars, up 22% and 43% year on year. Xbox Series units fell 27%, undercutting last year's record low. Total spending slipped 1% to $4.8bn, and non-mobile subscription spending fell 5% — its first annual drop since February 2024.
[Circana](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3myhpvs2p3c23)

### Football Manager 27 will be free for five days before anyone can buy it
Sports Interactive opens the Launch Preview to everyone on PC and Mac from 6pm GMT on 29 October until 3 November — Steam, Epic, the Microsoft Store and Game Pass, all modes, no sign-up. Saves carry through to the 10 November release. Call it penance for FM26, which is still Mostly Negative on Steam.
[Football Manager](https://www.footballmanager.com/news/play-football-manager-27-launch-preview-free)

### Atari is shutting the VCS store but handing the console back to its owners
Support for the 2021 box ends with the store closing on 1 February 2027, last purchases the day before. Atari says it will then ship an OS update that drops the authentication dependency on its servers, plus a Home Brew app so people can keep adding games. The catch: no re-downloads once you migrate.
[VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/atari-is-ending-support-for-its-2021-vcs-console-will-release-a-homebrew-app-to-let-players-keep-making-and-adding-games/) · [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/10/atari-is-officially-pulling-support-for-the-atari-vcs)
