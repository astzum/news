# Fri 20 Nov 2026

### Ubisoft set up a generative AI subsidiary and cut 166 more jobs the same day
Thursday's half-year results put net bookings at €644.7m, down 16.5%, with headcount down close to 1,100 over six months. The same report names Motherbrain — the team behind AI-NPC demo Teammates — as a subsidiary "focused on AI for gameplay innovation". The 166 cuts hit production and support roles in Montréal and Québec City, Vantage Studios included.

### Roblox's terms of service are now the subject of a US Senate bill
Durbin and Hawley introduced the No Forced Arbitration for Child Sexual Exploitation and Cyber Injuries Act the day before their subcommittee's first hearing on the platform. It would let families sue in court despite click-through arbitration clauses. CEO David Baszucki declined to testify; Roblox says a hearing built around arbitration "fails to advance safety".
[Senate](https://www.durbin.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/durbin-hawley-introduce-bill-empowering-survivors-of-online-exploitation-ahead-of-major-hearing-on-roblox) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/us-senate-hearing-examines-child-safety-on-roblox-considers-bipartisan-bill-to-bypass-forced-arbitration)

### Call of Duty topped October in the US while total spending fell 12%
Circana has Modern Warfare 4 first — the 19th straight year a Call of Duty leads its launch month. Content spending dropped 14% to $3.7bn against last October's Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A. Hardware dollars rose 2% to $357m purely on price: Xbox Series' average selling price up 29%, PS5's up 13%.
[Circana](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2oaaiw43u2i) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-debuts-as-top-selling-game-in-us-for-october-2026)

### GameStop's CEO says code-in-a-box is gutting the trade-in business
Ryan Cohen told Bloomberg that going all-digital "has hurt consumers, and it's also hurt GameStop" — buy a game and you should be able to sell, trade or lend it. Pre-owned and refurbished stock is 22% of GameStop's sales, down 32% year on year. GTA 6 shipped with no disc at all.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-discless-release-code-in-a-box-gamestop-ceo)

### Two million Xbox owners became New Zealanders for a night
Ampere Analysis counts 1.5m US and 600,000 UK players switching console region to buy GTA 6 where it unlocked first, plus 200,000 each in Canada and Brazil. Only 100,000 US PlayStation owners bothered — Sony wants a regional account and local currency. New Zealand's tourism board told them "we'd love to have you".
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-new-zealand-trick-2m-players-early)
