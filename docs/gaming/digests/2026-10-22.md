# Thu 22 Oct 2026

### EA is closing BioWare and the next Mass Effect is cancelled
BioWare's own post ends more than 30 years: "BioWare is closing and early development on the next Mass Effect project is ending." Bloomberg puts the wider cut at about 400 EA jobs, roughly 50 of them at BioWare, with a Sims game cancelled and skate's team downsized. The PIF-led buyout closed on 4 August.
[BioWare](https://blog.bioware.com/2026/10/21/bioware-update/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/bioware-is-closing-after-30-years)

### Xbox will sell cloud gaming by the hour, with no Game Pass needed
Insiders without a subscription can now buy playtime packs at introductory pricing: 10 hours for $3.99, 25 for $7.99, 50 for $11.99. Ad-free, and the hours sit there until used. Broader rollout in November, alongside the free ad-supported tier Xbox is also testing. Pricing is US; availability varies by market.
[Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/21/cloud-playtime-now-available-to-test-with-xbox-insiders/)

### Cyberpunk 2077's free 2.5 update lands today with cross-platform mods
The Edgerunners 2 tie-in hits PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on 22 October, with Switch 2 following in the days after. Patch notes are already up: a side quest called Clampdown, an Edgerunners radio station, dual-wielding pistols behind a new Cool perk, cyberware skins, and mod.io-powered mod support on console.
[CD Projekt Red](https://www.cyberpunk.net/en/news/52120/check-out-whats-coming-to-night-city-in-the-edgerunners-2-update-for-cyberpunk-2077)

### Resident Evil Requiem's PlayStation discs out-earned every Xbox copy
Alinea Analytics estimates 1.3m physical PlayStation copies worth $92.3m — 15% of the game's revenue everywhere, 30.2% of its PlayStation revenue, and 1.8x what all Xbox sales made. Japan skews it: 44% of Pragmata's copies were physical. Still modelled estimates, and 2026 physical is under 20% of PlayStation premium revenue overall.
[Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/physical-game-revenues-on-playstation)

### FSR 4.1 is coming to Xbox Series X and S
AMD is expanding its machine-learning upscaler to Radeon RX 6000 cards, Ryzen AI Max chips, Z2-series handhelds, and — "coming soon", no date — Series X|S. Temper it: developers have to patch each game in, and the Series consoles have no dedicated ML hardware. Sony already ships its own QSSR upscaler on PS5.
[AMD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fT6TIXnmv-s)
