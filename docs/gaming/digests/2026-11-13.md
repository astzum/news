# Fri 13 Nov 2026

### A third of game developers have been laid off in the past two years
GDC's 15th State of the Game Industry, from 2,600-plus professionals, puts it at 34% — 39% in the US — and says 68% of AAA staff saw cuts at their studio. Over half are still unemployed; 27% have sent 100-plus applications. Generative AI use rose to 40% from 36%, and 55% still call its effect negative.
[GDC](https://gdconf.com/state-of-the-game-industry-november-2026/) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/one-in-three-games-industry-professionals-surveyed-by-gdc-were-laid-off-in-the-past-two-years-more-than-in-its-last-report/)

### Nintendo pulled 217 GitHub repositories in a single sweep
Four DMCA notices filed on 6 November take down the Picofly modchip firmware along with its entire network of 144 forks, a Citron emulator network of 71, and two Yuzu mirrors. The argument is the familiar one: the code defeats the Switch's technical protection measures, citing Mario Kart 8 Deluxe and Breath of the Wild.
[GitHub DMCA](https://github.com/github/dmca/blob/master/2026/11/2026-11-06-nintendo-2.md) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/nintendo-github-dmca-takedowns-picofly-modchips-piracy-repositories-2000756232)

### Steam's approval queue is still eating indie release dates
Nine days after Valve conceded build review had stretched to 12 business days, more games are slipping. Rami Ismail's Australia Did It has sat in the queue since 20 October. He told Eurogamer that Valve's refusal to take a position on AI leaves only bad options — "constraints on game distribution, increase fees, or make the process more complicated".
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/steam-game-submission-review-backlog-ai-slop-approval-delays)

### Fredrik Wester is handing Paradox to his deputy on 4 January
Mattias Lilja, currently deputy CEO and the company's first producer back in 2009, takes over; Wester stays on the board and remains largest shareholder. He led Paradox from 2009 to 2018, returned in 2021 for what the board calls a reset, and took it onto Nasdaq Stockholm's main market in June. "My plan was never to be CEO for life."
[Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/media/press-releases/paradox-interactive/mattias-lilja-takes-over-as-ceo-and-will-lead-paradox-interactive-ab-publ-to-its-next-level) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/fredrik-wester-steps-down-as-paradox-interactive-ceo)

### Resident Evil Requiem won Ultimate Game of the Year — and a tiny indie matched it
Capcom's game took four at the 44th Golden Joysticks, including Console GOTY and Best Lead Performer for Angela Sant'Albano, with Capcom on seven overall plus Studio of the Year. Meccha Chameleon also won four: multiplayer, self-published indie, Streamer's Choice and Developers' Choice. These are public votes, so read them as popularity rather than criticism.
[GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/all-the-golden-joystick-awards-2026-winners-revealed/)
