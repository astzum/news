# Tue 03 Nov 2026

### Phantom Blade Zero is the biggest Steam launch of 2026
S-Game says it sold over 4 million copies in 72 hours across PS5, Steam and China's WeGame, with no per-platform split. The Steam concurrent peak settled at 833,921 — past Forza Horizon 6 and everything else this year, though barely a third of Black Myth: Wukong's 2.4m debut.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/phantom-blade-zero-4-million-copies-steam-biggest-launch)

### Toshihiro Nagoshi's studio is legally dead and Gang of Dragon with it
Japan's Kanpo gazette listed Nagoshi Studio among Monday's dissolutions; it was wound up by shareholders' resolution on 31 October. NetEase, which founded it in 2021, pulled funding in March after finding the Ma Dong-seok-fronted action game needed another ¥7bn to finish. Announced last December as "deep into development"; now nothing.
[Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/nagoshi-studio-shuts-down) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/nagoshi-studio-officially-closes-after-netease-cuts-funding/)

### Xbox's former COO says she was not fired for a policy violation
Helen Chiang, out after 21 years and three months into the job, posted a point-by-point denial on LinkedIn: no press leaks, no competitor, no misconduct, not passed over. Then the line that matters — she has "serious concerns" about how Microsoft applied its own standards "in my case". Microsoft has not commented.
[Helen Chiang](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/helenchiang_clarity-is-kindness-there-has-been-some-share-7523070825940467712-Rzit/)

### Aonuma had Ocarina of Time remade partly to teach his own team
Nintendo's Ask the Developer confirms the main Zelda studio, EPD Production Group No. 3, built it — not Grezzo, who handled the 3DS port. Aonuma says most current staff have only shipped Breath of the Wild-era games, and rebuilding the 1998 structure "may trigger new insights". Switch 2 exclusive, Thursday.
[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-24-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-part-1/)

### Anthropic has told Claude Code to stop rebuilding other people's games
Addy Osmani confirmed two sentences added to the Opus 5.5 prompt "to steer it away from recreating someone else's specific game", on the grounds that infringing IP breaches the usage policy. Original and genre games still work. It follows weeks of AI-made clones and recompilations piling up on Steam and itch.io.
[Addy Osmani, Anthropic](https://x.com/addyosmani/status/2116213240363729013)
