# Mon 16 Nov 2026

### Coffee Stain has bought Chronicles: Medieval out of its developer's bankruptcy
Raw Power Games went bankrupt on 30 October. Coffee Stain won the estate's sale with DKK 36.75m cash, and is parking the assets in a new company it owns 75% of — the other 25% goes to key original developers. It will fund the rest of development. The game sits in Steam's top 30 most wishlisted, due 2027.
[Coffee Stain Group](https://coffeestain.com/release/coffee-stain-enters-into-agreement-to-acquire-chronicles-medieval-ip/) · [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/strategy/chronicles-medieval-developer-declared-bankruptcy-on-the-day-it-announced-it-was-in-trouble-but-development-will-continue-after-a-usd5-million-reprieve-from-coffee-stain/)

### Riot is rebuilding League of Legends and calling it New Dawn
A teaser from League studio head Andrei "Meddler" van Roon promises a modernised client, the first graphical overhaul of Summoner's Rift since 2014, and per-champion "new ways to play" — a Blitzcrank mastery tree, Vayne dashing through walls. Riot says the full reveal lands in January and the update ships later in 2027.
[Riot](https://leagueoflegends.com/new-dawn/) · [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/moba/league-of-legends-will-get-an-overhaul-called-new-dawn-with-new-visuals-build-options-and-an-updated-client/)

### A drive-by shooting hit the office campus housing Xbox's inXile
Tustin police responded to reports of gunfire at Flight at Tustin Legacy on 11 November, found multiple shell casings, and took a suspect into custody on Friday morning. Nobody was hurt, and no motive has been given. inXile, currently making Clockwork Revolution, says it is working closely with law enforcement.
[Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/inxile-shooting-police-microsoft-xbox-clockwork-revolution/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/inxile-office-shooting-tustin-suspect-arrested)

### Treyarch has laid off the writer who built Call of Duty's Zombies mode
Craig Houston posted that he was "sadly one of those affected by recent layoffs" after approximately 20 years at the studio. He worked on campaigns from Call of Duty 3 onward and was the main Zombies writer from World at War. Eurogamer ties the cut to the 3,200-job Xbox reorganisation announced earlier this year.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/craig-houston-layoff-treyarch-call-of-duty-layoffs)

### GTA 6 can be nominated for Game of the Year before anyone outside Rockstar has played it
The Game Awards' jury of 100-plus media and influencer outlets reveals nominees in more than 30 categories on 17 November, two days before GTA 6 ships. The rules explicitly allow publishers to hand out pre-release access "for critical evaluation", with an eligibility cutoff of 20 November at 9pm PT.
[The Game Awards](https://thegameawards.com/faq) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-to-be-announced-next-week-zelda-and-gta-6-eligible)
