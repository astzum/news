# Tue 17 Nov 2026

### The man who leaked GTA 6 in 2022 pleaded guilty three days before it ships
Arion Kurtaj, 21, admitted the Rockstar breach at Woolwich Crown Court along with hacks on Uber, Revolut and BT/EE — around £10m of damage. He took 90 clips using a Fire Stick plugged into a Travelodge TV while under police protection. Rockstar put recovery at $5m. Sentencing comes later.
[BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq99exy89dngo) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-leaker-pleads-guilty)

### Xbox is framing Project Helix around access, not horsepower
Next-gen VP Jason Ronald, in Microsoft's own 25th-anniversary magazine, says it is "a console that is also capable of playing games from your PC. It is not a PC that can also play console games." Four generations of library come with you. 8K isn't the target; "neural rendering" is. No date, no price.
[Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/15/xbox-25th-anniversary-the-magazine/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/microsoft-project-helix-interview-details)

### European game unions are asking players to buy games made without genAI
Unions from six countries met in Manchester and published "On Generative AI Usage in the Games Industry". It blames genAI for a marked decline in junior positions, points at models trained on stolen data, and urges players to be vocal — while arguing workers, not employers, should decide when it is used.
[IWGB Game Workers](https://www.gameworkers.co.uk/ai-manifesto/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/video-game-union-manifesto-calls-on-consumers-to-support-games-made-without-genai-2000757423)

### Pikmin 4's paid Switch 2 upgrade is being torched by the people who bought it
Critics gave Pikmin 4: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy an 83 Metascore across 26 reviews. Users have it at 1.9 from 328 ratings. The complaints repeat: still 30fps, load times longer than the original, and bonus levels built from maps already in the base game.
[Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/pikmin-4-nintendo-switch-2-edition-plus-dandori/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/actively-makes-the-game-worse-fans-are-furious-with-pikmin-4s-paid-nintendo-switch-2-upgrade-2000757449)

### Unity will give ten indie teams $50,000 with no strings attached
The Unity Fellowship takes no equity, no revenue share, no IP claim and sets no milestone gates — unusual terms in a year of grim indie funding. It wants Unity games roughly twelve months from launch. Applications close 18 December, 5pm ET; recipients are named at GDC in March 2027.
[Unity](https://unity.com/fellowship) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/unity-launches-50000-grant-for-indie-developers-with-no-equity-or-repayment)
