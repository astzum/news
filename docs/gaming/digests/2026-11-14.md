# Sat 14 Nov 2026

### Xbox marked 25 years by announcing games from the studios it gutted
Asha Sharma used the anniversary FanFest to confirm Wolfenstein 3 is in production at MachineGames, with Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson back as BJ and Grace, and that id Software's next project has started. Microsoft cut 96 of id's 136 staff in July. Also real: 21 more original Xbox games on PC, and free Firefight for Halo: Campaign Evolved.
[Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/wolfenstein-3-new-id-software-game-announced-xbox)

### Twenty of the id developers Microsoft laid off now own their own studio
They have formed Hidden Horses, a democratically governed, developer-owned shop with "over 250 collective years" between its co-founders and a stated preference for "long-term health over short-term growth". First game is Danger Pay, a single-player arena shooter set on a lethal televised game show, pitched between The Running Man and Smash TV. The Kickstarter hasn't launched.
[PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/former-id-software-developers-form-their-own-employee-owned-shooter-studio-in-the-wake-of-microsofts-mass-layoffs/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/laid-off-id-software-devs-form-employee-owned-studio-hidden-horses)

### Pearl Abyss is pushing AI through its entire development pipeline
Its Q3 letter says AI already covers "automation of repetitive tasks, content creation support, and test optimization", and that the studio is expanding it "throughout our entire development pipeline" — design concepts now get validated by AI before anyone builds them. Crimson Desert made ₩40.6bn last quarter, down 70% QoQ. DokeV is H2 2028.
[Pearl Abyss](https://www.pearlabyss.com/en-US/Board/Detail?_boardNo=15330) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/crimson-desert-ai-pipeline-dokev-2028)

### CD Projekt is using AI to restore Geralt's pre-stroke Polish voice
Jacek Rozenek recorded some 10,000 Polish lines as Geralt in 2014, then had a stroke in 2019; his voice came back lower and gruffer. Rather than recast him for next year's Songs of the Past, CDPR is running his new lines through Respeecher, with his consent. Doug Cockle's English is untouched. The studio calls it "a special case".
[Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/witcher-3-jacek-rozenek-voice-ai-cd-projekt-respeecher) · [RPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/one-of-those-ways-where-you-can-use-ai-ethically-cdpr-have-modified-geralts-polish-voice-in-the-witcher-3-songs-of-the-past-with-genai-due-to-actors-stroke)

### The PS5 Pro's upscaler is quietly spreading to base PS5s
QSSR — same Project Amethyst foundation as PSSR, same developer interface — shipped last month and is picking up games. Marvel's Spider-Man 2 has it now, Control Resonant gets it in December, and The Finals, ARC Raiders and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam follow. Sharper edges and a steadier image on hardware you already own.
[PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/qssr-arrives-in-marvels-spider-man-2-control-resonant-the-finals-arc-raiders-and-hell-let-loose-vietnam/) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/6-more-games-receive-ps5-performance-updates-via-qssr-including-arc-raiders-and-control-resonant/)
