# Sat 24 Oct 2026

### Arkane Lyon has three weeks to find a buyer or close
Le Monde reports the mandatory works council consultation over Microsoft's closure plan ends on 13 November, with 217 jobs at stake. A union delegate says Marvel's Blade is "rather near the end" of production. Microsoft says it is "actively looking for a partner to support the next phase of development". Bloomberg's Jason Schreier says several rescue paths are live.
[Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/report-arkane-studios-has-until-november-13-to-find-a-buyer)

### The Switch 2 has its first software-only jailbreak
Security researcher Sammy Hajhamid chained an integer overflow in SmileBASIC 4 with a stack overflow in the console's filesystem sysmodule to run native code at sysmodule privilege on firmware 23.0.1. It is tethered — nothing survives a reboot — and installs no custom firmware. He leaned on OpenAI's Codex throughout, over roughly two weeks.
[pepsipu](https://pepsipu.com/blog/2026-10-switch2-jailbreak-ns2jb)

### Don't Nod is under court protection and may run out of cash in January
The Paris business court granted the Life is Strange studio a safeguard procedure on 19 October, opening a six-month observation period that freezes existing debt. First-half revenue fell 14% to €6.1m against a €7.1m net loss, with €9.9m cash at 30 June. Headcount is already down to 194, and up to 85 French roles may go.
[Don't Nod](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/actus-2026-10-20-dont-nod-2026-half-year-results-and-financial-situation-update)

### Modern Warfare 4 landed at 85, and the Switch 2 port is not the afterthought
OpenCritic has it in the Mighty tier from 21 reviews with every critic recommending it. Nintendo Life's 9/10 covers the Switch 2 version specifically — the first Call of Duty on Nintendo hardware in over a decade, shipping day and date. GameSpot's 7/10 is the low mark. Multiplayer and DMZ opened Friday.
[OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/20690/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4)

### GameMaker's next runtime will be free to ship commercial games with
From 1 January 2027, GMRT drops the Professional licence requirement for desktop, mobile, web and GX Games, and every tier gains runtime source access. Consoles still need an Enterprise licence and approved-developer status. Existing Professional holders keep commercial rights on the older GMS2 runtime, and no tier prices change.
[GameMaker](https://gamemaker.io/en/blog/gmrt-update)
