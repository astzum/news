# Wed 21 Oct 2026

### Xbox has internally priced its next console at $1,200 to $1,800
Fortune's profile of CEO Asha Sharma reports that is what Project Helix is projected to cost players unless Microsoft subsidises it. The console is planned for the 2028 holidays and a next-gen handheld for 2027. Also reported: a tiered Game Pass overhaul letting players buy cloud, multiplayer and console-only games à la carte.
[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/20/the-non-gamer-ceo-racing-to-save-microsofts-xbox/)

### Final Fantasy Resonance works everywhere except its story
OpenCritic has it at 85 from 72 reviews, with 92% of critics recommending, two days before Thursday's launch on PC, PS5, Switch 2 and Xbox Series X/S. The job system, HD-2D art and score carry it; Eurogamer's 3/5 and GameSpot's 7/10 both pin the problem on the writing.
[OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/20820/final-fantasy-resonance)

### GTA 6 pre-orders are running 148% ahead of the biggest Call of Duty
Sensor Tower estimates 6.08m US digital pre-orders as of 13 October, worth roughly $594m, with 74% on PlayStation and most buyers taking the $100 Ultimate edition. The caveat matters: this is modelled panel data covering US digital only, so no boxed copies and no other territories.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/sensor-tower-data-shows-the-yawning-gap-between-gta-6-and-everything-else)

### Studio AI use is near-universal and mostly shallow
Consultancy Vivrato interviewed 101 senior studio leaders between April and September. 94% use AI in at least one of nine production stages, but the median score for how deeply it is built into the whole pipeline is 3 out of 10, and 52% rate it 3 or lower. Adoption is not integration.
[Vivrato](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Vivrato-VAIA-Report-94-of-Studio-Leaders-Report-AI-Adoption-While-Pipe)

### Japan's actors union wants consent before a voice is trained on
Nippairen, which represents about 2,600 actors and voice actors, published voice-AI guidelines on 15 October: prior permission at the training, generation and output stages, royalties because AI output counts as a new performance, and limits on automatic dubbing. Japan has no statutory voice right, so these are voluntary — the union wants them legislated.
[Japan Actors Union](https://www.nippairen.com/about/jau_guideline.html)
