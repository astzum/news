# Fri 06 Nov 2026

### Switch 2 hardware sales fell 37% and Nintendo did not blink
First-half sell-in was 6.49m Switch 2 units, down 37.4% year on year; Switch 1 fell 32.7% to 1.27m. Software carried it — Switch 2 titles up 18.8% to 24.49m, Tomodachi Life alone 8.81m — and operating profit rose 49.1% to ¥216.5bn on a ~$300m US tariff refund. Guidance unchanged, still absorbing ~¥100bn of component costs.
[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2026/261105_3e.pdf)

### PlayStation is making much more money selling far fewer consoles
Game & Network Services operating income jumped 83% to ¥220bn for the September quarter on sales up 9%, even as PS5 shipments fell to 2.6m from 4m a year earlier. Marvel's Wolverine has sold 2.4m since 15 September; monthly actives hit a record 127m. Sony raised full-year group guidance and says PS6 is "progressing as scheduled".
[Sony 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/313838/000110465926122935/tm2628525d4_6k.htm) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/sony-playstation-fy-2026-wolverine-sales)

### Steam's build review queue has stretched to 12 business days
Valve says its usual three-to-five-day target has slipped under a surge of submissions, and blames re-reviews of games that fail basic checks. The release checklist now demands five minutes of real playtime launched through Steam, declared redistributables, a working Cloud save and five gameplay-tagged screenshots. Valve never mentions AI; developers waiting in the queue do.
[Steamworks](https://store.steampowered.com/news/group/4145017/view/696528162820984745)

### Who buys a studio predicts its fate better than anything about the studio
Sebastian Cardoso graded 303 PC and console studios acquired since 2000: 60.8% declined or died, one in three closed outright, half of those within five years. All 17 Activision Blizzard purchases ended in decline or closure; Nintendo has bought six and lost none. Studios the giants founded themselves fared worse still, at 87.3%.
[Game Studio Unlocked](https://gamestudiounlocked.substack.com/p/the-tragic-fate-of-game-studios-after-an-acquisition) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/analysis-one-in-three-acquired-game-studios-has-been-shut-down-since-2000)

### EA has put 25 of its 90s back catalogue on Steam
All nine Ultimas, both Ultima Underworlds, the Wing Commander line, Theme Hospital, Strike Commander and the Crusader pair, most on Steam for the first time, also on GOG and the EA App. Seven Anniversary Editions add quality-of-life work and FMV recut from master tapes found in a 20,000-item vault at Redwood Shores. Half price to 19 November.
[EA](https://www.ea.com/en-au/news/ea-classics-launch)
