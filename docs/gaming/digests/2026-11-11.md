# Wed 11 Nov 2026

### Europe's draft consumer law would switch loot boxes off by default for minors
A draft Digital Fairness Act regulation, leaked to French outlet Contexte ahead of today's Commission proposal, would bar pay-to-win and pay-to-progress mechanics aimed at minors, force disclosure of loot box odds and the real cash value of virtual currencies, and let any adult turn variable-reward offers off. Unconfirmed until publication, and it replaces the 2005 Unfair Commercial Practices Directive outright.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/digital-fairness-act-eu-europe-loot-boxes-regulation) · [Hogan Lovells Cadwalader](https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/eu-digital-fairness-act-leaked-draft-regulation-proposes-significant-reform-of-the-eu-consumer)

### Rockstar's UK staff start voting on union recognition tomorrow
The ballot runs 12 to 26 November, straddling GTA 6's launch and the tribunal brought by 31 workers the IWGB says were sacked for organising. Recognition would cover pay and bonuses, overtime, redundancies, flexible working and generative-AI policy, and make it the largest recognised union in UK games. SAG-AFTRA declared solidarity on Monday.
[Morning Star](https://morningstaronline.co.uk/article/rockstar-workers-could-make-gaming-history-ballot-win-union-recognition) · [SAG-AFTRA](https://bsky.app/profile/sagaftra.org/post/3mzvhcduk3a27) · [RPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/a-fight-for-all-workers-sag-aftra-throw-weight-behind-emerging-rockstar-union-ahead-of-its-gta-6-release-week-vote)

### Take-Two's net bookings fell 18% in the quarter before GTA 6
$1.60bn against $1.96bn a year ago, under its own $1.62-1.67bn guidance, with a $54m net loss. NBA 2K27 managed the series' second-biggest launch at over 4m units and still missed forecast. Full-year guidance holds at $8-8.2bn, and Zelnick says GTA 6 pre-orders are "tracking far in excess" of expectations.

### Steam store pages now say what framerate a game actually hits on a Deck
Averages and a histogram sit under Steam Deck Compatibility, behind "Learn more", for thousands of Verified, Playable and Unsupported titles. The data comes from players who opted in, so it is a sample rather than a benchmark — but the histogram's low-end spikes expose stutter a Verified badge hides. More devices are coming, Valve says.
[Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/593110/announcements/detail/685269801333948420) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/steam-deck-framerate-average-data-store-widget)

### Sony is retiring the PlayStation Plus app on PC on 10 December
Fewer than 0.4% of eligible Premium members used the cloud-streaming client, so it goes; memberships and benefits on PS5 and PS4 are unaffected. The same post set out November's Game Catalog, playable from the 17th: Death Stranding 2, Atomfall, Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake, with Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii following on the 24th.
