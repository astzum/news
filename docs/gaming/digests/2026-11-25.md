# Wed 25 Nov 2026

Platform-holder numbers, a studio switching itself off, and money owed to UK developers.

### GTA 6 is the biggest launch in PlayStation history, with 39 million players on PS5
Sony's figures for the window ending 22 November: 39m unique PS5 players, 683m hours logged, 17.4 hours each, and a new concurrent-player record for any game on the platform — multiplayer and free-to-play included. More new PS5s came online in a day than any since launch outside holidays, and GTA 6 was the first game on 70% of them.
[PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/23/thank-you-for-making-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-biggest-launch-in-playstation-history/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/gta-6-was-the-biggest-launch-in-playstation-history-with-the-highest-number-of-concurrent-players-ever)

### The ex-Respawn studio that never showed its game has switched itself off
Gravity Well lost its funding backer in spring, cut 40 jobs last month, and is now in "hibernation": "we still believe in the IP and game we were building." Drew McCoy, Jon Shiring and Mon Saetern left Respawn six years ago to found it and never announced the game. The statement doubles as a pitch to publishers and recruiters.
[Gravity Well](https://gravitywell.games/) · [Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/ex-respawn-studio-gravity-well-announces-hibernation-following-previous-failure-to-secure-funding-and-layoffs-but-insists-we-still-believe-in-the-ip-and-game-we-were-building)

### UK developers can now claim their cut of Google's £260m Play Store settlement
Claims are open to more than 10,000 UK businesses that paid Play commission on sales between August 2018 and July 2026. Every eligible one gets £200 minimum, more above £7,500 of Play sales, with million-pound payouts for the biggest sellers. Google settled Barry Rodger's opt-out class action in August without admitting liability. Claims close 24 March 2027.
[Official claim site](https://www.googleplaystoredeveloperclaim.com/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/uk-mobile-developers-can-now-apply-for-their-share-of-a-260-million-google-settlement)

### CD Projekt has signed The Witcher 4's marketing over to PlayStation, GTA 6-style
On the Q3 call, CFO Piotr Nielubowicz said new agreements with Sony cover the studio's games on PlayStation and in PS Plus, plus the Witcher 4 campaign — the arrangement that let Sony call PS5 the best way to play GTA 6. Witcher 4 is still 2028. The quarter: 208m PLN revenue, 109m net profit.
[CD Projekt](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/cd-projekt-red-wraps-up-the-third-quarter-of-2026/) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/cdpr-has-entered-a-marketing-agreement-with-sony-for-the-witcher-4/)

### The Steam Awards turn ten and add a Hall of Fame for the decade's best
Nominations run to 30 November across 14 categories, with Just 5 More Minutes, Defies Description and This Is My Job Now brought back. The Hall of Fame pits past Game of the Year winners against each other and appears when voting opens on 17 December alongside the Winter Sale. Winners are announced 2 January.
