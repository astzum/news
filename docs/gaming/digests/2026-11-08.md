# Sun 08 Nov 2026

### Path of Exile 2 leaves early access on 11 December, and goes free
Grinding Gear Games closed ExileCon with the date — 12 December AEDT, two years after early access began. 1.0 adds Acts 5 and 6 (24 areas, 18 bosses), the stealth-and-traps Shadow, the stance-based Duelist, a passive-tree overhaul, and a faction-war league, Conquerors of Wraeclast, layered over the Atlas endgame.
[Grinding Gear Games](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2694490/view/1848664643347683)

### Bit Reactor laid off staff ten weeks after shipping Star Wars Zero Company
Developers across several departments said on LinkedIn that Friday was their last day; the count is unspecified. The studio furloughed roughly 80% of its team weeks before the August launch, having run out of money, then brought more than half back in October. Those cut appear to be from the furlough group.
[Bespin Bulletin](https://bespinbulletin.com/star-wars-zero-company-developer-bit-reactor-hit-with-layoffs/) · [Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/after-star-wars-zero-company-dev-bit-reactor-brought-back-an-unspecified-number-of-furloughed-devs-some-of-those-who-werent-have-seemingly-been-laid-off)

### Sony has delayed its first PlayStation speakers indefinitely, five days out
Pulse Elevate was due 12 November at $219.99 / £199.99, pre-orders open since September. A Friday update appended to the original PlayStation Blog post says quality testing identified "a production issue" and gives no new date. Sony hasn't said what happens to orders already placed.
[PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/09/24/introducing-pulse-elevate-sony-interactive-entertainments-first-wireless-speakers-for-desktop-gaming/)

### A NieR: Automata patch told Steam it needed 366GB
The game's first Steam update in five years added Chinese and Korean text and broke install sizing: each of the eight DLC now reports the base game's weight, totalling 366.56GB for something that occupies 40–50GB installed. Square Enix is investigating. Unticking the DLC under Properties downloads the base game normally.
[Square Enix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/524220/view/1848664643345223) · [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/nier-automata-developers-working-on-fix-after-update-accidentally-raises-steam-versions-file-size-from-50gb-to-366gb/)

### PlayStation is killing its Ratchet & Clank mobile shooter a year in
Ranger Rumble took its final update on Friday, leaves the App Store and Google Play on 21 November, and shuts its servers on 1 May 2027. Oh BiBi's free-to-play arena shooter never got past soft launch: the Philippines and Indonesia at reveal last November, then the Nordics, Canada and the UK.
[Ranger Rumble](https://www.rangerrumble.com/)
