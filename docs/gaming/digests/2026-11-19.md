# Thu 19 Nov 2026

### GTA 6 arrived with a 99 and not one mixed review
Metacritic counts 147 critic reviews, every one of them positive: no mixed, no negative. OpenCritic's top-critic average is also 99, with 99% recommending. IGN, GameSpot and Easy Allies gave it a perfect ten, Eurogamer five stars. Out today on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only — still no PC date.
[Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi)

### Rockstar says no current console can run GTA 6 faster than 30fps
Technology production director Marc Chambers told Kotaku the PS5 Pro's processor is "slightly better than the PS5 base console" but "not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates". That rules out a 40fps mode too. The Pro spends its extra silicon on ray-traced shadows and PSSR instead.
[Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-wont-get-a-60-fps-mode-even-on-ps5-pro-because-its-not-powerful-enough-2000755979)

### Rockstar's union-busting tribunal wrapped the day before GTA 6 shipped
The Glasgow hearing over 34 workers sacked in 2025 has finished arguments. The IWGB says they were fired for organising; Rockstar says they leaked confidential information outside the company. People Make Games' Chris Bratt, who attended, reports a judgement could take weeks or months. The workers have asked supporters not to boycott the game.
[VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstars-union-busting-tribunal-comes-to-an-end-pending-verdict/)

### Console shipments fell by more than a third last quarter
S&P Global Market Intelligence Kagan counts 6.4m units in Q3, down 36.5% year on year. Switch 2 dropped from 5.5m to 3.3m, PlayStation 35.3% to 2.6m, Xbox 14.2% to 500,000. Analyst Neil Barbour points at the price rises, not the lull before GTA 6: "the prices are too high".
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/analyst-console-shipments-drop-365-to-64m-units-during-q3-on-run-up-to-major-releases)

### Build a Rocket Boy's plan to clear £61.7m of debt is lawsuits
Administrators for the MindsEye studio filed a proposal listing £36.4m owed to NetEase and £4.2m to a hedge fund. The repayment plan is litigation: co-CEO Mark Gerhard blames a "coordinated campaign of negative online reviews" funded by a "very big American company", plus an influencer agency and three unnamed journalists.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/build-a-rocket-boy-pursuing-litigation-against-alleged-saboteurs-to-repay-617m-in-debt)
