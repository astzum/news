# Tue 24 Nov 2026

Release schedules are still hiding from GTA 6, so today is tools, courts and patents.

### Epic has cut Blueprints out of Unreal Engine 6 entirely and is betting AI does the porting
In June it said Blueprints and Actors would stay through UE6 Early Access so UE5 projects could move across gradually. Marcus Wassmer now says neither ships at all: gameplay is Verse and Scene Graph only. The case for it is that AI turned 20,000-plus Fortnite Battle Royale Blueprints into roughly 3 million lines of Verse in two weeks.
[Epic](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/an-update-on-blueprints-and-the-path-to-ue6) · [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/unreal-engine-6-to-remove-blueprints-and-actor-gameplay-framework-from-the-beginning)

### The hacker who leaked GTA 6 in 2022 got seven years and walked out the same day
Arion Kurtaj, 21, pleaded guilty at Woolwich Crown Court to the Rockstar breach, which he ran while on police-protected bail from a Travelodge using a Fire Stick and a Bluetooth keyboard. His secure-hospital stay and remand counted as time served, so he left on licence under a five-year order barring Telegram and Discord. Rockstar put recovery at $5m.
[BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqpv9kr7pxmro)

### GTA 6's leakers have released the file that could get it running on PC
Cyberleek withheld the executable from last week's near-complete Xbox build until its crypto coin hit a $15m market cap or 23 November came, whichever was first. The coin missed; the file shipped regardless, with "the torch passes to the open source recomp and emulation projects". A lead on the Leonida recomp reckons playable is a week away. Unconfirmed by Rockstar.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/gta-6-leaker-reportedly-releases-file-needed-to-run-leaked-xbox-build) · [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/cyberleek-releases-executable-for-gta-6-xbox-build-leaked-last-week-the-torch-passes-to-the-open-source-recomp-and-emulation-projects/)

### Activision wants multiplayer NPCs trained on your voice chat
A patent application published on 12 November describes recording what players shout either side of a kill or death, correlating it with the event, and training a neural network to have NPCs react the same way — profiled per player, matched to their language, filtered for slurs, and able to reach into Discord. Filing is not shipping.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/activision-ai-voice-chat-npc-patent) · [Patentlyze](https://patentlyze.com/patent/activision-game-characters-trained-voice-chat/)

### Plug In Digital has bought Twin Sails to build an indie publishing group
Asmodee's former digital arm, publisher of News Tower, joins Plug In Digital's Dear Villagers label, porting studio Pixel Ratio and dev studio Celsius Online. Twin Sails boss Nicolas Godement takes over the group and insists both firms were financially sound. The offer to studios: distribution reach, up to €500,000 a game, six to eight titles a year.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/plug-in-digital-acquires-news-tower-publisher-twin-sails-interactive-creating-new-publishing-group-to-compete-in-a-highly-competitive-market)
