# Tue 10 Nov 2026

### Sega has leaked 2.19m email addresses through a newsletter contractor
A breach at Japanese email provider Arara exposed 2,196,044 addresses Sega used for product mailouts, 92,273 of them with associated delivery data. No names, birthdates, addresses, phone numbers, card details or passwords, and SEGA Account logins run on a separate system. Arara mailouts are paused; Sega Fave's arcade network is still down from a separate intrusion.
[Sega](https://www.sega.co.jp/release/261109_1.html) · [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/sega-reports-over-2-19-million-email-addresses-were-compromised-in-cyberattack-on-its-third-party-provider-sega-accounts-not-affected/)

### Tripwire cut 36 staff a week after expanding its publishing arm
The Killing Floor studio called the reduction "necessary for the organization" and says the rest of the team is on Maneater 2, Killing Floor 3 and the Tripwire Presents label, which opened to third-party indies five days earlier. It's the second round this year, after 23 in March. Embracer kept Tripwire when it sold Saber.
[Tripwire](https://x.com/TripwireInt/status/2119834389982966150) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/tripwire-interactive-lays-off-36)

### The PlayStation Portal is no longer a "remote player", and the new one has storage
Sony's Japan-only revision, out 10 December at ¥39,980 in midnight black, drops "Remote Player" from the name and bundles a month of PS Plus Premium: cloud streaming, added in 2025, is now the pitch rather than a bonus. It also quietly adds internal storage "with future functionality in mind", unexplained for now. Same 8-inch 1080p60 screen.
[PlayStation Japan](https://blog.ja.playstation.com/2026/11/09/20261109-playstation-portal-announcement-o/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/1029822/sony-playstation-portal-japan-model-additional-storage)

### Germany's games body says the EU KIDS Act will weaken age ratings
game argues the draft is built for social media and would let new European codes of conduct override national co-regulation, leaving the same game rated 12+ in one store and 16+ in another. It wants USK and PEGI made the European standard instead, citing a survey in which 89% of parents call USK labels reliable guidance.
[game](https://www.game.de/en/eu-kids-act-the-german-games-industry-association-warns-of-weaker-youth-protection-in-games/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/german-games-industry-association-warns-the-eu-kids-act-could-undermine-existing-age-rating-systems-for-video-games)

### Owlcat's next Warhammer CRPG is dated 28 January 2027
Dark Heresy follows Rogue Trader into the 41st millennium, but as an Inquisition acolyte running investigations rather than a dynasty — closing cases, not ruling worlds. Owlcat put the date on a new trailer. Worth remembering that its games historically ship rough and improve over a year of patches, Rogue Trader included.
