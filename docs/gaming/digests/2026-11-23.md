# Mon 23 Nov 2026

A quiet weekend, so four items. The AI-in-games fight moved from manifestos to a patch note.

### Nicalis pulled AI-generated music out of Binding of Isaac in a day
A GTA 6-themed daily run shipped with Repentance+'s console launch on 19 November. Players found Suno tags in the music metadata, Steam reviews turned, and Edmund McMillen said he "had no idea". Nicalis pushed a build removing the "generated music files", live on PC and submitted to Sony, Microsoft and Nintendo. It hasn't said how they got in.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/binding-of-isaac-repentance-plus-ai-music-removed) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/binding-of-isaac-update-review-bombed-over-ai-generated-music-in-gta-6-mode-woke-up-to-a-sht-show-obviously-i-had-no-idea-2000759213)

### Comcast says GTA 6 was a quarter of its network, not a quarter of the internet
Its own post says 19 November was the biggest traffic day in Comcast's network history, 31% above average, with the game "nearly a quarter of all network traffic" at peak. Headlines turned that into a quarter of the internet; it is one US ISP's pipes. Still a striking number for a console-only launch.
[Comcast](https://x.com/comcast/status/2123901400149094826)

### Arika has lost the engineer behind Street Fighter EX and Tetris 99
Akira Kurabayashi, its director and head of software development, died of heart failure on 14 November, the studio says. He was main programmer on Taito's Darius Gaiden before three decades at Arika covering Tetris: The Grand Master, Fighting EX Layer, the Forever Blue diving sims and PAC-MAN 99.
[Arika](https://www.arika.co.jp/info_jp/news-detail.php?id=115)

### E.T.'s designer expects an AI glut like 1983's, and says it won't cause a crash
Howard Scott Warshaw told DevGAMM Lisbon that AI will let far more people ship games, most of them bad: "there's going to be a lot of crap, because most games suck." Asked whether that means another 1983, he said no — games are too embedded now for investors to walk away. He expects "a reorganisation".
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/the-creator-of-et-on-the-atari-2600-likens-the-deluge-of-ai-made-games-to-the-glut-of-poor-quality-titles-released-ahead-of-the-us-video-game-crash-of-1983)
