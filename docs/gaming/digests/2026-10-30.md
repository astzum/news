# Fri 30 Oct 2026

### Xbox hardware revenue fell 14% on lower console volume
Microsoft's 10-Q puts total Xbox revenue down $129m, or 2%, with hardware off 14% "driven by lower volume of consoles sold" and content and services down 2% on weaker third-party sales and Game Pass. First-party grew. Xbox boss Asha Sharma blames supply, not demand; the anniversary Series X arrives in November.
[Microsoft 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436133/msft-20260930.htm) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/xbox-first-party-content-partially-offsets-2-drop-in-q1-revenue-hardware-decreases-14)

### The Duskbloods has slipped to 2027, with no date
FromSoftware's Switch 2 exclusive was vague-2026 and is now vague-2027; both the studio's own portfolio page and Nintendo's store list the new window. The stated reason is tuning "game rules and balancing" after August's network test, which drew more applicants than Elden Ring Nightreign despite the single-platform lock.
[FromSoftware](https://www.fromsoftware.jp/ww/) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/fromsoftwares-switch-2-exclusive-the-duskbloods-has-been-delayed-to-2027)

### Modern Warfare 4's opening cinematic uses real Itaewon crush footage
MBC matched the game's fake news broadcast — CPR, police tape, bodies on a road — to 2022 archive footage. Bereaved families and a citizens' group demanded its removal on the eve of the fourth anniversary. Activision says the clips are "not intended to reference or trivialize the Itaewon disaster" and has not said whether it will patch them.
[Kyunghyang](https://weekly.khan.co.kr/article/202610281634001) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/modern-warfare-4-south-korean-crowd-crush-footage)

### Celeste's developers pulled it from the Xbox store over the BDS boycott
Maddy Thorson confirmed on Bluesky that Celeste and TowerFall were delisted on Xbox deliberately, in support of the boycott of Microsoft over services supplied to the Israeli military. Both remain on PlayStation and Nintendo. House House kept Big Walk off Xbox earlier this month, so this is a pattern now, not a one-off.
[Maddy Thorson](https://bsky.app/profile/maddymakesgames.com/post/3myzioij5vk2f) · [Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/celeste-and-towerfall-have-been-removed-from-the-xbox-store-in-a-move-to-support-the-bds-boycott-against-microsoft)

### Capcom sold 36.86m games in six months, up from 23.85m
Half-year net sales hit ¥120.9bn, up 49%, with operating profit up 54.5% to ¥60.8bn — new IP Pragmata and Onimusha: Way of the Sword plus heavy catalogue discounting across 260 titles. Capcom's model of permanently re-selling its back catalogue is now doing more work than any single launch.
[Capcom IR](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/finance/review.html)
