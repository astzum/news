# Wed 28 Oct 2026

### Microsoft is offering a buyout to a quarter of the company, Xbox included
The Voluntary Exit Offer went out in a Monday memo to about 60,000 people across the US, UK, Canada and India, level 70 and below. The Verge reports Xbox is a participating team; sales is not. Terms are standard severance, decisions are due in the first week of December, and Microsoft says this does not replace layoffs.
[GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-makes-new-voluntary-exit-offer-to-more-than-a-quarter-of-its-workforce-including-some-execs/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/xbox-staff-among-up-to-60000-microsoft-employees-offered-voluntary-exit)

### Rockstar's UK staff will vote on union recognition during GTA 6 launch week
The IWGB says the ballot runs 12 to 26 November, with GTA 6 out on the 19th. The deal, already agreed with management, would cover pay and bonuses, overtime, redundancies, flexible working and generative AI policy, and would make this the largest recognised union in UK games. Closing arguments in the tribunal over last year's 31 sackings land 17-18 November.
[Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/uk-based-rockstar-devs-will-be-putting-their-union-recognition-to-a-vote-during-gta-6s-launch-next-month)

### Warhorse has been hit by what it calls "a serious incident"
The Kingdom Come: Deliverance studio says it is working with specialists to secure its systems and establish the full extent, and has said nothing yet about data. CEO Martin Frývaldský named employees, partners and community as the exposure. Warhorse is building a Tolkien open-world RPG and a new Kingdom Come, so there is a lot to leak.
[Warhorse](https://www.warhorsestudios.cz/en/incident) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/warhorse-cyberattack-kingdom-come-deliverance)

### Pokémon Unite closes in March, and you can no longer buy anything in it
Aeos Gem sales stopped on 27 October and Unite Club signups are shut; servers go dark 23 March 2027 at 00:00 UTC. A 12 November update adds a Library feature that unlocks every Unite licence and Holowear the game has had — but you have to download it before the servers close, or it is gone.
[Pokémon Unite](https://unite.pokemon.com/en-us/news/unite-end-of-service/)

### Phantom Blade Zero wants 300GB free to pre-load a 145GB game
S-Game's Steam notice asks PC players to reserve at least 300GB and install on an SSD; the game itself is roughly 145GB and you get the rest back. The reason is that temporary and final files coexist during the unlock. It goes live Thursday at 1pm AEDT on PC and PS5.
