# Thu 26 Nov 2026

Memory prices pin consoles where they are, and the week's biggest game has a surprise favourite.

### Console prices are not coming down before 2028
S&P Global Kagan's numbers: DRAM wafer capacity additions reach 184,000 this year, 434,000 by end-2027, then 819,000 in 2028 — the earliest point analyst Neil Barbour calls prudent for launching a console at an accessible price. Data-centre demand could swallow the extra supply anyway. He expects no cuts through 2027. Project Helix, the next Xbox, is rumoured for 2028.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/analyst-dont-expect-hardware-price-cuts-until-at-least-2028)

### GTA 6 holds its frame-rate better on Series X than on PS5
Digital Foundry compared all four current consoles. PS5 and Series X both render at 1440p with TAA, target 30fps and look near-identical, but PS5 "suffers substantially more frequent frame-rate drops" in heavy traversal and airport crowds, and has no 120Hz VRR to smooth the dips. PS5 Pro wins outright on PSSR; Series S sinks to the mid-teens.
[Digital Foundry](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/the-big-gta-6-console-comparison-ps5-vs-ps5-pro-xbox-series-x-and-series-s)

### EA switches on an AI-cloned dead commentator in FC 27 today
John Motson was the series' first real commentator, from FIFA 96 to FIFA 2005, and died in 2023. EA synthesised his voice from old recording sessions plus generative AI, paid his widow and son for the rights, and turns it on 26 November. Campaigner Peter Caulfield: "being a family member shouldn't automatically give you that right either."
[BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c65y10j841jpo)

### Team Cherry will finally talk about Silksong's Sea of Sorrow on Friday
"Cherry Time" premieres on YouTube at 10am ET Friday — 2am Saturday AEDT — with "a bunch of exciting announcements" and an update on the DLC. The nautical expansion was teased last December for sometime in 2026, which leaves the Adelaide studio five weeks to either ship it or move it.
[Team Cherry](https://www.teamcherry.com.au/blog/cherrytimeannounce)

### A union hardship fund has paid $290,000 to more than 150 laid-off developers
United Videogame Workers-CWA's impact report credits member dues, other game unions, and the 1,800-plus people who bought the itch.io bundle Necrosoft ran mid-year. Of the recipients, 30% have since found work and over 75% say they now intend to organise their own workplace. A Game Worker Bill of Rights is next.
[UVW-CWA](https://uvw-cwa.org/news/hardship-2026)
