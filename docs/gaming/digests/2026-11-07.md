# Sat 07 Nov 2026

### The buyers who took EA private are reportedly circling Unity
Reuters says Saudi Arabia's PIF and Silver Lake — two of the backers of the $55bn EA buyout — have held talks to acquire Unity at a valuation near $25bn, against a market cap of about $21.5bn. Unconfirmed and early: no agreement exists. Silver Lake already owns roughly 8% and sits on Unity's board.
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/silver-lake-pif-have-held-recent-talks-buy-gaming-software-provider-unity-2026-11-05/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/unity-buyout-saudi-arabia-pif-ea-silver-lake-engine-controversy-2000754368)

### Ubisoft is one of three reported bidders for Arkane Lyon
Bloomberg's sources say Xbox has multiple suitors for the Dishonored studio, with a management buyout also under consideration — the exit Double Fine and Compulsion took, both of which regained their IP. Xbox will only say it is "exploring options ahead of a December deadline", after extending Arkane's consultation period while Marvel's Blade development continues.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/ubisoft-reportedly-seeking-to-acquire-arkane-lyon-from-xbox)

### The UK ad regulator is now banning mobile ads that don't show the game
Four rulings landed Wednesday. King's Farm Heroes Saga ad showed falling nails and a saw descending on a cow, none of it in the game; Scopely's Pixel Flow ad opened on a pipe puzzle that doesn't exist; Omnilojo's Dark War Survival invented award badges. A "Not actual gameplay" disclaimer did not save King.
[ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/king-com-ltd.html) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/advertising-standards-authority-bans-mobile-ads-from-king-scopely-and-others-for-misrepresenting-gameplay)

### World of Warcraft's third branch launched without the usual queues
WoW: Forever is live as a permanent sibling to retail and Classic: a reimagined level 1–60 run across Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, 1,000-plus new quests, the Skyborne race, and per-server rulesets. The megaserver tech held — short queues, little lag — leaving broken zeppelins and thinly populated shards as the main complaints.
[Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/world-of-warcraft-forever-launch-smooth)

### Borderlands has crossed 100 million units sold
Gearbox put the franchise figure in a community letter marking a year of Borderlands 4 post-launch content. The same letter commits 2K Foundations and Gearbox to matching donations to The Hunt, which starts today and raised a record $118,995.80 for St. Jude last time, potentially pushing the total past $230,000.
[Gearbox](https://borderlands.2k.com/borderlands-4/news/message-to-borderlands-community/)
