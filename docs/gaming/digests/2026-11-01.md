# Sun 01 Nov 2026

### id Software confirms about half the studio left in Xbox's cuts
Asked at a Doom: The Dark Ages – Revelations press event how the studio was doing, co-director Marty Stratton said "we're doing great" — then, "everybody knows we had about half of our studio exit." id is now "working more closely with MachineGames than ever" on engine technology. IGN reads that as a hint toward the rumoured Wolfenstein 3.
[IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xboxs-doom-wolfenstein-developers-working-more-closely-than-ever-following-xbox-reset-layoffs)

### The Elder Scrolls 6 is reportedly called Sentinel, with the name due next month
Unconfirmed: The Verge's Tom Warren, citing sources familiar with Bethesda's plans, expects a name reveal and nothing more, timed to Skyrim's fifteenth anniversary. He also reports the game is set in Hammerfell and is the first Elder Scrolls built around the scrolls themselves. Bethesda has been showing gameplay internally; Asha Sharma saw a playthrough in August.
[The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022023/microsoft-exit-offer-layoffs-notepad)

### Final Fantasy 14's Evercold has slipped from January to 23 February
Square Enix's July press release said January 2027; the Lodestone now gives 23 February, early access from the 19th and pre-orders from 4 November. No reason offered. The expansion adds two jobs — Bastion (tank) and Zephyr (physical ranged) — raises the level cap to 110, and brings an Evangelion alliance raid, Ghosts of Desire.
[The Lodestone](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/topics/detail/8874f1c53cc82ba16ae400053034af16e65dbe3f) · [Evercold site](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/evercold/)

### Walmart is giving $519 gift cards to Zelda Switch 2 buyers whose orders it cancelled
The retailer oversold the Ocarina of Time 40th Anniversary console, then cancelled pre-orders the day before delivery. Affected customers now have an email calling the cancellations an error, an "Exclusive Reservation" in their cart good for 48 hours, and in some cases a gift card worth the console's full $519 — making it effectively free.
[Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/if-walmart-canceled-your-zelda-switch-2-pre-order-the-stores-giving-you-48-hours-to-get-a-new-one-2000751739) · [Wario64](https://bsky.app/profile/wario64.bsky.social/post/3mz55bosta26x)

### Nintendo has named 1 November Pikmin Day, and marked it with a Direct carrying no new games
Miyamoto's logic: three Pikmin side by side look like 11.1. The substance was Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy on 12 November, with voice commands for Oatchi and timed treasure runs scored every Friday to Sunday. Pikmin Bloom gets Secret Caves this winter; a Treehouse stream runs today.
[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/nintendo-direct/11-1-2026/)
