# Thu 05 Nov 2026

### Ocarina of Time is the best-reviewed game of 2026
Out today on Switch 2, the remake sits at 95 on OpenCritic with all 44 critics recommending it — two points clear of Mythmatch at the top of the year. Eurogamer gave it 5/5, IGN and GameSpot 9/10. Forbes supplies the lone grumble, calling the new character designs "off-putting".
[OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21344/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time)

### God of War Laufey slips three months to 20 May 2027
Santa Monica Studio gave no reason beyond "putting in the finishing touches and pushing the game to its fullest potential". The move clears a February 2027 that already holds the Fable reboot, Persona 4 Revival and Metro 2039. Faye's game is the first in the series not led by Kratos.
[Santa Monica Studio](https://x.com/SonySantaMonica/status/2118025571020271817) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/god-of-war-laufey-delayed-may-three-months)

### Arkane's union says Microsoft manufactured the reasons to close it
French union STJV claims a 19-month hiring freeze "artificially caused delay and additional cost" that Microsoft then cited as grounds for closure, and that Arkane's cash is systematically funnelled upward by group cash-pooling. 217 staff are at risk. The demand: €200,000 redundancy each plus two years' redeployment support. "Arkane is profitable. Just not profitable enough."
[STJV](https://www.stjv.fr/en/2026/11/arkane-studios-might-close-without-having-ever-seen-failure/) · [RPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/arkane-is-profitable-say-marvels-blade-developers-in-fiery-new-statement-just-not-profitable-enough-for-microsoft)

### Nintendo's cartridge supply has lost a chip maker
Macronix told its Q3 call it is phasing out ROM products — the chips inside Switch and Switch 2 cards — with no new parts coming and Q3 the last quarter it reports ROM sales separately. MST analyst David Gibson expects more Game-Key Cards as a result. ROM was 5% of sales, down from 26%.
[David Gibson](https://x.com/gibbogame/status/2117805196130582970) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-switch-2-cartridge-macronix-ending-rom-manufacturing)

### Nexus Mods will label AI mods rather than ban them
Owner Victor Folmann argues a ban "will not eliminate AI from the modding process but will prevent people from mentioning its use". Uploads must now declare themselves AI-generated, AI-assisted or neither, with the tag shown just after adult-content warnings. Every existing mod is covered too, with a grace period to 2 January.
[PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/nexus-mods-takes-the-valve-approach-to-ai-generated-content-no-outright-bans-but-mandatory-disclosures/)
