# Mon 26 Oct 2026

### Capcom has told a fan frame-data site to delete its Street Fighter 6 section
Ultimate Frame Data's MetalMusicMan published the letter from Capcom's IP department, which asks him to pull the SF6 pages by 30 October. Capcom's objection is that hitbox and collision data "is not ordinarily visible or accessible through normal gameplay", and that it does not authorise the modding tools used to extract it. The site's Smash Ultimate data is untouched.
[Ultimate Frame Data](https://ultimateframedata.com/)

### Indies took 38% of first-month sales among the year's big launches
Sensor Tower counted games passing 500,000 units in month one: 76 in the year to August 2026, up from 55. Indies were 33 of those, up from 13, and 35 titles were brand-new IP. Co-op party games drove roughly half of indie sales but only nine of the 33 hits. AAA was flat at 28.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/indies-and-original-ip-blew-up-in-2026)

### Canada's opposition leader wants federal money behind a BioWare worker buyout
Avi Lewis, who leads the New Democratic Party, posted the evening the closure was announced that the Edmonton studio's developers "should be given the first opportunity - and the federal support - to buy it and run it themselves". There is no mechanism for any of that, but it is the loudest political response to EA's cuts so far.
[Avi Lewis](https://bsky.app/profile/avilewis.ca/post/3myfzwhsfbs2s)

### An indie dev cut the AI voices from his anti-capitalist satire and offered refunds
Paolo Pedercini replaced two text-to-speech robots in Future? No Thanks! with human actors, after disclosing the AI use himself and getting piled on for it. He will refund anyone who still feels betrayed, by PayPal or Venmo. His own verdict: "the game is worse now, but only, like, 0.001% worse."
[Molleindustria](https://www.molleindustria.org/blog/)

### Knightmare is returning as a free point-and-click with its original cast
Ewen Watson's officially licensed adventure is hand-drawn, fully voiced and due on PC in 2027, the ITV show's 40th anniversary. Hugo Myatt is back as Treguard and Mark Knight as Lord Fear, with six more of the original cast behind them. Tim Child, who created the series in 1987, is credited on the game.
[Knightmare](https://www.knightmaregame.co.uk/)
