# Sat 31 Oct 2026

### EA closed BioWare to pay for Star Wars Battlefront 3
Bloomberg reports executives reviewing the slate against $18bn of buyout debt found two sci-fi action games each costing hundreds of millions, and killed the riskier one. Battlefront 3 hasn't started development; the next Mass Effect had a trailer cut. EA says the decision followed "months of planning, review, and analysis". DICE and Respawn are reportedly taking the Star Wars game.
[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-30/why-electronic-arts-shut-down-bioware-and-cancelled-mass-effect) · [Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/ea-execs-choosing-star-wars-battlefront-3-over-mass-effect-5-was-the-main-factor-in-biowares-demise-according-to-report)

### Roblox bookings fell 12% as it bet the platform on generative AI
Q3 bookings were $1.7bn on revenue of $1.6bn, with Q4 guided to fall another 10-15%. The shareholder letter spends more space on AI than on that: over 80% of the top 100 games used AI tools last month, and a "Universal Player" initiative would import any existing console or PC game into Roblox.
[Roblox shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)

### Rockstar's UK union vote would cover more than 1,600 workers
The IWGB told Game File the bargaining unit is nearly everyone below director level at the UK studios, out of 4,700 staff worldwide. Terms agreed with management cover pay, overtime, redundancies and generative AI policy. Ballots run 12-26 November, bracketing GTA 6's console launch on the 19th.
[Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/rockstar-union-size-xbox-missing-reviews)

### Activision will cut the Itaewon footage from Modern Warfare 4 on 12 November
Two days after saying the archive clips were never meant to evoke the 2022 crowd crush, Activision apologised to bereaved families and committed to removing them in a scheduled update. Korea's interior minister had called the use "secondary harm" and said sanctions were warranted.
[Inven](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=322788) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/activision-apologizes-and-says-it-will-remove-south-korean-crush-footage-from-modern-warfare-4-after-victims-families-speak-out/)

### Romero Games saved its shooter by rebuilding it for 16 people
John Romero says the game Microsoft defunded has a new, unnamed publisher. The studio fell from 110 staff to five, then rehired ten leads. The original is unfinishable — "it's a 110-person game" — so he designed a smaller one around the art and code they already own outright.
[VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/john-romero-confirms-his-axed-xbox-game-has-a-new-publisher-and-hes-rehired-some-staff/)
