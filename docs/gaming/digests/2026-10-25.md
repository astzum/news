# Sun 25 Oct 2026

### Xbox's first chief operating officer lasted three months
Asha Sharma told staff in a short Friday memo that Helen Chiang was leaving Microsoft that day, after 21 years there and three months in a role invented for the Xbox "reset". Her responsibilities are being split between other leaders. Microsoft's Xbox communications VP would not say why: "We don't discuss individual personnel matters."
[The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/1018037/xbox-chief-operating-officer-helen-chiang-departure)

### Creative Assembly disowns the Shogun 2 remaster and rules out AI-made art
Total War VP Roger Collum apologised on Reddit for the Complete Edition, pulled from sale last week over bugs and apparent AI upscaling: "This was not a Creative Assembly developed game." The studio says it uses AI for debugging and prototyping but never for assets, and demands the same of partners. Sega is investigating; Mataboo Games is the listed co-developer.
[Rock Paper Shotgun](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/this-was-not-a-creative-assembly-developed-game-total-war-boss-points-the-finger-at-external-partner-for-shogun-2-complete-edition-disaster)

### Factorio has shipped what Wube calls its last major update
2.1 went stable on Friday, two years after Space Age. No new planets, enemies or research: mixed rockets, platform-to-platform transfers, circuit-network additions, a rebalanced biter expansion. It also adds native ARM64 Linux, so it now runs on Steam Frame and Raspberry Pi servers. Wube said in May it would then move to long-term support only.
[Factorio](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-449)

### Miyamoto is the first game developer to receive Japan's Order of Culture
The government named him one of eight 2026 recipients, under media arts; the Emperor presents it on Culture Day, 3 November. Nintendo announced the honour at a Kyoto press conference on Thursday. Miyamoto, who took the lesser Person of Cultural Merit award in 2019, said the achievements "were not the work of one person" and accepted on his colleagues' behalf.
[Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/shigeru-miyamoto-becomes-first-game-developer-to-receive-the-order-of-culture-japans-highest-cultural-honor/)
