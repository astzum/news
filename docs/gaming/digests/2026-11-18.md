# Wed 18 Nov 2026

### A UK tribunal has thrown out the £2bn claim against the PlayStation Store
Alex Neill's case for roughly 12.2 million players argued digital prices would be about 20% lower without Sony's conduct. The Competition Appeal Tribunal called the proposed market definition "unsafe" — physical discs and switching to Xbox both constrain Sony — so consoles and games are one "systems market", and Sony was not shown to be dominant.
[Competition Appeal Tribunal](https://www.catribunal.org.uk/judgments/15277722-alex-neill-class-representative-limited-v-sony-interactive-entertainment-3) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/playstation-store-pricing-uk-class-action-lawsuit-result)

### Firaxis is losing a third of its staff and the studio head who ran it
Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reports 60 redundancies at the 30-year-old Civilization and XCOM studio, Heather Hazen among them. 2K calls it "recentering around the studio's core strength". An unannounced XCOM sequel reportedly missed a significant milestone over the summer and may now face a shakeup. Firaxis also cut staff in September 2025.
[GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/report-civilization-dev-firaxis-lays-off-a-third-of-its-staff-following-development-difficulties-with-xcom-sequel) · [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/civilization-dev-firaxis-reportedly-lays-off-a-third-of-its-staff-studio-head-departs/)

### Game of the Year is a race between a game nobody has played and one from 1998
GTA 6, out tomorrow, leads with eight nominations. The rest of the shortlist: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and the Ocarina of Time remake. Control and Requiem took six nods each. A jury of 160-plus outlets picked them; public voting closes 9 December, ceremony the 10th.
[Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-announced) · [The Game Awards](https://thegameawards.com/nominees)

### GTA 6 is forecast to take a tenth of everyone else's players
Sensor Tower puts pre-orders at $1.1bn on 11.3m copies as of 10 November — the largest pre-launch total it has recorded, roughly double Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 at the same point. It expects daily players across every other game to drop more than 10% in the month after launch, Fortnite worst hit.
[Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/gta-vi-breaks-record-greater-than-1b-pre-order-or-sensor-tower-report)

### Unity bought an asset store to stock its AI game-maker with art it owns
Synty's 60,000-plus low-poly POLYGON assets are going into Unity Spark, the AI-native creation tool entering closed beta later this year. Unity says the brand, pricing, the Synty Store and support for Unreal and Godot all stay as they are, and most of the artists join full-time. Announced at Unite Tokyo.
[Unity](https://unity.com/blog/synty-studios-acquisition)
