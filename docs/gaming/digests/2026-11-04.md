# Wed 04 Nov 2026

### The Elder Scrolls 6 is called Sentinel, and that is all you get
Bethesda Game Studios broke an eight-year silence with a 40th-anniversary retrospective video and two posts: the name, and Hammerfell as the location. Its own words — "it'll be some time before we're ready to share more". A companion lore post dates the setting to 4E 206, under "High Queen Iradne of Hammerfell". No window, no footage.
[Bethesda Game Studios](https://bsky.app/profile/bethesdastudios.com/post/3mzg5pmb4mk2p) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvcfI8RPZYc)

### GTA 6 pre-loads on 10 November and wants 212GB
Rockstar dated it nine days before the 19 November launch. PlayStation's post asks PS5 owners to clear "a minimum of 212GB" and unlocks at local midnight; Xbox Series X|S opens simultaneously worldwide at midnight GMT, which is 11am AEDT. Pre-order required either way. No day-one patch size given, so treat 212GB as the floor.
[Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) · [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/) · [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/grand-theft-auto-6-pre-load-date)

### Call of Duty's launch week was smaller than last year's on console
Circana's Player Engagement Tracker put Modern Warfare 4 at 19% of active US PlayStation players in the week to 24 October, against 23% for Black Ops 7. Xbox fell from 29% worldwide to 17% — no day-one Game Pass this time. Steam went the other way, 1.5% to 4.2%.
[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-player-engagement-black-ops-7)

### itch.io has started deleting the AI slop rather than just graphing it
Two days after posting that new game pages had doubled, the store said it had wiped "a couple terabytes" and is extending its generative-AI disclosure filter to search results, where it had been "critically lacking". Tabletop, books, comics and soundtracks now need a disclosure to publish. The spam clause is two years old; the enforcement is new.
[itch.io](https://bsky.app/profile/itch.io/post/3mzev6ghw5s2x)

### Remedy tripled its revenue and still barely broke even
Control Resonant took July–September revenue to €39.6m from €12.2m — the studio's fastest-selling game, past a million copies by 15 October. Operating profit was €0.6m, a 1.5% margin, against a €16.4m loss a year earlier. Across nine months EBIT is still negative, at -€2.3m.
