# Mon 09 Nov 2026

### Canada's Parliament will have to answer a demand that it buy BioWare back
E-petition e-7898 asks the government to fund BioWare's independence and the purchase of its IP from EA. It has 6,114 validated signatures, well past the 500 needed for certification, and is sponsored by NDP MP Don Davies. Signing closes 4 March 2027; a government response is then due within 45 days.
[House of Commons](https://www.ourcommons.ca/petitions/en/Petition/Details?Petition=e-7898)

### Epic has published Fortnite's treasure odds: 1 in 137,500 per chest
A week of players gutting chests for Pirate Shop Access — which makes most Item Shop items free for 14 days — ended with fans trying to report Epic to the FTC over undisclosed odds. Epic's numbers: 1:137,500 a chest, 1:1,562,500 for the Gold Outfit, 1:127 for any Gizmo. Treasure Hours multiply those.
[Fortnite Status](https://x.com/FortniteStatus/status/2119202610322227327) · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/epic-reveals-drop-rate-for-fortnites-pirate-token-following-pressure-from-fans)

### SAG-AFTRA will let studios hire union actors before they have funding
The Pre-Funding Pitch Interactive Media Agreement covers demos, vertical slices, trailers and playable prototypes made to raise money. Projects qualify only with 30% or less of initial production funding secured, not appearing complete, not crowdfunded and not publicly playable. It targets the gap where small studios lose pitches to better-looking ones.
[VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/union-actors-could-appear-in-early-game-demos-under-new-sag-aftra-plan/) · [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/sag-aftra-launches-new-video-game-agreement-enabling-devs-to-hire-professional-actors-for-demos-and-vertical-slices)

### Stellar Blade runs on Switch 2 partly because it was never demanding
Digital Foundry calls Shift Up's Unreal Engine 4 game "a better than expected Switch 2 port, with gentler compromises than many more recent titles" — it never leaned on current-gen tech. The 60fps mode is frame-generated; turn that off and it sits near 40fps, which DF reckons should have been the target.
[Digital Foundry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=clNHjJi00Bw) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/digital-foundry-delivers-its-tech-verdict-on-stellar-blades-switch-2-port)

### GOG Galaxy is finally coming to Linux, in 2027
GOG used its first showcase to commit to a native Linux client plus rebuilt Windows and Mac ones, "simpler, run faster", with offline installers untouched and Galaxy still optional. It also announced GOG Social, a community feed. Heroic and Lutris already do this job, so the news is a storefront this size turning up at all.
[GOG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LcjptSS0-e4) · [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/11/a-new-gog-galaxy-is-coming-in-2027-with-full-linux-support/)
